# Rachel Darcy — Individual Backstory Working File

## Confirmed Facts

- **Birthday:** December 25, 2027
- Rachel Darcy and Jessica Isabella are born as conjoined identical twins joined at the lower abdomen.
- Rachel Darcy and Jessica Isabella share one reproductive system and one urinary tract at birth.
- Rachel Darcy and Jessica Isabella are Mike and Terri Anne's biological daughters.

## Tissue Expansion and Separation

- When Rachel Darcy and Jessica Isabella are three months old, doctors begin placing balloon tissue expanders beneath the twins' skin.
- The tissue expanders prepare additional skin for closure between three and six months of age.
- At eight months old, Rachel Darcy and Jessica Isabella undergo complete separation surgery.
- Doctors divide and reconstruct the urinary anatomy so Rachel Darcy and Jessica Isabella each have separate urinary function.
- The shared reproductive system can be given to only one twin.
- Mike and Terri Anne make the extremely difficult decision that Jessica Isabella will receive the reproductive system.
- Rachel Darcy does not receive the shared reproductive system.
- When Rachel Darcy and Jessica Isabella are seven years old, Jessica Isabella promises Rachel Darcy that Jessica Isabella will someday donate eggs to help Rachel Darcy build Rachel Darcy's family.
- At eleven, while police interview Jessica Isabella during treatment for severe scald burns in Miami, an emotional Jessica Isabella tells the officers about being born conjoined with Rachel Darcy and about the childhood promise.
- The police close the case after interviewing Jessica Isabella, Mike, and Terri Anne about the kitchen accident.
- During Jessica Isabella's later treatment in Boston, Rachel Darcy and the other older children repeatedly ask Mike, Terri Anne, and Aldoma Ferreira about Jessica Isabella's condition.
- Rachel Darcy misses Jessica Isabella deeply during the hospitalization.
- Jessica Isabella and Rachel Darcy share a bedroom, and Jessica Isabella's absence from the room is overwhelming for Rachel Darcy.
- Rachel Darcy cannot bring herself to practise ballet while Jessica Isabella is away.
- Mike, Terri Anne, and Aldoma Ferreira repeatedly reassure Rachel Darcy that Jessica Isabella will recover.
- During the final month of Jessica Isabella's hospitalization, approximately May 13 through June 13, 2039, Rachel Darcy is allowed to visit Jessica Isabella.
- Rachel Darcy regards Jessica Isabella as both her sister and her best friend.
- Rachel Darcy lies beside Jessica Isabella on the hospital bed while Jessica Isabella remains positioned safely for the burns and grafts on her back.
- Rachel Darcy and Jessica Isabella speak together in the twins' private twin language.
- Mike and Terri Anne leave the hospital room to give the twins privacy when Mike and Terri Anne see the twins communicating in their own language.
- Outside the room, Terri Anne breaks down crying in Mike's arms because Mike and Terri Anne nearly lost Jessica Isabella.
- Mike, Terri Anne, and Aldoma Ferreira help Jessica Isabella call Rachel Darcy, the other siblings, and Emma from the hospital.
- The family stays at Mike and Terri Anne's New York City penthouse and uses a Key Corporation plane for travel connected to Jessica Isabella's care.
- Every time Terri Anne leaves the burn unit, Terri Anne falls apart in Mike's arms because Terri Anne cannot bear seeing Jessica Isabella hurt.
- The childhood promise is later reaffirmed as Jessica Isabella's adult decision.
- Every one of Rachel Darcy's seven children is conceived using an egg donated by Jessica Isabella.

## Early Development and Estrogen Treatment

- While Rachel Darcy and Jessica Isabella are conjoined, Rachel Darcy and Jessica Isabella are unable to use their legs.
- After the separation surgery, Rachel Darcy and Jessica Isabella have delayed physical and motor development while learning to use their legs.
- Rachel Darcy and Jessica Isabella's teething occurs on time.
- At nine months old, one month after the separation surgery, Rachel Darcy and Jessica Isabella begin talking to each other in their private twin language.
- Mike and Terri Anne are amazed that Rachel Darcy and Jessica Isabella have developed their own way of communicating.
- Aldoma Ferreira is not surprised.
- Aldoma Ferreira explains that the twins build their private communication through repeated sounds, gestures, facial expressions, and early speech patterns that Rachel Darcy and Jessica Isabella learn to recognize through constant interaction.
- Rachel Darcy and Jessica Isabella's broader speech development occurs on time.
- Because Rachel Darcy does not receive the shared reproductive system, Rachel Darcy begins using estrogen patches at three years old.
- Rachel Darcy continues estrogen-patch treatment throughout Rachel Darcy's life.

## Supporting Sabrina Bryna at Five

- Rachel Darcy and Jessica Isabella are in the room when Mike and Terri Anne tell five-year-old Sabrina Bryna that another father complained about Sabrina Bryna using a mat at Burlington Gymnastics Club.
- Rachel Darcy and Jessica Isabella immediately hug Sabrina Bryna because Rachel Darcy and Jessica Isabella love Sabrina Bryna deeply.
- Rachel Darcy and Jessica Isabella tell Mike and Terri Anne that Sabrina Bryna has every right to do gymnastics.
- Mike and Terri Anne's hearts break as Mike and Terri Anne hear the twins' kind and protective words about Sabrina Bryna.

## Homeschooling and World Travel

- Mike and Terri Anne homeschool Rachel Darcy as part of the shared education plan for all eight children.
- Homeschooling allows Rachel Darcy to travel around the world with the family while continuing structured lessons.
- Travel experiences become part of Rachel Darcy's education, while outside activities and later university study remain available.

## Temple University and Meeting Antoine

- Rachel Darcy attends Temple University in Philadelphia.
- Rachel Darcy and Antoine meet at Temple University.
- Rachel Darcy and Antoine take a class together called **Ballet Enchainments**.
- The shared class brings Rachel Darcy and Antoine into each other's lives.
- Rachel Darcy lives in a single one-bedroom apartment in Morgan Hall during Rachel Darcy's first year.
- Rachel Darcy's Morgan Hall apartment includes a private bathroom and kitchen.
- During Rachel Darcy's second year, Mike and Terri Anne buy a house close to Temple University for Rachel Darcy.
- Antoine is always welcome to stay overnight at the house.
- At the end of their second year, Rachel Darcy and Antoine decide to begin living together in the house.

## Rachel Darcy and Antoine Become Parents

- Every one of Rachel Darcy's seven children is conceived using an egg donated by Jessica Isabella.
- Rachel Darcy and Antoine work with two women who serve as gestational surrogates.
- The first surrogate is twenty-seven years old and has previously given birth to one child.
- The first surrogate needs the compensation to help pay university debts.
- The first surrogate carries Larissa Leola and Armando Cortez Junior.
- The second surrogate is twenty-two years old and has previously given birth to two children.
- The second surrogate's partner is a deadbeat who does not provide dependable support.
- The second surrogate first carries Kella Nakeisha and Armella Beatrice.
- In a later pregnancy, the second surrogate carries triplets Melissa Lizette, Marcos Eduarte, and Annabella Nicki.
- Both surrogates remain part of the children's lives after the births.
- The first surrogate's husband is an evil man who uses the family's continuing contact to gain access to Armando Cortez Junior.
- The first surrogate's husband sexually abuses Armando Cortez Junior.
- The story treats this clearly as child abuse and never as Armando Cortez Junior's fault.
- Both surrogates kindly offer to pump breast milk for the first week after each birth because of the newborn children's health needs.
- Rachel Darcy and Antoine accept the surrogates' offer.
- Rachel Darcy wants to experience nursing Rachel Darcy's children.
- Before Larissa Leola and Armando Cortez Junior are born, Rachel Darcy follows a specialist-supervised nine-week induced-lactation protocol involving prescribed birth-control medication and a specific estrogen-and-progesterone medication.
- Rachel Darcy and Antoine contact a veterinarian who works at **6666**, one of Key's properties.
- The veterinarian helps Rachel Darcy and Antoine obtain and adapt a cow milking machine for Rachel Darcy's induced-lactation plan.
- Rachel Darcy's medical specialist supervises the prescribed medication plan but does not observe or supervise Rachel Darcy's use of the equipment.
- Rachel Darcy uses the adapted machine privately before the twins' birth to stimulate and express milk production.
- When the children are old enough to understand, Rachel Darcy and Antoine explain honestly how Rachel Darcy and Antoine became their parents and how Jessica Isabella and the two surrogates helped build the family.

## Rachel Darcy and Antoine

- Rachel Darcy and Antoine have a seven-child family.
- Rachel Darcy and Antoine live in Los Angeles with the seven children.
- When Melissa Lizette, Marcos Eduarte, and Annabella Nicki are five months old, Antoine suffers a massive, fatal heart attack.
- After Antoine's sudden death, Rachel Darcy wants a major change for Rachel Darcy and the children.
- Rachel Darcy decides to move the family from Los Angeles to New York City.
- Terri Anne helps Rachel Darcy with everything during the loss and transition.
- Terri Anne provides emotional support, helps care for the seven children, assists with packing and travel, and helps the family settle into New York City.
- The girls attend Convent of the Sacred Heart.
- The boys attend The Allen-Stevenson School and later Xavier High School.
