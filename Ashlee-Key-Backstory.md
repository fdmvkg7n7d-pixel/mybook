- On Christmas Day in New York City, Geoffrey proposes to Ashlee.
- Ashlee answers yes.

# Part Eight — Victoria, Jack, and Ric Attack the Relationship

## 42. Jack and Ric arrive in Los Angeles

- Jack and Ric unexpectedly visit Ashlee and Geoffrey's home.
- They mock and challenge the couple's relationship.
- Their behaviour reflects a larger campaign connected to Victoria and the struggle over Key Corporation.
- Ashlee and Geoffrey remain together despite the harassment.

## 43. Victoria invades the mansion

- While Mike is flying to Los Angeles, Victoria breaks into Ashlee and Geoffrey's mansion.
- She enters their bedroom while they sleep and sits on their bed.
- Victoria mocks Ashlee's right-arm sleeve tattoo, disability, relationship, and supportive equipment.

- Victoria breaks Ashlee and Geoffrey's iPhones.
- She destroys Ashlee's regular wheelchair.
- She damages the adapted pedals on Ashlee's grand piano.
- Ashlee must temporarily rely on her special power chair because her regular chair is unusable.
- Geoffrey remains with Ashlee instead of chasing Victoria.

## 45. Security remains after Mike leaves

- Mike places guards around Ashlee and Geoffrey's mansion.
- The guards remain when Mike returns to Toronto.
- The continuing security recognizes that Victoria and the new assassin remain threats.
- Protection does not remove Ashlee's right to control her home and movements.

# Part Nine — Key Porn Studios Los Angeles

## 46. Ashlee visits the studio

- Ashlee goes to Key Porn Studios Los Angeles.
- Two actresses mock her special power chair.
- Ashlee refuses to let their behaviour push her out of the studio.
- She tells Mike and Scott that she wants the lead disabled-woman role.

## 47. Ashlee tells Scott that she is transgender

- Scott initially understands Ashlee only as a disabled woman seeking the role.
- Ashlee tells Scott and Mike that she is transgender.
- This is the first time Scott learns that fact.
- Ashlee also explains that the existing straight script must be revised to fit her preferences as a transgender woman.

## 48. Script revisions and audition remain separate

- The script revisions are required because of Ashlee's stated preferences.
- The revisions do not depend on whether she auditions successfully.
- Her audition is a separate assessment of her performance.
- Before filming, Ashlee is tested for sexually transmitted diseases and HIV as part of the production's health process.
- The production cannot pressure Ashlee to follow a script that ignores the boundaries she already stated.

## 49. Ashlee's two movies are released during the summer

- Ashlee's audition recording is released as her first movie.
- The recording is not merely hidden casting material, and Ashlee knows the planned use before it is published.
- Key Porn Studios has a company-wide policy requiring every movie to wait six months after filming before website release.
- Ashlee's first movie reaches the Key Porn Studios website after that six-month policy period.
- Ashlee receives sixty percent of the purchase price each time the first movie is purchased through the website.
- Ashlee also completes a second movie based on the revised production script.
- Both of Ashlee's movies are released during the summer, when she is not attending UCLA classes.
- The same six-month policy applies to every later movie Ashlee films.

## 50. Ashlee chooses her adult-film career

- When Ashlee and Geoffrey return to UCLA after the summer, fans approach Ashlee on campus.
- The attention shows that the two released movies have made her publicly recognizable.
- UCLA's president asks Ashlee whether she wants a porn career or a music career.
- Ashlee tells the president that she wants a porn career instead of becoming a composer.
- Ashlee withdraws from UCLA for now so she can concentrate on her new career.
- Her withdrawal is temporary rather than a declaration that she can never return.
- During that summer, Ashlee films four additional movies beyond the first two.
- From January through May, she films six more movies.
- Ashlee has therefore made twelve movies in total: the original two, four more during the summer, and six between January and May.
- Ashlee also works at the Key Porn Studios booth during the AVN Adult Entertainment Expo.
- At the booth, she represents the studio and meets fans who recognize her work.

## 51. Conflict on the AVN Expo floor

- Another adult performer confronts Ashlee on the Expo floor.
- The performer is jealous of Ashlee's rapid success, twelve completed movies, growing fan base, and position at the Key Porn Studios booth.
- The performer also insults Ashlee because she uses a wheelchair and questions whether she belongs at the Expo.
- Ashlee refuses to answer with personal cruelty or allow the confrontation to become physical.
- She responds firmly and professionally in front of the fans and industry workers nearby.
- Ashlee makes clear that her disability does not diminish her work, career, or right to represent the studio.
- She ends the exchange and returns to working at the Key Porn Studios booth.

# Part Ten — Ashlee's Central Character Arc

## 52. What Ashlee wants

- To be treated as a whole woman rather than reduced to being transgender or disabled
- To control decisions about her body, career, home, education, and relationships
- To concentrate on her adult-film career after withdrawing from UCLA for now
- To preserve the option of returning to her music education later
- To build an independent adult life and pursue her chosen career on her own terms
- To protect her friendships and repair the Four Musketeers' broken connection
- To decide for herself whether to marry Geoffrey
- To act without being used as a symbol by Mike, Key Corporation, or Key Porn Studios

## 53. Ashlee's strengths

- She refuses to be ashamed of her body, tattoo, wheelchair, or relationship.
- She maintains interests in music, games, puzzles, school, friendship, and acting.
- She states what she wants directly when seeking the disabled-woman role.
- She answers a jealous performer's disability insult firmly and professionally on the AVN Expo floor.
- She accepts support without surrendering every decision to the people helping her.

## 54. Ashlee's unresolved fears and conflicts

- The effect of learning that Victoria arranged the shooting meant for Mike
- Fear connected to the second assassin
- Betrayal after learning Daniela secretly helped Victoria
- The effect of Kassandra disappearing from the Four Musketeers' group chat
- The consequences of Geoffrey choosing Ashlee after his family's ultimatum
- Whether her chosen adult-film career creates new pressure
- How she balances privacy with fans recognizing her after both movies are released
