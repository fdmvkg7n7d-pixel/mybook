# Chapter Thirty-Four — Trusting Terri Anne

## 1. Mike decides to stop withholding information

- After Terri Anne openly discusses Captain Morrison, the Key Porn Studios audition, and Marco, Mike recognizes that she has trusted him with nearly every part of her life.
- He does not want their relationship to depend on information that only he possesses.
- Mike decides that trusting Terri Anne requires showing her what Bryan's investigation uncovered.
- He tells her that he has something important to share in his home office.

## 2. Mike opens the wall safe

- Mike takes Terri Anne into his private home office.
- The photographs of Ashlee and the Four Musketeers remain displayed around them.
- Mike opens the wall safe where he secured Bryan's investigation report.
- He removes the complete file and places it in front of Terri Anne.

## 3. Mike explains why the report exists

- Mike tells Terri Anne that he hired Bryan after his first encounters with Adam and Don at the Blue Velvet Room.
- Their repeated attempts to intimidate him and their unusual attention toward Scout made him suspect corruption.
- Mike asked Bryan to investigate the manager and bouncer rather than Scout personally.
- He explains that information about Terri Anne appeared because Adam was using it as part of the blackmail scheme.

## 4. Mike shows her everything

- Mike tells Terri Anne that he will not hide any part of the report from her.
- He allows her to read the full investigation rather than offering a summary designed to make him look better.
- The report includes records, surveillance, witness information, photographs, and Bryan's conclusions.
- Mike answers her questions without deciding which truths she can handle.

## 5. Terri Anne sees what Bryan knew about her

- The report identifies Scout as **Terri Anne Williams**.
- It states that she has an eight-month-old daughter named Patti Anne.
- It also contains information about her trafficking history and Adam's use of her private life as leverage.
- Terri Anne realizes that Mike knew her real name and knew about Patti Anne before she personally trusted him with either truth.

## 6. Mike explains his silence

- Mike admits that Bryan's report gave him information Terri Anne had not chosen to disclose.
- He explains that he remained silent because he did not want to force her to reveal herself or use private knowledge to gain her trust.
- Mike acknowledges that reading the report may still feel like a violation even though the information was gathered to investigate Adam.
- He does not demand that Terri Anne immediately approve of his decision.

## 7. The evidence against Adam and Don

- Terri Anne reads the evidence showing that Adam and Don were blackmailing several dancers.
- The report confirms that the cousins coordinated threats and used Don's physical presence to frighten employees.
- It includes Don's confession and the account of Adam attempting to attack him inside the manager's office.
- Terri Anne sees that Bryan documented her claims as part of a larger pattern rather than treating her as an isolated problem.

## 8. The other dancers' privacy

- The report contains sensitive information about other dancers who were victimized by Adam and Don.
- Mike asks Terri Anne to protect their privacy just as he tried to protect hers.
- Terri Anne agrees that their experiences should not become club gossip.
- Her access to the complete report becomes an act of trust carrying responsibility as well as knowledge.

## 9. The photograph from The Well

- Mike shows Terri Anne the photograph taken by Bryan's undercover investigator at The Well.
- She sees Adam meeting with Cooper and Victoria Key.
- Mike identifies Cooper as his driver and Victoria as his twenty-eight-year-old second-youngest sister.
- Terri Anne understands why Mike no longer allowed Cooper to drive him on their first date.

## 10. Mike explains his suspicion about Victoria

- Mike tells Terri Anne that he suspects Victoria may have interfered with his past relationships.
- He believes she may want him to miss the deadline in their mother's will so she can get control of Key Corporation.
- Mike admits that the complete purpose of Victoria's plan remains unknown.
- He shows Terri Anne the difference between facts Bryan has established and suspicions Mike has not yet proven.

## 11. Don's surveillance and the reconstructed clothing

- Terri Anne reads that Bryan's investigators followed Don around Hamilton.
- She sees photographs of the reconstructed blue jacket and blue hat.
- Mike explains that the investigators watched Don scatter the cut-up pieces and recovered them after he left each location.
- The evidence confirms that the man following her and Patti Anne was Don.

## 12. Terri Anne reacts

- Terri Anne is frightened by how close the conspiracy came to Patti Anne.
- She is a little angry that Mike knew so much about her before she knew who he really was.
- Her anger remains limited because she understands that the information appeared in an investigation of Adam and Don rather than an attempt to control her.
- She also recognizes that Mike protected her opportunity to reveal her real name and daughter in her own time.

## 13. Terri Anne completely understands

- Mike tells Terri Anne that she may ask any question about the report.
- He explains again that revealing the investigation too early could have warned Adam, Don, Cooper, or Victoria and endangered the dancers.
- Terri Anne tells Mike that she completely understands why he kept the report hidden.
- She does not treat his secrecy as a betrayal of their developing relationship.

## 14. Terri Anne appreciates Mike's trust

- Terri Anne realizes that Mike has shared confidential corporate information, family suspicions, and the vulnerabilities of Bryan's investigation.
- He has trusted her with everything the report says about its key people: Adam, Don, Cooper, Victoria, Bryan, the other dancers, and Terri Anne herself.
- Terri Anne tells Mike that she appreciates him trusting her with all of the information.
- Mike's decision demonstrates that he trusts her with more than romance or physical intimacy.
- Her small amount of anger gives way to understanding and appreciation.

## 15. Terri Anne worries about protecting Patti Anne

- After reading the report, Terri Anne asks Mike whether she can truly protect Patti Anne from the people involved.
- She tells him that Patti Anne is everything to her.
- The reconstructed clothing and Victoria's hidden plan make Terri Anne fear that love and vigilance may not be enough.
- Mike tells her that asking for help does not make her less capable as a mother.

## 16. Mike promises to protect Patti Anne

- Mike tells Terri Anne that she will not have to protect Patti Anne alone.
- He promises to protect Patti Anne wholeheartedly.
- Mike explains that his promise is not only about security or responsibility—he is falling in love with Terri Anne.
- He wants to protect what matters most to her while respecting that she remains Patti Anne's mother and primary decision-maker.

## 17. Terri Anne becomes emotional about Patti Anne's safety

- Terri Anne becomes deeply emotional about the danger surrounding Patti Anne.
- Mike's wholehearted promise to help protect her daughter brings Terri Anne both relief and tears.
- Patti Anne's safety matters more to Terri Anne than wealth, employment, or romance.
- While responding to Mike's promise, Terri Anne tells him that she is falling in love with him too.
- Their feelings do not remove the danger, but they establish that neither person wants to face it alone.

## 18. Mike invites them to move into the mansion

- Mike asks Terri Anne whether she and Patti Anne would like to move into his Carlisle mansion.
- He explains that the mansion and its security would make it easier to protect both of them.
- Mike emphasizes that moving is entirely Terri Anne's choice.
- He does not make his protection or affection dependent on her accepting the invitation.

## 19. Mike offers separate bedrooms

- Mike tells Terri Anne that the mansion has enough space for her to stay in a different bedroom if she wants.
- Patti Anne can also have a safe room arranged near Terri Anne.
- Mike wants her to know that moving into the mansion does not obligate her to share his bed or continue their physical relationship.
- Terri Anne may decide what arrangement makes her and Patti Anne most comfortable.

## 20. Terri Anne accepts

- Terri Anne looks at Mike in amazement and says, **“Wow, you are an unbelievable man to welcome a baby and me into your house.”**
- She accepts his invitation for herself and Patti Anne to move into the mansion.
- Terri Anne also tells Mike that she is willing and wants to share his bedroom.
- Her choice to share his room is separate from her decision to accept greater safety for Patti Anne.

## 21. Terri Anne leads Mike to their bedroom

- After accepting Mike's invitation, Terri Anne takes his hand and leads him out of the home office.
- She refers to Mike's bedroom as **their bedroom** for the first time.
- The change in language shows that she is beginning to imagine the mansion as a home she and Patti Anne can share with him.
- Terri Anne and Mike sleep together again in the bedroom they have chosen to share.

## 22. Closing beat

- Mike and Terri Anne remain together in their bedroom after deciding to share a home.
- They will still need to plan Patti Anne's room, belongings, security, and transition into the mansion.
- Terri Anne now knows what Mike knows about the danger surrounding them.
- The chapter ends with Mike and Terri Anne falling in love, promising to protect Patti Anne together, and beginning their new life under the same roof.

## Chapter Purpose

This chapter:

- Shows Mike trusting Terri Anne with Bryan's complete investigation report.
- Reveals that Mike knew her real name and Patti Anne's identity before she told him.
- Gives Terri Anne access to the evidence against Adam and Don.
- Shows her the photograph of Adam, Cooper, and Victoria at The Well.
- Explains Mike's suspicion that Victoria sabotaged his previous relationships.
- Confirms through surveillance that Don followed Terri Anne and Patti Anne.
- Shows Terri Anne feeling a little angry but completely understanding why Mike hid the report.
- Shows her appreciating Mike's trust in sharing all the information about the report's key people.
- Has Terri Anne question whether she can protect Patti Anne from the conspiracy.
- Gives Mike a wholehearted promise to help protect Patti Anne.
- Has Mike and Terri Anne admit that they are falling in love with each other.
- Has Mike invite Terri Anne and Patti Anne to move into the mansion without pressure.
- Offers Terri Anne the option of a separate bedroom.
- Has Terri Anne accept the invitation and choose to share Mike's bedroom.
- Ends with Terri Anne leading Mike to their bedroom and sleeping with him again.
- Moves their relationship from selective disclosure toward full transparency and shared commitment.
