# Chapter Thirty-Seven — Audition Day

## 1. Terri Anne brings Patti Anne to Key Corporation

- Terri Anne arrives at Key Corporation with eight-month-old Patti Anne.
- She has brought her daughter because her Key Porn Studios audition is scheduled for that day.
- Terri Anne carries all the diapers, clothing, milk, feeding supplies, and comfort items Patti Anne may need.
- She is nervous about both the audition and leaving Patti Anne with unfamiliar caregivers.

## 2. Terri Anne and Patti Anne meet Mike

- Before going to the day care, Terri Anne meets Mike inside Key Corporation.
- Patti Anne remains with Terri Anne throughout the meeting.
- Mike is happy to see both of them and greets Patti Anne warmly.
- Terri Anne keeps Patti Anne close while she and Mike speak privately.

## 3. Mike explains Ashlee's weekly call

- Mike tells Terri Anne that Ashlee called him the previous night while he was working late at Key Corporation.
- He explains that he told Ashlee about their new relationship.
- Mike says he shared Terri Anne's age, the fact that she has an eight-month-old daughter, and the plan for Terri Anne and Patti Anne to move into his mansion.
- He tells Terri Anne directly because he does not want her to learn about the conversation from someone else.

## 4. Terri Anne reacts to what Ashlee knows

- Terri Anne appreciates Mike telling her exactly what he shared with Ashlee.
- She understands that Ashlee is deeply important to Mike and that he hopes the two women will meet.
- Terri Anne feels nervous because Ashlee now knows about the age difference, Patti Anne, and the move.
- Mike reassures her that he spoke honestly and did not share private sexual details.

## 5. Mike remains separate from the audition

- Mike reminds Terri Anne that Charlene and the Key Porn Studios team will handle her audition.
- His romantic relationship with Terri Anne prevents him from evaluating her performance or making the hiring decision.
- He can answer general questions about the company, but the choice to continue remains Terri Anne's.
- After their meeting ends, Terri Anne takes Patti Anne toward Key Corporation's day care.

## 6. Key Corporation's day care

- Key Corporation operates a secure on-site day care for employees' children and approved visitors.
- The facility is professionally staffed and separated from the adult-film studio operations.
- Terri Anne inspects the room carefully before leaving Patti Anne.
- Staff members explain their safety procedures, feeding routine, and process for contacting Terri Anne immediately.

## 7. Terri Anne provides Patti Anne's supplies

- Terri Anne gives the staff Patti Anne's diapers, clothing, milk, feeding supplies, and comfort items.
- She explains her daughter's routine and the best ways to calm her.
- Terri Anne makes certain they understand every instruction concerning feeding and naps.
- The staff record the information and confirm how to contact her during the audition.

## 8. Patti Anne starts crying

- Patti Anne looks around the day care and realizes that she does not know anyone there.
- The unfamiliar caregivers, voices, and surroundings frighten her.
- She begins crying and reaches for Terri Anne.
- Terri Anne holds her close and tries to reassure her, but Patti Anne continues crying whenever her mother attempts to step away.

## 9. Terri Anne struggles with the separation

- Terri Anne considers cancelling the audition and taking Patti Anne home.
- Hearing her daughter cry makes every step toward the door feel wrong.
- The day-care staff reassure Terri Anne that they will comfort Patti Anne and contact her if the crying continues or anything is wrong.
- Terri Anne reminds herself that Patti Anne is safe even though she is unhappy about the separation.

## 10. Terri Anne walks away heartbroken

- Terri Anne kisses Patti Anne and promises that she will return after the audition.
- She walks out of the day-care facility while Patti Anne is still crying.
- Hearing her daughter's cries behind her makes Terri Anne's heart feel as if it is breaking.
- She pauses outside the door, fighting the urge to run back inside.
- Leaving is one of the hardest things she has done as Patti Anne's mother.

## 11. Security protects Patti Anne

- Key Corporation security knows that Terri Anne and Patti Anne are in the building.
- Day-care staff may release Patti Anne only to Terri Anne or someone she has specifically authorized.
- Adam, Don, Cooper, and Victoria are not permitted access.
- The security precautions reassure Terri Anne intellectually, even though they do not make Patti Anne's crying easier to hear.

## 12. Charlene meets Terri Anne

- Charlene finds Terri Anne after she leaves the day care.
- She sees how upset Terri Anne is and gives her time to regain her composure.
- Charlene reminds her that she may check on Patti Anne and may stop the audition if she needs to return to her daughter.
- Terri Anne appreciates that motherhood is not being treated as an inconvenience.

## 13. Terri Anne enters the audition

- Terri Anne walks into Key Porn Studios with Patti Anne's crying still echoing in her mind.
- She knows the audition begins with the recorded interview Mike described.
- The finished audition may become her first movie on the studio's website.
- She reminds herself that she chose to explore the opportunity and may still stop.
- The cameras begin filming before she chooses her professional name.

## 14. Scout chooses her screen name on camera

- During the filmed interview, Terri Anne is asked what name she wants to use professionally.
- She chooses **Scout** as her adult-film screen name, continuing the identity she uses at the Blue Velvet Room.
- She explains on camera that Scout was originally a childhood nickname given to her by her grandmothers.
- They chose the nickname because Terri Anne loved digging in the ground and exploring.
- Her grandmothers also enrolled her in Boy Scouts instead of Girl Guides.
- The interviewer then asks about her performance preferences.
- Scout states that her preferences include **everything on the studio's list**.
- Her answer is recorded as part of the opening interview, while her right to set boundaries or stop remains in place.

## 15. Scout meets the other performers

- Scout learns that her audition scene will include **three other people, all adult men**.
- She meets the three male performers before filming the scene.
- The production team reviews the planned audition with everyone involved.
- Scout has an opportunity to ask questions, confirm her boundaries, and decide whether she still wants to proceed.
- The three men understand that the filmed audition is Scout's first production with Key Porn Studios.

## 16. Scout completes the audition

- Terri Anne thinks again about Patti Anne in the day care before the filmed scene begins.
- Scout proceeds with the audition alongside the three male performers.
- She communicates with the production team and handles the recorded scene with confidence.
- The audition is completed for review and possible release as her first Key Porn Studios movie.

## 17. Mike waits outside the studio

- Mike waits outside the studio rather than entering or watching the audition.
- His absence from the set keeps him separate from the evaluation and gives Terri Anne room to make professional choices without his presence influencing her.
- When Terri Anne comes out, Mike is eager to know how she feels about the experience.
- He asks whether she loved the audition.

## 18. Terri Anne loved the audition

- Terri Anne tells Mike that she **loved the audition**.
- Her answer reflects her own experience rather than a desire to please him.
- Mike is happy that she enjoyed something she freely chose to explore.
- He does not assume that enjoying one audition obligates her to continue in the industry.

## 19. Charlene delivers the studio's decision

- Charlene comes out of the studio and joins Mike and Terri Anne.
- She tells Mike that Terri Anne handled the audition like a professional.
- Charlene explains that Key Porn Studios wants Scout to return if Terri Anne decides she wants this career.
- The offer remains Terri Anne's choice, with no penalty if she decides not to continue.

## 20. Closing beat

- Terri Anne is proud of the studio's response and relieved that the audition is over.
- Mike congratulates her without taking credit for her success.
- Charlene prepares to explain the next professional steps if Terri Anne chooses to accept the career opportunity.
- Terri Anne's next priority is returning to the day care and reuniting with Patti Anne.
- The chapter ends with a successful audition, an open career offer, and Terri Anne free to decide what comes next.
