# Chapter Thirty-Eight — Ashlee's Brothers Arrive


## 1. Ashlee calls at eleven o'clock

- Ashlee calls Mike from Los Angeles at **11:00 a.m. Los Angeles time**.
- Mike answers at his Carlisle mansion, where Terri Anne and Patti Anne are staying.
- Patti Anne is sleeping peacefully inside Mike and Terri Anne's master bedroom.
- Because Carlisle and Los Angeles are three hours apart, it is approximately **2:00 p.m. in Ontario**.
- This is not their regular weekly call.
- Mike recognizes from Ashlee's voice that something has upset her.

## 2. Jack and Ric appeared unexpectedly

- Ashlee tells Mike that their brothers, **Jack** and **Ric**, arrived without warning.
- Neither brother arranged the visit with Ashlee or Geoffrey.
- Their sudden appearance makes Ashlee feel that her home and privacy have been invaded.
- Jack and Ric leave before Ashlee calls Mike.
- By the time of the call, Ashlee and Geoffrey are physically safe but emotionally shaken.

## 3. The brothers mock the relationship

- Ashlee explains that Jack and Ric mocked her relationship with Geoffrey.
- They treated the couple's love and future as something that did not deserve respect.
- Their comments were intended to embarrass Ashlee and provoke Geoffrey.
- Ashlee is hurt that her own brothers came to her home only to insult a relationship that makes her happy.

## 4. Geoffrey stands beside Ashlee

- Geoffrey refuses to abandon Ashlee or join the brothers' ridicule.
- He makes clear that Jack and Ric do not have the right to disrespect their relationship inside the home he shares with Ashlee.
- Ashlee appreciates his support but fears the confrontation could become worse.
- She calls Mike because he has protected her from their mother and siblings throughout her life.

## 5. Mike listens

- Mike gives Ashlee room to explain everything that happened.
- He does not minimize the incident as ordinary sibling teasing.
- Mike understands that Jack and Ric's behaviour is part of the larger pattern Ashlee has endured within the Key family.
- His protective anger grows as he hears how they treated her and Geoffrey.

## 6. Mike confirms that the brothers are gone

- Mike confirms that Jack and Ric left before Ashlee called.
- He is relieved that Ashlee and Geoffrey are no longer facing an active confrontation.
- Ashlee explains that neither brother is still inside or immediately outside their home.
- Mike understands that physical safety does not erase the emotional harm caused by their visit.

## 7. Ashlee explains why she called

- Ashlee tells Mike that she needed to hear the voice of the brother who has always believed and protected her.
- The call is not only a request for practical help; it is also a search for reassurance.
- She wants Mike to confirm that Jack and Ric do not get to define her relationship.
- Mike tells her that her love for Geoffrey belongs to her, not to their brothers.

## 8. Mike speaks to Ashlee and Geoffrey

- Geoffrey joins Ashlee during the telephone call so that Mike can speak with both of them.
- Mike asks what Jack and Ric said and how the visit affected them.
- He tells them that the brothers' mockery does not diminish their relationship.
- Mike is relieved that Jack and Ric have already left, but he takes the harassment seriously.

## 9. Mike plans to fly to Los Angeles

- Mike tells Ashlee and Geoffrey that he will fly to Los Angeles in a couple of days.
- He wants to see them in person and make certain they feel safe after the unexpected visit.
- Mike does not present the trip as taking control of their home or relationship.
- Ashlee appreciates knowing that the brother who has always protected her is coming.

## 10. Terri Anne asks about Ashlee

- After the call ends, Terri Anne asks Mike whether Ashlee is okay.
- Patti Anne remains with Terri Anne while they speak at Key Corporation.
- Mike says Ashlee and Geoffrey are physically safe, but the visit hurt and upset them.
- Terri Anne understands why Mike plans to travel to Los Angeles.

## 11. Mike suspects a Key Corporation motive

- Mike tells Terri Anne that he believes his siblings are starting to worry about Key Corporation.
- His serious relationship with Terri Anne and growing bond with Patti Anne may allow him to satisfy the conditions in their mother's will.
- Mike suspects that this fear is why Jack and Ric terrorized Ashlee and Geoffrey.
- He believes the brothers may be trying to intimidate the sibling who has always supported him.

## 12. Closing beat

- Terri Anne and Patti Anne will stay at Mike's mansion that night while he prepares for the Los Angeles trip.
- Mike remains concerned that Jack and Ric's visit may be connected to the same struggle over Key Corporation involving Victoria.
- Terri Anne supports his decision to check on Ashlee and Geoffrey in person.
- The chapter ends with Mike planning to fly to Los Angeles in a couple of days.

## Chapter Purpose

This chapter:

- Takes place on the same day as Terri Anne's audition.
- Has Ashlee call Mike at 11:00 a.m. Los Angeles time and 2:00 p.m. Ontario time.
- Introduces Mike and Ashlee's brothers, Jack and Ric.
- Shows the brothers arriving unexpectedly at Ashlee and Geoffrey's home.
- Establishes that they leave before Ashlee calls Mike.
- Reveals that they mocked Ashlee and Geoffrey's relationship.
- Shows Geoffrey standing beside Ashlee.
- Establishes that Terri Anne and Patti Anne will stay at Mike's mansion that night.
- Has Mike tell Ashlee and Geoffrey that he will fly to Los Angeles in a couple of days.
- Shows Terri Anne asking whether Ashlee is okay after the call.
- Reveals Mike's belief that his siblings are terrorizing Ashlee and Geoffrey because they fear losing Key Corporation.
- Reinforces Mike's lifelong role as Ashlee's protector and source of emotional support.
