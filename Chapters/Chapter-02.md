# Chapter Two — The Meeting at the Mansion

## 1. Mike returns home

- After leaving the Blue Velvet Room, Mike returns alone to his mansion.
- He thinks about his conversation with Scout, but he is especially troubled by the way Adam and Don confronted him.
- Because Mike was treated as an ordinary customer, he knows he witnessed their normal behavior rather than a performance staged for the club's owner.
- He suspects that Adam may be abusing his authority and that Don may be helping him intimidate employees and customers.

## 2. Mike calls Bryan

- Mike telephones **Bryan**, his private investigator, and requests an immediate, discreet investigation of both Adam and Don.
- He orders Bryan to examine:
  - Their employment histories;
  - Criminal and civil records;
  - Financial difficulties or unexplained income;
  - Complaints from former employees;
  - Their treatment of workers at the Blue Velvet Room;
  - Any evidence of threats, coercion, blackmail, theft, or other misconduct.
- Mike insists that Adam and Don must not learn that Bryan is investigating them.
- He does not yet mention Scout's hidden personal life because he does not know anything about Patti Anne or Adam's blackmail.

## 3. Mike calls Olivia

- Mike next calls **Olivia**, his executive secretary.
- He asks Olivia to come to his mansion for a confidential meeting.
- The unusual location and short notice make it clear that the matter is both personal and urgent.
- Mike instructs Olivia not to place the meeting on his official corporate calendar.

## 4. Mike summons Charlene

- Mike calls **Charlene**, the vice president for **Key Porn Studios Global Operations**.
- He asks Charlene to attend the same private meeting at his mansion.
- Mike provides few details over the telephone and says only that he wants to discuss an operation connected to the company's adult-entertainment business.
- Charlene realizes that Mike has discovered something serious.

## 5. Mike prepares for the meeting

- While waiting for the two executives to arrive, Mike retrieves the hidden ownership records for the Blue Velvet Room.
- The documents show the chain of shell companies separating Mike from the club.
- He reviews financial reports, staffing information, customer complaints, and anything bearing Adam's approval.
- Mike begins to question how much misconduct may have gone unnoticed because the club's true owner remained invisible.

## 6. Olivia and Charlene arrive

- Mike receives Olivia and Charlene in a private office inside the mansion.
- He ensures that no assistants, corporate staff members, or security personnel can overhear them.
- Mike places the Blue Velvet Room's ownership records on the table.
- He reveals that he personally owns the club through multiple shell companies.
- Neither Olivia nor Charlene knew about his ownership before this meeting.
- Mike tells them that he visited the club anonymously and was disturbed by what he observed.

## 7. Mike describes the confrontation

- Mike explains that Adam accused him of monopolizing Scout's time and approached him with Don as intimidation.
- He tells Olivia and Charlene that Adam became friendly only after Mike placed two hundred dollars on the bar.
- Mike also describes Adam's unusual attention toward Scout and her visible nervousness around the manager.
- He says he has a deep feeling that Adam and Don are corrupt and may be exploiting their positions at the club.
- Olivia and Charlene recognize Mike's conviction but understand that they need evidence before taking official action.

## 8. Mike orders a confidential review

- Mike directs Olivia to gather all documents connected to the Blue Velvet Room without alerting its management.
- He asks Charlene to arrange a confidential operational review of the club.
- Until Mike, Olivia, Charlene, and Bryan have evidence, neither Adam nor Don is to be confronted or dismissed.
- Mike wants to discover the full truth without frightening potential victims or giving the suspects time to destroy evidence.

## 9. Mike keeps Scout separate from the investigation

- When asked whether a particular employee prompted his concern, Mike mentions Scout's nervous reaction but does not request an investigation into her private life.
- He makes clear that the inquiry must focus on Adam, Don, and the club's management practices.
- This boundary shows that Mike is concerned about Scout but does not believe his interest gives him permission to invade her privacy.

## 10. Closing hook

- Bryan calls back with an early discovery: Adam or Don has concealed something significant in his employment history.
- Mike realizes that the confrontation at the bar may have exposed only a small part of what is happening inside the Blue Velvet Room.
- Meanwhile, Adam has no idea that the quiet customer he confronted has begun an investigation from the top of the organization.

## Chapter Purpose

This chapter:

- Reveals Mike's mansion and the private world behind his anonymous visit.
- Demonstrates his authority as the owner of a global business empire.
- Begins the investigation of Adam and Don.
- Brings Mike's executive secretary and the vice president for Key Porn Studios Global Operations into the story.
- Shows that Mike is interested in protecting Scout without improperly investigating her.
- Builds suspense around Adam's blackmail and the wider problems at the Blue Velvet Room.
