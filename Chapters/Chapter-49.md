# Chapter Forty-Nine — Kassandra Returns to the Set

## Timeline

- This is part of the future 2029 storyline.
- Ashlee, Kassandra, Yelena, and Isobel are twenty-one.
- Mike has known Kassandra for sixteen years, since the girls met at age five.

## 1. Kassandra invites Ashlee to come with her

- After their private conversation in the executive conference room, Kassandra prepares to return to the filming studio.
- She asks Ashlee to accompany her rather than remaining upstairs.
- Ashlee agrees because she wants to support her friend and understand Kassandra's new career without judging her.
- The unanswered question about whether Ashlee wants the disabled-woman role remains separate from Kassandra's immediate problem.

## 2. Kassandra takes Ashlee to the studio

- Kassandra leads Ashlee from the executive suites to the studio where her movie is being filmed.
- Ashlee follows in her special power chair.
- Kassandra remains somewhat embarrassed about Ashlee seeing her workplace, but she does not hide from her.
- Their walk to the studio is the first step toward rebuilding the trust lost during eighteen months of silence.

## 3. Mike and Scott follow them

- Mike and Scott leave the executive area and follow Kassandra and Ashlee to the filming studio.
- Mike comes as the owner of Key Porn Studios and as Ashlee's protective brother.
- Scott comes as the vice president responsible for the Los Angeles studio.
- Mike's guards remain positioned to protect him and Ashlee without interfering with the production.

## 4. The crew waits

- The director and crew have been waiting because Kassandra arrived late for filming her movie.
- Her absence disrupted the production schedule and left other performers and crew members uncertain about when work could begin.
- Some crew members are frustrated, while others know that Kassandra has been struggling since her father's appearance at the mountain scene.
- The room becomes quiet when Kassandra returns with Ashlee, Mike, and Scott.

## 5. Kassandra apologizes

- Kassandra faces the director and crew and apologizes for arriving late.
- She acknowledges that her lateness affected everyone involved in the production.
- She does not use her depression as an excuse, but she also does not pretend that she has been well.
- The apology shows that Kassandra wants to repair the working relationship rather than abandon her movie.

## 6. Mike identifies himself as the owner

- Mike introduces himself to the crew as the owner of Key Porn Studios.
- Several crew members realize that the visitor with the temporary badge is the person who owns the worldwide studio operation.
- Mike explains that he came to understand and solve problems rather than to humiliate employees.
- He asks the crew to listen before deciding what Kassandra's lateness says about her.

## 7. Mike discusses Kassandra's character

- Mike speaks with the crew about Kassandra's personal character rather than reducing her to a delayed production schedule.
- He tells them, **“I have known Kassandra for sixteen years, since she and Ashlee met at age five.”**
- Mike does not share private details about Ashlee being shot while horseback riding with him or about Kassandra learning the story afterward.
- Kassandra, Yelena, and Isobel already know the full story because Ashlee personally told her three closest friends everything, but the film crew does not need that information.
- Mike explains that sixteen years of knowing Kassandra allow him to speak confidently about her loyalty and character.
- Mike points out that one late arrival does not erase sixteen years of friendship, her previous work, her courage, or her value as a person.
- Kassandra survived being rejected by her father, rebuilt her life, and then faced him again unexpectedly during a mountain scene.
- Mike asks the crew to recognize that her deep depression is a serious wellbeing concern.
- He also tells Kassandra that respecting the crew requires honest communication when she may be late or unable to work.

## 8. Mike balances support and responsibility

- Mike does not excuse repeated disruption or tell the crew that schedules no longer matter.
- He asks the director and production team to give Kassandra a fair opportunity to continue.
- Kassandra must communicate earlier, while the studio must respond to her depression with support rather than shame.
- The solution treats Kassandra as both a valued person and a professional responsible to her coworkers.

## 9. Kassandra thanks Ashlee and Mike

- Kassandra thanks Ashlee for standing beside her after learning about her career.
- She thanks Mike for speaking to the crew without attacking or embarrassing her.
- Kassandra admits that she expected Ashlee to judge her and Mike to treat her as a production problem.
- Their support makes her feel less isolated from the people who once knew her best.

## 10. Kassandra promises to improve her punctuality

- Kassandra promises Mike that she will try to be on time for her next filming sessions.
- She also agrees to warn the director or Scott as early as possible if her depression makes punctuality difficult.
- Mike accepts the promise while making clear that asking for help is better than disappearing.
- The crew now has a practical expectation for Kassandra's future shoots.

## 11. Ashlee offers to help tell the others

- Ashlee tells Kassandra that she is willing to help her speak with Yelena and Isobel about her new career.
- She does not threaten to reveal Kassandra's work without permission.
- Ashlee offers to remain beside Kassandra during the conversation or help her decide what she wants to say.
- Kassandra no longer has to face the other members of the Four Musketeers alone.

## 12. Scott follows Mike's lead

- Scott accepts Mike's approach to the problem involving Kassandra.
- He does not punish or publicly shame her for the late arrival.
- Scott will work with the director to preserve the production schedule while helping Kassandra communicate and obtain support.
- He treats Kassandra's depression as a genuine concern without removing all professional expectations.

## 13. Kassandra gets ready for filming

- The director and crew accept Kassandra's apology and prepare to resume filming.
- Kassandra gets ready for her movie and returns to the set.
- She knows Ashlee has chosen to remain nearby rather than reject her for her new career.
- Scott follows Mike's lead and gives Kassandra the opportunity to rebuild trust through communication and punctuality.

## 14. Mike, Ashlee, and Scott watch

- Mike and Ashlee stay at the studio to watch Kassandra's filming.
- Scott remains with them and observes the production as the Los Angeles vice president.
- Ashlee watches as Kassandra regains her professional focus in front of the crew.
- Mike sees the third studio problem beginning to improve while the casting and budget questions remain open.

## 15. Ashlee praises Kassandra

- When the filming is finished, Ashlee tells Kassandra, **“Good job.”**
- The simple praise matters to Kassandra because she had feared Ashlee would judge her career.
- Kassandra understands that Ashlee still sees her as a friend rather than only as an adult-film performer.
- Ashlee remains willing to help Kassandra speak with Yelena and Isobel.

## 16. They prepare to leave the studio

- Mike, Ashlee, and Scott begin leaving the filming area.
- Ashlee operates her special power chair independently as they move through the studio.
- Three other performers—two actresses and one actor—walk past them.
- The performers do not initially realize that Mike owns Key Porn Studios.

## 17. The actresses mock Ashlee's power chair

- The two actresses make fun of Ashlee's special power chair as they pass.
- Their teasing targets the equipment that gives Ashlee mobility and allows her to stand.
- Mike immediately becomes angry at the disrespect shown to his sister.
- Scott is also disturbed that performers employed by the studio would mock a disabled visitor.

## 18. The actor apologizes

- The actor walking with the two actresses does not join their teasing.
- He apologizes to Ashlee for the actresses' behaviour.
- His apology makes clear that he recognizes the comments as cruel and unacceptable.
- Mike remains angry with the actresses but acknowledges that the actor tried to correct the situation.

## 19. Ashlee turns on a dime

- Ashlee sharply turns her power chair around on a dime to face the actresses.
- The precise turn demonstrates her control of the chair UCLA engineering students spent eighteen months building with her prototype feedback.
- She refuses to let the actresses speak about her as though she is helpless or absent.
- Ashlee faces them directly while Mike and Scott stand beside her.

## 20. Ashlee wants the role

- Ashlee tells Mike and Scott that she wants to play the disabled woman in the proposed film.
- She discloses to Scott for the first time that she is a transgender woman and says she wants the part **in spite of being transgender**.
- Scott now understands the private fact Mike protected throughout the presentation.
- Mike recognizes that Ashlee has finally answered his question truthfully.
- Her decision is her own response to the script and the way the actresses treated her, not something Mike or Scott forced upon her.

## 21. Scott must speak with the writer

- Scott explains to Mike and Ashlee that he cannot confirm the casting decision immediately.
- The movie was originally written for straight actors.
- Because Ashlee is a transgender woman who has not undergone gender-affirming surgery, the movie's planned preferences and scene requirements must change for her.
- Scott says he must speak with the writer so the script can be revised around Ashlee's preferences.
- Those revisions are separate from Ashlee's audition and do not depend on her taking the audition first.
- The discussion is not a rejection of Ashlee; it is a necessary adaptation for a willing potential lead performer.
- Mike expects the writer to treat Ashlee's disability, transgender identity, and stated preferences respectfully.
- Ashlee understands that wanting the role begins a serious production discussion rather than guaranteeing an immediate answer.

## 22. The actresses laugh at Ashlee

- The two actresses begin laughing loudly after hearing that Ashlee wants the lead role.
- One of them mocks Ashlee for **“trying to be a fucking actress.”**
- Their laughter attacks both Ashlee's disability and her confidence about entering a new profession.
- Ashlee remains facing them from her power chair and refuses to withdraw what she said.

## 23. Mike suspends the actresses without pay

- Mike identifies himself again as the owner of Key Porn Studios and immediately stops the confrontation.
- He suspends both actresses without pay because of their harassment of Ashlee.
- The actor who apologized is not suspended because he did not participate in the teasing or laughter.
- Scott supports Mike's decision and begins the necessary studio process for removing the actresses from the active production schedule.

## 24. Kassandra tells them about Ashlee

- Kassandra confronts the two suspended actresses for mocking Ashlee.
- She tells them that **she and Ashlee previously slept together on the swimming-pool deck**.
- Kassandra uses their shared history to make clear that Ashlee is confident in her body and capable of deciding whether she wants an adult-film career.
- She refuses to remain silent while coworkers attack one of her closest friends.
- The revelation surprises the actresses and ends their laughter.

## 25. Ashlee and Kassandra express their love

- Ashlee thanks Kassandra for standing up for her against the two actresses.
- She tells Kassandra that she loves her so much.
- Kassandra tells Ashlee that she is quite welcome and that she loves Ashlee so much, too.
- Both women become very emotional as eighteen months of separation gives way to renewed trust.
- The exchange confirms that their friendship and love for one another survived Kassandra's silence and embarrassment about her career.
- Mike watches them and smiles without saying anything.
- He recognizes that Kassandra is slowly beginning to heal and does not interrupt the moment.

## 26. Ashlee invites Kassandra to meet Geoffrey

- After their emotional exchange, Ashlee asks Kassandra whether she would like to come over to the mansion and meet her boyfriend.
- Kassandra knows Ashlee means Geoffrey, whose relationship with Ashlee has already been attacked by Jack, Ric, and Victoria.
- Kassandra agrees to visit and meet him.
- The invitation brings Kassandra back into Ashlee's personal life rather than limiting their reunion to the studio.

## 27. Closing hook

- Kassandra has successfully returned to filming and received Ashlee's praise.
- Ashlee refuses to abandon the disabled-woman role despite the actresses' cruelty.
- Mike has suspended both actresses without pay, while the actor's apology separates him from their behaviour.
- Scott must now speak with the writer about changing the new movie's preferences and requirements for Ashlee.
- The chapter ends with Ashlee and Kassandra beside each other, both emotional after expressing how much they love one another.
