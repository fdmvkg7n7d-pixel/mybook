# Chapter Thirty-Three — Their Second Date

## 1. Saturday arrives

- Mike has returned from his business trip to Tokyo.
- As he proposed during their telephone call, he and Terri Anne meet on Saturday after her shift at the Blue Velvet Room.
- This is their second date.
- Both are pleased to see each other after spending the week apart.

## 2. Terri Anne asks about Tokyo

- Once they settle into the date, Terri Anne asks Mike about his business trip.
- She wants to understand more about the work that takes him around the world.
- Mike tells her about the meetings and negotiations without overwhelming her with corporate details.
- Terri Anne is curious about how a trip of that scale affects Key Corporation.

## 3. Mike reveals the result

- Mike tells Terri Anne that Key Corporation made **two billion dollars** from the business connected to the Tokyo trip.
- The amount astonishes her.
- For Mike, the figure is a major business result; for Terri Anne, it is almost impossible to imagine.
- The conversation again reveals the enormous financial difference between their lives.

## 4. Mike asks about Terri Anne's week

- After discussing Tokyo, Mike turns the conversation back to Terri Anne.
- He asks how her week went while he was away.
- His question is sincere rather than a polite transition.
- Terri Anne decides that if their relationship is going to continue, she wants to answer honestly.

## 5. Terri Anne tells him about Charlene's offer

- Terri Anne tells Mike that Charlene called her into the manager's office.
- She explains how frightened she was because she initially believed she had done something wrong.
- Terri Anne reveals that Charlene offered her a possible job as an adult-film performer with Key Porn Studios.
- She tells Mike that Charlene repeatedly emphasized there was no pressure and that Mike knew nothing about the offer.

## 6. Mike learns about the offer for the first time

- Mike is surprised because Charlene correctly kept him outside the recruitment decision.
- He confirms that he had no idea the offer existed.
- Mike does not immediately encourage or discourage Terri Anne.
- He tells her that he can answer questions about the industry, but the career decision must remain hers.

## 7. Mike explains the Key Porn Studios audition

- Mike explains that if Terri Anne chooses to pursue the offer, Key Porn Studios requires a formal audition.
- The audition is recorded and, with the required performer agreement and release, placed on the Key Porn Studios website as her first movie.
- Mike makes certain she understands that the audition is not private test footage that will automatically remain unseen.
- She would know before participating that customers may watch the completed audition movie online.

## 8. The recorded interview

- The audition begins with an on-camera interview.
- Terri Anne would be required to answer a standard list of questions about her experience, interests, boundaries, and goals in the industry.
- One of the questions asks about her personal performance preferences.
- Mike explains that the preference question helps the studio understand what kinds of work she may or may not want to consider.
- Her answers do not remove her right to refuse a particular scene or change a boundary later.

## 9. Mike keeps the decision with Terri Anne

- Mike describes the process but does not ask Terri Anne to audition for him.
- He reminds her that Charlene made the offer independently and that accepting or refusing will not affect their relationship.
- Terri Anne may review the documents, ask the studio questions, or decline the opportunity.
- Mike's role during the date is to give her accurate information about the industry, not to recruit her.

## 10. Terri Anne tells him about Marco

- Terri Anne explains that she met Marco at Tim Hortons with Patti Anne.
- She tells Mike that she revealed her real name and introduced Patti Anne to Marco.
- Marco promised to protect her privacy and continue calling her Scout at work.
- Mike is pleased that Terri Anne has another trusted person at the Blue Velvet Room.

## 11. Terri Anne tells him about Captain Morrison

- Terri Anne tells Mike that Captain Morrison came to the club after his sister died from leukemia.
- She explains that he was devastated and did not want to be alone.
- Terri Anne offered to accompany him to his apartment after her shift.
- She tells Mike that she called Patti Anne's babysitter and left the club with Captain Morrison at 4:00 a.m.

## 12. Terri Anne tells Mike everything

- Terri Anne does not stop with a partial version of the story.
- She tells Mike that she chose to have sex with Captain Morrison and stayed the rest of the night.
- She also explains that an accidental breast-milk moment led her to reveal that she has an eight-month-old daughter.
- Terri Anne makes clear that the encounter was consensual and not a paid service.
- She does not know how Mike will react, but she refuses to build their relationship on concealment.

## 13. Mike completely understands

- Mike tells Terri Anne that he completely understands why she stayed with Captain Morrison.
- Morrison had just lost his sister to leukemia and needed someone to keep him company.
- Mike recognizes that Terri Anne acted out of compassion and made her own adult choice.
- He does not shame her, become jealous, or treat her honesty as a betrayal.

## 14. Mike appreciates Terri Anne's honesty

- Terri Anne explains that she told him because he asked about her week and because she wants to be honest with him.
- Mike thanks her for trusting him with the complete story.
- He tells her that her kindness toward a grieving man does not change his respect or feelings for her.
- Mike's response reassures Terri Anne that honesty will not automatically lead to rejection.

## 15. Terri Anne leads Mike to the gym

- After their honest conversation, Terri Anne takes Mike's right hand.
- She leads him through the mansion to the large gym.
- Her actual destination is one of the gym's two massage tables.
- Mike follows willingly and allows Terri Anne to set the pace.

## 16. They massage each other

- Terri Anne and Mike take turns massaging one another on the table.
- The mutual massages help them relax after the serious conversation about Tokyo, Key Porn Studios, and Captain Morrison.
- Each checks that the other is comfortable.
- The affectionate contact gradually becomes more intimate.

## 17. They sleep together on the massage table

- Terri Anne and Mike choose to have sex on the massage table.
- Terri Anne remains an active participant in deciding what happens.
- The moment reinforces that Mike's understanding of her week has strengthened rather than damaged their connection.

## 18. Terri Anne calls Charlene

- After their time together in the gym, Terri Anne decides to respond to Charlene's job offer.
- With Mike in the room, she calls Charlene as Scout.
- Scout tells Charlene that she would like to do the Key Porn Studios audition first before making a larger career decision.
- Charlene confirms that the audition process can be arranged and reminds her that she may still change her mind.
- Mike remains quiet during the call so that the decision and conversation remain Terri Anne's.

## 19. Terri Anne explains why Mike was present

- After ending the call, Terri Anne tells Mike that she wanted him in the room because she wants him to trust her fully.
- She did not want him to learn about the audition indirectly or wonder whether she was hiding it.
- Mike thanks Terri Anne for being open with him.
- He tells her that whatever she may choose to do at Key Porn Studios, he trusts her fully.
- He makes clear that his trust does not depend on controlling her professional choices.

## 20. Terri Anne calls the babysitter

- Terri Anne then calls Patti Anne's babysitter.
- She asks how Patti Anne is doing and confirms that her daughter is safe and comfortable.
- The call reminds both Terri Anne and Mike that Patti Anne remains central to every decision about work, dating, and travel.
- Once reassured, Terri Anne can return her attention to the rest of the evening.

## 21. Closing beat

- Mike remains impressed by Terri Anne's compassion and willingness to tell him everything.
- Terri Anne feels relieved that Mike understands her choices involving Captain Morrison and Key Porn Studios.
- Charlene can now begin arranging Scout's audition.
- The chapter ends with their second date strengthened by honesty, acceptance, trust, and mutual desire.
