# Chapter Twenty-Two — Their Date Continues

## 1. Mike and Scout arrive in Carlisle

- Mike drives Scout to his mansion in Carlisle after their dinner at Mandarin Restaurant.
- The size of the property immediately shows Scout how different Mike's private life is from the ordinary identity he first presented at the Blue Velvet Room.
- Mike gives her another opportunity to say if she would rather return home.
- Scout confirms that she wants to continue the date and see his home.

## 2. Mike welcomes Scout inside

- Mike brings Scout into the mansion without treating her as an employee, guest to impress, or person he has rescued.
- Scout is curious but overwhelmed by the scale of the building.
- Mike offers to show her around so that the unfamiliar house feels less intimidating.
- The tour also allows them to continue talking before deciding how the rest of the evening will unfold.

## 3. Scout removes her boots

- After entering the mansion, Scout takes off her boots.
- She explains that she wants to respect Mike's floors and avoid tracking anything through his home.
- Mike appreciates the gesture but tells her that she does not need to feel intimidated by the house.
- Removing her boots also helps Scout feel less like a visitor and more comfortable as their date continues.

## 4. Twenty bedrooms

- Mike tells Scout that the mansion contains **twenty bedrooms**.
- Scout is astonished that one home could have so many places to sleep.
- Mike explains that the rooms accommodate relatives, guests, employees when necessary, and visitors connected to his work.
- The number reinforces how physically large and often impersonal his life can feel.

## 5. Thirty bathrooms

- The mansion contains **thirty bathrooms**.
- Scout reacts with disbelief and humor, asking why any house could possibly need more bathrooms than bedrooms.
- Mike accepts that the number sounds excessive when spoken aloud.
- Their shared amusement helps reduce Scout's nervousness.

## 6. Two kitchens

- Mike shows Scout the mansion's **two kitchens**.
- The kitchens allow household meals and larger events to be handled separately.
- Scout compares the enormous spaces with the much smaller kitchen in her apartment.
- Mike avoids making the comparison about status or wealth.

## 7. Four swimming pools

- Mike tells Scout that the property has **four different swimming pools**.
- Scout immediately thinks about Patti Anne and their interrupted swimming tradition.
- She imagines how much her daughter might enjoy the water in a safe setting.
- Mike does not use the pools as an excuse to invite himself further into Patti Anne's life.

## 8. Four hot tubs

- The mansion also has **four hot tubs**.
- Scout is amused that the property has as many hot tubs as swimming pools.
- Mike acknowledges that the mansion was designed on a scale far beyond what one person needs.
- The conversation continues to humanize the extraordinary surroundings.

## 9. The barber room

- Mike shows Scout a dedicated **barber room**.
- The room allows Mike and his guests to receive haircuts and grooming services without leaving the property.
- Scout finds the idea of a barber room inside a private home surprising.
- Mike's explanation gives her another glimpse of how wealth has isolated ordinary services inside his mansion.

## 10. The large gym

- Mike takes Scout into the mansion's large gym.
- The gym contains many different kinds of exercise equipment.
- Scout realizes that this is where Mike was working out during their morning telephone call.
- Mike does not mention that he heard Patti Anne's squeals during that conversation.

## 11. Two massage tables

- The gym includes **two massage tables**.
- Mike explains that the tables are used for recovery, stretching, and professional massage treatments.
- Scout notices how completely the mansion is equipped for Mike to live without leaving the property.
- The room reinforces both the comfort and isolation created by his wealth.

## 12. Three dining rooms

- Mike shows Scout the mansion's **three different dining rooms**.
- Each room can serve a different kind or size of gathering.
- Scout jokes that deciding where to eat must take longer than eating the meal itself.
- Mike enjoys her ability to respond to his wealth with humor rather than awe.

## 13. Mike explains the photographs

- Photographs are displayed throughout Mike's mansion, and Scout asks him about them during the tour.
- Mike pauses to explain who the people are and why the pictures matter to him.
- He shows her photographs of his youngest sister, **Ashlee**.
- One photograph shows Mike and Ashlee in front of the UCLA Music Building, with Ashlee seated in her wheelchair.
- Another shows Ashlee with Kassandra, Yelena, and Isobel on their graduation day from Hillfield Strathallan College.
- Mike tells Scout that he affectionately calls the four friends **the Four Musketeers**.
- The photographs allow Scout to see Mike as a brother and family member rather than only as a billionaire.

## 14. Mike's home office

- The final important stop is Mike's private home office.
- The office contains the wall safe where he locked Bryan's report.
- More photographs of Ashlee and the Four Musketeers are displayed on the walls.
- Mike does not show Scout the confidential report or reveal what it contains.
- The office is the most personal room on the tour because it combines Mike's family history, corporate responsibilities, and private decisions.

## 15. Scout reacts to the mansion

- Scout tells Mike that the mansion is more enormous than she imagined.
- She is impressed, but the tour also makes the difference between their lives impossible to ignore.
- Mike admits that owning a vast home does not prevent loneliness.
- Scout begins to understand why being treated as an ordinary customer at the Blue Velvet Room mattered to him.

## 16. Scout chooses to stay

- The tour ends with Mike and Scout alone inside the mansion.
- Scout has now seen the private world Mike concealed when they met.
- Mike asks what she would like to do next rather than assuming the evening must continue in a particular way.
- Scout tells Mike that she wants to stay overnight and sleep with him.
- Mike makes certain that this is still what she freely wants after having time to see the house and consider her choice.
- Scout confirms that she is certain.
- Scout takes Mike's right hand and leads him toward his bedroom.
- By leading the way herself, she makes her desire and choice clear.
- Their intimacy is consensual adult intimacy, and the finished scene can be written at the level of detail chosen for the novel.
- The chapter focuses on Scout's freely made decision, mutual desire, and their growing trust.
- The chapter ends as Scout leads Mike into his bedroom.

## Chapter Purpose

This chapter:

- Continues Mike and Scout's first date at his Carlisle mansion.
- Shows Scout removing her boots to respect Mike's floors.
- Gives Scout a tour of Mike's private home.
- Establishes the mansion's twenty bedrooms and thirty bathrooms.
- Shows its two kitchens, four swimming pools, and four hot tubs.
- Introduces the barber room and large gym with two massage tables.
- Establishes the three dining rooms and Mike's home office.
- Has Mike explain the family photographs displayed throughout the mansion.
- Uses Scout's reactions to explore the contrast between her life and Mike's wealth.
- Ends with Scout freely choosing to stay overnight and sleep with Mike.
- Keeps Mike respectful of Scout's choices as the date continues.
