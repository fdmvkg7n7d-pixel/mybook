# Chapter Eighteen — A Simple Date

## 1. Scout decides to call Mike

- During the week after the frightening incident near the Y, Scout thinks about Mike and the way he stayed on the telephone until she fell asleep.
- She wants to see him somewhere unrelated to the Blue Velvet Room, Key Corporation, or an emergency.
- Asking him out makes her nervous, but she wants the decision to be hers.
- She calls Mike's private number as Scout, continuing to use her stage name with him.

## 2. Mike is happy to hear from her

- Mike answers and is immediately pleased to hear Scout's voice.
- He asks how she and Patti Anne are doing without pressing for private details.
- Scout tells him that she is calling for a different reason.
- Her hesitation makes Mike curious, but he waits patiently for her to explain.

## 3. Scout asks Mike on a date

- Scout asks Mike whether he would like to go on a simple date with her.
- She tells him that she wants to take him to **Mandarin Restaurant at 1508 Upper James Street** on Saturday.
- By inviting Mike herself, Scout makes clear that the date is her choice rather than repayment for his protection.
- She emphasizes that she wants the evening to remain simple.

## 4. Mike accepts

- Mike accepts Scout's invitation without hesitation.
- He tells her that he would be happy to go to Mandarin Restaurant with her.
- He does not try to replace her plan with an expensive or extravagant alternative.
- Mike understands that respecting the simplicity of the date is part of respecting Scout's independence.

## 5. They make their plans

- Scout and Mike agree that the date will take place on Saturday.
- The Saturday date will occur one day after Victoria's planned Friday-night meeting with Adam and Cooper at The Well.
- Scout confirms the restaurant details and the time they will meet.
- Mike allows Scout to lead the arrangements because she invited him.

## 6. Mike thinks about the will

- After the call, Mike is excited about their first date.
- He also remembers the eighteen-month deadline in his mother's will.
- Mike refuses to treat one invitation as evidence that Scout is ready for a serious commitment.
- He decides that the date must be about getting to know her, not about marriage, children, or control of Key Corporation.

## 7. Scout looks forward to Saturday

- Scout feels both nervous and proud after ending the call.
- She has chosen to spend time with Mike away from the place where he was secretly her employer.
- Mandarin Restaurant on Upper James offers neutral ground where they can begin learning who they are outside the Blue Velvet Room.
- Scout looks forward to being with Mike while still keeping parts of her private identity protected.

## 8. Closing beat

- Mike and Scout each mark Saturday in their own way.
- Neither knows that Victoria's Friday-night meeting may create new danger immediately before their date.
- The chapter ends with both of them anticipating their first evening together as two people who have freely chosen to meet.

## Chapter Purpose

This chapter:

- Has Scout initiate her and Mike's first date.
- Keeps Scout's chosen identity in place.
- Schedules the date at Mandarin Restaurant on Upper James Street on Saturday.
- Shows Mike accepting a simple plan without imposing his wealth.
- Separates their growing relationship from Mike's protection and ownership of the club.
- Places the date one day after Victoria's secret meeting at The Well.
- Shows Mike refusing to let his mother's will dictate the pace of the relationship.
