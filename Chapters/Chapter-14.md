# Chapter Fourteen — Meeting the Mysterious Owner

## 1. Mike turns to face Scout

- Scout stands inside the manager's office holding Mike's Johnnie Walker on ice and her virgin Bloody Mary with extra celery sticks.
- She recognizes Mike's voice.
- Mike turns around, confirming that the mysterious owner is the customer she has been meeting at the bar.
- He repeats his invitation for her to sit on the couch.
- Scout places Mike's drink within his reach, keeps her own drink, and sits, but she remains tense and uncertain.
- The room where Adam threatened her now belongs to a conversation she controls.

## 2. Scout's questions begin

- Scout tells Mike that she has many questions.
- Her first questions come quickly:
  - Who is he really?
  - Does he truly own the Blue Velvet Room?
  - Why did he pretend to be an ordinary customer?
  - Why did no one at the club know who he was?
- Mike allows her to ask without interrupting or demanding gratitude for the intervention.

## 3. Mike explains his identity

- Mike tells Scout his full name: **Mike Key**.
- He confirms that he is the head of Key Corporation and the true owner of the Blue Velvet Room.
- He explains that the club is held through multiple shell companies, which kept his ownership hidden from the employees.
- Mike admits that he introduced himself using only his first name and deliberately concealed his wealth and authority.
- He does not claim that withholding the truth was harmless.

## 4. Why Mike came to the club

- Mike explains that he originally visited anonymously because he wanted to see how the Blue Velvet Room operated when no one knew the owner was present.
- His questions to Marco were part of his effort to understand the club's daily management.
- Adam and Don's repeated confrontations revealed how they behaved when they believed a customer had no power over them.
- Scout realizes that Mike was quietly observing the same men who were blackmailing her.

## 5. Scout questions Mike's interest in her

- Scout asks whether their conversations were also part of his investigation.
- She wants to know whether the money he placed on the bar and the business card were merely tactics.
- Mike tells her that his concern about Adam and Don became part of the investigation, but his enjoyment of her company was genuine.
- He says he left the private number because he wanted her to have a choice about contacting him outside the club.
- Scout remains unsure whether she can separate the man she trusted from the owner who concealed his power.

## 6. Mike protects what he knows

- Scout continues using her stage name during the meeting.
- Mike does not tell her that Bryan's report identified her before the 4:30 a.m. telephone call.
- He does not mention her real name or Patti Anne.
- Mike understands that information uncovered during the investigation belongs in a confidential report and should not be used to force a personal disclosure.
- Scout therefore remains in control of what she chooses to reveal during the meeting.

## 7. Mike explains the intervention

- Mike tells Scout that her call confirmed the urgency of Bryan's evidence.
- He explains that he contacted Charlene and Olivia immediately after speaking with her.
- The Saturday operation was arranged before Adam and Don could carry out their threat.
- Mike assures her that the cousins no longer control her job, schedule, income, or safety inside the club.
- He also tells her that Don has begun confessing to the blackmail scheme.

## 8. Charlene and Olivia enter

- In the middle of the meeting, the office door opens and **Charlene** and **Olivia** enter.
- They have been working elsewhere in the Blue Velvet Room and did not know what Scout looked like.
- They pause when they see the young dancer sitting with Mike.
- Mike identifies her only as Scout at first, allowing Terri Anne to decide whether to share her real name.
- Charlene and Olivia understand that they are meeting the employee whose call forced the investigation into immediate action.

## 9. Scout meets Charlene and Olivia

- Charlene introduces herself as the vice president for Key Porn Studios Global Operations.
- Olivia introduces herself as Mike's executive secretary.
- They explain that they came to protect the dancers, secure the club's records, and remove Adam and Don's control.
- Neither woman blames Scout for what happened at the club.
- They assure her that the other dancers will also be offered confidential help.

## 10. Charlene accepts the temporary manager position

- Mike asks Charlene whether she is willing to serve as the Blue Velvet Room's temporary manager.
- He explains that the employees need an experienced leader they can trust while the investigation continues.
- The appointment will allow Charlene to protect the dancers, preserve their income, and rebuild the club's procedures after Adam's removal.
- Charlene accepts the temporary position.
- Scout listens carefully because Charlene's acceptance directly affects whether the Blue Velvet Room can become a safe workplace.

## 11. Marco checks on Scout

- Still worried about Scout's safety, Marco comes to the manager's office to check on her.
- The security guards open the door and allow him to enter.
- Marco is relieved to see Scout safely seated on the couch with the virgin Bloody Mary he prepared for her.
- Mike's Johnnie Walker on ice rests within Mike's reach.
- Marco's concern shows Scout that someone at the club noticed her fear and cared enough to make certain she was safe.

## 12. Mike restores the assistant manager position

- Mike explains that Adam, the former manager, had eliminated the title of assistant manager.
- He tells Marco that the Blue Velvet Room needs the position restored under Charlene's temporary leadership.
- Mike asks Marco whether he would like to become the club's assistant manager.
- Marco accepts the offer on the condition that he may continue serving as the head bartender.
- Mike agrees to Marco's condition.
- Charlene welcomes Marco as her assistant manager and recognizes that his knowledge of the employees and daily operations will help stabilize the club.

## 13. Scout remains Scout

- In front of Charlene, Olivia, and Marco, Scout continues using only her stage name.
- She does not reveal her real name or tell them about Patti Anne.
- Mike respects her choice and introduces or addresses her only as Scout.
- He does not disclose that he knows anything more about her private identity.

## 14. Mike offers Scout the night off

- Mike asks Scout whether she would like to take the night shift off.
- He makes clear that there will be no penalty, loss of standing, or retaliation if she chooses to leave.
- For the first time, Scout is offered a genuine choice about working rather than being pressured by Adam.
- Scout tells Mike that she would like to work the shift.
- Mike accepts her decision without questioning it or treating her as too fragile to decide for herself.

## 15. Closing beat

- Scout looks from Mike to Charlene, Olivia, and Marco, trying to absorb how completely the situation has changed.
- Adam and Don no longer run the Blue Velvet Room, and Scout has chosen for herself to remain for the night shift.
- She still must decide whether she can trust Mike after learning the truth about his ownership.
- The chapter ends with Scout taking a sip of her virgin Bloody Mary before preparing to work under the club's new temporary management.

## Chapter Purpose

This chapter:

- Gives Scout the opportunity to question Mike directly.
- Reveals Mike's full identity and ownership to Scout.
- Explains why he visited the club anonymously.
- Forces Mike to acknowledge the consequences of concealing his power.
- Keeps Mike from revealing what Bryan's report taught him about Scout's private identity.
- Brings Charlene and Olivia into the meeting midway through the conversation.
- Introduces Scout to both women while allowing her to use only her stage name.
- Has Charlene accept the club's temporary manager position.
- Brings Marco into the office because he is worried about Scout.
- Restores the assistant manager position Adam eliminated.
- Makes Marco assistant manager while allowing him to remain head bartender.
- Gives Scout the penalty-free choice to take the night off.
- Shows Scout choosing to work the shift under the new management.
- Leaves the future of Scout's relationship with Mike unresolved.
