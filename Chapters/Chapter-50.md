# Chapter Fifty — Adam and Cooper Are Getting Worried

## 1. Don remains missing

- Adam and Cooper still cannot contact Don.
- None of their calls or messages receives a response.
- Don has not appeared at any familiar location or contacted anyone they expect him to trust.
- Their earlier anger begins giving way to serious worry.

## 2. Adam fears Don has been taken

- Adam no longer believes Don is simply hiding after the failed pursuit of Terri Anne and Patti Anne.
- He becomes convinced that someone took Don off the streets.
- Adam worries that police, security personnel, or an unknown enemy may be questioning him.
- He still does not know that Bryan's team has Don.

## 3. Cooper reviews everything he told Adam

- Cooper repeats what he knows about Mike's Los Angeles trip and security arrangements.
- Mike travelled aboard a Key Corporation airplane and later moved with twenty guards.
- Before leaving, Mike made certain Cooper saw Charlene, Olivia, Mei, and Tsumugi staying with Terri Anne and Patti Anne.
- Cooper and Adam wonder whether Mike expected trouble before he left.

## 4. They worry about Mike's timing

- Don disappeared while Mike was in the air.
- The timing makes Adam and Cooper suspicious, but it also prevents them from proving Mike arranged anything.
- Cooper argues that Mike may have planned the operation before boarding the airplane.
- Adam responds that suspicion without evidence will not help them find Don.

## 5. Their greatest fear is that Don is talking

- Adam worries that Don will reveal Victoria's fifteen-million-dollar promise.
- Cooper fears Don will explain his pursuit of Terri Anne and Patti Anne.
- Both men know Don can identify their roles and describe how Victoria used them.
- They are especially afraid that Don will expose the hidden physical task for which Victoria needed his strength.

## 6. Cooper worries about his position

- Cooper understands that his access to Mike's travel, mansion, and family makes him vulnerable.
- If Don exposes the conspiracy, Mike may realize that his driver has been protecting Victoria's plan.
- Cooper fears losing his position before he can learn what Mike knows.
- He also worries that Mike may already be deliberately feeding him misleading information.

## 7. Adam worries about himself

- Adam fears that Don will blame him for directing and insulting him.
- He remembers treating Don as strength rather than as an equal conspirator.
- Adam knows Don resents being excluded from Victoria's meetings at The Well.
- The possibility of Don seeking revenge makes Adam increasingly anxious.

## 8. They distrust Victoria

- Adam and Cooper consider whether Victoria knows where Don is but has chosen not to tell them.
- They question whether she would sacrifice Don—or either of them—to protect herself.
- Her promise of fifteen million dollars each no longer feels secure.
- Their worry begins weakening their loyalty to her plan.

## 9. They fear the new security around Mike

- Cooper tells Adam that Mike's twenty-guard detail is far larger than his ordinary protection.
- Neither man knows that Victoria told Ashlee about hiring another professional assassin.
- They wonder whether Mike discovered a threat that has not yet appeared in Bryan's earlier surveillance evidence.
- The unexplained guards make them fear that Mike knows far more than he has revealed.

## 10. Adam wants to run

- Adam considers leaving before Don can identify him.
- Cooper argues that disappearing would make both of them look guilty.
- Adam accuses Cooper of wanting to remain near Mike only to protect his own position.
- Cooper insists that staying close is their only way to learn what Mike knows.

## 11. Cooper wants to warn Victoria

- Cooper says they can no longer delay telling Victoria that Don is missing.
- Adam worries that Victoria will blame them for failing to locate him.
- They both understand that her hidden plan may be impossible without Don's physical strength.
- Neither wants to be the person who tells her that Don may already be confessing.

## 12. Don is doing exactly what they fear

- While Adam and Cooper argue, Don is giving Bryan a full account.
- He believes Bryan's bluff that Adam and Cooper are already confessing to other investigators.
- Don's anger, fear, and desire for revenge keep him talking.
- Adam and Cooper do not know that their worst fear is already becoming reality.

## 13. Mike calls his investigator

- From Los Angeles, Mike calls his investigator for an update about Don.
- Mike says that he will fly overnight on the following night and arrive in Toronto at approximately **six o'clock in the morning**.
- The arrival time gives the investigator a deadline for preparing Don's full statement and any information Mike should know before returning.
- Mike does not want Cooper told that Don is being questioned or revealing the conspiracy.

## 14. Cooper will drive Mike home from the airport

- Cooper will collect Mike after the Key Corporation airplane arrives in Toronto at six o'clock in the morning.
- Cooper will then drive Mike home from the airport to the Carlisle mansion.
- Mike tells the investigator that he will sit in the front passenger seat beside Cooper during the drive home.
- Mike intends to use that early-morning journey home to confront Cooper directly about his betrayal and involvement with Victoria.
- Sitting beside Cooper will allow Mike to observe his reactions and prevent him from avoiding the conversation.
- Cooper will expect to be performing his normal driving duties and will not know that Mike has received information from Don.
- The investigator understands that nothing about Don's confession should be disclosed before Mike confronts Cooper.

## 15. Closing hook

- Adam and Cooper are frightened by Don's disappearance, Mike's security, and Victoria's possible reaction.
- Their trust in each other and Victoria continues to collapse.
- Cooper prepares to contact Victoria without knowing that Mike will arrive in Toronto at six o'clock the following morning.
- The chapter ends with Cooper expecting an ordinary airport pickup and drive to the Carlisle mansion while Mike prepares to confront him on the way home.

## Chapter Purpose

This chapter:

- Shows Adam and Cooper becoming increasingly worried about Don.
- Revisits Cooper's information about Mike's trip and Terri Anne's guests.
- Raises their fear that Don is exposing the conspiracy.
- Shows Cooper worrying about losing his access to Mike.
- Shows Adam fearing Don's revenge.
- Weakens their trust in Victoria and each other.
- Connects Mike's twenty guards to their uncertainty about what he knows.
- Confirms to the reader that Don is doing exactly what they fear.
- Has Mike tell his investigator that he will fly overnight and arrive in Toronto at six o'clock in the morning.
- Establishes that Cooper will drive Mike home from Toronto airport to the Carlisle mansion, with Mike sitting beside him.
- Sets up Mike's direct confrontation with Cooper about his betrayal and involvement with Victoria.
- Keeps Cooper unaware that Mike has received information from Don.
