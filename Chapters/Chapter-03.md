# Chapter Three — Mike's Second Visit

## 1. Mike returns to the Blue Velvet Room

- Mike makes a second anonymous visit to the Blue Velvet Room while Bryan's investigation and the confidential corporate review continue.
- Once again, no one at the club recognizes him as Mike Key or knows that he is the true owner.
- He wants to see Scout again, but he also wants to observe whether Adam and Don repeat their previous behavior.
- Mike takes the same seat beside the bar.

## 2. Marco serves Mike

- Marco recognizes Mike as the unusually curious and generous customer from his previous visit.
- He serves Mike another **Johnnie Walker on ice**.
- Marco is tempted to ask why Mike has returned, but Mike keeps the conversation casual.
- Mike quietly observes Adam, Don, and the general atmosphere inside the club.

## 3. Scout finishes her set

- Scout notices Mike while she is performing and recognizes him immediately.
- His return pleases her, although she tries not to show too much interest while Adam is watching.
- After finishing her set, Scout joins Mike at the bar.
- Marco prepares her usual **virgin Bloody Mary with extra celery sticks**.
- Mike and Scout resume their conversation with greater ease than during their first meeting.

## 4. Mike monopolizes Scout's time again

- Mike and Scout become absorbed in their conversation.
- Scout enjoys being treated as a person rather than merely as an employee of the club.
- Mike continues to conceal his surname, wealth, and ownership of the Blue Velvet Room.
- Scout continues to hide her real name, her home life, and Patti Anne.
- Adam notices that Scout is again spending most of her available time with Mike.

## 5. Adam and Don confront Mike again

- Adam approaches the bar with Don at his side.
- He again accuses Mike of monopolizing Scout's time.
- Don attempts to intimidate Mike just as he did during the first visit.
- Mike recognizes that their repeated routine supports his suspicion that Adam and Don use pressure to extract money from customers.
- Scout grows tense because she fears what Adam may do if Mike challenges him.
- Marco watches from behind the bar, remembering how Mike handled the previous confrontation.

## 6. Mike places five hundred dollars on the bar

- Mike remains completely calm.
- This time, he takes out **five hundred dollars** and lays it on the bar.
- He says that the money covers Scout's time and both of their drinks.
- Mike makes clear that Scout may remain only if she chooses to do so.
- He never reveals that he owns the club or that Adam and Don are already under investigation.

## 7. Adam's eyes widen

- Adam's eyes nearly pop out when he sees the five hundred dollars.
- His confrontational manner disappears immediately.
- Adam becomes excessively friendly and assures Mike that Scout may stay with him as long as he wishes.
- He signals Don to back away.
- Don obeys but remains suspicious of Mike's confidence and growing generosity.

## 8. Mike studies Adam's reaction

- Mike realizes that Adam's concern was never truly about club policy or fairness to the other customers.
- Adam's dramatic change in attitude confirms that money controls his decisions.
- Mike quietly memorizes Adam's words and behavior so he can report them to Olivia, Charlene, and Bryan.
- Adam sees Mike as an increasingly valuable customer, unaware that his greed is providing evidence against him.

## 9. Scout reacts to Mike's generosity

- Scout is astonished by the five-hundred-dollar payment.
- She asks Mike why he is willing to spend so much simply to talk with her.
- Mike answers that he values her company and dislikes seeing anyone pressure her.
- He makes clear that the money does not purchase affection, intimacy, or control over her.
- Scout becomes more curious about who Mike really is.

## 10. Marco grows more suspicious

- Marco serves their drinks while watching the exchange.
- Mike's questions, confidence, repeated visits, and increasing payments no longer seem like the behavior of an ordinary customer.
- Marco begins to suspect that Mike has a hidden reason for visiting the Blue Velvet Room.
- He wonders whether Mike is connected to law enforcement, the corporation, or the club's unknown owner.

## 11. Closing hook

- Mike and Scout continue talking over their familiar drinks while Adam watches greedily from across the room.
- Scout considers telling Mike something personal but loses her nerve before revealing Patti Anne.
- Mike senses that she is frightened and hiding an important truth, but he does not pressure her.
- The chapter ends with Adam privately deciding that Mike's interest in Scout can be used to make even more money.

## Chapter Purpose

This chapter:

- Brings Mike and Scout together for a second time.
- Repeats the confrontation while raising the payment from two hundred to five hundred dollars.
- Exposes Adam's greed through his astonished reaction to the money.
- Gives Mike further evidence that Adam and Don may be corrupt.
- Deepens the connection between Mike and Scout without revealing their secrets.
- Makes Marco increasingly suspicious of Mike's true identity.
- Sets up Adam's attempt to exploit Mike's interest in Scout.
