# Chapter Fifty-Seven — The Photograph of an Empty Bedroom

## 1. Mike and Olivia leave the investigator's office

- Mike and Olivia travel toward the Carlisle mansion in one of the security vehicles.
- Cooper remains behind with Don, Bryan, the investigators, and the three-million-dollar cheque.
- Mike is exhausted from the overnight flight, the airport pickup, and the confrontation at the investigator's office.
- He is eager to see Terri Anne and Patti Anne.

## 2. Mike checks his iPhone

- During the drive, Mike looks at his iPhone.
- A new message from Terri Anne is waiting for him.
- The message contains a photograph rather than a long written explanation.
- Seeing Terri Anne's name immediately draws Mike's full attention.

## 3. Terri Anne sends a room photograph

- Mike opens the photograph Terri Anne sent.
- It shows a room inside their Carlisle mansion.
- The room is one of the mansion's empty bedrooms.
- No people appear in the photograph, and nothing in the image immediately explains why Terri Anne chose that particular room.

## 4. Mike recognizes the bedroom

- Because the mansion has twenty bedrooms, several rooms remain unused.
- Mike studies the windows, doorway, and shape of the room until he recognizes where it is located.
- The photograph confirms that Terri Anne has been exploring more of the mansion while he was away.
- He wonders whether the room is connected to the welcome she is planning for him.

## 5. The empty room creates questions

- Mike considers several possible reasons Terri Anne might have sent the photograph.
- She may want to give the room a new purpose, ask him something about the mansion, or prepare a surprise.
- Mike does not assume that he knows what Terri Anne wants.
- He decides to let her explain the photograph when she is ready.

## 6. Olivia notices Mike's reaction

- Olivia notices Mike studying his phone and sees his expression soften.
- She asks whether Terri Anne sent him something.
- Mike says only that Terri Anne sent a photograph of one of the empty bedrooms.
- Olivia respects his privacy and does not ask him to show her the image.

## 7. Mike thinks about the mansion changing

- Before Terri Anne and Patti Anne began moving in, the empty bedrooms represented unused space in an enormous house.
- The photograph makes Mike think about how the mansion is slowly becoming a shared home.
- Terri Anne's interest in an empty room suggests that she is beginning to imagine a future there rather than treating herself as a temporary guest.
- Mike smiles at that possibility without deciding what the room will become.

## 8. Mike waits for Terri Anne's explanation

- Mike keeps the photograph open for another moment before locking his iPhone.
- He does not call Terri Anne because he is already on the way home and wants to hear the explanation in person.
- The security vehicle continues toward the Carlisle mansion.
- Mike wonders what Terri Anne has planned inside the empty bedroom.

## 9. Mike arrives at the mansion

- The security vehicle brings Mike and Olivia to the Carlisle mansion.
- Mike enters his home after removing Cooper from his trusted position and leaving Bryan to handle the three men.
- He expects Terri Anne to explain the photograph she sent.
- Instead of waiting for her to come to him, Mike begins looking for her.

## 10. Mike looks for Terri Anne

- Mike checks the areas where Terri Anne usually spends time with Patti Anne.
- Mei is at work, and Tsumugi is at school.
- Charlene and the others are elsewhere, leaving Mike and Terri Anne privacy inside the large mansion.
- Mike realizes Terri Anne is probably waiting inside the bedroom shown in the photograph.
- He follows the familiar route to the unused part of the house.

## 11. Mike enters the empty bedroom

- Mike reaches the bedroom and goes inside.
- The room is still largely empty and has not yet been given a normal household purpose.
- A dresser provides one of the few pieces of furniture in the room.
- Mike immediately sees Terri Anne waiting for him.

## 12. Terri Anne's pose

- Terri Anne is on her knees in the empty bedroom.
- Both of her palms are turned upward toward the ceiling.
- Her hair is arranged in French braids.
- She is wearing a pink thong and ivory thigh-high stockings.
- The careful pose and clothing reveal that the photograph was part of the welcome she planned for Mike.

## 13. The baby monitor

- Patti Anne's baby monitor rests on top of the dresser.
- Terri Anne has kept the monitor close enough to hear if her daughter needs her.
- Mike notices it immediately and understands that Patti Anne remains part of Terri Anne's awareness even while she welcomes him home.
- The monitor reassures them that they can respond promptly if Patti Anne wakes or becomes upset.

## 14. Terri Anne defines the bedroom role

- When Mike walks into the bedroom, Terri Anne looks at him from her kneeling position.
- She tells him, **“You are my master while we are in this bedroom.”**
- Her words define a role that applies specifically inside this room rather than changing their relationship everywhere else.
- Mike understands that the empty bedroom has become a private space for the dynamic Terri Anne chose.
- The active baby monitor remains on the dresser so Patti Anne's needs can interrupt the scene at any time.

## 15. Mike asks whether Terri Anne is certain

- Mike asks Terri Anne whether she is sure that she wants him to be her master while they are inside this bedroom.
- Terri Anne confirms that she is certain about the private role she proposed.
- Mike takes her answer seriously rather than assuming that her pose or clothing speaks for her.

## 16. Terri Anne explains the unfinished bedroom

- Terri Anne tells Mike that she is copying the idea from one of the specially equipped rooms at Key Porn Studios.
- She wanted to recreate that kind of private space inside their mansion.
- Terri Anne explains that she did not have enough time to buy the equipment or furniture needed to complete the bedroom before Mike returned.
- The mostly empty room is therefore only the beginning of what she imagines.
- Mike understands that the photograph showed both a welcome-home surprise and Terri Anne's idea for the room's future purpose.

## 17. Mike takes Terri Anne into the bathroom

- Mike accepts Terri Anne's answer and the private role she has chosen for the room.
- He helps her rise from her kneeling position.
- Mike takes Terri Anne into the bathroom adjoining the empty bedroom.
- They bring Patti Anne's baby monitor with them so they can continue listening for her.
- Terri Anne follows Mike while wearing her pink thong and ivory thigh-high stockings, with her hair still in French braids.

## 18. Patti Anne begins crying

- While Mike and Terri Anne are inside the bathroom, they hear Patti Anne begin crying through the baby monitor.
- Terri Anne immediately pays attention to her daughter's cries.
- Through the monitor, Mike and Terri Anne hear Olivia enter Patti Anne's separate bedroom and comfort her.
- They also hear Olivia change Patti Anne's little diaper before taking her downstairs.
- Olivia does not enter the empty bedroom or interrupt Mike and Terri Anne's privacy.

## 19. Olivia takes Patti Anne to the kitchen

- Olivia lifts Patti Anne from her bedroom and tells her softly that they are going downstairs.
- Mike and Terri Anne hear Patti Anne's crying begin to settle through the monitor.
- Olivia carries Patti Anne to the kitchen for her afternoon snack.
- Mei remains at work, and Tsumugi remains at school.

## 20. Closing hook

- Mike and Terri Anne remain in the bathroom with the monitor nearby.
- Terri Anne knows Patti Anne is safe with Olivia and that Olivia did not intrude on their private bedroom.
- Downstairs, Olivia prepares to feed Patti Anne her familiar cookie softened with Terri Anne's breast milk.
- The chapter ends with Patti Anne settling in the kitchen while Mike and Terri Anne's private room remains unfinished.
