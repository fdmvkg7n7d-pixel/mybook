# Chapter Twenty-Nine — Another Date

## 1. Terri Anne decides to call Mike

- After Captain Morrison's visit to the Blue Velvet Room, Terri Anne thinks about Mike and their first date.
- She wants to see him again and decides not to wait for him to make the next invitation.
- Her willingness to initiate another date shows that the embarrassment at the mansion did not make her regret their time together.
- She calls Mike on his private number.

## 2. Mike is happy to hear from her

- Mike answers and is pleased to hear Terri Anne's voice.
- Since she trusted him with her real name, he now addresses her as Terri Anne during their private conversations.
- He asks how she and Patti Anne are doing.
- Terri Anne tells him that she is calling because she would like another date.

## 3. Terri Anne suggests Sunday

- Terri Anne asks whether Mike is available for a date on the coming Sunday.
- She wants another opportunity to spend time together away from the Blue Velvet Room.
- Once again, the invitation comes from her rather than from Mike using his position to pursue an employee.
- Mike is touched that she wants to see him again.

## 4. Mike explains the Tokyo trip

- Unfortunately, Mike tells Terri Anne that he cannot meet her on Sunday.
- He will be in **Tokyo on business**.
- Mike makes clear that he is declining the date only because of the trip, not because he has changed his mind about her.
- Terri Anne is disappointed but appreciates his honesty.

## 5. Mike suggests the following Saturday

- Mike offers an alternative date: the following Saturday after Terri Anne finishes her shift at the Blue Velvet Room.
- He tells her that he would like to see her when he returns from Tokyo.
- The suggestion allows Terri Anne to keep her work schedule without asking Charlene or Marco for special treatment.

## 6. Mike removes the pressure

- Mike tells Terri Anne that there is **no pressure** to accept his suggested day.
- He makes clear that she may decline, choose another time, or change her mind.
- He does not want his ownership of the club or their first night together to make her feel obligated.
- Terri Anne recognizes that he is deliberately protecting her freedom to choose.

## 7. The relationship continues at Terri Anne's pace

- Terri Anne is pleased that Mike offers another day instead of simply ending the conversation.
- She considers the practical details of a late date after her Saturday shift and Patti Anne's childcare.
- Mike allows her time to decide rather than demanding an immediate commitment.
- Their conversation remains warm despite the disappointment about Sunday.

## 8. Closing beat

- Mike prepares for his business trip to Tokyo.
- Terri Anne looks ahead to the possibility of seeing him the following Saturday after her shift.
- The distance between Hamilton and Tokyo will temporarily separate them, but neither treats it as the end of their developing relationship.
- The chapter ends with the second date proposed and Terri Anne free to confirm it when she is ready.

## Chapter Purpose

This chapter:

- Has Terri Anne initiate another date with Mike.
- Shows her asking to see him on the coming Sunday.
- Establishes that Mike will be in Tokyo on business.
- Gives Mike an alternative date for the following Saturday after her shift.
- Reinforces that Terri Anne faces no pressure to accept.
- Continues developing their relationship at Terri Anne's chosen pace.
