# Chapter Fifteen — Someone Is Following Them

## 1. Terri Anne takes Patti Anne to the Y

- On **Sunday**, the day after the Saturday intervention at the Blue Velvet Room, Terri Anne sets out with eight-month-old Patti Anne for a swimming visit at **the Y on Hamilton Mountain**.
- Patti Anne rides in her stroller while Terri Anne pushes her along the street.
- They have not yet reached the Y or entered the swimming area.
- Terri Anne carries everything they will need for Patti Anne's swim in or alongside the stroller.
- She looks forward to giving her daughter a happy and ordinary experience away from the Blue Velvet Room.

## 2. Terri Anne notices someone behind them

- While walking through the streets toward the Y, Terri Anne becomes aware of someone behind her and Patti Anne.
- The same person remains nearby after more than one turn or change in direction.
- Terri Anne cannot tell whether the person is connected to Adam, Don, her trafficking history, or something else.
- She refuses to ignore her instincts because Patti Anne's safety is involved.

## 3. Terri Anne calls Mike

- Terri Anne quickly takes out her iPhone and calls Mike's private number while continuing to push Patti Anne's stroller.
- She tells him that she is taking Patti Anne swimming and believes a man is following them.
- Terri Anne describes the follower as wearing a **blue jacket and blue hat**.
- Her voice reveals how frightened and nervous she has become.
- She keeps Patti Anne close and avoids confronting or openly staring at the man.

## 4. Mike and Olivia are in the limousine

- Mike answers while riding in his limousine with **Olivia**.
- **Cooper**, Mike's driver, is behind the wheel.
- As soon as Mike hears Terri Anne's fear, he treats the situation as a possible emergency.
- Olivia listens as Mike asks Terri Anne for her location and a description of the follower.
- Terri Anne tells him that she is near the Y on Hamilton Mountain and repeats that the man is wearing a blue jacket and blue hat.

## 5. Mike redirects the limousine

- Mike immediately tells Cooper to take them to the Y as quickly as possible.
- Cooper changes course and heads toward Hamilton Mountain.
- Mike keeps Terri Anne on the telephone during the drive.
- Olivia prepares to help when they arrive.

## 6. Cooper recognizes the description

- Cooper recognizes the man Terri Anne describes as **Don**, the former bouncer at the Blue Velvet Room.
- Cooper knows Don through Adam, who is Cooper's half brother on their father's side.
- Cooper has never told Mike or Olivia about this family connection.
- He is also secretly involved in protecting a hidden plan belonging to Mike's twenty-eight-year-old sister, **Victoria Key**.
- Cooper fears that Don knows enough to expose or destroy Victoria's plan.
- Adam and Cooper regard Don as reckless and unintelligent, making them afraid that he will reveal the secret through carelessness.
- Cooper believes Don must be dealt with sooner rather than later, and he considers attacking him before he can expose the plan.
- Instead of warning Mike and Olivia that Don is following Terri Anne and Patti Anne, Cooper remains silent.
- His silence protects Victoria's secret but leaves Terri Anne and Patti Anne in danger.

## 7. Mike tells Terri Anne to remain calm

- Mike tells Terri Anne to stay as calm as she can for Patti Anne's sake.
- He does not dismiss her fear or tell her that she is imagining the danger.
- He instructs her not to confront the person following them.
- His steady voice helps her focus on reaching safety.

## 8. Terri Anne enters the Y

- Mike tells Terri Anne to walk calmly into the Y.
- The building will place her and Patti Anne around a crowd rather than leaving them exposed on the street.
- Terri Anne follows his instructions without running or alerting the possible follower.
- She enters the Y with Patti Anne and moves toward an area where staff members and other visitors are present.

## 9. Mike stays on the telephone

- Mike remains on the line while Terri Anne enters the building.
- He asks her to stay near the crowd and wait for him and Olivia.
- Terri Anne keeps Patti Anne close while watching the entrance from a safe distance.
- She does not know whether the unknown person followed them inside or remained on the street.

## 10. Mike and Olivia arrive at the Y

- Cooper brings the limousine to the Y with Mike and Olivia inside.
- Mike remains on the telephone with Scout as they arrive.
- He tells her, **“Olivia is running into the Y to get you.”**
- Mike asks Scout to remain in the crowded public area with Patti Anne and wait for Olivia.

## 11. Scout worries about Patti Anne

- Scout is relieved that help has arrived, but she remains frightened by the follower outside.
- She is also nervous about Patti Anne meeting someone new.
- Her experiences with Adam, Don, and trafficking have made her cautious about allowing unfamiliar people near her daughter.
- Although Mike trusts Olivia, Scout has only met her briefly at the Blue Velvet Room.
- She holds Patti Anne close and watches carefully as Olivia approaches.

## 12. Olivia finds them

- Olivia runs into the Y and looks for Scout and the baby in the stroller.
- She recognizes Scout from their meeting at the Blue Velvet Room.
- Olivia approaches calmly so that she does not frighten Scout or Patti Anne.
- She tells Scout that Mike is waiting outside and that they will leave together.

## 13. Patti Anne immediately likes Olivia

- Patti Anne studies Olivia when they meet.
- She is immediately drawn to Olivia, especially her **deep blue eyes**.
- Patti Anne responds happily instead of becoming upset by the unfamiliar person.
- Her reaction helps Scout relax enough to accept Olivia's assistance.
- Olivia speaks gently to Patti Anne while respecting Scout's protectiveness.

## 14. Mike contacts Bryan

- Once Olivia enters the Y, Mike sends a text message to **Bryan**, his private investigator.
- He reports that a man in a blue jacket and blue hat followed Scout and Patti Anne near the Y on Hamilton Mountain.
- Mike asks Bryan to investigate immediately and determine whether the follower is connected to Adam, Don, or the Blue Velvet Room.
- Mike does not know that Cooper has already recognized the follower as Don.

## 15. Olivia escorts Scout outside

- Olivia checks that the immediate route to the limousine is clear.
- Scout pushes Patti Anne's stroller while staying close to Olivia.
- They leave the crowded Y and move quickly but calmly toward Mike's limousine.
- Scout continues watching for the man in the blue jacket and blue hat.

## 16. Cooper helps them into the limousine

- Cooper gets out to assist when Scout, Patti Anne, and Olivia reach the limousine.
- He helps Scout and Olivia get Patti Anne safely inside.
- Cooper takes the stroller, folds or secures it, and places it in the limousine's trunk.
- To Mike and Olivia, Cooper appears to be a helpful and professional driver responding to an emergency.

## 17. Cooper hides his resentment

- Internally, Cooper hates having to help Scout and Patti Anne.
- He resents their connection to Mike and fears that protecting them will bring Mike closer to discovering Don, Adam, and Victoria's hidden plan.
- Cooper conceals his feelings behind a professional expression.
- Scout and Olivia do not realize that the man helping them enter the limousine withheld the follower's identity.

## 18. Scout and Patti Anne enter Mike's private world

- Scout climbs into the limousine with Patti Anne and Olivia.
- This is the first time Patti Anne is physically present with Mike.
- Scout remains protective and uncertain, but Patti Anne's comfort with Olivia gives her reassurance.
- Mike is relieved that both mother and daughter are safely inside the vehicle.
- He continues referring to Terri Anne as Scout, preserving the identity she has chosen to use around Olivia.

## 19. Scout thanks Mike and Olivia

- Once everyone is safely inside the limousine, Scout thanks Mike and Olivia for coming to help her and Patti Anne.
- Mike and Olivia tell her that it was no problem to help her and her lovely daughter.
- Their response reassures Scout that Patti Anne is not an inconvenience or a secret they resent having to protect.

## 20. Patti Anne meets Mike

- Patti Anne sees Mike in person for the first time.
- She immediately likes him without any hesitation or doubt.
- Her relaxed and happy response surprises Scout, who remains cautious about introducing her daughter to new people.
- Mike responds warmly without overwhelming Patti Anne or trying to take her from Scout.

## 21. Mike asks what Scout wants to do

- Mike asks Scout whether she wants to go home or do something else.
- The question gives her control over what happens next instead of deciding for her.
- Scout says that she wants to stop at **Tim Hortons** to buy lunch.
- She explains that going for lunch is part of her and Patti Anne's swimming tradition.
- Even though they did not get to swim, Scout wants to preserve part of their familiar routine.

## 22. Mike suggests a safer arrangement

- Mike tells Scout that Don may still be nearby.
- He suggests that it would be safer for Scout and Patti Anne to remain inside the limousine while Olivia goes into Tim Hortons for them.
- Scout agrees because the suggestion protects Patti Anne without completely abandoning their tradition.

## 23. The Tim Hortons order

- Scout tells Olivia that she would like a **bagel with cream cheese** and a **green tea**.
- Mike asks Olivia to bring him a **black coffee**.
- From the driver's seat, Cooper says that he would also like a **black coffee**.
- Olivia confirms the orders before preparing to enter Tim Hortons.
- Cooper continues acting like a cooperative employee, concealing his connection to Adam, Don, and Victoria's hidden plan.

## 24. Olivia returns with lunch and coffee

- Olivia returns to the limousine with Scout's bagel with cream cheese and green tea.
- She also brings black coffees for Mike and Cooper.
- The limousine remains parked while Scout eats and the others begin drinking their coffees.
- Patti Anne remains calm with the group.

## 25. Scout asks to care for Patti Anne

- Scout asks Mike and Olivia whether they mind if she changes Patti Anne's diaper and nurses her while they drink their coffees.
- Mike and Olivia tell her that it is no problem.
- They assure Scout that she does not need to cover herself while nursing unless doing so would make her more comfortable.
- Their response helps Scout understand that caring for Patti Anne will be treated as normal rather than embarrassing or inconvenient.
- Mike and Olivia give her the space and respect she needs while remaining nearby for safety.

## 26. Mike orders Cooper to drive to Scout's apartment

- After Scout finishes caring for Patti Anne, Mike tells Cooper to drive to Scout's apartment.
- Cooper obeys without revealing his resentment or his knowledge of Don's identity.
- Mike remains concerned that Don may know where Scout and Patti Anne live.

## 27. Mike assigns protection

- Mike tells Scout that he will assign **four Key Corporation security guards** to protect her and Patti Anne.
- The guards will watch the apartment and remain alert for Don or anyone connected to Adam.
- Mike explains that the protection is intended to help her feel safe, not to control her movements.
- Scout understands that the follower has turned the danger into something larger than a single frightening walk to the Y.

## 28. Closing hook

- Cooper drives toward Scout's apartment while Mike and Olivia remain with Scout and Patti Anne.
- Bryan begins investigating the follower based on Mike's private text message, which Cooper does not know about.
- Mike believes his driver helped bring Scout and Patti Anne to safety.
- Cooper knows that Don is the follower, but he does not know Mike hired an investigator.
- The chapter ends with Cooper planning to confront Don before Mike's security arrangements expose the connection to Victoria's hidden plan.
