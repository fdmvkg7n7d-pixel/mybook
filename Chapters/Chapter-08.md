# Chapter Eight — The Morning Call

## 1. Scout finds the courage to call

- The morning after Mike's third visit to the Blue Velvet Room, Scout uses the private number from his business card.
- Calling him is a significant act of trust because Adam and Don have taught her to fear giving anyone access to her private life.
- She calls as Scout and does not yet tell Mike that her real name is Terri Anne Williams.
- Mike recognizes or quickly realizes who is calling and is genuinely pleased to hear from her.

## 2. Mike is working out

- Mike answers his iPhone while exercising in the gym at his Carlisle mansion.
- He asks Scout whether she minds if he places his iPhone on speaker so he can continue his workout.
- Scout agrees to his request.
- She tells him that her own iPhone is already on speaker, although she does not explain why.

## 3. Terri Anne nurses Patti Anne

- At home, Terri Anne is nursing eight-month-old Patti Anne while speaking with Mike.
- Having her iPhone on speaker allows her to keep her hands free and care for her daughter.
- Terri Anne does not tell Mike that she is nursing or that a baby is present.
- She remains frightened that revealing Patti Anne could expose both of them to further danger.

## 4. Mike hears Patti Anne

- Patti Anne makes happy squealing sounds in the background.
- Mike hears the squeals clearly through the speakerphone.
- Because of Bryan's report, he knows that the sounds belong to Terri Anne's daughter.
- He smiles but does not reveal that he knows Patti Anne's name or even that Scout has a child.
- The warm sounds between mother and daughter reinforce his belief that Terri Anne is an excellent and loving mother.

## 5. An ordinary conversation

- Mike and Scout do not discuss anything dramatic or especially important.
- They talk about ordinary subjects, their morning, and small pieces of daily life.
- The apparent lack of importance is meaningful: Scout has called simply because she wants to hear Mike's voice.
- Mike enjoys speaking with her without asking questions that might expose her secrets.
- Their easy conversation begins moving their relationship beyond the controlled setting of the Blue Velvet Room.

## 6. Two concealed lives

- Terri Anne continues nursing Patti Anne without telling Mike what she is doing.
- Mike continues exercising without admitting that the gym is inside his mansion.
- Terri Anne does not know she is speaking to the wealthiest man in the world or the secret owner of her workplace.
- Mike knows more about her private life than she realizes, but he carefully avoids using that knowledge.
- Their friendly call is therefore built on genuine affection as well as major unspoken truths.

## 7. Wolfee gives Mike a silent reminder

- Mike's house manager, **Wolfee**, enters the mansion's gym while the call remains on speaker.
- Wolfee does not speak because he does not want Scout to hear him or learn anything about Mike's private surroundings.
- Instead, Wolfee points silently to his right wrist, using the familiar gesture to remind Mike of the time.
- Mike understands that he must finish the call and prepare for his trip to **Buffalo**.
- Scout remains unaware that Mike is taking the call inside the private gym of his mansion.

## 8. Mike ends the call

- Mike tells Scout that he is glad she called.
- Scout is relieved that the conversation felt comfortable and that he did not pressure her for personal information.
- After they say goodbye, Mike continues smiling as he remembers Patti Anne's cheerful squeals.
- Terri Anne looks down at her daughter, knowing she has taken a small but important step toward trusting Mike.

## 9. The Buffalo Old Train Terminal project

- Once the call has ended, Mike thanks Wolfee for the discreet reminder.
- Wolfee tells him, "No problem."
- Mike asks whether the papers concerning **Buffalo's Old Train Terminal** are waiting on his desk.
- Wolfee confirms that the documents have been placed there for Mike to review before the trip.
- Key Corporation is acquiring the rights to restore and redevelop the old terminal.
- Mike's plan is to transform the historic structure into offices for Key Corporation.
- The chapter ends with Mike leaving the gym to review the papers and prepare for Buffalo.

## Chapter Purpose

This chapter:

- Shows Scout using Mike's private number for the first time.
- Moves their relationship outside the Blue Velvet Room.
- Shows both characters using their iPhones on speaker.
- Places Mike in the gym at his Carlisle mansion.
- Shows Terri Anne nursing Patti Anne while keeping her daughter secret.
- Allows Mike to hear Patti Anne's happy squeals without exposing what he knows.
- Uses an ordinary conversation to deepen Mike and Terri Anne's connection.
- Introduces Mike's house manager, Wolfee, and shows his discretion.
- Sets up Mike's trip to Buffalo.
- Introduces Key Corporation's plan to restore Buffalo's Old Train Terminal as corporate offices.
