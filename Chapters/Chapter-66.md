## 9. Kyoto as a family city

- They visit gardens, historic streets, and quiet public spaces at a pace suitable for Patti Anne.

- Mike makes certain that security does not prevent mother and daughter from enjoying ordinary moments.

## 10. Kyoto Railway Museum

- Mike takes Terri Anne and Patti Anne to the Kyoto Railway Museum.

- Patti Anne responds to the movement, lights, and unfamiliar sounds.

- The family visits Kyoto Aquarium on another day.
- Terri Anne carries Patti Anne where the baby can watch fish moving through the water.
- Mike enjoys seeing Patti Anne focus on the changing colours and shapes.

## 12. A day in Osaka

- Mike, Terri Anne, and Patti Anne travel from Kyoto to Osaka for a day.

- The family visits Osaka Aquarium Kaiyukan and walks through a shopping district without rushing.

## 13. Terri Anne begins to feel less like a visitor

- As the first week ends, Terri Anne becomes more confident navigating the hotel and nearby streets.
- She learns a few basic Japanese greetings and polite expressions.
- Mike does not speak for her when she wants to ask a simple question herself.
- Terri Anne enjoys being treated as an adult traveller and Patti Anne's mother.

## 14. Travelling to Tokyo

- During the second week, Mike, Terri Anne, and Patti Anne travel to Tokyo.
- They stay in the Key family penthouse rather than a hotel.
- The penthouse occupies one floor and has no internal staircase.
- Security checks the penthouse before Terri Anne and Patti Anne enter.

- Temporary safety gates prevent her from reaching service areas and unsafe rooms.
- Mike repeats that the gates protect Patti Anne without turning the home into a place where she cannot explore.

- Terri Anne and Patti Anne remain together at the Tokyo penthouse.

## 18. Four days dealing with Ric's problem

## 19. Mike returns to his family

- Mike's final return flight is also scheduled at night.
- After four days, he returns to Tokyo and rejoins Terri Anne and Patti Anne at the penthouse.
- The nighttime travel lets him finish the urgent work and return to his family without adding unnecessary days away.
- Mike is tired, but he is grateful to be with Terri Anne and Patti Anne again.

- They visit Asakusa and view the city from a high observation level.
- Terri Anne buys small keepsakes connected to Patti Anne's first trip abroad.

## 21. Christmas shopping begins

- Terri Anne remembers crying in Whistler because she believed she could not afford gifts for her daughter.
- Mike treats each choice as a shared parental decision rather than taking over the shopping.
- They arrange for the presents to be sent securely to the Carlisle mansion without being placed under a tree yet.

## 22. A quiet evening in Tokyo

- After a long day, Mike, Terri Anne, and Patti Anne spend the evening inside the penthouse.
- Terri Anne and Mike talk about which parts of Japan they would like Patti Anne to remember through photographs and stories.
- Patti Anne falls asleep after another day of unfamiliar sights.
- The family enjoys the penthouse as a home rather than merely a luxury property.

## 23. Travelling north to Sapporo

- During the third week, the family travels to Sapporo.
- They stay at the Key family mansion rather than booking another hotel.
- Terri Anne sees the indoor onsen that Mike rebuilt for Ashlee.
- The accessible deck and indoor design allow Ashlee to approach the water with her wheelchair and support equipment when she visits.

## 24. Patti Anne and the indoor onsen

- Before Patti Anne enters, the water is adjusted to an infant-safe temperature.
- Terri Anne holds Patti Anne continuously.

- Patti Anne is never placed alone in the water.

## 25. Terri Anne thinks about Ashlee

- The accessible onsen reminds Terri Anne that Mike altered an entire property so Ashlee could participate rather than watch from outside.

- The Sapporo mansion gives Terri Anne another view of Mike's relationship with his youngest sister.

## 26. Seeing Sapporo

- Mike takes Terri Anne and Patti Anne into Sapporo for gentle outings.
- They visit central public spaces, shops, and places where Terri Anne can experience Hokkaido food and culture.
- Their schedule remains flexible around Patti Anne's sleep and feeding.
- Terri Anne begins imagining future family visits that might include Ashlee and Geoffrey.

## 27. Terri Anne checks both contracts again

- During a quiet day at the Sapporo mansion, Terri Anne rereads both performance agreements.
- She writes down additional questions rather than pretending she understands everything immediately.
- The lawyers answer her through a secure call.
- Terri Anne continues choosing both films but knows she may still change her mind.

## 28. Returning to Kyoto

- During the fourth week, the family returns to Kyoto.
- They move back into the hotel's presidential suite.
- The production teams are not allowed to treat their return as permission to begin filming early.

## 29. Meeting the two women

- Terri Anne meets the two adult women scheduled for her first movie in a non-filming conference setting.
- The meeting allows all three performers to discuss boundaries and the production plan without cameras running.
- The women's nationalities remain unspecified.
- No explicit rehearsal takes place during the waiting period.
- Terri Anne is reminded that each performer may refuse any part of the eventual filming.

## 30. Information about the second cast

- Terri Anne receives the names and required information for the ten Japanese adult men in the second movie.
- All ten men are Japanese.
- The studio confirms health, safety, scheduling, and performer documentation before filming.
- Terri Anne does not meet them in the special equipment room during the waiting period.
- Any pre-production meeting remains professional and non-explicit.

## 31. The final legal review

- Near the end of the month, the lawyers meet Terri Anne again.
- They confirm whether the waiting period, contracts, work authorization, location, safety plans, and distribution disclosures satisfy Japanese requirements.
- They make clear that the passage of one month does not force Terri Anne to perform.
- Terri Anne confirms again that she wants to make both movies.

## 32. Planning the filming day

- The first movie with the two women is scheduled for the morning of the first lawful filming day.
- Terri Anne will receive a four-hour break afterward.
- The second movie with the ten adult men is scheduled only after the break and only if Terri Anne still wants to proceed.
- Medical support, security, transportation, and the secured day care are arranged in advance.

## 33. Patti Anne remains the priority

- Terri Anne prepares Patti Anne's food, clothing, comfort items, and written routine for the day-care staff.
- She plans to check on her daughter between obligations whenever possible.
- Mike promises to remain available to Patti Anne and Terri Anne throughout the day.
- Terri Anne does not have to choose between being a working performer and being recognized as Patti Anne's mother.

## 34. Mike and Terri Anne make time for each other

- During every week of their month in Japan, Mike and Terri Anne try to reserve private time to sleep together.
- Travel, Patti Anne's needs, legal meetings, and Mike's four-day trip to Perth and Bali sometimes make that time difficult to arrange.
- They wait until Patti Anne is safe and keep her baby monitor active whenever appropriate.
- Their private time remains part of their relationship rather than an obligation either person owes the other.

- He does not order her to kneel before knowing whether she wants to enter their private role.
- Terri Anne chooses to kneel on the floor.

## 36. The month changes Terri Anne

- Terri Anne has travelled through several Japanese cities, reviewed her legal rights, and made decisions without being threatened.
