# Chapter Sixty-One — The Diamond Pendant

## 1. Terri Anne chooses another memory

- The conversation continues in the Carlisle mansion's kitchen with Mike, Charlene, Olivia, Terri Anne, and Patti Anne.
- Mike, Charlene, and Olivia have just told Terri Anne that she does not owe them details about the exploitation she survived as a minor.
- Terri Anne chooses to tell them a different story from her childhood.
- She begins speaking about her paternal grandmother.

## 2. Terri Anne was thirteen

- Terri Anne explains that she was thirteen years old when her paternal grandmother died.
- Her grandmother had stomach cancer.
- By the final hospital visit, Terri Anne understood that her grandmother was extremely ill.
- The memory remains clear because Terri Anne was beside her at the end.

## 3. The hospital room

- Terri Anne describes being inside her grandmother's hospital room.
- Medical equipment and hospital sounds surrounded them, but Terri Anne focused on the woman in the bed.
- She stayed close rather than leaving the room when death approached.
- No one could make the experience easy for a thirteen-year-old.

## 4. Terri Anne holds her grandmother's hand

- Terri Anne sat beside the hospital bed and held her grandmother's right hand.
- She continued holding it as her grandmother's condition worsened.
- The physical connection allowed Terri Anne to remain present when words were no longer enough.
- Her paternal grandmother died while Terri Anne was there with her.

## 5. The gift one hour earlier

- Approximately one hour before she died, Terri Anne's grandmother gave her a necklace.
- A diamond pendant hangs from the necklace.
- The grandmother wanted Terri Anne to have something personal that would remain with her after the death.
- Terri Anne accepted the gift without fully understanding how soon she would lose her.

## 6. Terri Anne shows them the necklace

- Terri Anne touches the necklace she is wearing while telling the story.
- She shows Mike, Charlene, and Olivia the diamond pendant.
- It is the same necklace her paternal grandmother placed in her care at the hospital.
- Terri Anne is wearing it during the breakfast conversation.

## 7. The pendant's meaning

- The necklace is not important to Terri Anne merely because the pendant contains a diamond.
- It represents her grandmother's hand, voice, love, and final hour.
- Wearing it allows Terri Anne to carry a connection to the woman who helped shape her childhood.
- The pendant survived everything that happened to Terri Anne afterward.

## 8. Her grandmother and the name Scout

- Terri Anne remembers that her grandmothers called her Scout because she loved digging in the ground.
- They enrolled her in Boy Scouts rather than Girl Guides.
- The paternal grandmother in the hospital story was part of the family history attached to that nickname.
- The diamond pendant therefore connects Terri Anne's present identity with the childhood that existed before her exploitation.

## 9. Mike listens

- Mike remains beside Terri Anne and listens without interrupting.
- He understands that she is choosing to share this memory rather than being pressured to explain her past.
- Mike looks at the pendant differently after learning where it came from.
- He recognizes that its emotional value cannot be measured by the price of the diamond.

## 10. Charlene and Olivia respond

- Charlene expresses sympathy for Terri Anne's loss.
- Olivia acknowledges how difficult it must have been for a thirteen-year-old to hold her grandmother's hand as she died.
- Neither woman turns the story into questions Terri Anne does not want to answer.
- They understand that the necklace is part of Terri Anne's family history and identity.

## 11. Patti Anne and the family connection

- Patti Anne remains close to Terri Anne while the adults talk.
- Terri Anne thinks about how Patti Anne will one day learn about the great-grandmother connected to the necklace.
- The pendant may become a way to tell Patti Anne about the family history she never witnessed.
- Terri Anne keeps the necklace secure while holding her daughter.

## 12. Terri Anne finishes the story

- Terri Anne says her grandmother died from stomach cancer while Terri Anne held her right hand.
- She repeats that the necklace was given to her only one hour before the death.
- Mike, Charlene, and Olivia now understand why Terri Anne continues wearing it.
- The room remains quiet for a moment after she finishes.

## 13. The verbal abuse begins that night

- Terri Anne explains that the night her paternal grandmother died, her parents began verbally abusing her.
- Instead of giving their thirteen-year-old daughter space to grieve, they directed hurtful words and anger toward her.
- The timing permanently connects the loss of her grandmother with the beginning of abuse inside her home.
- Terri Anne does not repeat every word her parents used, and Mike, Charlene, and Olivia do not demand that she do so.
- The diamond pendant became something private she could hold onto when the adults around her made the night even more painful.

## 14. Her parents remove her from Scouts

- After Terri Anne's paternal grandmother died, her parents took Terri Anne out of Scouts.
- Without her grandmother there to support her participation, Terri Anne lost an activity that had been part of her childhood identity.
- The decision deepened her grief because Scouts was connected to the grandmothers who called her Scout and enrolled her in Boy Scouts rather than Girl Guides.
- Terri Anne remembers the removal as another way her parents changed her life immediately after the death.

## 15. Terri Anne plans something different for Patti Anne

- Terri Anne tells Mike, Charlene, and Olivia that she intends to enrol Patti Anne in Scouts or Girl Guides when Patti Anne is old enough.
- She has not yet decided which organization will suit Patti Anne best.
- Terri Anne wants her daughter to have the opportunities, friendships, and support that were taken away from her.
- Mike, Charlene, and Olivia understand that the plan is part of Terri Anne building a different childhood for Patti Anne.

## 16. They laugh at the coincidence

- Mike, Charlene, and Olivia start laughing out loud after Terri Anne mentions Scouts or Girl Guides.
- Their laughter is not directed at Terri Anne or her painful memory.
- Mike explains that age-based Scouts Canada sections and Girl Guides of Canada units meet on the Key Corporation campus.
- Terri Anne is surprised that Key Corporation has individual groups for the different age sections and branches.
- Mike tells her that Key Corporation is trying to make its employees happy and give their families useful programs close to their workplaces.
- When Patti Anne turns five, she can begin with Beaver Scouts, for ages five to seven, or Sparks, for ages five to six.
- As she grows, she can move through the older age sections or branches.
- Terri Anne joins their laughter when she realizes that Patti Anne will have a direct connection to either organization through the campus.

## 17. Charlene talks about her daughter

- Charlene tells Terri Anne that her own daughter participates in Pathfinders through Key Porn Studios Los Angeles.
- Pathfinders is the Girl Guides branch for girls ages twelve through fourteen.
- Charlene's experience reassures Terri Anne that the employee-family programs are active parts of life across Key Corporation's operations.
- Terri Anne realizes that Charlene is speaking as both a company executive and a parent whose child uses one of the programs.

## 18. Olivia talks about Tsumugi's camps

- Olivia tells Terri Anne that fourteen-year-old Tsumugi attends Key Corporation Summer Camp and Key Corporation March Break Camp.
- Olivia explains that she and Mei cannot leave Tsumugi home alone.
- Thanks to Mike, they have special permission to keep Tsumugi in the company camp programs until she turns twenty-one.
- The camp staff completely understand that Tsumugi has Asperger syndrome.
- They understand her communication style and do not treat her differences or age as reasons to exclude her.
- The arrangement gives Tsumugi a safe, understanding place to be when Mei and Olivia cannot remain home with her.
- Olivia's example shows Terri Anne that the corporation's family programs include teenagers and young adults with different support needs.

## 19. Terri Anne sees Mike differently

- Learning that Mike gave Tsumugi permission to remain in the company camps until she turns twenty-one changes the way Terri Anne sees him.
- She realizes that his influence over Key Corporation can be used to make individual employees and their families feel safe, supported, and included.
- Terri Anne tells Mike that she sees him in a completely different light.
- She tells him that she loves him so much.

## 20. Patti Anne crawls around the kitchen

- Terri Anne gently places Patti Anne on the kitchen floor.
- Eight-month-old Patti Anne begins crawling around the kitchen while the adults watch her.
- The baby's movement lightens the room after the painful memories and serious conversation.
- Mike tells Terri Anne that he loves her so much too.

## 21. Mike suggests safety gates

- Mike notices that they need to prevent Patti Anne from crawling out of the kitchen unnoticed.
- He asks Terri Anne whether she would like him to install safety gates at certain doorways, including the kitchen entrances.
- Mike offers rather than making the decision for her because he wants Terri Anne to have a say in how they childproof their home.
- He explains to Terri Anne, Charlene, and Olivia that the mansion is a home, not a museum.
- Patti Anne has every right to crawl around and explore the mansion; the gates would protect her from unsafe areas, not confine her to the kitchen.
- Mike tells them that when Ashlee was a crawling baby, their mother kept her in one room and would not allow her to crawl around the home.
- He does not want Patti Anne to experience the same restriction or grow up feeling that the mansion's rooms and possessions matter more than she does.
- Terri Anne is genuinely surprised by how kind and thoughtful Mike is to offer this for Patti Anne.
- The practical suggestion gives Terri Anne another reason to see Mike as a caring partner and father.

## 22. Ashlee's secret room

- Mike tells Terri Anne, Charlene, and Olivia that Ashlee discovered a secret room inside the front foyer closet when she was four years old.
- Whenever Ashlee came home from Hillfield Strathallan College, she hid herself inside the secret room.
- During the summer, Ashlee finally showed the hidden space to Mike.
- The room had nothing inside for Ashlee to sit on, play with, or use.

## 23. Mike creates a room for Ashlee

- Mike used his own money to buy a small table and chairs for Ashlee's secret room.
- He also bought pink paint and puzzles for her to put together.
- Mike transformed the empty hiding place into a welcoming space that belonged to his little sister.
- After Ashlee completed her puzzles, Mike displayed them around the mansion.
- His actions showed Ashlee that the things she created deserved to be seen throughout the home rather than hidden away with her.

## 24. The puzzle in Mike's office

- Charlene and Olivia tell Terri Anne that they have heard the story of Ashlee's secret room before.
- They know the story because one of Ashlee's completed puzzles hangs in Mike's office.
- The puzzle displayed there contains two thousand pieces.
- Terri Anne understands that Mike did not merely give Ashlee puzzles to occupy her; he preserved and displayed the work she completed.

## 25. Terri Anne asks about the secret room

- Terri Anne asks Mike whether Ashlee would be okay with Patti Anne using her secret room.
- Mike tells Terri Anne that Ashlee will be okay with Patti Anne using it.
- The room that once gave four-year-old Ashlee a private refuge can now become a safe place for Patti Anne as she grows.
- Terri Anne appreciates that the room remains connected to Ashlee even as it becomes part of Patti Anne's childhood.

## 26. Terri Anne promises to accept Patti Anne

- While watching Patti Anne crawl, Terri Anne tells Mike, Charlene, and Olivia that she will accept her daughter for who she is.
- Terri Anne says that if Patti Anne ever comes to her and says that she wants to be a boy, Terri Anne will let her be one.
- She will not force Patti Anne to hide or punish her for expressing her identity.
- Terri Anne wants Patti Anne to receive the acceptance and freedom that children in both Mike's and Terri Anne's families were denied.
- Mike, Charlene, and Olivia tell Terri Anne that they already know she would accept Patti Anne because Terri Anne is a different person.
- Mike emphasizes this most strongly because he has seen how differently Terri Anne treats Patti Anne from the way their own parents treated their children.
- He believes Terri Anne will allow Patti Anne to become her own person rather than forcing her to satisfy someone else's expectations.

## 27. Charlene receives a text message

- Charlene's iPhone rings as a text message comes through.
- She quickly picks it up and reads the message.
- Her expression changes when she sees the production update.
- Charlene says, **“Goddamn—sorry. An actress is sick.”**
- She apologizes especially because she swore in front of Patti Anne.

## 28. Terri Anne responds to the apology

- Terri Anne tells Charlene that she does not need to apologize because Patti Anne is still a baby and cannot speak right now.
- Patti Anne continues crawling without understanding the adults' production problem.
- Mike and Terri Anne ask Charlene what the actress is needed for and which production may be affected.
- Charlene keeps the text open as she prepares to explain the situation.

## 29. Charlene explains the scheduled scene

- Charlene tells Mike and Terri Anne that the sick actress was scheduled to film a scene with a group of ten adult men.
- The scene is supposed to be made inside the special equipment room at Key Porn Studios.
- The actress's illness leaves Charlene with an immediate casting and scheduling problem.

## 30. Terri Anne asks whether Charlene wants her for the movie

- Terri Anne asks Charlene whether she wants Terri Anne to act in the movie with the group of ten adult men.
- Charlene tells her that it would be a very difficult movie for a brand-new actress.
- She does not want Terri Anne moved into such a demanding production merely because another performer became sick.
- Mike agrees with Charlene that the movie would be too difficult for Terri Anne at this stage of her acting career.

## 31. The two productions appear to conflict

- Charlene explains that the movie involving the ten men is being made in Kyoto, Japan.
- Both the Kyoto movie and Terri Anne's film with two other adult women are scheduled for Saturday.
- At first, it appears that accepting the Kyoto movie would require Terri Anne's original Saturday film to be cancelled.
- Charlene makes sure Terri Anne understands the apparent scheduling conflict before any decision is made.

## 32. Terri Anne accepts the Kyoto movie

- Terri Anne tells Mike, Charlene, and Olivia that she thinks she can be in the Kyoto movie with the group of ten adult men.
- They ask whether she is certain that she wants to take on such a demanding production.
- Terri Anne tells them that she is certain.
- She looks toward Patti Anne and says, **“We will be visiting another country.”**
- Her words make clear that she intends for Patti Anne to accompany her on the trip to Japan.

## 33. Mike arranges the travel documents

- Mike asks Olivia whether she can take Terri Anne and Patti Anne back to their old apartment.
- Terri Anne needs to collect the documents required for international travel.
- After retrieving them, Olivia will take Terri Anne and Patti Anne to the passport office.
- Mike tells the group that they will fly to Kyoto on Thursday.
- Terri Anne and Olivia both tell Mike that the plan is okay.
- Olivia agrees to take Terri Anne and Patti Anne to the apartment and passport office.
- Travelling on Thursday gives them time to complete the production arrangements before Saturday.

## 34. Mike asks whether the first movie can continue

- Mike asks Charlene whether the two adult women can make the originally scheduled film without Terri Anne.
- Charlene tells him they cannot make that film without Terri Anne.
- She believes the two women can travel to Kyoto instead of cancelling the production.
- All three women can then make their film together in Kyoto on Saturday morning.

## 35. Terri Anne will make both movies

- Charlene proposes that Terri Anne complete the movie with the two adult women first on Saturday morning.
- Terri Anne will then receive a four-hour break.
- After the break, she will make the second movie with the ten adult men in Kyoto's special equipment room.
- The revised plan allows both Saturday productions to proceed without replacing Terri Anne in the first movie.

## 36. Mike and Terri Anne reveal their own plan

- Mike and Terri Anne start laughing out loud when Charlene mentions the special equipment room.
- They explain to Charlene and Olivia that they are creating a private special equipment room modelled after the one at Key Porn Studios.
- Their laughter comes from the unexpected coincidence between Charlene's production problem and their own plans.

## 37. Olivia asks about moving out

- While the group is discussing plans and responsibilities, Olivia asks Mike whether she, Mei, and Tsumugi can move out of the mansion.
- Olivia appreciates the protection and hospitality, but she wants to know when the three of them can return to their regular home life.
- Her question is practical rather than ungrateful.

## 38. Mike asks them to remain

- Mike tells both Charlene and Olivia that he wants them to stay nearby until Victoria's threat is over.
- He specifically wants Olivia, Mei, and Tsumugi to remain protected rather than move out while the danger is unresolved.
- Mike is not treating the mansion as a permanent restriction; his request is tied to the continuing threat from Victoria.
- Charlene understands that Mike also wants trusted people close while he prepares to stop his middle siblings.

## 39. Closing beat

- Terri Anne touches the diamond pendant while watching Patti Anne explore the kitchen floor.
- Mike's stories about Ashlee help Terri Anne understand why he wants Patti Anne to feel free and welcome throughout the mansion.
- Charlene's text introduces an urgent casting problem involving a production in Kyoto, while Olivia's question reminds everyone that Victoria's threat continues to affect their living arrangements.
- Terri Anne accepts the demanding Kyoto movie without losing her original film with the two women.
- Olivia will help Terri Anne and Patti Anne collect the documents and visit the passport office before everyone flies to Kyoto on Thursday.
- On Saturday, Terri Anne will complete the first movie in the morning, rest for four hours, and then make the second movie.
- Mike and Terri Anne's plan for their own special equipment room gives the conversation an unexpected moment of humour before attention turns to travel, safety, and production arrangements.
