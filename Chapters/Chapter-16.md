# Chapter Sixteen — Cooper Confronts Don

## 1. Cooper returns to the shared house

- Later on **Sunday**, after completing his driving responsibilities, Cooper goes to the house he shares with Adam and Don.
- His professional calm disappears as soon as he enters.
- Cooper is furious that Don followed Scout and Patti Anne in clothing that Terri Anne could easily describe.
- He knows Mike has already sent the description to Bryan.

## 2. Cooper explodes at Don

- Cooper storms into the room where Adam and Don are waiting.
- He shouts, **“Don, you fuckin' idiot!”**
- Don is startled by Cooper's anger, while Adam immediately realizes that something has gone wrong.
- Cooper tells them that Scout saw Don following her and described him to Mike.

## 3. Cooper reveals the danger

- Cooper explains that Terri Anne identified a blue jacket and blue hat.
- He tells Adam and Don that Mike heard the description and has the resources to pursue the follower.
- Mike has also assigned four Key Corporation security guards to protect Scout and Patti Anne.
- Cooper warns that Don's actions have brought Mike's attention closer to all three men and to Victoria Key's hidden plan.

## 4. Cooper orders Don to destroy the clothing

- Cooper orders Don to cut up the blue jacket and blue hat.
- He wants the recognizable clothing destroyed before Mike's security team or the police can connect it to Don.
- Don hesitates, but Cooper insists that the items have become evidence.
- Adam supports Cooper's order and blames Don for acting without thinking.

## 5. Adam and Cooper condemn Don's carelessness

- Adam and Cooper tell Don that following Terri Anne in such noticeable clothing was reckless.
- They accuse him of risking Victoria's plan through his carelessness.
- Their contempt makes clear that they see Don as useful for intimidation but unreliable with secrets.
- Don becomes angry at being treated as the weakest member of the group.

## 6. Cooper conceals his own role

- Cooper tells Adam and Don that Mike and Olivia still do not know about his family connection to Adam.
- He intends to continue working as Mike's driver so he can hear information and redirect suspicion.
- Cooper does not admit how close he came to being exposed when Mike ordered him to race to the Y.
- His access to Mike's movements makes him valuable to Adam and Victoria's plan.

## 7. Don resents Cooper's order

- Don begins cutting up the blue jacket and hat, but he does so angrily.
- He resents Cooper for shouting at him and Adam for taking Cooper's side.
- Don's resentment makes him even more unpredictable.
- Cooper realizes that Don may expose the plan intentionally if he feels betrayed.

## 8. Cooper calls Victoria

- Cooper decides that Don's mistake is too serious to conceal from **Victoria Key**.
- He calls Victoria and tells her about Don's fuck-up.
- Cooper explains that Don followed Scout and Patti Anne in a recognizable blue jacket and hat.
- He also reports that Scout described the clothing to Mike and that Mike sent the information to Bryan.
- Victoria realizes that Don may have brought Mike dangerously close to discovering her hidden plan.

## 9. Victoria orders a Friday-night meeting at The Well

- Victoria tells Cooper that she wants to meet with him and Adam at **The Well on Friday night**.
- The meeting will take place five days after Don's Sunday mistake.
- She chooses the bar as the location for a private discussion about Don's actions and the danger to her plan.
- Cooper gives Adam the Friday-night meeting instructions after ending the call.

## 10. Adam objects to the location

- Adam does not like meeting at The Well because it is a gay bar.
- He complains that men there often try to pick him up.
- His objection is based on the unwanted attention he believes he receives there, but Victoria has already chosen the location.
- Cooper tells Adam that they cannot afford to argue with her while Mike is suspicious and protecting Terri Anne.
- Adam reluctantly agrees to attend.

## 11. Don wants to accompany them

- Don asks to go to The Well with Adam and Cooper.
- Both men tell him, **“No way.”**
- They do not trust Don to attend a sensitive meeting after his careless decision to follow Scout.
- Don is angry at being excluded from a plan he already knows enough to endanger.

## 12. Don receives another instruction

- After arranging the Friday meeting, Adam and Cooper give Don another order.
- They tell him to take the cut-up pieces of his blue jacket and blue hat and scatter them throughout Hamilton.
- They hope that separating the pieces will prevent Mike's security team or the police from recovering enough clothing to connect Don to Terri Anne's description.
- Don resents being left behind to clean up the evidence while Adam and Cooper meet privately with Victoria.

## 13. Closing hook

- Adam and Cooper have five days to prepare for their Friday-night meeting with Victoria at The Well.
- Don remains responsible for scattering the cut-up pieces of his jacket and hat throughout Hamilton.
- He is humiliated, angry, and tempted to disobey their instructions.
- Don silently considers how much he knows about Victoria's plan and what that knowledge may be worth.
- The chapter ends on Sunday evening with the alliance among Adam, Cooper, and Don beginning to fracture.

## Chapter Purpose

This chapter:

- Reveals that Cooper, Adam, and Don share a house.
- Shows Cooper dropping his professional disguise when he confronts Don.
- Uses Cooper's angry outburst to demonstrate the seriousness of Don's mistake.
- Establishes that Bryan has received the follower's description.
- Has Cooper order Don to cut up the blue jacket and blue hat.
- Connects Don's actions to the danger of exposing Victoria's hidden plan.
- Shows Cooper using his position as Mike's driver to protect the conspiracy.
- Has Cooper report Don's mistake to Victoria.
- Establishes that Don's mistake happens on Sunday and Victoria's meeting with Adam and Cooper occurs the following Friday night at The Well.
- Excludes Don from the meeting and increases his resentment.
- Orders Don to scatter the cut-up jacket and hat throughout Hamilton.
- Begins breaking the alliance among Cooper, Adam, and Don.
