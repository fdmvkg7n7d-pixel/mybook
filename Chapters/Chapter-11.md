# Chapter Eleven — Adam's Ultimatum

## 1. Scout is summoned

- During her additional shift at the Blue Velvet Room, Scout is told that Adam wants to see her in his office.
- She immediately becomes anxious because Adam rarely calls her into the office without using what he knows about Patti Anne against her.
- Scout enters reluctantly and discovers that Don is already inside the manager's office.
- Adam closes the office door, isolating her with both cousins and separating her from Marco and the other dancers.
- Don's presence makes the meeting physically intimidating before Adam even states his demand.

## 2. Adam invokes Patti Anne

- Adam reminds Scout that he knows her real name is Terri Anne Williams and that she has an eight-month-old daughter.
- He emphasizes how carefully she has kept her work and her daughter secret from other people.
- Adam presents his knowledge as power over her rather than information he has any legitimate reason to possess.
- Terri Anne realizes that he has called her into the office to increase the blackmail.

## 3. Adam makes his demand

- With Don present and listening, Adam tells Scout that after the Saturday night shift, they expect her to have sex with both of them.
- The demand is stated without graphic detail.
- Neither man gives her any genuine freedom to refuse.
- Adam treats her compliance as something the cousins are entitled to rather than a choice she is free to make.
- Their demand reveals that the blackmail has escalated from financial and workplace control to sexual coercion.

## 4. The threat

- Adam says that if Scout refuses, he will tell the police about her work at the Blue Velvet Room and about Patti Anne.
- He implies that the disclosure could lead to Terri Anne being investigated, arrested, or judged unfit as a mother.
- Whether or not Adam could carry out every consequence he threatens, Terri Anne believes he has enough influence and information to endanger her and her daughter.
- Her trafficking history makes the threat especially terrifying because she has experienced powerful people using fear and sexual demands to control her before.

## 5. Terri Anne's survival response

- Terri Anne feels trapped but tries not to reveal the full extent of her fear.
- She does not agree to Adam's demand.
- Instead, she delays, saying as little as possible while trying to leave the office safely.
- Internally, she understands that Saturday has become a deadline.
- She also realizes that Adam and Don may become dangerous if they suspect she intends to seek help.

## 6. Adam believes he is in control

- Adam mistakes Terri Anne's quiet response for surrender.
- He warns her not to tell the other dancers, Marco, or anyone outside the club.
- He reminds her that Don will be present after Saturday's shift.
- Adam lets her leave believing that fear will guarantee her compliance.

## 7. Scout returns to work

- Terri Anne leaves the office and returns to the public area of the Blue Velvet Room.
- She forces herself to resume the Scout persona so Adam and Don will not realize she is considering resistance.
- Marco notices that something is wrong, but Terri Anne is too frightened to explain.
- Don watches her from across the club, reinforcing Adam's threat without needing to speak.

## 8. Terri Anne returns to Patti Anne

- Scout's night shift ends at **4:30 in the morning** after hours under Adam and Don's observation.
- Once she is safely away from the Blue Velvet Room, she returns to Patti Anne.
- The fear she concealed at work overwhelms her, and she begins to cry while holding her daughter.
- Saturday is approaching, leaving her little time to act.
- She realizes that remaining silent now places both her and Patti Anne in greater danger.

## 9. Terri Anne calls Mike at 4:30 a.m.

- Just after the night shift, at approximately **4:30 a.m.**, Terri Anne takes out Mike's business card.
- Still crying and holding Patti Anne, she calls his private telephone number and asks him for help.
- Mike hears the distress in her voice immediately.
- He listens without interrupting or pressuring her to calm down before she is ready.
- Patti Anne remains in Terri Anne's arms while she makes the most difficult disclosure of her life.


## 10. Terri Anne tells Mike about Patti Anne

- Terri Anne reveals that she has an eight-month-old daughter named **Patti Anne**.
- She admits that she is holding Patti Anne while they speak.
- She explains that fear and blackmail—not shame about her daughter—kept her from telling him earlier.
- Mike hears Patti Anne in the background but does not reveal that he recognized her squeals during their previous call.
- He assures Terri Anne that having a daughter does not change his respect for her.

## 11. Terri Anne tells Mike everything

- Terri Anne describes how Adam and Don have been blackmailing her and other dancers.
- She tells Mike that Don was waiting in Adam's office when she was summoned.
- She recounts Adam's demand that she have sex with both cousins after the Saturday night shift.
- She explains their threat to tell the police about her work and Patti Anne if she refuses.
- She also tells Mike about her history of being trafficked and why the cousins' coercion terrifies her.
- Mike allows her to finish without revealing how much Bryan's investigation has already uncovered.

## 12. Mike responds

- Mike tells Terri Anne that he believes her.
- He makes clear that Adam and Don's demand is coercion and that none of it is her fault.
- He asks whether she and Patti Anne are presently somewhere safe.
- Mike promises that she will not have to face the cousins alone.
- He tells her that someone will arrive at the Blue Velvet Room very soon to help her and the other dancers.
- Mike also tells Terri Anne that she may call his private number whenever she wants or needs to speak with him.
- He does not yet burden the frightened Terri Anne with every detail of his ownership, wealth, and investigation; his immediate priority is helping her feel safe.

## 13. Mike calls Charlene and Olivia

- As soon as his call with Terri Anne ends, Mike immediately contacts **Charlene** and **Olivia**.
- Despite the early hour, he tells them that the situation involving Adam and Don has become urgent.
- He explains that Scout has personally confirmed the blackmail and has revealed the cousins' sexual ultimatum.
- Mike directs Charlene and Olivia to put the emergency response into motion.
- Someone must reach the Blue Velvet Room quickly to protect the dancers, secure evidence, and prevent Adam and Don from retaliating.
- Mike makes clear that all of the dancers are to be treated as victims and offered help.

## 14. Closing hook

- Terri Anne holds Patti Anne close, knowing that Mike has promised help and that she may call him whenever she wishes.
- For the first time, she has revealed her daughter and described the full extent of Adam and Don's threats, although she still keeps her real name private.
- Mike's calls to Charlene and Olivia end the cautious phase of the investigation.
- The chapter closes with help being mobilized for the Blue Velvet Room before Adam and Don realize that Terri Anne has exposed them.

## Chapter Purpose

This chapter:

- Escalates Adam and Don's blackmail into sexual coercion.
- Makes clear that Adam's demand cannot produce genuine consent.
- Uses Terri Anne's trafficking history to explain the intensity of her fear without depicting past abuse.
- Establishes the end of Saturday's shift as a deadline.
- Shows Terri Anne using survival skills rather than openly confronting two dangerous men while isolated.
- Has Terri Anne call Mike at 4:30 a.m. while crying and holding Patti Anne.
- Allows Scout to reveal her daughter, her trafficking history, and the entire blackmail scheme in her own words while keeping her real name private.
- Shows Mike believing her, promising that help is coming, and inviting her to call whenever she wishes.
- Has Mike immediately alert Charlene and Olivia.
- Forces Mike to accelerate the investigation and act before Saturday night.
