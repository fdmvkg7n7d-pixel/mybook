# Chapter Ten — The Old Train Terminal

## 1. Mike arrives in Buffalo

- Mike travels to Buffalo on Key Corporation business.
- His purpose is to inspect the old train terminal and review the redevelopment plans in person.
- Key Corporation is acquiring the necessary rights to restore the neglected building and convert it into corporate offices.
- Mike wants the project to preserve the terminal's character rather than erase its history.

## 2. Mike tours the terminal

- Mike walks through the old terminal with the project's architect.
- He sees the extent of the deterioration for himself.
- The architect points out the areas requiring structural stabilization, restoration, and modern mechanical systems.
- Mike studies the damage closely and asks direct questions about what can be saved.
- The neglected condition of the building angers him, but its remaining grandeur strengthens his commitment to the project.

## 3. The architect presents the plans

- The architect spreads the plans out for Mike to inspect.
- The proposed design restores the most important historic spaces while adapting other areas into modern Key Corporation offices.
- The plans include safe access, updated utilities, workspaces, meeting rooms, and the infrastructure needed for a major corporate site.
- Mike reviews the drawings carefully rather than approving them simply because others recommend them.
- He asks whether the restoration will remain faithful to the terminal's original design.

## 4. A great project for Buffalo

- The architect tells Mike that Key Corporation is doing something great for the old train terminal.
- The project will rescue a neglected landmark and give it a productive new purpose.
- The architect expresses appreciation that Mike is willing to invest in restoration instead of demolition and replacement.
- Mike acknowledges that the finished project could become a source of pride for both Key Corporation and Buffalo.

## 5. Mike condemns the neglect

- Mike tells the architect that he already knows Key Corporation is doing something important.
- He adds that Buffalo's city officials should be fired for allowing the old train terminal to deteriorate so badly.
- His statement reflects his anger that a building with such history was permitted to fall into ruin.
- The architect is momentarily taken aback by Mike's bluntness.
- Mike makes clear that his criticism is about years of neglect, not the people now trying to help save the terminal.

## 6. Mike sets his expectations

- Mike tells the architect that Key Corporation must treat the restoration as more than a real-estate transaction.
- He expects the project to respect the building's history while creating functional offices.
- Mike asks for regular progress reports, transparent cost estimates, and immediate notice of any threat to the terminal's important features.
- He would rather accept a longer schedule than allow careless work to destroy what remains.

## 7. The architect explains the timeline

- The architect assures Mike that the design team shares his commitment to preserving the terminal.
- They review which spaces can be restored closely to their original appearance and which must be adapted.
- The architect estimates that the complete restoration and conversion will require **four to seven years**.
- The wide range accounts for the building's deterioration, structural discoveries, preservation work, permits, and the scale of the office conversion.
- Mike accepts that a responsible restoration cannot be rushed.
- The architect agrees to revise any details that do not meet Mike's preservation standards.

## 8. Mike departs for Miami

- Mike stands inside the old terminal and imagines Key Corporation employees filling the restored building years in the future.
- The contrast between its present decay and its possible future gives him a renewed sense of purpose.
- He authorizes the architect to continue refining the plans, subject to Mike's final review.
- After completing the inspection, Mike leaves Buffalo and begins his journey to **Miami**.
- The chapter ends with Mike reviewing the terminal project while on his way to his next destination.

## Chapter Purpose

This chapter:

- Brings Mike to Buffalo.
- Introduces the architect and the plans for the old train terminal.
- Establishes Key Corporation's intention to restore the terminal as corporate offices.
- Shows Mike's interest in historic preservation and adaptive reuse.
- Demonstrates Mike's direct, demanding leadership style.
- Expresses his anger at the officials who allowed the terminal to deteriorate.
- Establishes a four-to-seven-year restoration timeline.
- Sends Mike from Buffalo to Miami for the next part of his trip.
- Advances the Key Corporation side of the novel beyond the Blue Velvet Room.
