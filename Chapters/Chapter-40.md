# Chapter Forty — The Report on Victoria

## 1. Bryan arranges a private meeting

- Bryan contacts Mike and says that his investigator has completed a report on Victoria's second meeting at The Well.
- Because the report concerns Mike's sister, driver, former club manager, and control of Key Corporation, Bryan refuses to discuss the details through an ordinary telephone call.
- Bryan meets Mike at the Carlisle mansion and brings the written report, photographs, and audio evidence.
- **Charlene** and **Olivia** join Mike for the confidential meeting.
- Mike understands that the investigation has reached a serious new stage.

## 2. The investigator's report

- Bryan gives Mike the investigator's complete report about Victoria.
- The report documents the date, time, location, and duration of the meeting at The Well.
- It identifies Victoria, Adam, and Cooper as the three participants.
- Don's absence is also noted because he was deliberately excluded from the strategy meeting.

## 3. Terri Anne passes the meeting

- While Bryan, Mike, Charlene, and Olivia are reviewing the report, Terri Anne walks past the meeting area carrying Patti Anne.
- Terri Anne is wearing a towel around only the lower part of her body.
- She did not intend to enter the confidential meeting and is merely passing through the area with her daughter.
- When she realizes that everyone can see her, she says, **“I am really sorry for bothering you.”**
- Mike, Charlene, Olivia, and Bryan treat the interruption respectfully and do not make Terri Anne feel ashamed.
- Terri Anne does not panic, hide, or run away as she once did when Wolfee entered the industrial kitchen.
- Walking confidently past Bryan while outside the Blue Velvet Room and outside her Scout persona shows that she has gained self-confidence in her own body.
- Her confidence belongs to Terri Anne herself; it is not dependent on being a dancer or adult-film performer.
- Terri Anne continues on with Patti Anne so the meeting can resume.

## 4. The undercover approach

- The report explains that a second investigator approached Adam and asked him out.
- Adam believed the man was an ordinary customer at the gay bar.
- The approach allowed the investigator to confirm Adam's identity without exposing the surveillance.
- Mike realizes that Adam and Cooper still have no idea Bryan's team is watching them.

## 5. The bartender and the listening bug

- Bryan tells Mike that the investigator paid The Well's bartender for permission to place a listening bug beneath the table.
- The bartender's cooperation allowed the device to be installed before Victoria's conversation began.
- The report contains a record of the captured audio and the investigator's supporting observations.
- Mike listens to or reviews the relevant portions of the recording.

## 6. Victoria leads the conspiracy

- The audio makes clear that Victoria directs Adam and Cooper.
- She decides where they meet, who receives information, and what the group should do next.
- Adam and Cooper defer to her because the plan and its financial rewards depend on her.
- Mike now has evidence that his sister is not merely associated with the men; she is leading them.

## 7. The fifteen-million-dollar payments

- The report confirms that Victoria promised **fifteen million dollars each** to Adam, Cooper, and Don.
- The men receive nothing if her plan fails.
- Their combined promised reward totals forty-five million dollars.
- Mike understands why they are willing to remain involved despite their growing distrust of one another.

## 8. The plan targets Mike's future

- Victoria's conversation confirms that Mike and Terri Anne's relationship threatens her scheme.
- If Mike marries and has a child before turning forty, the terms of their mother's will allow him to keep Key Corporation.
- Victoria wants to prevent that outcome so control of the corporation passes away from Mike.
- Mike's suspicion that his previous relationships may have been sabotaged becomes more credible.

## 9. Terri Anne is now a target

- The report states that Victoria wants Adam and Cooper to learn more about Terri Anne.
- She is searching for emotional pressure points that could separate Terri Anne from Mike.
- Adam warns that direct intimidation previously strengthened the couple's bond.
- Victoria therefore wants the next action to appear unrelated to the conspiracy.

## 10. Don's limited role

- The audio confirms that Victoria still wants Don's physical strength but no longer trusts his judgment.
- Don will receive only the information needed for specific physical tasks.
- He remains excluded from strategy meetings.
- Mike realizes that Don may become even more dangerous if he feels disposable or betrayed.

## 11. Cooper's betrayal

- The report confirms that Cooper is willingly working with Victoria and Adam.
- His position as Mike's driver gave the conspiracy access to Mike's movements and private conversations.
- Mike thinks about Cooper helping Terri Anne, Olivia, and Patti Anne into the limousine while secretly protecting Don.
- Cooper's betrayal feels both personal and dangerous.

## 12. Mike reacts to Victoria's role

- Mike is angry and hurt that his own sister is directing a plan against his relationships and Key Corporation.
- The report forces him to reconsider Jack and Ric's visit to Ashlee and Geoffrey.
- He begins wondering whether Victoria also encouraged the brothers' harassment.
- Don's pursuit of Terri Anne and Patti Anne convinces Mike that continued observation alone is no longer enough.

## 13. Mike orders Don taken off the streets

- Mike tells Bryan that he wants Don taken off the streets.
- He wants Don separated from Adam, Cooper, and Victoria before they can use his strength for another task.
- Mike does not want Don anywhere near Terri Anne or Patti Anne.
- Bryan understands that the order represents a major escalation from surveillance to direct action.

## 14. Charlene and Olivia question Mike

- Charlene and Olivia ask Mike whether he is certain about the order.
- They understand the danger Don presents, but they also recognize the risks of acting before the entire conspiracy has been exposed.
- Bryan waits for Mike to confirm exactly what he wants.
- Mike tells Charlene, Olivia, and Bryan that he knows what he is doing.

## 15. Mike plans to speak with Don

- Mike explains that once Don has been taken, Mike intends to speak with him personally.
- He believes Don's resentment toward Adam and Cooper may make him willing to reveal Victoria's complete plan.
- Mike wants answers about the pursuit near the Y, the destroyed clothing, and the promised fifteen million dollars.
- He also wants to know what physical task Victoria still needs Don to perform.

## 16. Mike chooses the timing

- Mike tells Bryan that he wants Don taken while Mike is **in the air** flying to Los Angeles.
- Mike specifically does not want Cooper or Adam to believe that he arranged for Don to be taken.
- Being in the air gives Mike distance from the immediate operation and helps conceal where the order came from.
- By the time Mike lands, Bryan should be able to report whether Don has been secured.
- Mike plans to speak with Don after dealing with the situation involving Ashlee and Geoffrey.

## 17. Mike gives Bryan additional instructions

- Mike tells Bryan to continue monitoring Victoria, Adam, and Cooper without alerting them.
- Bryan is to preserve the audio, photographs, surveillance notes, and reconstructed clothing.
- Mike also orders a review of Cooper's access to his travel, household, and corporate information.
- Victoria must continue believing that her meetings at The Well remained private.

## 18. They tell Terri Anne what is happening

- Before the meeting ends, Mike, Charlene, and Olivia speak with Terri Anne.
- They explain what the investigator recorded at The Well and confirm that Victoria is working with Adam and Cooper.
- They tell her about the fifteen-million-dollar payments and Victoria's interest in breaking up her relationship with Mike.
- Mike also tells Terri Anne that he ordered Don taken off the streets while Mike is flying to Los Angeles.
- He explains that the timing is intended to stop Cooper and Adam from connecting the operation to him.
- Terri Anne appreciates that Mike is keeping his promise not to hide major information affecting her and Patti Anne.

## 19. Mike locks away the report

- While Mike, Charlene, and Olivia finish explaining the situation, Mike gathers the photographs, audio summary, and written report.
- He places everything inside the wall safe in his home office.
- Terri Anne watches him secure the evidence and understands how dangerous the information has become.
- Charlene and Olivia remain concerned, but Mike does not withdraw his order.

## 20. Mike asks Charlene and Olivia to stay

- Because Mike will be flying to Los Angeles, he asks Charlene and Olivia whether they can stay at the mansion with Terri Anne and Patti Anne.
- Their presence will give Terri Anne trusted company in addition to the Key Corporation security guards.
- Charlene agrees to stay.
- Olivia is willing but asks Mike and Terri Anne about bringing two additional people.

## 21. Olivia asks about Mei and Tsumugi

- Olivia asks whether her girlfriend, **Mei**, may stay at the mansion with her.
- Mei is twenty-six years old.
- Olivia also asks whether Mei's fourteen-year-old sister, **Tsumugi**, may accompany them.
- With twenty bedrooms available, space is not a problem, but Olivia still asks permission rather than assuming they are welcome.

## 22. Mike and Terri Anne welcome them

- Mike and Terri Anne discuss the request together.
- They tell Olivia that Mei and Tsumugi are both welcome to stay.
- Terri Anne appreciates knowing that the mansion will contain several trusted people while Mike is away.
- Mike makes certain that security is informed that Mei and Tsumugi are approved guests.

## 23. Closing hook

- Bryan leaves the meeting knowing that Don is to be taken while Mike's flight is in the air.
- Terri Anne now knows what will happen and why Mike chose that timing.
- Charlene, Olivia, Mei, and Tsumugi will stay with Terri Anne and Patti Anne at the mansion.
- Mike prepares for Los Angeles while Don unknowingly approaches the end of his freedom.
- The chapter ends with the evidence locked in the safe and the operation set in motion.
