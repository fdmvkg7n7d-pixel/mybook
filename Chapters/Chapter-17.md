# Chapter Seventeen — Stay on the Line

## 1. Scout cannot sleep

- Sunday night, Scout is inside her apartment with Patti Anne.
- Four Key Corporation security guards are protecting the apartment, but the day's frightening events have left Scout unable to relax.
- She keeps thinking about Don following them on the way to the Y.
- Every sound outside the apartment makes her wonder whether the danger has returned.

## 2. Patti Anne sleeps peacefully

- Patti Anne is sleeping peacefully nearby.
- Her calm breathing reassures Scout, but it also intensifies Scout's determination to keep her safe.
- Scout does not want to wake or frighten her daughter.
- She tries to remain quiet while her own fear prevents her from sleeping.

## 3. Scout calls Mike

- Scout takes out her iPhone and calls Mike's private number.
- She worries that she may be disturbing him, but she remembers that he told her she could call whenever she wanted.
- Mike answers and is genuinely happy to hear her voice.
- He immediately recognizes that she sounds nervous.

## 4. Scout explains her fear

- Scout tells Mike that Patti Anne is sleeping peacefully but that she is too nervous to sleep.
- She admits that the security guards outside have not stopped her from replaying the moment she realized someone was following them.
- Mike listens without dismissing her fear or telling her that she should already feel safe.
- He reassures her that the guards will remain alert throughout the night.

## 5. Scout makes a quiet request

- Scout asks Mike whether he would stay on the telephone until she falls asleep.
- The request makes her feel vulnerable because it reveals how much comfort she finds in his presence.
- Mike agrees without hesitation.
- He tells her that she does not need to keep talking and that he will remain on the line for as long as she needs him.

## 6. Mike helps Scout settle

- Mike speaks calmly and keeps the conversation gentle.
- They discuss ordinary things rather than Don, Adam, or the investigation.
- His voice gradually helps Scout's breathing slow and her fear ease.
- Scout remains close to Patti Anne while listening to Mike.

## 7. Scout falls asleep

- Scout's responses become quieter and less frequent.
- Eventually, she stops answering because she has fallen asleep.
- Mike remains on the telephone for a while to make certain she is resting peacefully.
- He hears only the quiet sounds of Scout's apartment and understands how deeply she trusted him by making the call.

## 8. Mike recognizes his feelings

- As Mike listens to the silence, he admits privately that his concern for Scout has become something more.
- He is falling in love with **Terri Anne**, the young woman behind the stage name.
- The realization makes him happy, but it also reminds him of the power imbalance created by his age, wealth, and ownership of her workplace.
- Mike decides that protecting her cannot entitle him to her affection or trust.
- He must allow Terri Anne to choose the future of their relationship freely.

## 9. Mike remembers his mother's will

- Mike's happiness is interrupted by the thought of his **goddamn mother's will**.
- He is now thirty-eight and a half years old.
- The will requires him to marry and have a child by his fortieth birthday.
- If he fails to satisfy both conditions, control of Key Corporation will pass to his middle siblings.
- Mike therefore has only eighteen months remaining before the deadline.

## 10. The deadline complicates his love

- Mike realizes that the will could make any relationship appear calculated.
- If Terri Anne eventually learns about the deadline, she may wonder whether he loves her or merely sees her as a way to keep Key Corporation.
- Mike refuses to exploit Terri Anne's vulnerability, rush her into marriage, or treat her as a solution to an inheritance problem.
- Patti Anne's presence does not give Mike permission to pretend that the will's child requirement has already been satisfied.
- He wants any future relationship with Terri Anne to be based on freely chosen love rather than corporate succession.

## 11. Closing beat

- Mike softly wishes Scout and Patti Anne goodnight, even though Scout is already asleep.
- He keeps the call connected a little longer before finally ending it.
- Scout sleeps peacefully with Patti Anne nearby and four guards protecting the apartment.
- The chapter ends with Mike holding his iPhone, accepting that he is falling in love with Terri Anne, and resenting the eighteen-month deadline hanging over his future.

## Chapter Purpose

This chapter:

- Shows Scout's fear after being followed by Don.
- Contrasts her nervousness with Patti Anne's peaceful sleep.
- Demonstrates that Scout trusts Mike enough to call him for comfort.
- Has Mike stay on the telephone until Scout falls asleep.
- Deepens the emotional connection between them.
- Reveals that Mike is falling in love with Terri Anne.
- Introduces his mother's will and its eighteen-month deadline.
- Establishes that Mike must marry and have a child by age forty or lose Key Corporation to his middle siblings.
- Acknowledges that Mike must not use his power, protection, ownership, or inheritance crisis to pressure Terri Anne into a relationship.
