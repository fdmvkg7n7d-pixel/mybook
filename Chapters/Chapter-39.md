# Chapter Thirty-Nine — Another Meeting at The Well

## 1. Adam and Cooper return to The Well

- Adam and Cooper arrive at The Well for the meeting Victoria ordered.
- Don is excluded because Victoria no longer trusts him in strategy discussions.
- Adam remains unhappy about returning to the gay bar.
- Cooper reminds him that their promised fifteen-million-dollar payments depend on following Victoria's instructions.

## 2. Adam complains about the bar

- Adam again complains that men at The Well try to pick him up.
- He tells Cooper that Victoria could have chosen a different private location.
- Cooper has little sympathy and tells him to concentrate on the threat created by Mike and Terri Anne's relationship.
- Adam watches the room impatiently while they wait for Victoria.

## 3. Another man approaches Adam

- Before Victoria arrives, another man approaches Adam and tries to pick him up.
- Adam rejects the invitation and becomes even more irritated.
- The man accepts the rejection without creating a scene and moves away.
- Adam complains to Cooper that his prediction about the bar has come true again.

## 4. The man is another investigator

- The man who approached Adam is actually **another member of Bryan's investigative team**.
- He is not the same investigator who approached Adam during the previous meeting.
- The invitation gives him a natural reason to get close enough to confirm Adam's identity.
- The investigator quietly approaches The Well's bartender and pays him for permission to place a listening bug beneath the table.
- With the bartender's cooperation, the investigator discreetly puts the bug underneath the table Adam and Cooper are using.
- Adam and Cooper have no idea that Mike has a private investigator, that The Well is under surveillance, or that their table has been bugged.

## 5. Victoria arrives

- Victoria enters The Well and joins Adam and Cooper.
- She takes control of the meeting immediately.
- The three choose a place where they believe they can speak privately.
- Bryan's investigator remains nearby without making his interest obvious.

## 6. Cooper reports on the relationship

- Cooper tells Victoria that Mike and Terri Anne's relationship has become serious.
- Mike is emotionally attached to Terri Anne and protective of Patti Anne.
- Cooper warns that the relationship could lead to marriage and help Mike satisfy the conditions in their mother's will.
- If that happens, Victoria's plan to gain control of Key Corporation will fail.

## 7. Victoria focuses on the money

- Victoria reminds Adam and Cooper that she promised Adam, Cooper, and Don **fifteen million dollars each** if the plan succeeds.
- She warns that nobody will receive the money if Mike keeps Key Corporation.
- The promised payments give Adam and Cooper a personal reason to disrupt the relationship.
- Victoria is furious that Don's mistakes helped bring Mike and Terri Anne closer together.

## 8. Don remains excluded

- Adam asks whether Don should be told about the meeting.
- Victoria says no.
- She still needs Don's strength but does not trust his judgment or ability to keep a secret.
- Cooper agrees that Don should receive only the instructions necessary for a specific task.

## 9. Victoria considers the next move

- Victoria tells Adam and Cooper that they must learn more about Terri Anne without exposing themselves.
- She wants to identify emotional pressure points that could separate Terri Anne from Mike.
- Adam warns that direct intimidation previously strengthened the couple's trust.
- Victoria insists that their next action must appear unrelated to the conspiracy.

## 10. The investigator records the meeting

- The listening bug beneath the table captures Victoria, Adam, and Cooper's conversation despite the noise of the bar.
- The investigator also observes them meeting together and obtains new photographs.
- He records the time, location, and length of the meeting.
- The audio and photographs strengthen the evidence that the three are coordinating.

## 11. The conspirators remain unaware

- Adam believes the second man was simply another customer trying to pick him up.
- Cooper does not notice the surveillance.
- Victoria believes The Well remains a safe meeting location because the crowd provides anonymity.
- None of them knows that Bryan now has evidence from two separate meetings.

## 12. Closing hook

- Victoria ends the meeting without revealing every detail of her next move.
- Adam and Cooper leave believing their conversation remained secret.
- Bryan's investigator prepares to report the second meeting.
- The chapter ends with Victoria's conspiracy under closer observation than any of its members realizes.

## Chapter Purpose

This chapter:

- Brings Victoria, Adam, and Cooper back to The Well.
- Excludes Don from another strategy meeting.
- Has another man try to pick Adam up.
- Reveals that the man is a second undercover investigator working for Bryan.
- Keeps the conspirators unaware of Bryan's investigation.
- Repeats the fifteen-million-dollar incentive promised to each man.
- Establishes Mike and Terri Anne's relationship as the primary threat to Victoria's plan.
- Has the investigator pay The Well's bartender for permission to plant a listening bug underneath their table.
- Gives Bryan audio and photographic evidence of the second secret meeting.
