# Chapter Forty-One — Mei and Tsumugi Arrive

## 1. Guests arrive at the mansion

- Before Mike leaves for Los Angeles, Olivia brings her girlfriend, Mei, and Mei's fourteen-year-old half sister, Tsumugi, to the Carlisle mansion.
- Charlene is also preparing to stay with Terri Anne and Patti Anne while Mike is away.
- Mei and Tsumugi already know Charlene from previous visits and interactions.
- Security has already been told that Mei and Tsumugi are approved guests.
- Mei and Tsumugi are already familiar with the mansion because Mike invited them there on previous occasions.
- They know the house well enough that arriving does not feel like entering an unknown or overwhelming place.

## 2. Olivia introduces Mei

- Olivia introduces Mei to Terri Anne.
- Mei is twenty-six years old and is Olivia's girlfriend.
- She is a tattoo artist whose body is covered with many different and unique tattoos.
- Each tattoo reflects part of Mei's artistic style, experiences, or personal interests.
- Terri Anne is fascinated by the variety and detail of the artwork.
- Terri Anne welcomes Mei and thanks her for being willing to stay while Mike travels.
- Mei makes clear that she wants to help without intruding on Terri Anne's role as Patti Anne's mother.

## 3. Olivia introduces Tsumugi

- Olivia introduces fourteen-year-old Tsumugi.
- Tsumugi is Mei's paternal half sister; the sisters share the same father.
- Terri Anne speaks to Tsumugi directly rather than treating her as an extension of Mei.
- Tsumugi studies Terri Anne and Patti Anne before responding, while remaining comfortable in the mansion she already knows.

## 4. Tsumugi communicates through metaphors

- Tsumugi has Asperger syndrome and often uses metaphors to express what she sees and feels.
- The metaphors are a personal communication style rather than something every autistic person shares.
- Because she already knows the mansion, she uses familiar parts of the house as metaphors when describing her feelings about meeting Terri Anne and Patti Anne.
- Terri Anne listens for the meaning behind her words instead of demanding that she communicate differently.

## 5. Tsumugi meets Patti Anne

- Terri Anne introduces eight-month-old Patti Anne.
- Tsumugi watches Patti Anne carefully and uses another metaphor to describe the baby's curious attention.
- She might say that Patti Anne's eyes are two little windows trying to open onto the whole room.
- Terri Anne understands that the metaphor is Tsumugi's way of expressing interest and warmth.

## 6. Patti Anne crawls toward the newcomers

- Terri Anne puts Patti Anne down safely on the floor because the eight-month-old has started crawling.
- Patti Anne studies Mei and Tsumugi while moving across the floor at her own pace.
- She is immediately fascinated by the many different colours in the tattoos covering Mei's body.
- Patti Anne crawls closer so she can look at Mei's colourful artwork.
- Mei approaches gently and waits for Terri Anne's permission before getting closer.
- Tsumugi does not force interaction and allows Patti Anne to become familiar with her presence.
- Patti Anne's new ability to crawl lets her choose how closely she wants to approach them.

## 7. Terri Anne learns that the sisters are related through their father

- During the conversation, Mei explains that she and Tsumugi have the same father but different mothers.
- Tsumugi is therefore Mei's half sister rather than her full sister.
- Their age difference has shaped Mei's protective role in Tsumugi's life.
- Terri Anne recognizes the strength of their bond.

## 8. Tsumugi's parents died in a vehicle accident

- Mei explains that both of Tsumugi's parents were killed in a vehicle accident.
- The accident took away Tsumugi's father—who was also Mei's father—and Tsumugi's mother.
- Tsumugi's use of metaphor sometimes helps her discuss grief that is difficult to express directly.
- Terri Anne offers sympathy without forcing Tsumugi to describe the accident.

## 9. Mei protects Tsumugi

- Mei has become the central family figure in Tsumugi's life since the accident.
- She pays attention to Tsumugi's need for predictability, quiet, and clear information.
- Mei explains that unfamiliar places are easier when Tsumugi knows what will happen next.
- Terri Anne understands the instinct to protect someone who means everything to you.

## 10. They discuss the household arrangements

- Mei and Tsumugi already know the mansion's kitchens, dining rooms, swimming pools, gym, and guest bedrooms from their previous visits.
- Terri Anne explains only the new arrangements involving herself, Patti Anne, Charlene, Olivia, and the security detail.
- Tsumugi's familiarity with the building helps her understand the temporary household routine.
- Everyone agrees on a simple schedule so the new group of people, rather than the mansion itself, feels predictable.

## 11. A connection between Terri Anne and Mei

- Terri Anne and Mei recognize a shared protectiveness toward their younger family members.
- Terri Anne's life centres on Patti Anne, while Mei's responsibilities centre heavily on Tsumugi.
- Neither woman treats the comparison as making an infant and a teenager the same.
- The shared understanding begins building trust between them.

## 12. Mike makes certain Cooper sees the household

- Cooper arrives at the mansion in connection with Mike's upcoming flight to Los Angeles.
- Mike deliberately allows Cooper to see that Terri Anne and Patti Anne are not staying alone.
- Charlene, Olivia, Mei, and Tsumugi are visibly settled inside the mansion with them.
- Mike wants Cooper to understand that Terri Anne and Patti Anne have trusted friends and protection around them.
- At the same time, Mike does not reveal that he knows Cooper is working with Victoria and Adam.

## 13. Charlene asks Mike to visit the Los Angeles studio

- Before Mike leaves, Charlene asks whether he can visit **Key Porn Studios Los Angeles** during his trip.
- Charlene knows that the Los Angeles studio is experiencing three different problems.
- Even as vice president of Key Porn Studios Global Operations, she has not yet been able to determine what the three problems really are.
- She asks Mike to visit in person, observe the studio, and see whether he can identify what is going wrong.
- Mike tells Charlene that he will try to visit while he is in Los Angeles.
- He does not promise because helping Ashlee and Geoffrey remains the primary reason for his trip.

## 14. Mike says goodbye

- Before leaving, Mike kisses Terri Anne goodbye.
- He also gives Patti Anne a gentle goodbye kiss.
- Terri Anne reassures him that she and Patti Anne will be surrounded by people they trust.
- Mike reminds Terri Anne that she can contact him at any time during the trip.

## 15. Closing beat

- Mei and Tsumugi settle into the mansion with Olivia and Charlene.
- Tsumugi's metaphors help Terri Anne understand how she experiences meeting new people in a familiar home.
- Patti Anne remains fascinated by the colours of Mei's tattoos.
- Cooper leaves knowing that Terri Anne and Patti Anne have friends living at the mansion.
- The chapter ends with Mike departing for Los Angeles after kissing Terri Anne and Patti Anne goodbye.

- Introduces Mei and Tsumugi to Terri Anne and Patti Anne.
- Establishes that Mei is a tattoo artist covered in distinctive tattoos.
- Shows Patti Anne beginning to crawl and becoming fascinated by the colours of Mei's tattoos.
- Establishes that Mei and Tsumugi already know Charlene.
- Establishes that Mei and Tsumugi are paternal half sisters with the same father.
- Establishes that Tsumugi has Asperger syndrome and communicates through metaphors.
- Reveals that Tsumugi's parents died in a vehicle accident.
- Shows Mei's protective role in Tsumugi's life.
- Establishes that Mike invited Mei and Tsumugi to the mansion before and that they know it well.
- Builds trust among the mansion's temporary residents.
- Creates a connection between Terri Anne's protectiveness of Patti Anne and Mei's protectiveness of Tsumugi.
- Shows Mike making certain Cooper sees that Terri Anne and Patti Anne have trusted friends staying with them.
- Introduces three unidentified problems at Key Porn Studios Los Angeles.
- Has Charlene ask Mike to investigate the studio if his trip permits.
- Ends with Mike kissing Terri Anne and Patti Anne goodbye before leaving for Los Angeles.
