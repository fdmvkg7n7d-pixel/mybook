# Chapter Nine — Patti Anne's Bath

## 1. A quiet morning at home

- After her telephone conversation with Mike, Terri Anne turns her full attention back to Patti Anne.
- She prepares a warm bath and joins her eight-month-old daughter in the bathtub.
- Terri Anne places her iPhone within reach on the side of the bathtub.
- The domestic scene contrasts sharply with Terri Anne's guarded life as Scout at the Blue Velvet Room.
- At home with Patti Anne, she can briefly stop performing and simply be a mother.

## 2. Patti Anne enjoys her bath

- Terri Anne gently bathes Patti Anne while talking and smiling to her.
- Patti Anne splashes in the water and responds with the same happy squeals Mike heard through the telephone.
- Terri Anne remains attentive and keeps Patti Anne safely supported throughout the shared bath.
- The scene demonstrates the loving care Mike sensed but could not see during their call.

## 3. Adam interrupts

- In the middle of their bath, the iPhone resting on the side of the bathtub rings.
- Terri Anne checks the screen and sees that **Adam**, the Blue Velvet Room's manager, is calling.
- Terri Anne does not want to answer, but she knows that ignoring Adam could make him suspicious or angry.
- She keeps Patti Anne secure and answers as Scout, separating her work identity from her life as a mother.

## 4. Adam offers another shift

- Adam asks Scout whether she is free to work another night shift that evening.
- His request sounds optional, but Terri Anne knows that refusing him can carry consequences because he is blackmailing her.
- Adam does not acknowledge Patti Anne or indicate that he knows he has interrupted the baby's bath.
- Terri Anne tries to keep her voice steady so he cannot hear her fear or Patti Anne in the background.

## 5. Terri Anne thinks about Christmas

- Terri Anne hesitates before answering.
- She still has the money Mike paid for her time during his most recent visit.
- Although the money helps, she realizes it will not be enough by itself to create the first Christmas she believes Patti Anne deserves.
- Ordinary living expenses will consume much of it, and Terri Anne wants more for her daughter's first Christmas than whatever remains.
- She imagines a small tree, decorations, presents, warm clothes, and a joyful day without fear.
- The additional shift would bring her closer to providing those things.

## 6. Scout accepts

- Terri Anne tells Adam that she will work the additional night shift.
- Her decision is shaped by both financial need and the pressure Adam holds over her.
- Adam sounds pleased and tells her when he expects her to arrive.
- Terri Anne understands that he assumes he can summon Scout whenever he wants.

## 7. Mother and daughter

- After the call ends, Terri Anne returns her full attention to Patti Anne.
- She finishes the bath, wraps her daughter in a warm towel, and holds her close.
- Terri Anne promises Patti Anne that her first Christmas will be special.
- The promise strengthens her determination but also reveals how easily Adam can exploit her devotion to her daughter.

## 8. Closing beat

- Terri Anne begins arranging childcare for another unexpected night shift.
- She prepares herself to become Scout again, hiding every sign of her life as Patti Anne's mother.
- Mike's business card and private telephone number remain nearby, representing help she is not yet ready to request.
- The chapter ends with Terri Anne looking forward to Patti Anne's first Christmas while dreading another night under Adam's control.

## Chapter Purpose

This chapter:

- Shows Terri Anne sharing Patti Anne's bath and caring lovingly for her.
- Establishes that Mike's money is helpful but not enough to create the first Christmas Terri Anne wants for Patti Anne.
- Contrasts Terri Anne's private life with her Scout identity.
- Brings Adam's control into Terri Anne's home through his telephone call.
- Establishes that Scout accepts an additional night shift.
- Explains that she needs the extra income to make Patti Anne's first Christmas special.
- Shows how Terri Anne's love for her daughter can be used against her.
- Keeps Mike's private number available as a possible path to help.
