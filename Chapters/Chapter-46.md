# Chapter Forty-Six — Kassandra Walks In

## 1. The presentation ends

- Mike, Ashlee, and Scott remain in the conference room inside the Key Porn Studios Los Angeles executive suites.
- Kassandra's photograph and the summary of the three studio problems remain visible on the presentation screen.
- Ashlee is still processing what she has learned about her friend's departure from the group chat and severe depression.

## 2. Ashlee returns to the first problem

- Ashlee asks Scott why Key Porn Studios could not find a **real disabled woman** for the lead role.
- Her question carries personal weight because Ashlee is a disabled woman.
- Scott does not know that Ashlee is transgender.
- She wants to understand whether the studio searched seriously and respectfully rather than treating disabled women as an idea for a script.

## 3. Scott explains the difficulty

- Scott tells Mike and Ashlee that it is extremely difficult to approach disability groups and ask whether a disabled woman might be interested in an adult-film role.
- Disability organizations may worry about exploitation, privacy, stigma, and their members being targeted because of their disabilities.
- Scott says the studio has not found an appropriate way to make the request without making anyone feel pressured or reduced to her disability.
- He insists that the studio wants an interested adult woman who can choose the role freely and understand the production.

## 4. Ashlee recognizes that she fits the description

- Ashlee points out that she is a disabled woman sitting in the conference room listening to the casting problem.
- Scott acknowledges that Ashlee fits the basic description, but he does not assume she wants the role.
- Ashlee does not tell Scott that she is transgender.
- Mike knows that his sister is transgender but respects her privacy and says nothing about it.
- Mike watches Ashlee carefully and realizes that her interest may no longer be theoretical.

## 5. Mike asks Ashlee directly

- Mike asks Ashlee whether she is thinking about taking the role herself.
- He tells her that she must be truthful with him about what she is considering.
- Mike makes clear that he wants her honest answer, not the answer she thinks her brother or the studio wants to hear.
- Ashlee pauses before responding.

## 6. The conference-room door opens

- Before Ashlee answers Mike, the conference-room door opens.
- Scott looks toward the doorway and the discussion stops.
- A woman enters while Kassandra's photograph remains displayed on the presentation screen.

## 7. Kassandra walks into the room

- Kassandra walks into the conference room and sees Mike and Ashlee sitting with Scott.
- She had not expected to find either of them at Key Porn Studios Los Angeles.
- Ashlee immediately recognizes the friend who disappeared from the Four Musketeers' group chat eighteen months ago.
- Kassandra sees her own photograph and personal history displayed behind them.

## 8. Kassandra is embarrassed about her new career

- Kassandra is a little embarrassed that Ashlee has discovered her new career at Key Porn Studios.
- Her embarrassment is intensified by finding her photograph and personal history displayed on the presentation screen.
- She does not know whether Ashlee will judge her for becoming an adult-film performer.
- Ashlee recognizes Kassandra's discomfort and does not shame her or demand an immediate explanation.

## 9. The director sent Kassandra to Scott

- Scott asks Kassandra why she has come to the executive conference room.
- Kassandra explains that the director of her current movie sent her to see Scott.
- She was late for the filming of her movie, and the director wants Scott to address the delay.
- Kassandra's late arrival adds a practical production concern to the wellbeing problem Scott has just described.
- Mike and Ashlee now understand that Kassandra is currently working on a movie at the Los Angeles studio.

## 10. Scott sees more than a scheduling problem

- Scott does not immediately reprimand Kassandra in front of Mike and Ashlee.
- He knows that her recent lateness may be connected to the deep depression that followed her father's appearance at the mountain scene.
- Scott asks whether Kassandra is able to continue the day's filming rather than treating the schedule as more important than her wellbeing.
- Kassandra remains embarrassed and avoids looking directly at Ashlee.

## 11. Ashlee and Kassandra face each other

- For the first time since Kassandra left the group chat, Ashlee and Kassandra are in the same room.
- Ashlee feels relief at seeing her friend alive, concern about her depression, and hurt over the long silence.
- Kassandra realizes that Ashlee now knows about the amateur night, her father's rejection, the mountain scene, her depression, and her new career.
- The unanswered question about whether Ashlee wants the disabled-woman role remains suspended between Mike and his sister.

## 12. Mike offers them privacy

- Mike recognizes that Ashlee and Kassandra may speak more honestly without him and Scott in the room.
- He asks both women whether they want some time alone together.
- Ashlee and Kassandra tell Mike that they do.
- Mike respects their answer without asking what they intend to discuss.

## 13. Mike and Scott leave the conference room

- Mike and Scott leave the executive conference room together.
- They close the door so Ashlee and Kassandra can speak privately.
- The two women are left facing each other for their first private conversation since Kassandra abandoned the Four Musketeers' group chat.
- Scott waits with Mike outside rather than returning to reprimand Kassandra for being late.

## 14. Mike receives a text about Don

- While Mike is outside the conference room with Scott, his phone receives a text message.
- The message is from his investigator and concerns Don.
- Seeing Don's name reminds Mike of his order to have Don taken off the streets while Mike was in the air.
- The contents of the investigator's update are not yet revealed.

## 15. Closing hook

- Ashlee and Kassandra remain alone inside the conference room.
- Mike stands outside with Scott while looking at the investigator's message about Don.
- The chapter leaves two private conversations ready to unfold: one between the estranged friends and one concerning Mike's operation against Don.
- The chapter ends before Kassandra explains why she left the group chat or Mike reveals what the message says.

## Chapter Purpose

This chapter:

- Moves Ashlee's casting question, Scott's explanation, and Mike's direct question out of Chapter Forty-Five.
- Has Ashlee ask why the studio could not find a real disabled woman for the role.
- Explains why Scott found it difficult to approach disability groups about adult-film casting.
- Establishes that Scott knows Ashlee is a disabled woman but does not know she is transgender.
- Shows Mike respecting Ashlee's privacy by not disclosing her transgender identity.
- Has Mike ask whether Ashlee is considering the role and insist that she answer truthfully.
- Interrupts Ashlee's answer when Kassandra walks into the conference room.
- Reveals that Kassandra is somewhat embarrassed about Ashlee discovering her new career.
- Explains that Kassandra's director sent her to Scott because she was late for filming her movie.
- Reunites Ashlee and Kassandra after eighteen months of silence.
- Has Mike ask whether the women want time alone and honours their answer.
- Moves Mike and Scott outside the conference room.
- Gives Mike a text from his investigator concerning Don.
- Leaves Ashlee's answer, Kassandra's explanation, and the message's contents unresolved.
