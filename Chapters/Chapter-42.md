# Chapter Forty-Two — Victoria Invades Their Home

## 1. Mike is in the air

- At approximately seven o'clock in the morning Eastern Time, Mike is flying to Los Angeles aboard one of Key Corporation's airplanes.
- It is approximately four o'clock in the morning in Los Angeles when Mike is in the air.
- He believes Ashlee and Geoffrey are safely waiting for him at their mansion.
- With Mike in the air aboard the corporate aircraft, Ashlee cannot immediately reach him for help.
- Victoria chooses this vulnerable period to make her move.

## 2. Victoria breaks into the mansion

- Victoria secretly gains entry to Ashlee and Geoffrey's Los Angeles mansion.
- The break-in violates the one place where Ashlee and Geoffrey should feel safe together.
- Victoria moves through the house and reaches their bedroom without waking them.
- Her ability to get that far makes the intrusion especially frightening.

## 3. Victoria sits on their bed

- Victoria enters the bedroom while Ashlee and Geoffrey are asleep.
- Instead of waking them from a distance, she sits down on their bed.
- The movement and the presence of another person finally wake the couple.
- They open their eyes and discover Victoria sitting beside them as though she belongs there.

## 4. Terror in the bedroom

- Ashlee and Geoffrey are terrified by the realization that Victoria broke into their home and reached them while they slept.
- Geoffrey immediately positions himself to protect Ashlee.
- Ashlee understands that transferring into her wheelchair or moving quickly will take time.
- Victoria uses that physical vulnerability to make her presence more intimidating.

## 5. Victoria mocks Ashlee's sleeve tattoo

- Victoria turns her attention to the sleeve tattoo covering Ashlee's right arm.
- She mocks the design, its placement, and Ashlee's decision to cover her arm with artwork.
- Her comments are intended to make Ashlee feel ridiculous and ashamed of her body.
- Ashlee refuses to apologize for artwork that she chose for herself.
- Geoffrey tells Victoria that Ashlee's tattoo is beautiful and none of Victoria's business.

## 6. Victoria notices their supportive equipment

- Victoria looks around the bedroom and notices the equipment Ashlee and Geoffrey use to support Ashlee's body when they are physically close.
- The room includes a supportive swing, a specialized chair with straps that stabilize Ashlee's arms and legs, and a padded horse-style apparatus.
- The equipment allows Ashlee to be held safely and comfortably in positions her T11 injury otherwise makes difficult.
- Every item reflects the couple's willingness to adapt their shared life around Ashlee's needs.

## 7. Victoria mocks how they sleep together

- Victoria cruelly mocks Ashlee and Geoffrey for using the swing, supportive chair, and horse-style apparatus.
- She treats their adaptations as something shameful rather than practical equipment that gives Ashlee comfort, support, and choice.
- She makes degrading remarks about how the couple sleeps together and questions why Geoffrey would want their relationship.
- Geoffrey refuses to let Victoria define their intimacy or speak for him.
- Ashlee tells Victoria that the equipment helps her live the life she chooses with the man she loves.

## 8. Ashlee refuses to be shamed

- Victoria expects Ashlee to become embarrassed by the tattoo and the equipment.
- Ashlee is frightened by the break-in but will not accept Victoria's contempt.
- She tells Victoria that her disability does not prevent her from having love, privacy, or an intimate relationship.
- Geoffrey remains beside her and reinforces that they chose their life together.

## 9. Victoria confesses that Mike was the target

- Victoria tells Ashlee and Geoffrey that she hired the assassin responsible for the shooting that destroyed Ashlee's T11 vertebra.
- She cruelly says that the assassin **badly missed the real target**.
- Victoria reveals that **Mike was the intended target**.
- Ashlee realizes that she was struck by a bullet meant to kill her brother.
- The confession transforms the intrusion from family harassment into evidence that Victoria arranged Mike's attempted assassination.

## 10. Victoria reveals another assassin

- Victoria tells Ashlee and Geoffrey that she has hired another professional assassin.
- She does not explain the new assassin's assignment or identify the intended target.
- Ashlee immediately fears for Mike because he is presently flying to Los Angeles.
- Geoffrey recognizes the statement as both a confession and a threat of further violence.
- Because Victoria soon destroys both iPhones, Ashlee and Geoffrey cannot use them to record or immediately report what she admitted.

## 11. Victoria continues the intimidation

- Victoria reminds them that Jack and Ric have already warned them about their relationship.
- Her arrival reveals that the brothers' visit was not an isolated act of cruelty.
- She attempts to convince Ashlee that Geoffrey will eventually leave and that Mike cannot protect her from everything.
- Victoria avoids revealing the complete conspiracy or the promises she made to Adam, Cooper, and Don.

## 12. Ashlee recognizes the pattern

- Ashlee realizes that Victoria is continuing the campaign Jack and Ric began.
- The timing—while Mike is flying to Los Angeles—cannot be accidental.
- Geoffrey concludes that Victoria knew enough about Mike's travel to choose the moment carefully.
- Neither of them yet knows whether Cooper gave Victoria access to Mike's itinerary.

## 13. Victoria destroys their iPhones and Ashlee's wheelchair

- Geoffrey orders Victoria to leave their bedroom and their home.
- Before leaving, Victoria grabs Ashlee and Geoffrey's iPhones and deliberately breaks both devices.
- She then damages Ashlee's wheelchair so severely that Ashlee cannot use it.
- Destroying the phones prevents the couple from immediately calling Mike or summoning help through their usual devices.
- Destroying the wheelchair is a calculated attack on Ashlee's independence and leaves her stranded in the bedroom.
- Geoffrey remains with Ashlee rather than chasing Victoria, placing Ashlee's immediate safety first.

## 14. Victoria attacks Ashlee's grand piano

- Victoria runs out of the bedroom and enters the mansion's living room.
- She passes Ashlee's grand piano and targets its specially adapted pedal system.
- Ashlee must use those pedals to control the piano's expression and produce the beautiful music for which she has practised.
- Victoria breaks the pedals before running out of the mansion.
- The damage attacks another part of Ashlee's independence and identity: her ability to make music.

## 15. The couple stands together

- Ashlee tells Victoria that terrorizing them will not end their relationship.
- Their refusal to turn against each other frustrates Victoria.
- Even while afraid and now cut off from their phones, they remain united.
- Geoffrey reassures Ashlee that the wheelchair and piano can be repaired, but Victoria cannot decide who they are.

## 16. After the intrusion

- Once Victoria has run out of the mansion, Geoffrey checks Ashlee and helps her into a safe, supported position.
- Because Ashlee's wheelchair is broken, he must find another safe way to support her until assistance arrives.
- They examine the immediate area and begin determining how Victoria entered the mansion.
- With both iPhones destroyed, they must find another way to contact security and tell Mike when his airplane lands.
- Geoffrey insists that they must preserve evidence of the break-in and damage rather than simply clean up.

## 17. Closing hook

- Mike continues flying toward Los Angeles aboard the Key Corporation airplane without knowing that Victoria has already reached Ashlee and Geoffrey.
- Ashlee refuses to feel ashamed of her tattoo, her relationship, or the supportive equipment Victoria mocked.
- The broken wheelchair and ruined piano pedals reveal that Victoria intended to attack Ashlee's freedom as well as frighten her.
- With both iPhones destroyed, Ashlee and Geoffrey remain unable to warn Mike or summon help through their usual devices.
- The chapter ends with Ashlee crying in the bedroom while Geoffrey stays beside her.

- Shows Victoria sitting on their bed and waking them.

- Has Victoria mock Ashlee's tattoo and relationship with Geoffrey.
- Introduces the swing, supportive strapped chair, and padded horse-style apparatus the couple uses to support Ashlee's body.
- Shows Victoria using Ashlee's disability and private adaptations as tools of intimidation.
- Reveals that Victoria hired the assassin whose misplaced bullet destroyed Ashlee's T11 vertebra.
- Confirms that Mike was the assassin's intended target.
- Reveals that Victoria has hired another professional assassin whose assignment remains unknown.
- Has Victoria destroy Ashlee and Geoffrey's iPhones and Ashlee's wheelchair.
- Establishes Ashlee's grand piano and the adapted pedals she needs to make expressive music.
- Has Victoria break the piano pedals while running out through the living room.
- Shows Ashlee and Geoffrey refusing to feel ashamed or turn against each other.
- Leaves the couple isolated after Victoria destroys their iPhones.
- Connects Victoria's intrusion to Jack and Ric's earlier harassment.
- Keeps Mike unaware of the attack while the Key Corporation airplane remains in the air.
