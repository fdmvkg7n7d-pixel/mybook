# Chapter Fifty-One — Tsumugi's Questions About Patti Anne

## 1. Tsumugi comes home from school

- While Mike remains away, Tsumugi returns from school to the Carlisle mansion in the evening.
- Charlene, Olivia, Mei, and Tsumugi continue staying with Terri Anne and Patti Anne as Mike requested.
- Patti Anne is awake on the floor, practising the crawling she has recently begun.
- After settling in from school, Tsumugi watches the baby with careful interest.

## 2. Tsumugi begins asking questions

- Tsumugi sits near Terri Anne and asks whether she may ask questions about Patti Anne.
- Terri Anne tells her that she may ask as many questions as she wants.
- Tsumugi explains that questions help her turn unfamiliar people into understandable patterns.
- Terri Anne promises to answer honestly and tell Tsumugi whenever she does not know an answer.

## 3. Questions about Patti Anne's age

- Tsumugi asks exactly how old Patti Anne is.
- Terri Anne explains that Patti Anne is eight months old.
- Tsumugi asks what an eight-month-old baby usually understands and what Patti Anne understands specifically.
- Terri Anne explains that babies develop differently and that she pays attention to Patti Anne rather than expecting her to follow an exact schedule.

## 4. Questions about crawling

- Tsumugi asks when Patti Anne first began trying to crawl.
- She wants to know whether Patti Anne chooses a destination or simply follows whatever catches her attention.
- Terri Anne explains that Patti Anne often crawls toward interesting colours, familiar voices, toys, or people she trusts.
- Tsumugi describes the baby as a tiny explorer whose map changes every time someone enters the room.

## 5. Questions about Mei's tattoos

- Tsumugi notices Patti Anne looking toward Mei again.
- She asks whether Patti Anne understands that the colours covering Mei's body are tattoos.
- Terri Anne says Patti Anne probably does not understand tattoos yet but clearly enjoys their different colours and shapes.
- Tsumugi calls Mei a walking picture book that Patti Anne cannot stop trying to read.
- Mei smiles at her sister's metaphor and lets Patti Anne look without forcing her closer.

## 6. Questions about Patti Anne's sounds

- Tsumugi asks how Terri Anne knows what Patti Anne wants before she can speak.
- Terri Anne explains the differences she hears in Patti Anne's cries, laughter, babbling, and quiet sounds.
- She also watches where Patti Anne looks, how she moves, and whether she reaches for something.
- Tsumugi compares the sounds to a language with no dictionary that Terri Anne has learned by listening every day.

## 7. Questions about food

- Tsumugi asks what Patti Anne eats and how Terri Anne knows when she is hungry.
- Terri Anne describes Patti Anne's current feeding routine without claiming every baby follows the same one.
- She explains that Patti Anne has preferences and communicates when she has had enough.
- Tsumugi asks whether babies experience a new flavour like opening a door into a room they have never visited.

## 8. Questions about sleep

- Tsumugi asks where Patti Anne sleeps and what helps her settle.
- Terri Anne explains Patti Anne's bedtime routine and the comfort she takes from familiar voices and a calm environment.
- She confirms that there is no playpen in their arrangement.
- Tsumugi asks whether sleep is harder when a baby's home and surroundings change.
- Terri Anne says she tries to keep Patti Anne's routine familiar even when their location changes.

## 9. Questions about nursing Patti Anne

- Tsumugi asks Terri Anne about nursing Patti Anne.
- She wants to understand how Terri Anne knows when Patti Anne wants to nurse and when she has had enough.
- Terri Anne explains that nursing is part of Patti Anne's feeding routine and that she watches the baby's sounds, movements, and responses.
- Tsumugi remembers a visit when Mei took her to see their doctor.
- In the waiting area, Tsumugi saw a mother cover herself while nursing her baby.
- She asks why that mother used a cover while Terri Anne does not cover herself when nursing Patti Anne.
- Terri Anne explains that a nursing cover is a personal choice: some mothers feel more comfortable using one, while others prefer not to.
- She tells Tsumugi that neither choice makes someone a better or worse mother.
- Tsumugi accepts that the two mothers can meet the same need in different ways.
- She compares nursing to a quiet conversation in which Patti Anne speaks through hunger, comfort, and trust rather than words.

## 10. Questions about being Patti Anne's mother

- Tsumugi asks Terri Anne how she knows whether she is making the right decisions for Patti Anne.
- Terri Anne admits that she does not always know with complete certainty.
- She makes choices by watching Patti Anne, learning from mistakes, and putting her daughter's safety and needs first.
- Tsumugi says motherhood sounds like carrying a compass that sometimes shakes even when it points in the right direction.

## 11. Tsumugi asks how she can help

- After asking many questions, Tsumugi asks what she should do if Patti Anne crawls toward her.
- Terri Anne tells her she can remain calm, speak gently, and allow Patti Anne to choose the distance.
- She asks Tsumugi not to pick Patti Anne up without checking first.
- Tsumugi appreciates the clear instruction and promises to follow it.

## 12. Patti Anne approaches Tsumugi

- Patti Anne begins crawling toward Tsumugi while Terri Anne watches.
- Tsumugi remains still enough to avoid overwhelming her but continues speaking softly.
- She describes Patti Anne as a small boat crossing a wide carpet sea.
- Terri Anne understands that Tsumugi's metaphor expresses affection rather than distance.

## 13. A growing connection

- Patti Anne pauses near Tsumugi and studies her face.
- Tsumugi does not touch or lift her without permission.
- Terri Anne thanks Tsumugi for being patient and respectful.
- The successful interaction gives Tsumugi confidence that she can understand Patti Anne without needing every answer immediately.

## 14. Mike calls Terri Anne

- During the evening, Mike calls Terri Anne from Los Angeles.
- He tells her that he will be coming home in the morning on the day after tomorrow.
- His overnight flight is expected to reach Toronto at approximately six o'clock that morning.
- Terri Anne is relieved to know when Mike will return to the Carlisle mansion.
- Mike does not discuss his planned confrontation with Cooper during this call.

## 15. Olivia and Mei thank Terri Anne

- Olivia and Mei thank Terri Anne for patiently answering Tsumugi's many questions about Patti Anne.
- Mei appreciates that Terri Anne treated her younger half sister's questions seriously rather than dismissing them.
- Olivia recognizes that the conversation helped Tsumugi become more comfortable around Patti Anne.
- Terri Anne says she was glad to answer and does not mind continuing the conversation another evening.

## 16. Charlene offers Terri Anne another scene

- Charlene asks Terri Anne whether she would like to perform in another Key Porn Studios scene.
- The proposed scene would include Terri Anne and two other adult women.
- This is a paid job offer rather than another audition.
- Charlene makes the offer because Terri Anne performed professionally during her recorded audition and was invited to return.

## 17. Terri Anne accepts the job

- Terri Anne accepts Charlene's offer.
- She will perform professionally under the name **Scout**, as she did during her audition.
- Charlene will provide the production information so Terri Anne can prepare for the scene.
- Terri Anne is excited to continue exploring the career she enjoyed during her first studio experience.

## 18. Closing beat

- Tsumugi still has more questions, and Terri Anne tells her that they can continue another time after school.
- Patti Anne returns her attention to the colours on Mei's tattoos.
- Mike will return home the morning after next, while Terri Anne now has another Key Porn Studios job ahead of her.
- The chapter ends with the household sharing an evening shaped by questions, trust, new plans, and Terri Anne's growing career.
