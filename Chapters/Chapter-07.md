# Chapter Seven — Mike's Third Visit

## 1. Mike returns to the Blue Velvet Room

- Mike makes his third anonymous visit to the Blue Velvet Room.
- He still has not revealed that he is Mike Key or that he secretly owns the club.
- He returns partly to see Scout and partly to continue observing Adam and Don while Bryan investigates their backgrounds.
- Mike takes his familiar place beside the bar.

## 2. Marco serves the familiar drink

- **Marco** is once again working behind the bar.
- He recognizes Mike immediately and serves him his usual **Johnnie Walker on ice**.
- Marco's curiosity about Mike has grown after two visits, two confrontations, and Mike's increasingly large payments for Scout's time.
- Mike remains polite but gives Marco no additional information about his identity.

## 3. Scout sees Mike again

- Scout notices Mike during her shift and is genuinely glad that he has returned.
- Her happiness is visible before she can hide it.
- Mike recognizes her reaction and is equally pleased to see her.
- Adam also notices Scout's response and watches the pair closely.

## 4. Scout joins Mike at the bar

- After finishing her set, Scout comes to Mike at the bar.
- Marco prepares her usual **virgin Bloody Mary with extra celery sticks**.
- The familiar drinks create a comforting ritual for Mike and Scout.
- Their conversation begins more naturally than on either of Mike's previous visits.
- Scout admits that she had hoped Mike would return, although she does not explain how much his visits have come to mean to her.

## 5. Scout remains guarded

- Despite her happiness, Scout continues to avoid questions about her home and family life.
- She does not tell Mike that her real name is Terri Anne Williams.
- She remains terrified to reveal that she has an eight-month-old daughter named Patti Anne.
- Adam's blackmail has taught her that personal information can be turned into a weapon.
- Mike already knows Terri Anne's identity and Patti Anne's existence from Bryan's report, but he refuses to expose that knowledge or pressure her into a confession.

## 6. The shadow of Terri Anne's past

- Scout's fear is rooted in more than Adam's present blackmail.
- Before working at the Blue Velvet Room, Terri Anne survived being trafficked.
- The experience left her deeply cautious about powerful men, promises of protection, and anyone who asks for private details.
- Her past is acknowledged without graphic description, keeping the focus on its continuing emotional effects.
- She has learned to separate Scout, the identity she presents at work, from Terri Anne, the young mother she is protecting.

## 7. Mike recognizes her fear

- Mike notices that Scout becomes tense whenever their conversation approaches her past or home life.
- He tells her that she does not have to disclose anything before she is ready.
- His patience surprises her because other people have treated access to her time as access to her entire life.
- Mike feels guilty that he knows facts she has not chosen to share, but he keeps Bryan's report confidential.
- He concentrates on making their conversation feel safe rather than extracting information.

## 8. Their connection deepens

- Mike and Scout talk about neutral subjects that allow her personality to emerge without exposing her secrets.
- Scout becomes more relaxed and allows herself to laugh with him.
- Mike sees the difference between her public stage persona and the thoughtful young woman behind it.
- Scout senses that Mike is also hiding a major part of his life, but she does not yet know whether his secrecy is harmless or dangerous.

## 9. Adam watches

- Adam observes that Scout is becoming emotionally attached to Mike.
- He views their growing connection as another source of leverage.
- Don remains nearby, prepared to support Adam in another confrontation.
- Mike notices both men watching but does not reveal that he knows about their blackmail scheme.
- The investigation is still active, and Mike does not want to warn them prematurely.

## 10. Adam and Don confront Mike again

- Adam approaches the bar with Don for the third time.
- He again accuses Mike of monopolizing Scout's time.
- Don stands beside his cousin as an intimidating presence.
- Scout becomes tense, knowing that Adam's interest is about money and control rather than club policy.
- Marco watches from behind the bar, anticipating another extraordinary response from Mike.

## 11. Mike lays down two thousand dollars

- Mike calmly takes out **two thousand dollars** and lays the money on the bar.
- He says that it covers Scout's time and their drinks.
- The amount is far greater than the two hundred dollars from his first visit or the five hundred dollars from his second.
- Adam is astonished and immediately abandons the confrontation.
- He signals Don to back away and encourages Scout to remain with the generous customer.
- Mike studies Adam's reaction as further confirmation that greed controls his treatment of the dancers.

## 12. The business card

- A business card is paperclipped to one of the bills.
- The card contains only the name **Mike** and his private telephone number.
- It does not show Mike's surname, Key Corporation, or any clue that he owns the Blue Velvet Room.
- The card is intended for Scout, giving her a way to contact Mike directly outside Adam's control.
- Mike does not draw Scout's attention to the card because Adam and Don are constantly watching them.
- He leaves it discreetly paperclipped to the bill and trusts that Scout will discover it when she handles the money.
- When Scout later finds it, she understands that the private number gives her a choice: she can call him if and when she feels ready.

## 13. Closing hook

- Scout nearly tells Mike that Scout is not her real name.
- Fear stops her before she can say "Terri Anne."
- Mike gently changes the subject rather than forcing her to continue.
- Scout keeps the card bearing Mike's private number.
- The chapter ends with her wondering whether Mike might be the first person she can trust with the truth—and fearing what Adam will do if she tries.

## Chapter Purpose

This chapter:

- Brings Mike to the Blue Velvet Room for a third visit.
- Repeats the familiar drinks and Marco's role as bartender.
- Shows Scout's genuine happiness at seeing Mike again.
- Deepens their emotional connection.
- Explains why Scout remains guarded about Patti Anne.
- Reveals that Terri Anne is a trafficking survivor without depicting her abuse graphically.
- Shows Mike respecting Terri Anne's privacy despite what he learned from Bryan's report.
- Repeats Adam and Don's confrontation while raising Mike's payment to two thousand dollars.
- Gives Scout Mike's private telephone number on a business card paperclipped to one of the bills.
- Establishes that Adam sees their relationship as a new opportunity for control.
