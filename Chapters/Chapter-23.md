# Chapter Twenty-Three — Blueberry Waffles

## 1. The morning after their first date

- Morning arrives at Mike's Carlisle mansion after Scout chooses to spend the night with him.
- Mike wakes and discovers that Scout is no longer beside him.
- He dresses and goes downstairs to look for her.
- The sound and smell of breakfast lead him toward the mansion's industrial kitchen.

## 2. Mike finds Scout in the kitchen

- Mike enters the industrial kitchen and finds Scout preparing breakfast.
- She is wearing one of his **blue shirts**.
- The shirt looks oversized on her and reminds Mike of the intimacy and trust they shared during the night.
- Scout appears comfortable enough to move around the enormous kitchen on her own.

## 3. Homemade blueberry waffles

- Scout is making **homemade blueberry waffles** for both of them.
- She has found the ingredients and learned enough about the industrial equipment to begin cooking.
- The ordinary act of making breakfast brings warmth to a kitchen designed on an extraordinary scale.
- Mike is touched that she chose to make something for them rather than wait to be served.

## 4. Scout asks about permission

- Scout looks up when Mike enters.
- With a mixture of playfulness and uncertainty, she asks whether she needs his permission to use the wonderful industrial kitchen.
- Her question reflects how unfamiliar she is with Mike's mansion and how careful she remains about crossing boundaries.

## 5. Mike answers Scout

- Mike tells Scout that she does not need his permission to use the industrial kitchen.
- He assures her that she may use it to make anything she wants while she is his guest.
- Mike then tells her that she looks very gorgeous wearing his blue shirt.
- His answer removes her uncertainty while turning the moment into affectionate teasing.

## 6. Scout reacts to the compliment

- Scout smiles at Mike's compliment and continues preparing the waffles.
- She tells him that his shirt is comfortable.
- Mike enjoys seeing someone he cares about bring ordinary life into the mansion.
- The kitchen feels less formal and lonely with Scout inside it.

## 7. They prepare breakfast together

- Mike offers to help rather than expecting Scout to serve him.
- Scout gives him a simple task while she finishes the blueberry waffles.
- Their cooperation allows them to enjoy a relaxed domestic moment after the emotional importance of the previous night.
- They speak comfortably without immediately defining their relationship.

## 8. Patti Anne remains important

- Scout checks the time and thinks about Patti Anne with the babysitter.
- She remembers that her milk is ready in the refrigerator for Patti Anne's morning feeding.
- Knowing Patti Anne is being cared for allows Scout to enjoy breakfast, but she intends to return to her daughter soon.
- Mike respects that Patti Anne remains Scout's first responsibility.

## 9. Scout reveals her real name

- While they continue breakfast, Scout tells Mike that she wants him to know her real name.
- She introduces herself as **Terri Anne Williams**.
- This is the first time she has personally given Mike her real name.
- Mike does not reveal that Bryan's confidential report had already identified her.
- Instead, he tells her, **“Thank you for trusting me with your real name, Terri Anne.”**
- Hearing Mike use her real name by her own choice makes the moment deeply personal.

## 10. Terri Anne deliberately drops a piece of waffle

- In the middle of breakfast, Terri Anne deliberately lets a piece of blueberry waffle fall onto the blue shirt she borrowed from Mike.
- She reacts quickly, takes off the stained shirt, and places it in the sink.
- Beneath the oversized shirt, Terri Anne is just wearing a **black thong**.
- Mike's chin nearly drops to the floor because he is stunned by Terri Anne's beauty.
- Mike realizes from her playful expression that the accident was intentional.
- The moment adds flirtation and humor to their first breakfast together.

## 11. Wolfee enters the kitchen

- Before Terri Anne can continue teasing Mike, **Wolfee** unexpectedly walks into the industrial kitchen.
- Terri Anne immediately becomes embarrassed.
- She starts running out of the kitchen to find privacy.
- Wolfee realizes that he has interrupted a private moment and gives her space.
- Wolfee apologizes to Mike for walking in unexpectedly.
- Mike is left caught between surprise, amusement, and concern for Terri Anne's embarrassment.

## 12. Closing beat

- Terri Anne disappears from the industrial kitchen while Wolfee remains near the entrance.
- The homemade blueberry waffles and Mike's stained blue shirt remain behind.
- Mike realizes how naturally Terri Anne has brought warmth, humor, affection, and unpredictability into his private home.
- The chapter ends with Mike now permitted to call her Terri Anne and preparing to reassure her after Wolfee's unexpected interruption.

## Chapter Purpose

This chapter:

- Continues the morning after Mike and Scout's first date.
- Shows Scout wearing Mike's blue shirt.
- Places Scout in the mansion's industrial kitchen.
- Establishes that she is making homemade blueberry waffles.
- Has Scout ask whether she needs permission to use the kitchen.
- Shows Mike reassuring her and complimenting how gorgeous she looks in his shirt.
- Makes breakfast the moment Scout personally reveals that her real name is Terri Anne Williams.
- Shows Mike thanking her for trusting him without revealing what Bryan's report had already disclosed.
- Adds a playful moment in which Terri Anne deliberately stains and removes the borrowed shirt.
- Has Wolfee unexpectedly enter the kitchen and cause Terri Anne to run out in embarrassment.
- Gives Mike and Terri Anne a warm domestic moment without forcing them to define the relationship immediately.
