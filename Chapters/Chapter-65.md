# Chapter Sixty-Five — Arriving in Kyoto

## 1. The flight from British Columbia

- Mike, Terri Anne, and Patti Anne leave British Columbia aboard a Key Corporation aircraft on Thursday.
- The flight follows their Wednesday afternoon and evening at the Whistler mansion.
- Because they cross the International Date Line and enter Japan's time zone, the local date is Friday by the time they arrive.
- Terri Anne knows that this is the first stage of a month in Japan rather than an immediate trip to a film set.

## 2. Terri Anne looks down at Japan

- As the aircraft descends, Terri Anne looks through the window at Japan below them.
- She holds Patti Anne close enough for the baby to see the changing light and movement outside.
- Terri Anne quietly tells Patti Anne that they have reached another country together.
- Mike watches mother and daughter experience the arrival.

## 3. Mike reviews the plan one more time

- Mike reminds Terri Anne that neither movie will be filmed during the original Saturday schedule.
- Her full statutory waiting period must expire before the first lawful filming date.
- The first movie with the two adult women is intended for audiences outside Japan.
- The second movie with the ten adult men has separate contract and distribution requirements.

## 4. They land near Kyoto

- The Key Corporation aircraft lands at Kansai International Airport.
- From the airport, Mike, Terri Anne, and Patti Anne will travel by secure ground transportation to Kyoto.
- The studio has not been allowed to turn the airport arrival into publicity.
- Mike wants Terri Anne and Patti Anne protected from unnecessary attention.

## 5. Patti Anne's documents

- Terri Anne keeps Patti Anne's travel documents with her own.
- The documents establish Patti Anne's identity and Terri Anne's relationship to her daughter.
- Mike makes certain that no employee separates mother and baby during the arrival process.
- Patti Anne remains with Terri Anne while the adults complete the required entry formalities.

## 6. Terri Anne enters Japan as a visitor

- Terri Anne enters Japan for family travel and legal preparation, not to begin unauthorized work.
- Mike reminds her that a passport and permission to enter the country do not by themselves authorize paid filming.
- Key Porn Studios' Japanese legal and immigration teams must complete the proper work authorization before either production.
- Until that process is complete and the waiting period expires, Terri Anne will not perform for the studio.

## 7. The drive toward Kyoto

- A secure vehicle takes Mike, Terri Anne, and Patti Anne away from the airport.
- Terri Anne watches the landscape and city districts pass outside the window.
- Mike explains that Kyoto contains historic neighbourhoods, modern districts, temples, gardens, shops, and family attractions.
- He does not present the city only as the location of Key Porn Studios' special equipment room.

## 8. Terri Anne asks about the month

- Terri Anne asks how much time they will actually spend in Kyoto.
- Mike says they can decide together rather than fixing the entire month in advance.
- They may remain in Kyoto, travel to the Tokyo penthouse, visit the Sapporo mansion and its indoor onsen, or explore other cities.
- The production schedule will not prevent Terri Anne and Patti Anne from experiencing Japan.

## 9. Patti Anne reacts to the long journey

- Patti Anne alternates between watching the unfamiliar surroundings and leaning against Terri Anne.
- Mike keeps the baby's supplies within reach in the vehicle.
- Terri Anne recognizes that Patti Anne may need time to adjust to the long flight and time change.
- They agree not to overload their first day with plans.

## 10. The Kyoto hotel

- Key Corporation has reserved the presidential suite at a Kyoto hotel for Mike, Terri Anne, and Patti Anne.
- The suite contains a separate sleeping area for Patti Anne and enough space for Terri Anne to care for her without entering any production area.
- Security has checked the presidential suite and the surrounding hotel floor before their arrival.

## 11. The contracts arrive separately

- Terri Anne receives separate documents for the movie with the two women and the movie with the ten men.
- Each set of documents identifies the relevant production, performers, proposed location, compensation, release plan, and safety arrangements.
- Receipt of the contracts and explanatory documents allows the Japanese legal team to calculate the waiting period for each movie.
- Mike insists that Terri Anne have time to review them without Charlene, the directors, or the other performers pressuring her.

## 12. Independent explanation

- A qualified adviser will explain both contracts to Terri Anne in clear language.
- A Japanese legal professional will explain the local performer-protection rules.
- Terri Anne may ask questions privately and may still reject either production.
- Mike does not sit in on any confidential conversation unless Terri Anne asks him to be present.

## 13. The first movie's audience

- Terri Anne confirms that the movie with the two adult women is not intended for a Japanese audience.
- Its planned distribution is in other countries where the finished version may be shown without Japan's mosaic censorship if local law allows.
- The contract must state the international release plan clearly.
- Filming in Kyoto still requires compliance with Japanese performer protections even when the audience is outside Japan.

## 14. The second movie's audience

- The second movie has a separate release and compliance plan.
- Any version distributed in Japan must undergo the required Japanese legal and industry review.
- International versions may differ where other countries' laws permit.
- Terri Anne must understand and approve how her performance may appear in each market before filming.

## 15. The secured day care at Key Porn Studios Kyoto

- Mike tells Terri Anne that the secured day care is located on the Key Porn Studios Kyoto site.
- The day care is separated from the adult sets, special equipment room, and all production activity by controlled access.
- Patti Anne will never be taken into an adult production room.
- The day-care staff and security team must know that Patti Anne is an eight-month-old infant and prepare for her needs in advance.
- No production meeting may interrupt Terri Anne's care of Patti Anne without Terri Anne's agreement.
- During the waiting month, Terri Anne remains free to spend her days with Patti Anne and Mike.

## 16. Terri Anne reaches Kyoto

- The secure vehicle enters Kyoto.
- Terri Anne takes in her first view of the city while Patti Anne rests against her.
- Mike tells Terri Anne that they have no film to rush toward and no director waiting to order her out of the vehicle.
- Their first responsibility is to settle Patti Anne and recover from the journey.

## 17. Their first quiet moment in the city

- At the hotel, Terri Anne carries Patti Anne into the presidential suite.
- Mike brings the travel documents and baby supplies while hotel staff handle the remaining luggage.
- Terri Anne pauses and realizes that she is standing in Kyoto with her daughter.
- She is here by choice, with time to understand every agreement before she performs.
- Mike orders a traditional Japanese dinner for their first evening in Kyoto.
- A private chef comes to the presidential suite to prepare and serve the meal for them there.
- The chef prepares tofu and wakame miso soup.
- The main dish is shiozake, Japanese salted grilled salmon.
- Dessert is fluffy Japanese cheesecake.
- This allows Terri Anne and Patti Anne to recover privately from the long journey without going to the hotel restaurant.

## 18. Bryan calls Mike

- During the family's first day in Kyoto, Mike receives a telephone call from Bryan, his private investigator.
- Bryan reports that his team has located Ric.
- Ric is now in Western Australia.
- Bryan does not yet know whether Ric travelled there to hide, meet someone, or continue Victoria's plan.
- His exact city and activities in Western Australia remain under investigation.

## 19. Mike calls Key Corporation Australia's CEO

- As soon as the call with Bryan ends, Mike quickly calls the chief executive officer of Key Corporation Australia.
- Mike reports that Ric is believed to be in Western Australia.
- He asks the CEO to determine whether Ric has contacted any Key Corporation offices, employees, contractors, properties, or transportation services in Australia.
- Mike tells the CEO to cooperate with Bryan's investigators and preserve any relevant records.
- He does not want Ric warned that Mike knows where he is.

## 20. The lawyers will meet Terri Anne

- Mike tells Terri Anne that the lawyers will meet with her on Monday at nine o'clock in the morning.
- The meeting will be held over breakfast.
- The lawyers will review the separate contracts, waiting periods, international distribution plans, work authorization, safety requirements, and Terri Anne's cancellation rights.
- Terri Anne will be able to ask questions before agreeing to any final production schedule.

## 21. The family trip and the investigation overlap

- Mike understands that reaching Kyoto has not ended the danger involving his middle siblings.
- He does not hide Bryan's report or his call to the Australian CEO from Terri Anne.
- Mike tells her that investigators still need to learn why Ric is in Western Australia.
- Security continues protecting Terri Anne and Patti Anne while the Australian inquiry begins.

## 22. Closing beat

- Mike asks Terri Anne what she and Patti Anne would like to see first after they have rested.
- Terri Anne looks around the presidential suite before answering.
- Her breakfast meeting with the lawyers is scheduled for Monday at 9:00 a.m.
- Ric's presence in Western Australia creates a new investigative question as the family's month in Japan begins.
