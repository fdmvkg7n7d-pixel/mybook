# Chapter Sixty — Breakfast the Next Morning

## 1. The next day at the Carlisle mansion

- The morning after Terri Anne's Blue Velvet Room shift, Mike, Terri Anne, and Patti Anne have breakfast together in one of the Carlisle mansion's kitchens.
- They use the kitchen as an informal family space rather than eating in one of the mansion's formal dining rooms.
- The quiet meal initially gives them time together without investigators, conspirators, or club customers around them.
- Mike appreciates finally being home with Terri Anne and Patti Anne.

## 2. Patti Anne joins the breakfast

- Eight-month-old Patti Anne remains close to her mother and Mike throughout the meal.
- Terri Anne prepares an age-appropriate breakfast for her daughter.
- Mike and Terri Anne take turns paying attention to Patti Anne while eating their own food.
- Patti Anne watches both adults and responds to their voices.

## 3. The morning feels different

- Mike and Terri Anne are aware that they told each other they were in love during the drive to the Blue Velvet Room.
- Neither tries to pretend that the declaration did not happen.
- Their conversation feels warmer and more settled than it did before the trip to Los Angeles.
- Patti Anne's presence keeps the moment grounded in their developing family life.

## 4. Mike asks about Terri Anne's shift

- Mike asks Terri Anne how her shift at the Blue Velvet Room went.
- Terri Anne tells him what it felt like to return as Scout after everything that happened with Adam, Don, Cooper, and Victoria.
- She is glad to see familiar people and work in a place where she understands the routine.
- Mike listens without treating the club as a threat to their relationship.

## 5. Terri Anne talks about Marco

- Terri Anne explains that seeing Marco reminds her why she would miss the Blue Velvet Room if she left completely.
- Marco knew about Terri Anne and Patti Anne before many other people did and still calls her Scout while she is working.
- His role as assistant manager and head bartender makes him part of the club's daily stability.
- Mike repeats that Marco is welcome to visit the mansion as Terri Anne's friend.

## 6. Terri Anne talks about Captain Morrison

- Terri Anne also mentions Captain Morrison and her concern for him.
- Mike understands that their connection is based on friendship, care, and the difficult period after the captain's sister died.
- He is not jealous and does not ask Terri Anne to end the friendship.
- Mike reminds her that Captain Morrison may also visit the mansion if he wishes.

## 7. The mansion belongs to Terri Anne too

- Mike again tells Terri Anne that the mansion is her home, not merely a place where he allows her to stay.
- She can invite trusted friends and make decisions about shared spaces.
- The unfinished bedroom she selected is one example of her beginning to shape the house around their life together.
- Terri Anne is still adjusting to the idea that such an enormous property belongs to her home life too.

## 8. Terri Anne mentions Mei's tattoo offer

- Terri Anne tells Mike that she asked Mei to give her a tattoo and that Mei agreed.
- She has not yet settled on the design, size, meaning, or placement.
- Mike is interested in what Terri Anne chooses but does not try to choose for her.
- Patti Anne's fascination with Mei's colourful tattoos helped inspire the conversation.

## 9. They discuss Terri Anne's work choices

- Terri Anne says she wants to continue deciding between Blue Velvet Room shifts and Key Porn Studios work rather than abandoning one immediately.
- Key Porn Studios gives her another professional option and a different kind of income.
- The Blue Velvet Room gives her familiar friendships and a workplace identity she still values.
- Mike supports her taking time to decide what balance feels right.

## 10. Mike thinks about the danger

- Even during the peaceful breakfast, Mike remembers that Victoria remains unresolved.
- He tells Terri Anne that he will find his middle siblings **to stop them**.
- Mike intends to prevent them from causing further harm to Terri Anne, Patti Anne, Ashlee, Geoffrey, or anyone else in the family.
- He also wants to determine the full extent of their involvement and hold them accountable for the attacks, criminal interference, and efforts to gain control of Key Corporation.
- Adam, Cooper, and Don are expected to leave Ontario under the payment arrangement.
- The investigators retain their confessions and the evidence against them.
- Mike does not allow the remaining danger to destroy the morning with Terri Anne and Patti Anne.

## 11. Charlene and Olivia enter the kitchen

- Charlene and Olivia come into the kitchen while Mike, Terri Anne, and Patti Anne are finishing breakfast.
- Olivia is already staying at the mansion, while Charlene has come to speak with Mike and Terri Anne.
- Their arrival changes the quiet family breakfast into an informal discussion among people Mike trusts.
- Patti Anne recognizes Olivia and looks toward her deep blue eyes.

## 12. Terri Anne thanks Charlene

- Terri Anne thanks Charlene for calling her and offering another shift at the Blue Velvet Room.
- She says returning to the club allowed her to see Marco, Captain Morrison, and the other familiar people she would miss.
- Charlene tells Terri Anne that she is quite welcome.
- Charlene is pleased that Terri Anne felt comfortable returning to work as Scout.
- She also reminds Terri Anne that her next Key Porn Studios film is scheduled to be made this weekend.
- The film is the paid scene with two other adult women that Terri Anne previously accepted.
- Terri Anne tells Charlene that she knows about the schedule and will be there for the filming.
- She asks Charlene whether the Key Porn Studios day care will be open that day.
- Charlene tells Terri Anne that the day care will be open for the employees' children.
- Approximately ten other children are expected to attend, ranging from two to ten years old.
- Patti Anne will be the youngest child there, but the day-care staff already know that she is an eight-month-old infant.
- Terri Anne now has the information she needs to decide whether Patti Anne will accompany her to the studio.
- Mike looks at Terri Anne and says, **“Wow, you are going to be in a porn movie with two other women.”**
- His reaction reflects surprise and excitement about Terri Anne's growing career at Key Porn Studios.

## 13. Terri Anne discloses part of her coercive past

- Terri Anne tells Mike, Charlene, and Olivia that when she was being exploited in prostitution, she was made to sleep with other women.
- She did not control those encounters or choose the women involved.
- Terri Anne explains that the upcoming Key Porn Studios film is different because she chose to accept the job and can state her own preferences.
- Mike understands that his surprised comment touched a part of Terri Anne's history that he had not fully considered.
- Charlene and Olivia listen without asking Terri Anne for graphic details or treating her disclosure as studio entertainment.
- The conversation distinguishes Terri Anne's former exploitation from the professional decisions she is making for herself now.

## 14. Mike moves beside Terri Anne and Patti Anne

- Mike quickly stands and walks toward Terri Anne and Patti Anne.
- He tells Terri Anne that he does not need to hear details about anything she was made to do in her past while she was a minor.
- Mike makes clear that Terri Anne does not owe him, Charlene, Olivia, or anyone else an account of that exploitation.
- His response is not a rejection of Terri Anne; he wants her to know that she never has to describe those experiences to prove anything.
- Charlene and Olivia agree with Mike.
- All three remain willing to listen if Terri Anne ever chooses to talk, but none will demand the details from her.

## 15. Mike explains his plans

- Mike tells Charlene and Olivia that he intends to find his middle siblings and stop them.
- He wants to prevent Victoria, Jack, Ric, or any other involved sibling from causing further harm to Terri Anne, Patti Anne, Ashlee, Geoffrey, or the rest of the family.
- Mike will use Bryan's evidence, the recorded confessions, and the investigators rather than ignoring the danger.
- He explains that Adam, Cooper, and Don have been ordered to leave Ontario under the forty-five-million-dollar arrangement.
- Victoria and the middle siblings remain the unresolved threat, and Mike wants Charlene and Olivia prepared for the next stage.

## 16. Patti Anne brings them back to the moment

- Patti Anne makes a sound or reaches toward something on the breakfast table.
- Mike and Terri Anne immediately return their attention to her.
- They laugh at the way an eight-month-old can redirect an important conversation without understanding it.
- The small interruption reinforces the ordinary family life they are trying to build.

## 17. Closing beat

- Mike, Terri Anne, and Patti Anne finish breakfast together.
- Mike and Terri Anne have moved from privately falling in love to saying it aloud and sharing the next morning as a family.
- The Carlisle mansion feels less like Mike's enormous property and more like their home.
- The chapter ends with the three of them remaining at the table after breakfast, enjoying the calm before the next problem arrives.
