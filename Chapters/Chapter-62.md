# Chapter Sixty-Two — Mike Confronts Victoria

## 1. Mike prepares for the confrontation

- After the kitchen conversation, Mike prepares to confront Victoria about everything she has done.
- He will not allow her to continue threatening Terri Anne, Patti Anne, Ashlee, or Geoffrey.
- Bryan's investigation, the recording from The Well, the physical evidence, and the confessions from Cooper and Don give Mike a documented case.
- Mike decides to go directly to Victoria's house in the middle of the night rather than summon her to a meeting.

## 2. Mike protects the family first

- Mike ensures that Terri Anne and Patti Anne remain protected at the Carlisle mansion.
- Olivia, Mei, and Tsumugi are also to stay under protection while Victoria remains dangerous.
- Guards continue watching Ashlee and Geoffrey's Los Angeles mansion.
- Security accompanies Mike to Victoria's property but remains outside and ready to intervene if needed.

## 3. Mike finds Victoria and Jack in the same bed

- Mike arrives at Victoria's home in the middle of the night and enters the house.
- He reaches Victoria's bedroom and discovers that Jack is in the same bed with her.
- Mike sits down on the bed before waking them.
- His position deliberately mirrors the way Victoria sat on Ashlee and Geoffrey's bed after invading their Los Angeles home.
- Victoria and Jack wake to find Mike sitting beside them.
- Both are shocked that Mike has reached them in what they believed was a private and secure room.

## 4. Mike tells Victoria and Jack that he knows

- With both siblings awake, Mike tells Victoria that her conspiracy is over.
- Jack remains in the bed and hears the confrontation from the beginning.
- Mike says he knows about Victoria's meetings with Adam and Cooper at The Well.
- He knows that she promised Adam, Cooper, and Don fifteen million dollars each if her plan succeeded.
- He knows Victoria and the other involved siblings used them to interfere with his relationships and manipulate their mother's inheritance condition.

## 5. The evidence from The Well

- Mike explains that Bryan's investigators photographed Victoria meeting Adam and Cooper.
- A listening device beneath the table recorded Victoria directing the conspiracy.
- The recording captured the promised payments, the concern about Mike and Terri Anne's relationship, and Victoria's continuing use of Don.
- Victoria can no longer dismiss the meeting as a coincidence or deny that she was in charge.

## 6. Cooper's betrayal

- Mike confronts Victoria about using Cooper's position as his limousine driver.
- Cooper gave the conspiracy access to Mike's movements, travel, household information, and private conversations.
- Mike tells Victoria that she turned a trusted employee into a source of information against his family.
- He also knows Cooper attended her meetings with Adam and protected her plans while pretending to serve Mike.

## 7. Don and the threat to Terri Anne and Patti Anne

- Mike confronts Victoria about Don following Terri Anne and Patti Anne.
- He reminds Victoria that Don wore a blue jacket and blue hat and pursued them near the Y.
- Victoria continued planning to use Don's physical strength even after deciding that he was unreliable.
- Mike knows about the hidden physical task Victoria intended Don to perform.

## 8. Adam's role

- Mike tells Victoria that Adam used Patti Anne to blackmail Terri Anne.
- Adam tried to control Terri Anne through fear while working toward Victoria's larger objective.
- Mike holds Victoria responsible for continuing to use Adam after knowing what he was willing to do.
- Victoria's plan placed an eighteen-year-old mother and her eight-month-old baby in danger.

## 9. The confessions

- Mike reveals that Cooper and Don have confessed everything they know.
- Their statements explain Victoria's instructions, Adam's involvement, the promised money, and the roles assigned to each man.
- Their separate accounts support the evidence Bryan's investigators already collected.
- Victoria realizes that the men she paid and controlled are no longer protecting her.

## 10. Mike dismantled her team

- Mike tells Victoria that he offered Adam, Cooper, and Don forty-five million dollars total to leave Ontario and abandon her conspiracy.
- A single three-million-dollar cheque was issued immediately, with the remaining forty-two million to be paid in instalments.
- If any of the three returns to Ontario, the unpaid instalments end and Mike gives the evidence to police.
- Mike deliberately matched the total amount Victoria had promised them and removed their financial reason to remain loyal to her.

## 11. Their mother's will

- Mike confronts Victoria about trying to exploit their mother's condition that he marry and have a child before turning forty.
- He tells her that the condition is void because the middle siblings committed crimes to manipulate its outcome.
- Victoria, Jack, Ric, and any other involved sibling cannot sabotage Mike's relationships and then profit from that sabotage.
- Control of Key Corporation will not pass to them through criminal conduct.

## 12. Mike names Terri Anne and Patti Anne

- Mike tells Victoria that Terri Anne and Patti Anne are his family.
- He will not permit Victoria to treat either of them as an obstacle to an inheritance.
- Terri Anne is not a tool Victoria can frighten away, and Patti Anne is not leverage to be used against her mother.
- Mike makes clear that Victoria's actions strengthened his determination to protect them.

## 13. Mike confronts her about Ashlee's shooting

- Mike tells Victoria that Ashlee and Geoffrey reported her confession in Los Angeles.
- Victoria admitted hiring the assassin whose bullet destroyed Ashlee's T11 vertebra.
- She also admitted that Mike had been the intended target.
- Mike forces Victoria to face the fact that her attempt to have him killed permanently injured their youngest sister.

## 14. The attack on Ashlee and Geoffrey's home

- Mike confronts Victoria about breaking into Ashlee and Geoffrey's mansion while they slept.
- He names the fear she caused by reaching their bedroom and sitting on their bed.
- He condemns her for mocking Ashlee's body, disability, tattoo, relationship, and supportive equipment.
- Mike tells Victoria that Ashlee and Geoffrey's private life was never hers to invade or judge.

## 15. The destroyed property

- Mike lists what Victoria destroyed during the invasion.
- She broke Ashlee and Geoffrey's iPhones, cutting off their immediate access to help.
- She destroyed Ashlee's regular wheelchair and attacked her independence.
- She also damaged the adapted pedal system Ashlee needs to play her grand piano.

## 16. Mike confronts Jack as well

- Because Jack is present in Victoria's bed, Mike confronts him directly about the earlier harassment of Ashlee and Geoffrey.
- Mike also questions Victoria about the connection between her campaign and the actions taken by Jack and Ric.
- Their attempts to break apart Ashlee and Geoffrey were not harmless family disagreements.
- Mike tells Jack and Victoria that he will confront every middle sibling who participated.
- None of them will be allowed to use Ashlee's disability or relationship as a weapon against her.

## 17. The second assassin

- Mike reminds Victoria that she told Ashlee and Geoffrey she hired another professional assassin.
- The intended target and assignment remain unknown.
- Mike demands the assassin's identity, location, instructions, and intended target.
- He tells Victoria that refusing to answer will be treated as an ongoing threat to the entire family.

## 18. Victoria tries to regain control

- Victoria attempts to redirect the discussion toward the inheritance and Key Corporation.
- Mike refuses to debate whether she deserved control of the company.
- He tells her that no corporate dispute excuses stalking, blackmail, home invasion, property destruction, or attempted murder.
- Every time Victoria tries to shift blame, Mike returns to the evidence.

## 19. Mike refuses excuses

- Mike will not accept claims that Adam, Cooper, or Don acted alone.
- The recording and confessions establish that Victoria directed and financed the conspiracy.
- He will not accept the idea that Ashlee's injuries were merely an assassin's mistake.
- Hiring the assassin made Victoria responsible for the shot, regardless of which sibling the bullet struck.

## 20. Mike has already contacted the OPP

- Mike tells Victoria and Jack that he has a friend in the Ontario Provincial Police.
- Before coming to the house, Mike gave his OPP friend the evidence concerning Victoria and Jack.
- He turned over the material documenting the attempted assassination, the threats, the home invasion, the destroyed property, the conspiracy, and Jack's participation.
- Mike did not include Adam, Cooper, or Don in what he gave the OPP because his separate agreement requires the three men to leave Ontario and stay away.
- The evidence involving those three men remains secured and can still be disclosed if any of them violates the agreement.

## 21. The police will arrive in one hour

- Mike tells Victoria and Jack that the police are already on their way.
- They will arrive at Victoria's house in approximately one hour to arrest both Victoria and Jack.
- Mike makes clear that this is not a threat or a bluff; he has already given the OPP what they need.
- The remaining hour gives Victoria one final opportunity to reveal the second assassin's identity and target.

## 22. Victoria responds with an insult

- Victoria looks at Mike and says, **“Go fuck yourself.”**
- Mike does not raise his voice, leave the room, or allow the insult to redirect the confrontation.
- He reminds Victoria that the police are still coming and that refusing to cooperate will not prevent her arrest.

## 23. One final demand

- Mike gives Victoria a direct opportunity to tell the complete truth.
- He demands the names of everyone who helped her, including anyone connected to the break-in and the new assassin.
- He demands to know whether Jack and Ric received instructions from her.
- He also demands a clear account of every remaining part of the plan that Cooper and Don may not have known.

## 24. Mike speaks as Ashlee's brother

- Mike tells Victoria that Ashlee spent years living with the consequences of a bullet meant for him.
- Victoria later invaded Ashlee's home and deliberately damaged the equipment that gave her independence.
- Mike says he will not forgive or overlook those actions merely because Victoria is his sister.
- Being family increases her responsibility; it does not protect her from consequences.

## 25. Mike speaks as Terri Anne's partner and Patti Anne's father

- Mike tells Victoria that he loves Terri Anne and Patti Anne.
- He will continue building a life with them regardless of what their mother's will once demanded.
- Victoria will not decide whom he loves, whether Patti Anne belongs in the Key family, or how they raise their daughter.
- The mansion, Key Corporation, and Mike's future are no longer tools Victoria can use to divide them.

## 26. Victoria understands what she has lost

- Victoria now knows that Adam, Cooper, and Don have abandoned her plan.
- She knows the inheritance condition cannot give the middle siblings control of Key Corporation.
- She knows Mike has connected the conspiracy, Ashlee's shooting, the Los Angeles invasion, and the second assassin.
- Her remaining leverage depends on information she has not yet surrendered.

## 27. Closing beat

- Mike looks directly at Victoria and repeats his demand for the second assassin's identity and target.
- Jack remains beside Victoria, knowing that the OPP intends to arrest him as well.
- Security remains outside while the police move closer to the house.
- The confrontation has placed every known accusation in front of Victoria and Jack.
- The chapter ends with approximately one hour remaining before their arrests and the unknown assassin still posing the most urgent danger.
