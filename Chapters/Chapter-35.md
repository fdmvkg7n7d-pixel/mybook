# Chapter Thirty-Five — Cooper Turns on Don

## 1. Cooper gathers Adam and Don

- Cooper returns to the house he shares with Adam and Don.
- He tells both men that he has important news about Mike.
- His anger is obvious before he explains what he has learned.
- Adam realizes that the news may threaten Victoria's hidden plan.

## 2. Cooper reveals the relationship

- Cooper tells Adam and Don that Mike and Terri Anne are now romantically involved.
- Their connection is no longer limited to Mike protecting a dancer from the Blue Velvet Room.
- Mike trusts Terri Anne and has brought her deeply into his private life.
- The relationship creates a serious obstacle for anyone trying to keep Mike unmarried before his fortieth birthday.

## 3. Adam recognizes the threat

- Adam understands that Mike's relationship with Terri Anne could interfere with Victoria's plan for Key Corporation.
- If Mike marries and has a child before turning forty, his mother's will no longer transfers the corporation to his middle siblings.
- Terri Anne therefore represents more than a romantic interest; she may prevent the entire succession scheme from succeeding.
- Victoria has promised **fifteen million dollars each** to Adam, Cooper, and Don if her plan succeeds.
- Mike and Terri Anne's relationship now threatens each man's fifteen-million-dollar payment.
- Adam asks Cooper how serious the relationship has become.

## 4. Cooper blames Don

- Cooper turns his anger toward Don.
- He argues that Don's reckless decision to follow Terri Anne and Patti Anne made Mike more protective of them.
- The danger pushed Terri Anne to trust Mike and brought the couple closer together.
- Cooper tells Don that his mistake helped create the exact relationship Victoria's plan was supposed to prevent.

## 5. Cooper wants to rip Don apart

- Cooper becomes so furious that he says he wants to **rip Don apart**.
- He moves toward Don aggressively and appears ready to attack him.
- Don rises to defend himself and refuses to accept all the blame.
- The confrontation threatens to become violent inside the shared house.

## 6. Don defends himself

- Don argues that nobody told him Mike and Terri Anne would fall in love.
- He insists that following them was meant to gather information and protect the plan.
- Don blames Cooper for failing to predict how quickly Mike would increase Terri Anne's security.
- Their accusations reveal how little trust remains among the conspirators.

## 7. Adam intervenes

- Adam steps between Cooper and Don before they can seriously injure one another.
- He reminds Cooper that Victoria still needs Don's physical strength.
- Attacking Don would also risk driving him to Mike or the police with everything he knows.
- Cooper reluctantly stops, but his desire to hurt Don does not disappear.

## 8. Don realizes his value

- Don understands that Adam and Cooper despise him but cannot easily remove him from the plan.
- His physical strength and knowledge of Victoria's conspiracy give him leverage.
- He warns them that threatening him may have consequences.
- Adam recognizes that Don is becoming more dangerous as an ally than he was as an employee.

## 9. The relationship changes Victoria's plan

- Cooper tells Adam that Victoria must be informed about Mike and Terri Anne.
- They need to determine whether the relationship can be disrupted before it threatens the will's deadline.
- Adam worries that direct action against Terri Anne could bring even more protection from Mike and attention from the police.
- The conspirators must now work around a relationship strengthened by their previous mistakes.

## 10. Cooper calls Victoria

- Cooper calls Victoria and tells her that Mike and Terri Anne's relationship has become serious.
- He explains that the relationship could allow Mike to satisfy the marriage requirement in their mother's will.
- Cooper also warns that Don's mistakes helped push Mike and Terri Anne closer together.
- Victoria immediately recognizes the threat to her plan and the promised fifteen-million-dollar payments.

## 11. Victoria orders another meeting at The Well

- Victoria tells Cooper that she wants to meet with him and Adam at **The Well**.
- Don is not invited because Victoria does not trust him in a strategy meeting.
- Cooper relays the meeting instructions to Adam.
- Victoria refuses to discuss the details of her response over the telephone.

## 12. Adam complains again

- Adam complains about having to return to The Well.
- He reminds Cooper that he dislikes the gay bar because men there frequently try to pick him up.
- Cooper tells him that Victoria chose the location and is not asking for his opinion.
- Adam reluctantly agrees because refusing could cost him the fifteen million dollars Victoria promised.

## 13. Closing hook

- Cooper leaves Don with a final warning not to make another independent move.
- Don remains furious that Cooper wanted to attack him and that he is excluded from another meeting.
- Adam and Cooper prepare to meet Victoria at The Well.
- The chapter ends with Mike and Terri Anne's relationship unknowingly placing them at the centre of Victoria's succession scheme.

## Chapter Purpose

This chapter:

- Reveals Mike and Terri Anne's relationship to Adam and Don.
- Shows Adam recognizing the threat to Victoria's plan, the inheritance deadline, and their promised fifteen-million-dollar payments.
- Has Cooper blame Don for bringing Mike and Terri Anne closer together.
- Pushes Cooper to the point of wanting to rip Don apart.
- Shows Adam intervening because Victoria still needs Don's physical strength.
- Has Cooper call Victoria about Mike and Terri Anne's relationship.
- Establishes another meeting for Victoria, Adam, and Cooper at The Well.
- Shows Adam complaining about returning to the gay bar.
- Further fractures the alliance among Adam, Cooper, and Don.
- Makes Mike and Terri Anne's relationship a direct target of the conspiracy.
