# Chapter Nineteen — Victoria's Meeting at The Well

## 1. Friday night arrives

- Five days have passed since Don followed Scout and Patti Anne on Sunday.
- On Friday night, Adam and Cooper go to **The Well** for their secret meeting with Victoria Key.
- Don remains excluded from the meeting because Adam and Cooper no longer trust his judgment.
- The gathering is intended to assess the threat to Victoria's hidden plan.

## 2. Adam remains uncomfortable

- Adam dislikes being at The Well because it is a gay bar and he believes men there frequently try to pick him up.
- Cooper reminds him that Victoria chose the location and expects them to obey.
- Adam agrees to stay but remains visibly tense and impatient.
- Cooper watches the room while they wait for Victoria.

## 3. A man approaches Adam

- Before Victoria arrives, a man at the bar notices Adam and asks him out.
- Adam makes clear that he is not interested.
- The man accepts the rejection and moves away.
- Cooper is amused that Adam's complaint about the bar has immediately come true.
- Adam becomes even more irritated about Victoria's choice of meeting place.

## 4. The man is Bryan's investigator

- The man who approached Adam is actually one of **Bryan's investigators** working undercover inside The Well.
- Asking Adam out gives the investigator a believable reason to approach him closely and confirm his identity.
- After Adam rejects him, the investigator withdraws without drawing attention to his real purpose.
- Adam and Cooper believe the encounter was an ordinary attempt at flirtation.
- The investigator remains inside the bar and continues observing them from a safe distance.

## 5. Victoria arrives

- Victoria enters The Well and joins Adam and Cooper.
- At twenty-eight, she is Mike's second-youngest sister.
- She takes control of the conversation immediately.
- Her manner makes clear that Adam and Cooper answer to her in connection with the hidden plan.

## 6. Victoria condemns Don

- Cooper gives Victoria a detailed account of Don following Scout and Patti Anne.
- He explains that Terri Anne described Don's blue jacket and blue hat to Mike.
- He also reports that Mike assigned four Key Corporation guards to Scout's apartment and is using his resources to identify the follower.
- Victoria calls Don a **foolish idiot** for drawing attention to himself and risking the entire operation.

## 7. Victoria wants Don removed

- Victoria tells Adam and Cooper that she would like to cut Don out of her plans.
- She no longer trusts him to follow instructions or protect confidential information.
- Don's decision to follow Scout without permission has made him a direct threat to the conspiracy.
- Adam agrees that his cousin is becoming increasingly difficult to control.

## 8. Victoria still needs Don's strength

- Despite wanting Don removed, Victoria admits that she still needs his strong muscles.
- Don's physical strength remains useful for work that Adam, Cooper, and Victoria cannot or do not want to perform themselves.
- Victoria resents being dependent on someone she considers unreliable.
- Cooper warns that continuing to use Don will give him more knowledge and leverage over them.

## 9. Adam and Cooper assess the risk

- Adam explains that Don was ordered to cut up the blue jacket and hat and scatter the pieces throughout Hamilton.
- None of them can be certain that Don followed those instructions correctly.
- Cooper worries that Don may keep a piece of the clothing or other evidence as protection against them.
- Victoria realizes that excluding him from the meeting may make him even more resentful.

## 10. Victoria tightens control

- Victoria decides that Don will remain part of the plan only in a limited physical role.
- He will not be invited to strategy meetings or trusted with information beyond what he needs for a particular task.
- Adam and Cooper will monitor him closely.
- Victoria warns them that another mistake will force her to remove Don completely, regardless of how useful his strength may be.

## 11. The hidden plan remains secret

- The three conspirators discuss the danger surrounding Victoria's plan without revealing its complete purpose to the reader.
- Their concern makes clear that the plan involves Mike, Key Corporation, or both.
- Cooper's position as Mike's driver remains essential because it gives them access to Mike's movements and private conversations.
- Adam's removal from the Blue Velvet Room has damaged the plan, making Cooper's access even more valuable.
- From elsewhere in the bar, Bryan's investigator observes that Adam and Cooper are meeting with Victoria Key.
- Even if he cannot hear every word, the meeting itself reveals a connection that Bryan did not previously know existed.

## 12. Closing hook

- Victoria ends the meeting with new restrictions on Don and renewed instructions for Adam and Cooper.
- Adam is relieved to leave The Well, while Cooper remains concerned about what Don may do while excluded.
- None of the three realizes that Bryan's undercover investigator has identified them together.
- The investigator prepares to report the meeting to Bryan.
- The chapter ends on Friday night, only hours before Mike and Scout's first date at Mandarin Restaurant on Saturday.

## Chapter Purpose

This chapter:

- Brings Victoria, Adam, and Cooper together at The Well on Friday night.
- Shows a man asking Adam out before the meeting.
- Reveals that the man is one of Bryan's investigators working undercover.
- Allows the investigator to identify Adam, Cooper, and Victoria together.
- Has Victoria call Don a foolish idiot.
- Reveals that Victoria wants to remove Don from her plans.
- Explains that she still needs Don because of his physical strength.
- Limits Don's future access to the conspiracy's strategy.
- Keeps Victoria's complete plan hidden.
- Leads directly into Mike and Scout's Saturday date at Mandarin Restaurant.
