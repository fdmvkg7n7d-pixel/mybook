# Chapter Thirty-Six — Telling Ashlee

## 1. Ashlee makes the weekly call

- Ashlee calls Mike from Los Angeles for their regular weekly conversation.
- Mike is working late at **Key Corporation** when he answers from his office.
- Their familiar routine gives Mike an opportunity to share an important change in his personal life.
- Ashlee senses that he has news before he begins explaining it.

## 2. Mike reveals his new relationship

- Mike tells Ashlee that he has started a new relationship.
- He identifies the woman as **Terri Anne Williams**.
- Mike explains that they met through the Blue Velvet Room but does not disclose private details about Terri Anne's work, history, or Bryan's report.
- He tells Ashlee that the relationship has become serious and that they are falling in love.

## 3. Ashlee asks Terri Anne's age

- Ashlee asks how old Terri Anne is.
- Mike tells her that Terri Anne is **eighteen years old**.
- Ashlee immediately recognizes the twenty-year age difference between Terri Anne and her thirty-eight-year-old brother.
- Mike acknowledges the age difference rather than pretending it is unimportant.

## 4. Mike explains how the relationship began

- Mike tells Ashlee that Terri Anne initiated both of their first two dates.
- He explains that he repeatedly confirmed her choices and has tried not to use his wealth or authority to pressure her.
- Mike also says that he reported the relationship under Key Corporation's workplace policy.
- Ashlee listens carefully because she knows how much power Mike's position gives him.

## 5. Mike tells Ashlee about Patti Anne

- Mike tells Ashlee that Terri Anne has an **eight-month-old daughter named Patti Anne**.
- He explains that Patti Anne is the most important person in Terri Anne's life.
- Mike speaks warmly about the baby and says that Patti Anne immediately liked him.
- He tells Ashlee that protecting Patti Anne has become personally important to him.

## 6. Ashlee asks about Mike's role

- Ashlee asks whether Mike understands that Patti Anne already has a mother who makes decisions for her.
- Mike says that he does not intend to replace Terri Anne's authority or use protection to take control.
- He wants to support both mother and daughter while allowing the relationship to develop naturally.
- Ashlee appreciates that Mike recognizes the distinction between helping and controlling.

## 7. Mike describes Terri Anne

- Mike tells Ashlee that Terri Anne is loving, compassionate, funny, and fiercely protective of Patti Anne.
- He mentions her homemade blueberry waffles and the warmth she brought into the mansion's industrial kitchen.
- Mike's happiness is obvious in the way he speaks about her.
- Ashlee realizes that this relationship matters to him more than his past relationships did.

## 8. The invitation to move in

- Mike tells Ashlee that he invited Terri Anne and Patti Anne to move into the Carlisle mansion.
- He explains that Terri Anne accepted and chose to share his bedroom, while Patti Anne will have a safe space arranged nearby.
- Mike emphasizes that he offered Terri Anne a separate room and left the choice with her.
- Ashlee understands that she may soon meet both Terri Anne and Patti Anne as part of Mike's household.

## 9. Ashlee processes the news

- Ashlee is surprised by Terri Anne's age, the baby, and the speed of the relationship.
- She asks direct questions because she cares about Mike and wants Terri Anne to be treated fairly.
- Mike answers honestly without becoming defensive.
- The conversation reflects the trust that has always existed between the siblings.

## 10. The weekly call ends

- Mike tells Ashlee that he hopes she will meet Terri Anne and Patti Anne soon.
- Ashlee understands that the new relationship may change Mike's future and the future of Key Corporation.
- Mike does not reduce Terri Anne to a solution for their mother's will.
- After the call, Mike remains at Key Corporation to finish his late-night work.

## 11. Closing beat

- Mike ends the weekly call after telling Ashlee that he hopes she will meet Terri Anne and Patti Anne soon.
- He remains at Key Corporation to finish his late-night work.
- Ashlee considers the two new people who may become part of her family.
- The conversation about the call with Terri Anne will take place when she brings Patti Anne to Key Corporation for the audition.

## Chapter Purpose

This chapter:

- Continues Mike and Ashlee's weekly-call tradition while Mike works late at Key Corporation.
- Has Mike reveal his new relationship with Terri Anne.
- Establishes that Terri Anne is eighteen and Mike is thirty-eight and a half.
- Tells Ashlee about eight-month-old Patti Anne.
- Shows Mike acknowledging the relationship's age and power differences.
- Reveals that Terri Anne and Patti Anne plan to move into the mansion.
- Prepares for Mike to tell Terri Anne about the call when she visits Key Corporation for her audition.
- Prepares for Ashlee to meet Terri Anne and Patti Anne in the future.
