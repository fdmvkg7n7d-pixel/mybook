# Chapter Sixty-Four — Kneeling on the Floor

## 1. Later at the Whistler mansion

- The chapter begins later on Wednesday at the Whistler mansion.
- Mike and Terri Anne have finished discussing Christmas, travelling around Japan, and Terri Anne's decision to make both movies after the legal waiting period.
- The mansion has become quiet around them.

## 2. Patti Anne remains asleep

- Patti Anne has fallen asleep on Mike's right shoulder for the first time.
- Mike carries her carefully and makes certain she is resting safely.
- He remains moved by the trust Patti Anne showed by falling asleep against him.

## 3. Mike returns to their Whistler master bedroom

- After checking that Patti Anne is safe, Mike returns to the master bedroom he and Terri Anne share at the Whistler mansion.
- Their master bedroom is the room where Terri Anne's private master role with Mike applies.
- The permission and boundaries they previously established apply inside this room.

## 4. Terri Anne repeats her earlier pose

- Terri Anne is on her knees in their Whistler master bedroom.
- Both of her palms are turned upward toward the ceiling.
- Her hair is arranged in French braids.
- She is wearing a pink thong and ivory thigh-high stockings.
- She has carefully recreated the same pose and clothing she used when she first introduced the private bedroom role at the Carlisle mansion.

## 5. The baby monitor

- Patti Anne's baby monitor rests on top of the dresser.
- Terri Anne has kept the monitor close enough to hear if her daughter needs her.
- Mike notices it immediately and understands that Patti Anne remains part of Terri Anne's awareness.
- The monitor reassures them that they can respond promptly if Patti Anne wakes or becomes upset.

## 6. Terri Anne repeats the bedroom permission

- When Mike walks into the master bedroom, Terri Anne looks at him from her kneeling position.
- She tells him, **“You are my master while we are in this bedroom.”**
- Her words define a role that applies specifically inside their Whistler master bedroom rather than changing their relationship everywhere else.
- The active baby monitor remains on the dresser so Patti Anne's needs can interrupt the scene at any time.

## 7. Mike asks whether Terri Anne is certain

- Mike asks Terri Anne whether she is sure that she wants him to be her master while they are inside their Whistler master bedroom.
- Terri Anne confirms that she is certain about the private role.
- Mike takes her answer seriously rather than assuming that her pose or clothing speaks for her.
- Terri Anne may pause or end the role whenever she chooses.

## 8. Mike takes Terri Anne's hand

- After Terri Anne confirms that she is certain, Mike quickly gets undressed.
- He approaches Terri Anne while she remains kneeling inside their Whistler master bedroom.
- Mike takes Terri Anne's left hand.
- Within the private role Terri Anne authorized for their master bedroom, Mike orders her, **“Stand up.”**
- The active baby monitor remains on the dresser so Patti Anne's needs can interrupt them at any time.

## 9. Mike leads Terri Anne to their bed

- Terri Anne stands in response to Mike's order.
- Still holding her left hand, Mike leads her to their bed.
- They have sex with Mike positioned behind Terri Anne.
- The encounter remains within the private role and permission Terri Anne established for their master bedroom.
- Patti Anne's baby monitor remains active throughout.

## 10. They talk together afterward

- Afterward, Mike and Terri Anne lie down together on their bed.
- The private role gives way to their ordinary relationship as a couple.
- They relax and talk to each other about nothing in particular.
- Neither of them needs the conversation to have a serious purpose.
- The quiet time allows them to enjoy simply being together.

## 11. Patti Anne begins crying

- Patti Anne suddenly begins crying.
- Her cries come through the active baby monitor on the dresser.
- Mike and Terri Anne immediately stop their casual conversation and turn their attention toward the monitor.
- Patti Anne's needs bring their private time to an end.

## 12. Closing beat

- Mike and Terri Anne lie beside each other for only a moment after hearing Patti Anne.
- Both understand that their daughter needs one of them.
- The chapter ends with Patti Anne crying and her parents preparing to respond.
