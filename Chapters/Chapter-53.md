# Chapter Fifty-Three — Geoffrey Asks for Mike's Blessing

## 1. Ashlee and Kassandra prepare dinner

- Ashlee and Kassandra decide to make dinner for everyone at the Los Angeles mansion.
- Ashlee uses her special power chair to move comfortably around the accessible kitchen.
- Kassandra follows Ashlee's directions and enjoys spending ordinary time with her after eighteen months apart.
- Preparing dinner gives the two friends another opportunity to reconnect away from the studio.

## 2. Geoffrey asks to speak with Mike

- While Ashlee and Kassandra are busy in the kitchen, Geoffrey asks Mike whether they can speak privately.
- Mike recognizes that Geoffrey is nervous but agrees to hear him out.
- They move far enough from the kitchen that Ashlee will not accidentally hear the conversation.
- Geoffrey does not want to ruin the surprise before he is ready to speak to her.

## 3. Geoffrey talks about loving Ashlee

- Geoffrey tells Mike that he loves Ashlee deeply.
- He says Victoria, Jack, and Ric have not changed his commitment to her.
- The break-in, destroyed wheelchair, and threats have made him more certain that he wants to build a life with Ashlee.
- Geoffrey does not describe himself as rescuing her; he wants a partnership with her.

## 4. Geoffrey asks for Mike's blessing

- Geoffrey tells Mike that he wants to propose to Ashlee.
- He asks Mike for his blessing rather than asking for ownership or control over Ashlee's choice.
- Geoffrey understands that only Ashlee can decide whether she wants to marry him.
- He asks because Mike is important to Ashlee and has protected her throughout her life.

## 5. Mike clarifies that the decision belongs to Ashlee

- Mike tells Geoffrey that Ashlee does not require her brother's permission to marry anyone.
- He says Ashlee is an adult woman capable of making her own decision.
- Mike appreciates that Geoffrey asked for a blessing rather than treating Ashlee as someone to be given away.
- He makes clear that Ashlee's answer will be the only answer that determines whether they become engaged.

## 6. Mike asks about Geoffrey's intentions

- Mike asks whether Geoffrey is prepared to stand beside Ashlee when family pressure becomes difficult.
- He asks whether Geoffrey respects Ashlee's education in UCLA's music program, independence, career decisions, and desire for a family.
- Mike also asks whether Geoffrey understands that Ashlee's wheelchair and support needs are parts of their shared practical life, not reasons to control her.
- Geoffrey answers that he loves the whole person Ashlee is.

## 7. Geoffrey discusses his conservative family

- Geoffrey acknowledges that his family is very conservative.
- He does not promise that every relative will immediately accept his relationship with a disabled transgender woman.
- He promises that he will not allow his family's beliefs to dictate Ashlee's worth or determine their future.
- Mike listens carefully because he does not want Ashlee exposed to another campaign of family rejection.

## 8. Mike considers what he has witnessed

- Mike remembers Geoffrey standing beside Ashlee during Jack and Ric's harassment.
- He remembers Geoffrey protecting and supporting her during Victoria's invasion of the mansion.
- Geoffrey stayed with Ashlee when her wheelchair and phones were destroyed rather than chasing Victoria.
- Mike recognizes that Geoffrey's actions have already demonstrated commitment.

## 9. Mike gives his blessing

- Mike tells Geoffrey that he has his blessing to propose.
- He repeats that the blessing is support, not permission on Ashlee's behalf.
- Mike says he will welcome Geoffrey into the family if Ashlee accepts the proposal.
- Geoffrey thanks Mike and promises that he will ask Ashlee directly when the time is right.

## 10. They keep the proposal private

- Geoffrey asks Mike not to tell Ashlee before he proposes.
- Mike agrees to keep the conversation private.
- Neither man tells Kassandra because she is in the kitchen with Ashlee and might unintentionally reveal the surprise.
- They return to the others without behaving as though a major decision has just been made.

## 11. Dinner is ready

- Ashlee and Kassandra finish preparing dinner and call Mike and Geoffrey to join them.
- Ashlee notices that Geoffrey still seems slightly nervous but assumes the morning's events are responsible.
- Kassandra enjoys seeing Ashlee and Geoffrey together after hearing so much about their relationship.
- Mike quietly observes the couple while protecting Geoffrey's secret.

## 12. Closing beat

- Geoffrey now has Mike's blessing but still must ask the only person who can accept his proposal: Ashlee.
- Ashlee and Kassandra serve the dinner they prepared together.
- Mike will soon return to Toronto, leaving guards around the mansion as promised.
- The chapter ends with Geoffrey sharing dinner beside Ashlee while privately considering how and when to propose.

## Future result of Geoffrey's plan

- Geoffrey's conservative family later orders him to choose between the family and Ashlee.
- Geoffrey chooses Ashlee.
- Ashlee calls Mike, and Mike sends one of Key Corporation's airplanes for Ashlee and Geoffrey.
- They travel to New York City for Christmas.
- Geoffrey proposes on Christmas Day.
- Ashlee says yes.

## Chapter Purpose

This chapter:

- Shows Ashlee and Kassandra preparing dinner together.
- Has Geoffrey privately ask Mike for his blessing to propose to Ashlee.
- Preserves Ashlee's right to make her own marriage decision.
- Explores Geoffrey's commitment despite hostility from both families.
- Has Mike evaluate Geoffrey by his actions during the recent attacks.
- Gives Geoffrey Mike's blessing.
- Keeps the planned proposal secret from Ashlee and Kassandra.
