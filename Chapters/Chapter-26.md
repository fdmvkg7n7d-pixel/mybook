# Chapter Twenty-Six — Two Telephone Calls

## 1. Scout worries about the workplace consequences

- After speaking to Patti Anne about the date, Scout begins worrying about what her relationship with Mike could mean at the Blue Velvet Room.
- Mike owns the club, while Charlene is its temporary manager and Marco is its assistant manager.
- Scout fears that spending the night at Mike's mansion may affect how they see her or how she is treated at work.
- She decides to make two separate telephone calls.

## 2. Scout calls Charlene

- Scout first calls Charlene privately.
- She continues using her stage name and does not reveal that Mike now knows her as Terri Anne Williams.
- Scout sounds frightened and hesitant as she explains that she needs to discuss something personal involving Mike.
- Charlene listens without rushing or judging her.

## 3. Scout tells Charlene about the date

- Scout tells Charlene that she invited Mike on a simple date at Mandarin Restaurant.
- She explains that the date continued at Mike's Carlisle mansion.
- Scout admits that she chose to spend the night with him.
- She also tells Charlene about the mansion tour and the blueberry-waffle breakfast the following morning.
- Scout is afraid Charlene may believe she crossed a workplace boundary or used her relationship with Mike to gain an advantage.

## 4. Charlene says Scout did nothing wrong

- Charlene tells Scout that she did not do anything wrong merely by going on a consensual date with Mike.
- She explains that Mike's ownership creates a workplace conflict that management must handle, but it does not make Scout guilty of misconduct.
- Charlene reassures her that she will not be punished, shamed, or given special treatment because of the date.

## 5. Charlene asks about pressure

- Charlene asks Scout an important question: did Mike pressure her to go on the date or accompany him to his mansion?
- Her tone is protective rather than accusatory.
- Charlene wants to make certain that Mike did not use his ownership, wealth, protection, or authority to obtain Scout's agreement.
- She makes clear that Scout can answer honestly without risking her job.

## 6. Scout explains that the date was her idea

- Scout tells Charlene that Mike did not pressure her at all.
- She explains that inviting Mike to Mandarin Restaurant was her idea.
- She also says that she asked Mike to take her back to his home.
- Mike checked whether she was certain, and she confirmed that she was.
- Charlene accepts Scout's account and documents that the date was initiated freely.

## 7. Charlene reassures Scout

- Charlene tells Scout that workplace safeguards will remain in place.
- Mike will not make decisions about Scout's schedule, pay, discipline, or performance.
- Charlene and Marco will continue handling club operations.
- Scout is free to continue, pause, or end the relationship without workplace retaliation.
- The reassurance helps reduce Scout's fear that her private choice will cost her employment.

## 8. Scout makes a second call

- After ending the call with Charlene, Scout telephones Marco separately.
- She does not repeat everything she told Charlene over the telephone.
- Instead, she asks Marco whether he will meet her somewhere outside the Blue Velvet Room.
- Marco can hear that the request matters to her.

## 9. Scout suggests Tim Hortons

- Scout asks Marco to meet her at **Tim Hortons**.
- She chooses a familiar public place where they can speak without being overheard by club employees.
- Meeting outside the Blue Velvet Room also separates the conversation from their manager-and-employee roles.
- Marco agrees to meet her.

## 10. Marco wonders what Scout needs

- Marco is concerned because Scout rarely asks to meet him away from work.
- He wonders whether she wants to discuss Mike, the former blackmail scheme, or her future at the club.
- He does not pressure her to explain over the telephone.
- Marco promises to listen when they meet at Tim Hortons.

## 11. Charlene checks the reporting system

- After speaking with Scout, Charlene opens Key Corporation's secure workplace-compliance system.
- She checks whether Mike formally reported his romantic relationship with Scout as required by company policy.
- Charlene finds the disclosure in the system.
- The record shows that Mike made the report and that Olivia entered the relationship into the confidential file.
- Charlene is reassured that Mike did not try to conceal the relationship or exempt himself from the policy.
- Scout does not yet know that both her conversation with Charlene and Mike's independent report support the same account: she freely initiated the date.

## 12. Closing beat

- Scout ends the second call knowing that she has honestly answered Charlene's questions.
- She feels less afraid about the workplace consequences of her relationship with Mike.
- Charlene knows that Olivia has properly recorded Mike's disclosure.
- Scout's upcoming conversation with Marco remains more personal and uncertain.
- The chapter ends with Scout preparing to bring Patti Anne to meet Marco outside the Blue Velvet Room.

## Chapter Purpose

This chapter:

- Gives Scout two separate calls with Charlene and Marco.
- Allows Scout to tell Charlene about the date, night, and breakfast at Mike's mansion.
- Shows Scout's fear that she may have done something wrong.
- Has Charlene ask whether Mike pressured Scout.
- Establishes that Scout initiated the date and freely chose to visit Mike's home.
- Reassures Scout that her job will remain protected.
- Shows Charlene confirming that Mike reported the relationship and Olivia entered it into the secure system.
- Arranges a private meeting with Marco at Tim Hortons.
