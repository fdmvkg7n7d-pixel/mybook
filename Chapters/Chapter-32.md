# Chapter Thirty-Two — The Reconstructed Jacket and Hat

## 1. Bryan calls Mike

- Bryan calls Mike with an urgent update about Don.
- One of Bryan's investigative teams has been secretly following Don around Hamilton.
- Don does not realize that the investigators are watching him.
- His attempt to destroy evidence has instead allowed Bryan's team to document his movements.

## 2. Don scatters the pieces

- The investigators watch Don travel to different locations around Hamilton.
- At each location, he disposes of cut-up pieces from his **blue jacket and blue hat**.
- The team does not confront him or reveal the surveillance.
- They record the places, times, and order in which Don leaves the pieces.

## 3. The investigators recover the evidence

- After Don moves away from each location, investigators discreetly recover the discarded material.
- They preserve the jacket and hat pieces separately and document where each one was found.
- Because the team witnessed Don disposing of them, the evidence is directly connected to his actions.
- Don believes the pieces are scattered beyond recovery.

## 4. The jacket and hat are reconstructed

- Bryan's investigators lay out all the recovered pieces.
- They begin fitting the material together like two large puzzles.
- Enough pieces are recovered to reconstruct most of the blue jacket and blue hat.
- Both items match the clothing Terri Anne described when Don followed her and Patti Anne near the Y.

## 5. Bryan explains the significance

- Bryan tells Mike that the investigators did more than find discarded clothing.
- They personally observed Don scattering the pieces throughout Hamilton.
- The surveillance demonstrates that Don deliberately attempted to destroy and conceal recognizable evidence.
- The reconstructed clothing supports Terri Anne's description and strengthens the case connecting Don to the pursuit.

## 6. Mike suspects Cooper warned Don

- Mike remembers that Cooper heard Terri Anne describe the follower's blue jacket and blue hat.
- Cooper then secretly met Adam and Victoria at The Well.
- Mike suspects Cooper passed the description to Adam and Don and warned them that the clothing could identify Don.
- Don's decision to cut up both items suggests that someone told him exactly what Terri Anne had reported.

## 7. Bryan continues following Don

- Bryan explains that his investigators have not yet confronted Don.
- They continue following him to learn where he goes and whom he contacts.
- Bryan hopes Don will lead the team to Adam, Cooper, Victoria, or evidence of their hidden plan.
- Don's belief that he successfully disposed of the clothing makes him less likely to realize he is under surveillance.

## 8. Mike protects Terri Anne and Patti Anne

- Mike orders the four Key Corporation guards assigned to Terri Anne's apartment to remain in place.
- He worries that Don may approach her again if Adam or Cooper realizes the evidence was recovered.
- Mike does not immediately tell Terri Anne about the surveillance because he does not want to frighten her or compromise Bryan's work.
- Patti Anne's safety remains his immediate priority.

## 9. Bryan preserves the reconstructed clothing

- Bryan's team photographs the reconstructed jacket and hat.
- Every recovered piece is placed into secure evidence storage.
- The investigators preserve their surveillance records showing Don at each disposal location.
- Bryan prepares a written report for Mike and Olivia.

## 10. Closing hook

- Mike studies the photographs of the reconstructed blue jacket and blue hat.
- Don believes scattering the pieces throughout Hamilton erased the evidence.
- Adam and Cooper believe the same thing and assume their instructions protected the conspiracy.
- None of the three men realizes Bryan's investigators watched the entire attempt and recovered what Don discarded.
- The chapter ends with Don still under surveillance and unknowingly leading Bryan closer to the conspiracy.

## Chapter Purpose

This chapter:

- Has Bryan call Mike with an investigative breakthrough.
- Reveals that investigators secretly followed Don around Hamilton.
- Shows Don scattering pieces of both his blue jacket and blue hat.
- Has investigators recover the pieces after watching Don discard them.
- Reconstructs both items and connects them to Terri Anne's description.
- Strengthens Mike's suspicion that Cooper warned Don.
- Keeps Don under surveillance so he may lead investigators to the wider conspiracy.
