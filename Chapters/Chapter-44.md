# Chapter Forty-Four — Mike Arrives with Twenty Guards

## 1. The sitting room becomes a resting place

- After finishing their blueberry-pancake breakfast, Ashlee and Geoffrey do not return to their damaged bedroom to sleep.
- They settle together in the mansion's sitting room instead.
- Ashlee wears only the orange leggings she chose after her shower.
- Emotional exhaustion finally causes both Ashlee and Geoffrey to fall asleep.

## 2. Ashlee and Geoffrey sleep together

- Ashlee rests safely with the support she needs while Geoffrey remains close beside her.
- Their decision to sleep in the sitting room reflects how unsafe Victoria made their bedroom feel.
- Even asleep, they remain close after refusing to let Victoria divide them.
- Ashlee's special power chair remains nearby and available when she wakes.

## 3. Daniela remains in the room

- Daniela stays in the sitting room while the couple sleeps.
- Ashlee and Geoffrey continue to regard her as a trusted personal support worker.
- Neither knows that Daniela helped Victoria.
- Daniela behaves as though she is simply remaining available in case Ashlee needs assistance.

## 4. Daniela reads from her iPad

- Daniela sits quietly and reads a book on her iPad.
- The ordinary activity makes the sitting room appear calm despite the violence that occurred earlier.
- From time to time, she looks up at Ashlee and Geoffrey.
- Her private knowledge of Victoria's attack remains hidden behind her composed behaviour.

## 5. Mike reaches the mansion

- Mike's Key Corporation airplane lands in Los Angeles, and he travels directly to Ashlee and Geoffrey's mansion.
- He does not stop at Key Porn Studios Los Angeles before checking on his sister.
- Ashlee's call has made the danger immediate and personal.
- Mike arrives determined to see Ashlee, assess the damage, and respond to Victoria's confession.

## 6. Twenty guards surround Mike

- Mike arrives with **twenty security guards** positioned around him.
- The large protective detail reflects Victoria's admission that she hired another professional assassin.
- Some guards watch the approaches to the mansion while others remain close to Mike.
- Mike refuses to let the security presence delay him from entering and reaching Ashlee.

## 7. The guards secure the property

- The security team begins checking the exterior and entrances without disturbing evidence from Victoria's break-in.
- They look for immediate threats and prevent unauthorized people from approaching Mike or the household.
- Mike instructs them to protect Ashlee and Geoffrey as well as him.
- Daniela hears the arrival and recognizes that the mansion is rapidly becoming more secure.

## 8. Daniela sees Mike enter

- Daniela looks up from her iPad when Mike enters the mansion.
- She understands that Ashlee has already told him about Victoria's attack and assassin confession.
- Daniela keeps her connection to Victoria hidden and presents herself as the support worker who arrived to help.
- Mike has no immediate reason to suspect her.

## 9. Mike wakes Ashlee and Geoffrey

- Mike enters the sitting room and finds Ashlee and Geoffrey asleep together.
- He sees the exhaustion left by the attack but gently wakes them because he wants them to know that he has arrived.
- Ashlee and Geoffrey wake to find Mike safely inside their mansion with twenty guards protecting the property.
- Mike notices Ashlee's special power chair nearby and understands that Geoffrey found another mobility option for her.

## 10. Mike and Ashlee express their love

- Mike moves close to Ashlee and reassures her that he is there.
- He tells Ashlee that he loves her so much.
- Ashlee tells Mike that she loves him so much, too.
- The exchange reassures them both after Ashlee learned that she was injured by a bullet intended to kill her brother.
- Geoffrey gives the siblings space for the emotional reunion.

## 11. Mike says the police are coming

- Mike tells Ashlee, Geoffrey, and Daniela that the police are on their way to investigate Victoria Key's break-in.
- He explains that the officers will need to document the bedroom, the destroyed iPhones, the damaged wheelchair, and the broken piano pedals.
- The announcement reassures Ashlee and Geoffrey that the attack is being treated seriously.
- Daniela now knows that investigators will soon examine the mansion where she secretly helped Victoria.

## 12. Ashlee chooses her blue denim dress

- Because Ashlee is still wearing only her orange leggings, Mike gently suggests that she put something on top before the police arrive.
- He leaves the final clothing decision entirely to Ashlee.
- Ashlee asks Daniela to get her blue denim dress.
- She tells Daniela that she wants to wear the dress without a bra.
- Daniela goes to retrieve exactly what Ashlee requested.

## 13. Mike speaks quietly with Daniela

- Daniela quietly tells Mike that she arrived around six o'clock, heard Ashlee crying, and ran to the bedroom.
- She explains that she provided her cell phone after learning Victoria destroyed both iPhones.
- Daniela omits the fact that she gave Victoria the mansion's security code.
- Mike thanks her for staying with Ashlee and Geoffrey, unknowingly placing trust in someone connected to the attack.

## 14. Mike sees the damage

- Mike is shown Ashlee's destroyed regular wheelchair and the broken adapted pedals on her grand piano.
- The damage makes Victoria's cruelty tangible in a way that the telephone call could not.
- Mike recognizes that Victoria attacked Ashlee's mobility, music, privacy, and sense of safety.
- He orders the guards not to disturb anything that could serve as evidence.

## 15. Closing hook

- Twenty guards now surround Mike and protect the mansion, but the person who helped Victoria is already inside with the family.
- Ashlee prepares to put on her blue denim dress while Geoffrey stays close to her.
- Daniela knows that police investigators are approaching and carefully maintains her calm appearance.
- The chapter ends with Mike only a short distance from Daniela, unaware that she betrayed Ashlee.
