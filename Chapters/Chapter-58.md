# Chapter Fifty-Eight — Patti Anne in the Kitchen

## 1. Olivia responds to Patti Anne's crying

- While Mike and Terri Anne are in the adjoining bathroom of the empty bedroom, Patti Anne begins crying in her own bedroom.
- Olivia enters Patti Anne's bedroom and comforts her.
- She changes Patti Anne's little diaper before taking her downstairs.
- She does not enter the empty bedroom or disturb Mike and Terri Anne.
- Olivia carries Patti Anne downstairs to one of the mansion's kitchens.
- Mei is still at work, and Tsumugi is still at school.

## 2. Olivia prepares Patti Anne's afternoon snack

- In the kitchen, Olivia prepares Patti Anne's familiar afternoon snack.
- She uses a baby-appropriate cookie and softens it with Terri Anne's breast milk.
- Patti Anne has eaten the same cookie-and-milk preparation before.
- Olivia supervises Patti Anne closely while feeding her.
- Later, Mei returns from work and Tsumugi returns from school, finding Olivia and Patti Anne together in the kitchen.

## 3. Terri Anne enters the kitchen

- Terri Anne walks into the kitchen and finds Patti Anne there with Olivia, Mei, and Tsumugi.
- She knows Olivia brought Patti Anne downstairs after responding to her cries.
- The scene is calm and domestic, and all three are paying attention to Patti Anne.
- Terri Anne pauses at the doorway and smiles at the small group gathered around her daughter.

## 4. Olivia stays close to Patti Anne

- Olivia remains close enough to respond if Patti Anne crawls toward anything unsafe.
- She speaks gently and allows Patti Anne to move at her own pace.
- Patti Anne recognizes Olivia's voice and deep blue eyes from earlier visits.
- Olivia is pleased that Patti Anne is becoming more comfortable around her.

## 5. Patti Anne studies Mei's tattoos

- Mei sits where Patti Anne can see the different colours covering her tattooed body.
- Patti Anne remains fascinated by the shapes and colours.
- As one of Patti Anne's trusted caregivers in the mansion, Mei does not need to ask Terri Anne's permission each time she picks Patti Anne up or helps her.
- Mei still pays attention to Patti Anne's reactions and lets her decide whether to crawl closer.

## 6. Tsumugi applies what she learned

- Tsumugi remembers the answers Terri Anne gave her about interacting with Patti Anne.
- She stays calm, speaks softly, and lets Patti Anne choose the distance between them.
- Tsumugi does not lift Patti Anne without asking.
- Terri Anne notices that Tsumugi has remembered the conversation and put it into practice.

## 7. Tsumugi describes the kitchen scene

- Tsumugi says Patti Anne looks like a tiny guest of honour holding a meeting without needing any words.
- Olivia and Mei understand that the metaphor reflects how everyone has gathered around the baby.
- Terri Anne answers Tsumugi as she would any other teenager rather than simplifying her response.
- Mei quietly appreciates the respect Terri Anne continues to show her sister.

## 8. Terri Anne greets Patti Anne

- Terri Anne speaks to Patti Anne as she enters the kitchen.
- Patti Anne recognizes her mother's voice and turns toward her.
- Terri Anne comes closer but allows Patti Anne to decide whether to crawl to her or remain with the others.
- The moment shows that Patti Anne can feel secure with trusted people while still seeking her mother.

## 9. Olivia explains what they were doing

- Terri Anne tells Olivia that she and Mike could hear Olivia enter Patti Anne's bedroom through the baby monitor.
- She says they also heard Olivia comfort Patti Anne and change her little diaper.
- Olivia explains that she then brought Patti Anne downstairs and fed her the familiar afternoon cookie softened with Terri Anne's breast milk.
- Mei and Tsumugi joined them after returning from work and school.
- Terri Anne thanks Olivia for responding to Patti Anne without disturbing her and Mike.
- She also thanks Mei and Tsumugi for spending time with Patti Anne.

## 10. The household feels more settled

- The kitchen gathering shows how the mansion has changed since Terri Anne and Patti Anne began moving in.
- Olivia, Mei, and Tsumugi no longer feel like distant visitors.
- Patti Anne is learning their voices, faces, and ways of interacting with her.
- Terri Anne feels less alone as a mother without losing her place at the centre of Patti Anne's care.

## 11. Terri Anne does not know what happened at the office

- Terri Anne knows Mike visited the investigator's office before coming home.
- She does not know what happened there because Mike had no time to tell her everything before they went into the empty bedroom.
- She does not yet know the full details of Cooper and Don's confessions, the forty-five-million-dollar arrangement, or the order for the three men to leave Ontario.
- Terri Anne expects Mike to explain everything when they have time to talk.
- For now, she focuses on Patti Anne and the trusted people gathered in the kitchen.

## 12. Olivia is staying at the mansion

- Olivia is staying at Mike and Terri Anne's Carlisle mansion rather than merely visiting for the afternoon.
- Her presence allows her to continue helping the household while the danger involving Victoria remains unresolved.
- Terri Anne appreciates having someone she trusts nearby for both ordinary family moments and emergencies.
- Olivia feels increasingly included in the shared household.

## 13. Terri Anne asks Mei about a tattoo

- While looking at the colourful artwork covering Mei's body, Terri Anne asks whether Mei would like to give her a tattoo.
- Mei tells Terri Anne yes and is pleased that Terri Anne trusts her as a tattoo artist.
- They agree to discuss the design, meaning, size, and placement before Mei begins any work.
- Patti Anne continues looking at Mei's colours without understanding that her mother is considering artwork of her own.

## 14. Charlene calls Terri Anne

- Terri Anne receives a telephone call from Charlene.
- Charlene asks whether Terri Anne wants to pick up another shift at the Blue Velvet Room.
- The offer would let Terri Anne return to her work as Scout while Charlene continues overseeing the club.
- Terri Anne considers the security situation and Patti Anne's care before answering.

## 15. Olivia says Victoria's plan is almost over

- Olivia tells Terri Anne that she can pick up the shift.
- She explains that Victoria's plan is almost over because Mike and the investigators have made major progress.
- Olivia does not yet give Terri Anne the full account of Cooper and Don's confessions or Mike's payment arrangement.
- Her reassurance lets Terri Anne know that returning to work no longer carries the same immediate danger.

## 16. Mei offers to care for Patti Anne

- Mei tells Terri Anne that they want to take care of Patti Anne while she works at the Blue Velvet Room.
- Olivia and Mei will remain the responsible adults caring for Patti Anne, with Tsumugi spending time with them after school.
- Mei's offer allows Terri Anne to consider the shift without worrying that Patti Anne will be left with strangers.
- Terri Anne sees that Patti Anne is already becoming comfortable with Olivia, Mei, and Tsumugi.

## 17. Terri Anne accepts the shift

- Terri Anne sends Charlene a message saying that she can come into the Blue Velvet Room and work the shift.
- Charlene confirms that Scout will be added to the schedule.
- Terri Anne feels comfortable accepting because Patti Anne will remain at the mansion with people she already knows.

## 18. Terri Anne thanks Olivia, Mei, and Tsumugi

- Terri Anne thanks Olivia, Mei, and Tsumugi for helping with Patti Anne.
- She explains that without their help, she would have to call a babysitter.
- Terri Anne would also have to pay the babysitter extra money for an Uber to and from the mansion.
- Olivia and Mei reassure her that they want to care for Patti Anne and are not helping merely to save her money.
- Tsumugi is pleased that Terri Anne includes her in the thanks while Olivia and Mei remain the responsible adults.

## 19. Closing beat

- Terri Anne has accepted another shift at the Blue Velvet Room.
- Patti Anne will remain at the mansion with Olivia, Mei, and Tsumugi.
- Mei has also agreed to give Terri Anne a tattoo after they choose its design and placement.
- The chapter ends with Terri Anne preparing to return to work as Scout without needing to arrange a babysitter or pay for the babysitter's Uber.
