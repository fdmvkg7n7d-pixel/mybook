# Chapter Twenty-Five — Calling Olivia

## 1. Mike returns from Terri Anne's apartment

- After driving Terri Anne back to her apartment, Mike returns to his Carlisle mansion.
- He thinks about their first date, the night they spent together, and their breakfast the following morning.
- Mike understands that their relationship is no longer limited to conversations between a customer and a dancer.
- Because Scout works at a business he owns, the relationship must be formally reported.

## 2. Key Corporation's reporting policy

- Key Corporation requires employees and executives to disclose romantic relationships that may create a workplace conflict of interest.
- The policy applies to Mike even though he owns and leads the corporation.
- His authority over the Blue Velvet Room makes disclosure especially important.
- Mike refuses to create a secret exception for himself.

## 3. Mike calls Olivia

- Mike calls Olivia on her private telephone number.
- He tells her that the call concerns Key Corporation's relationship-reporting policy.
- Olivia recognizes from his serious tone that the disclosure is personal.
- Mike asks her to document the conversation confidentially.

## 4. Terri Anne plays with Patti Anne

- While Mike is calling Olivia, Terri Anne is back in her apartment with Patti Anne.
- Mother and daughter sit and play together on the floor.
- Terri Anne tells Patti Anne about her date, the night she spent at Mike's mansion, and their blueberry-waffle breakfast.
- She describes the enormous mansion and tells Patti Anne that it has **four different swimming pools**.
- Knowing how much Patti Anne enjoys the water, Terri Anne playfully imagines taking her swimming there one day.
- She leaves out adult details and speaks as a mother thinking aloud to her baby.
- Patti Anne listens, plays, and responds with happy infant sounds.

## 5. Terri Anne asks a rhetorical question

- Terri Anne looks at Patti Anne and asks, **“Am I crazy to get involved with Mike Key, the wealthiest man in the world?”**
- She does not expect Patti Anne to answer the question.
- Saying it aloud allows Terri Anne to acknowledge how enormous and complicated the difference between their lives feels.
- She is attracted to Mike and trusts him more than she expected, but she remains uncertain about becoming involved with someone so powerful.
- Playing with Patti Anne keeps Terri Anne grounded in the part of her life that matters most.

## 6. Mike reports the relationship

- Mike tells Olivia that his relationship with Scout has become romantic.
- He explains that they went on their first date and that she spent the night at his mansion.
- He does not provide intimate details because the policy does not require them.
- Mike continues identifying her as Scout and does not disclose that she personally told him her real name.

## 7. Olivia confirms the policy applies

- Olivia confirms that Mike is right to report the relationship.
- Scout is an employee of the Blue Velvet Room, and Mike is the club's owner and the head of Key Corporation.
- The difference in authority creates a potential conflict even if the relationship is consensual.
- Olivia records the disclosure and the date on which Mike made it.

## 8. Mike protects Scout's employment

- Mike tells Olivia that Scout must not receive special treatment or punishment because of their relationship.
- He also makes clear that she must remain free to end either the relationship or her employment without retaliation.
- Mike does not want gifts, protection, or his ownership position to become pressure on her.
- Olivia agrees that those protections must be documented.

## 9. Charlene and Marco retain workplace authority

- Olivia recommends that Mike remove himself from decisions involving Scout's schedule, compensation, performance reviews, discipline, and daily working conditions.
- Charlene, as temporary manager, will handle management decisions.
- Marco, as assistant manager and head bartender, will help oversee daily operations.
- Mike agrees that he should not directly supervise Scout while their romantic relationship continues.

## 10. Confidentiality

- Olivia tells Mike that only the people who need to manage the conflict should receive the disclosure.
- The other dancers and club employees do not need access to Scout's private life.
- Scout's real name and Patti Anne remain outside the workplace disclosure.
- Mike asks Olivia to protect Scout from gossip and retaliation.

## 11. Olivia asks whether Scout knows

- Olivia asks whether Scout knows that Mike is reporting the relationship.
- Mike admits that he has not yet discussed the corporate policy with her.
- Olivia advises him to tell Scout directly so she does not feel that decisions about her are being made in secret.
- Mike agrees that Scout deserves a clear explanation of the policy and its protections.

## 12. The will remains private

- Mike does not mention his mother's marriage-and-child deadline as a reason for the relationship.
- He knows the will has no place in a workplace disclosure.
- His call is intended to protect Scout and the corporation, not to create evidence that their relationship is more advanced than it is.
- Mike remains determined not to let the inheritance deadline dictate his treatment of Terri Anne.

## 13. Closing beat

- Olivia completes the initial confidential record of Mike's disclosure.
- Mike thanks her for handling the matter professionally.
- He ends the call knowing that reporting the relationship was necessary but that he must now explain the policy to Scout.
- The chapter closes with Mike preparing to have that conversation without making Scout feel that their relationship belongs to Key Corporation.

## Chapter Purpose

This chapter:

- Shows Mike complying with Key Corporation's relationship-reporting policy.
- Intercuts his formal disclosure with Terri Anne playing on the floor with Patti Anne.
- Shows Terri Anne telling Patti Anne about the night and breakfast at Mike's mansion.
- Gives Terri Anne a rhetorical question about becoming involved with the wealthiest man in the world.
- Formally establishes his romantic relationship with Scout.
- Acknowledges the power imbalance created by Mike's ownership of her workplace.
- Removes Mike from direct employment decisions affecting Scout.
- Gives Charlene and Marco continued authority over club operations.
- Protects Scout's real name, daughter, employment, and freedom to end the relationship.
- Sets up Mike's obligation to explain the corporate disclosure to Scout.
