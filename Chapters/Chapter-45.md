# Chapter Forty-Five — Ashlee Goes with Mike

## 1. The morning after the police arrive

- After the police complete their initial response to Victoria's break-in, Mike begins deciding what everyone should do next.
- The mansion remains under heavy security while evidence is preserved and the damaged property is documented.
- Mike still intends to examine the three unidentified problems at Key Porn Studios Los Angeles if circumstances allow.

## 2. Geoffrey must go to the university

- Geoffrey explains that he needs to go to the university for his teaching position.
- He works there as a professor assistant and has responsibilities he cannot ignore without notifying the university.
- Geoffrey does not want to leave Ashlee after the attack, but he also knows Mike is now with her.
- Mike assures Geoffrey that Ashlee will remain surrounded by protection.

## 3. Ashlee is nervous about being alone

- Ashlee admits that she is extremely nervous about remaining at the mansion without Geoffrey or Mike.
- Victoria reached the bedroom once, and the presence of guards does not immediately restore Ashlee's sense of safety.
- She does not want fear to control her life, but she is honest about what she can manage that day.
- Mike listens rather than dismissing her anxiety.

## 4. Mike suggests that Ashlee accompany him

- Mike tells Ashlee that she does not have to remain behind at the mansion.
- He invites her to go with him to Key Porn Studios Los Angeles.
- Ashlee will be able to remain near Mike while he looks into the three problems Charlene mentioned.
- The twenty-guard security detail can protect both siblings during the visit.

## 5. Ashlee agrees to go

- Ashlee accepts Mike's invitation because she does not want to be alone after Victoria's attack.
- She will use the special power chair created by UCLA's Engineering Department.
- The chair remains fully functional despite Victoria's destruction of her regular wheelchair.
- Mike makes certain the transportation and studio entrance can accommodate Ashlee and the chair.

## 6. The Key Corporation grant

- Mike and Ashlee remember that Key Corporation provided the grant and that Mike told Ashlee about it.
- UCLA engineering students began the project during Ashlee's first year in the music program and spent eighteen months designing and building the chair.
- Ashlee helped test prototypes and cried when she tried the completed wheelchair for the first time.
- The chair's ability to raise Ashlee into a supported standing position allows her to play pool with Geoffrey and their friends.
- On this day, the chair also gives her a reliable way to leave the damaged mansion and accompany Mike.

## 7. Ashlee contacts her professors

- Ashlee knows that she is expected in classes within UCLA's music program.
- She contacts her professors and tells them that she will not be attending her classes that day.
- She provides the necessary notice without feeling obligated to explain every private detail of Victoria's attack.
- Ashlee takes responsibility for communicating about her absence herself.

## 8. Geoffrey prepares to leave

- Geoffrey gathers what he needs for his university teaching responsibilities.
- He and Ashlee agree to remain in contact using a replacement or borrowed phone until their iPhones can be replaced.
- Geoffrey is relieved that Ashlee will be with Mike rather than waiting nervously at home.
- Before leaving, he makes certain Mike understands how shaken Ashlee remains.

## 9. Daniela observes the new plan

- Daniela learns that Ashlee will accompany Mike to Key Porn Studios Los Angeles.
- She maintains her professional appearance and does not reveal that she gave Victoria the mansion's security code.
- The change in plans places Ashlee within Mike's twenty-guard security detail rather than alone with Daniela.
- Daniela must decide what, if anything, she will report to Victoria.

## 10. Ashlee drives her special van

- Ashlee uses her specially adapted van to drive Mike and herself to Key Porn Studios Los Angeles.
- The van gives Ashlee direct control of the journey and accommodates her special power chair.
- Mike rides as her passenger rather than assigning one of his guards to drive.
- The twenty guards travel in separate security vehicles and maintain protection around the siblings without taking away Ashlee's independence.
- Mike remains alert because Victoria said she hired another professional assassin.

## 11. Mike withholds his name at security

- Ashlee drives into the Key Porn Studios Los Angeles property and approaches the security checkpoint.
- Mike deliberately does not give the security guard his name or identify himself as the owner of Key Porn Studios.
- The guard therefore treats Mike and Ashlee as ordinary visitors rather than senior members of the Key family.
- Following normal visitor procedure, the security guard gives both of them temporary badges.
- Mike accepts the badges because he wants to observe how the studio operates when employees do not know the owner is present.

## 12. They enter the Los Angeles studio

- Mike and Ashlee enter Key Porn Studios Los Angeles wearing their temporary visitor badges.
- Ashlee enters as Mike's sister and companion, not as a performer.
- Mike intends to observe the studio and identify the three problems Charlene could not explain.
- The security detail remains close enough to protect them while avoiding unnecessary disruption inside the building.

## 13. Scott comes downstairs

- Word reaches **Scott**, the vice president responsible for Key Porn Studios Los Angeles, that two unexpected visitors have arrived with temporary badges.
- Scott comes downstairs to meet Mike and Ashlee personally.
- Mike watches carefully to see whether Scott recognizes him despite the temporary badge and lack of introduction.
- Ashlee remains beside Mike in her special power chair as the meeting begins.

## 14. Scott takes them to the executive suites

- Scott welcomes Mike and Ashlee and escorts them upstairs to the executive suites.
- He chooses a private presentation room where they can discuss confidential studio business.
- Mike and Ashlee continue wearing the temporary badges issued by security.
- Scott now understands that the unannounced visitor is Mike Key, the studio's owner.

## 15. Mike explains why Ashlee is with him

- Before discussing the studio, Mike explains why his sister is accompanying him.
- He says that Victoria broke into Ashlee and Geoffrey's mansion and terrorized them.
- Ashlee is extremely nervous about being alone, and Geoffrey had to go to the university for his teaching position.
- Mike makes clear that Ashlee came for safety and companionship, not because she expected to become involved in a studio production.
- Scott treats Ashlee respectfully and welcomes her to remain throughout the presentation.

## 16. Scott begins the presentation

- Scott displays a prepared presentation describing the three problems facing Key Porn Studios Los Angeles.
- Mike recognizes that the problems are connected to one proposed film rather than three unrelated operational failures.
- Ashlee watches from her special power chair while Scott begins with the script.

## 17. First problem: casting a disabled woman

- A writer created what Scott describes as a **stupendous script** centred on a disabled woman.
- The studio believes the lead role should be played by a disabled woman rather than by a performer pretending to have a disability.
- Key Porn Studios Los Angeles has been unable to find a disabled adult woman who is right for the role and willing to accept it.
- No one assumes or pressures Ashlee to take the role merely because she is present.
- Scott does not know that Ashlee is a transgender woman.
- Mike knows but respects Ashlee's privacy and does not disclose that information to Scott.
- The casting problem has prevented the project from moving forward.

## 18. Second problem: the film budget

- The presentation identifies the proposed film's budget as **three hundred thousand dollars**.
- Scott explains that the studio needs a decision about whether the project justifies that level of investment.
- Casting delays and unresolved production questions make the budget more difficult to approve.
- Mike studies the figures and asks Scott to distinguish necessary production costs from avoidable expenses.

## 19. Third problem: Kassandra

- The final part of the presentation identifies the third problem as **Kassandra**.
- Scott adds a photograph of Kassandra to the presentation screen.
- Ashlee immediately recognizes one of her three closest girlfriends and a member of the Four Musketeers.
- Ashlee tells Mike and Scott that Kassandra dropped out of the Four Musketeers' group chat eighteen months earlier.
- Kassandra stopped participating without giving Ashlee, Yelena, or Isobel a clear explanation.
- The timing means the group-chat withdrawal happened around six months after Kassandra's father kicked her out of the family.
- Kassandra's photograph connects the studio problem directly to Ashlee's personal life.

## 20. Kassandra's amateur-night performance

- The presentation explains that approximately two years earlier, Kassandra entered an amateur dance night at a club in Florida.
- Her father happened to be at the club for a **bachelor party**, a celebration held for a groom before his wedding.
- Kassandra did not know that her father was in the audience when she went onto the stage.
- Her father saw her dancing and reacted with anger rather than trying to understand her choices.
- After the performance, he kicked Kassandra out of his family.

## 21. A casting agent discovers Kassandra

- Kassandra continued trying to support herself after losing her family.
- Six months after her father rejected her, a casting agent for **Key Porn Studios Berlin** saw Kassandra performing onstage.
- The agent recognized her presence, confidence, and potential as a performer.
- The agent contacted Charlene and told her about Kassandra's stage presence.
- After reviewing the recommendation, Charlene hired Kassandra to work for Key Porn Studios.

## 22. Kassandra's father appears at the mountain scene

- A couple of months before Mike and Ashlee's visit, Kassandra was working on a scene being filmed in the mountains.
- Her father unexpectedly appeared at the location while the scene was taking place.
- Seeing him again forced Kassandra to confront the rejection and humiliation she had experienced two years earlier.
- Scott does not yet know why her father came to the remote filming location or how he learned where she would be.

## 23. Kassandra's depression

- After her father's appearance at the mountain scene, Kassandra fell into a very deep depression.
- The change affected her wellbeing and her ability to continue working normally.
- Scott identifies Kassandra as a human and workplace concern rather than merely a production delay.
- Ashlee is upset that one of her closest friends has been suffering so severely.
- Mike understands that the third problem requires care and support, not punishment or pressure to perform.

## 24. Closing hook

- Geoffrey remains at the university for his professor-assistant teaching position while Ashlee sits beside Mike in the executive suite.
- Mike now knows that the three problems involve casting a disabled woman, a three-hundred-thousand-dollar budget, and Kassandra's severe depression.
- Ashlee looks at Kassandra's photograph on the presentation screen and wants to know where her friend is now.
- The chapter ends with Mike recognizing that the studio's most urgent problem may be the wellbeing of Ashlee's friend.
