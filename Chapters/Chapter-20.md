# Chapter Twenty — The Photograph

## 1. Saturday morning

- Saturday morning begins only hours after the secret Friday-night meeting at The Well.
- Mike is preparing for his first date with Scout later that day.
- He remains unaware that Victoria, Cooper, and Adam met to discuss Don and the hidden plan.
- Bryan calls Mike before the date.

## 2. Bryan reports the meeting

- Bryan tells Mike that one of his investigators located Adam at The Well on Friday night.
- The investigator confirmed Adam's identity by approaching him and asking him out.
- Adam believed the interaction was ordinary flirtation and did not realize he was under surveillance.
- Bryan explains that Adam later met with **two unidentified people**.

## 3. The two mystery people

- Bryan says that his investigator did not immediately recognize Adam's companions.
- One was a man and the other was a woman.
- Their decision to meet Adam privately at The Well suggests that they may be connected to him or his activities.
- Bryan tells Mike that the investigator obtained a photograph of all three together.

## 4. Bryan sends the photograph

- While they remain on the telephone, Bryan sends the photograph to Mike.
- Mike opens the image on his iPhone.
- The photograph clearly shows Adam with the two mystery people.
- Mike recognizes both of them immediately.

## 5. Mike recognizes Cooper

- The man in the photograph is **Cooper**, Mike's limousine driver.
- Mike is shocked because Cooper was driving him and Olivia when Scout called about the follower.
- Cooper heard Terri Anne describe the blue jacket and blue hat.
- He drove Mike and Olivia to the Y and helped Scout and Patti Anne into the limousine.
- The photograph forces Mike to consider that Cooper may have concealed information or deliberately placed them in danger.

## 6. Mike recognizes Victoria

- The woman in the photograph is Mike's twenty-eight-year-old sister, **Victoria Key**.
- Mike recognizes her without any doubt.
- He does not understand why his sister would meet secretly with the former manager of the Blue Velvet Room.
- Victoria's connection to Adam makes the investigation personal in a way Mike did not expect.

## 7. Mike suspects Victoria sabotaged his relationships

- The photograph causes Mike to reconsider the failures of his previous romantic relationships.
- He begins to suspect that Victoria may have interfered with them or deliberately driven his former partners away.
- If Mike remains unmarried and childless at forty, his mother's will transfers Key Corporation to his middle siblings.
- Mike wonders whether Victoria has been trying to ensure that he misses the deadline so she can get her **dirty hands on Key Corporation**.
- He does not yet have proof, but her secret meeting with Adam and Cooper makes the suspicion impossible to dismiss.

## 8. Mike tells Bryan who they are

- Mike identifies both people for Bryan.
- He explains that Cooper is his trusted driver and that Victoria is his second-youngest sister.
- Bryan immediately understands the seriousness of the discovery.
- Someone with access to Mike's movements and someone inside the Key family are connected to Adam.

## 9. Mike reassesses the danger

- Mike remembers Cooper's behavior during the emergency trip to the Y.
- He realizes Cooper heard every detail but never admitted recognizing Don.
- Mike begins to question how much private information Cooper has passed to Adam or Victoria.
- He is especially disturbed that Cooper was close to Scout and Patti Anne while secretly connected to the people threatening them.

## 10. Mike orders discretion

- Mike tells Bryan not to alert Cooper, Victoria, or Adam that they were photographed.
- He asks Bryan to investigate the connection among all three and determine whether Don was involved.
- Mike wants to know how long Cooper has been betraying his trust and what Victoria is planning.
- Until Bryan has more evidence, Mike intends to let Cooper and Victoria believe their meeting remained secret.

## 11. The date remains ahead

- After ending the call, Mike looks again at the photograph.
- His first date with Scout at Mandarin Restaurant is still scheduled for later that Saturday.
- Mike must protect Scout without allowing Victoria's conspiracy or his anger at Cooper to control their evening.
- He also knows that Cooper can no longer be trusted to drive them.

## 12. Closing hook

- Mike saves the photograph securely and begins arranging transportation that does not involve Cooper.
- Bryan starts tracing the connection among Victoria, Adam, Cooper, and Don.
- Mike looks at his sister's face in the photograph and wonders what could make her work with men who endangered Terri Anne and Patti Anne.
- The chapter ends with Mike preparing for his first date while realizing the threat may come from inside his own family.

## Chapter Purpose

This chapter:

- Brings Bryan's investigation to Mike on Saturday morning.
- Reveals that Adam met two mystery people at The Well.
- Delivers the investigator's photograph to Mike.
- Identifies the two people as Cooper and Victoria.
- Exposes Cooper's possible betrayal.
- Connects Victoria directly to Adam.
- Makes Mike suspect that Victoria sabotaged his past relationships to gain control of Key Corporation.
- Expands the threat from the Blue Velvet Room into Mike's family and household.
- Creates additional tension immediately before Mike and Scout's first date.
