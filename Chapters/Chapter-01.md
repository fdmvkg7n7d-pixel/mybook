# Chapter One — Mike Meets Scout

## 1. Mike enters unnoticed

- Mike visits the Blue Velvet Room alone and anonymously.
- He sits beside the bar rather than choosing a table close to the stage.
- Nobody recognizes him as Mike Key or knows that he secretly owns the club through multiple shell companies.
- Mike intends to observe how the club operates when its employees do not know the owner is present.

## 2. Mike questions Marco

- Marco serves Mike his favorite drink: **Johnnie Walker on ice**.
- Mike casually asks Marco questions about the Blue Velvet Room:
  - How long has Marco worked there?
  - Is the club always this busy?
  - Does the manager treat the employees well?
  - Who handles complaints and security?
  - Does the club's mysterious owner ever visit?
- Marco answers cautiously and cannot understand why an ordinary customer is asking so many operational questions.
- When Marco asks whether Mike is in the nightclub business, Mike remains vague and says that he is merely curious.

## 3. Scout performs

- Terri Anne performs under her stage name, **Scout**.
- Mike does not initially know her real name.
- He is struck by her confidence and presence onstage.
- Mike notices that Adam watches Scout more closely than he watches the other performers.
- Don remains nearby and responds to Adam's signals.

## 4. Scout joins Mike at the bar

- After finishing her set, Scout comes to the bar and sits beside Mike.
- She orders her favorite drink: a **virgin Bloody Mary with extra celery sticks**.
- Their contrasting drinks create an opening for playful conversation.
- Mike introduces himself using only his first name.
- Terri Anne introduces herself as Scout, keeping her real identity and the existence of Patti Anne secret.

## 5. An unexpected connection forms

- Scout expects Mike to behave like a typical customer, but he speaks to her respectfully.
- He asks about her interests rather than making demands.
- Scout carefully redirects questions about her home and personal life because she is frightened to reveal that she has a daughter.
- Mike remains vague about his surname, occupation, immense wealth, and ownership of the club.
- Each senses that the other is withholding something.
- Mike enjoys being treated like an ordinary man rather than the wealthiest man in the world.

## 6. Adam becomes suspicious

- Scout remains with Mike longer than Adam considers acceptable.
- Adam believes Mike is monopolizing Scout's time and preventing her from circulating among other customers.
- Adam is also irritated that Scout appears to be enjoying a genuine conversation.
- He signals Don and approaches the bar.
- Scout immediately becomes tense because she knows Adam's apparently professional manner can become threatening.

## 7. Adam and Don confront Mike

- Adam tells Mike that he has been monopolizing Scout's time.
- Don stands beside Adam as a physically intimidating warning.
- Mike remains calm and refuses to be intimidated.
- Marco watches from behind the bar, sensing that the confrontation could escalate.

## 8. Mike lays down two hundred dollars

- Without arguing, Mike takes **two hundred dollars** from his wallet and lays it on the bar.
- He says that the money covers Scout's time and both of their drinks.
- The gesture proves that Mike means business and understands how the club operates.
- He makes it clear that he wants to continue talking to Scout, provided that Scout is willing to remain with him.
- Mike does not reveal his larger fortune, his position at Key Corporation, or his ownership of the club.

## 9. Adam backs down

- Adam is pleased by the payment and immediately becomes accommodating.
- He tells Don that there is no longer a problem.
- Don steps away but continues watching Mike from across the room.
- Adam encourages Scout to continue entertaining her generous customer.
- Although satisfied for now, Adam begins to see Mike as a wealthy customer whom he may be able to exploit later.

## 10. Scout and Marco react

- Scout is relieved that the confrontation has ended.
- She is also intrigued by Mike's confidence and his ability to produce two hundred dollars without hesitation.
- Mike's manner makes clear that his payment does not entitle him to anything from her; it only compensates for her time and prevents Adam from interrupting them.
- Marco collects the payment for the drinks and notices that Mike has left far more money than necessary.
- Mike's earlier questions and calm display of money make Marco increasingly curious about who he really is.

## 11. Mike and Scout resume their conversation

- Scout returns to her virgin Bloody Mary and extra celery sticks.
- Mike lifts his Johnnie Walker on ice.
- Their conversation resumes, and the attraction between them begins to grow.
- Neither tells the other the full truth: Scout conceals Terri Anne and Patti Anne, while Mike conceals his surname, wealth, corporate position, and ownership of the Blue Velvet Room.
- Adam watches from a distance, unaware that the customer he confronted is the club's true owner.

## Chapter Purpose

This opening chapter:

- Brings Mike and Terri Anne together for the first time.
- Establishes their attraction and mutual secrecy.
- Introduces the Blue Velvet Room and its key employees.
- Shows Mike's quiet confidence without exposing his identity.
- Establishes Adam as a controlling antagonist.
- Hints at Terri Anne's hidden fear without mentioning Patti Anne during the meeting.
- Creates dramatic irony because the reader knows Mike owns the club while its employees do not.
