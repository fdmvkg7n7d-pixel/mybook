# Chapter Twenty-Seven — Meeting Marco

## 1. Scout prepares for the meeting

- Scout prepares to meet Marco at Tim Hortons away from the Blue Velvet Room.
- She decides to bring Patti Anne with her rather than hide her daughter from him any longer.
- The choice makes Scout nervous because Adam used Patti Anne's existence to blackmail her.
- She reminds herself that Adam is no longer Marco's manager and cannot control their conversation.

## 2. Scout brings Patti Anne to Tim Hortons

- Scout arrives at Tim Hortons with eight-month-old Patti Anne.
- Patti Anne rides in her stroller with the things she may need during the meeting.
- Marco is surprised to see Scout arrive with a baby.
- He immediately understands that she invited him outside the club because she wants to trust him with something deeply personal.

## 3. They find a place to talk

- Scout and Marco choose a table where they can speak without other customers easily overhearing them.
- Marco helps make room for Patti Anne's stroller without taking control away from Scout.
- He does not demand an explanation before she is ready.
- Scout takes a moment to gather the courage to begin.

## 4. Scout introduces Patti Anne

- Scout tells Marco that the baby is her eight-month-old daughter, **Patti Anne**.
- She explains that Patti Anne is the reason she was so guarded whenever anyone at the Blue Velvet Room asked about her personal life.
- Marco responds warmly to Patti Anne and does not judge Scout for being a young mother.
- His reaction begins easing Scout's fear.

## 5. Scout reveals her real name

- Scout tells Marco that Scout is only her stage name.
- She reveals that her real name is **Terri Anne Williams**.
- She asks him to continue using Scout at the Blue Velvet Room unless she tells the other employees herself.
- Marco agrees to protect her privacy.
- He thanks Terri Anne for trusting him with both her name and her daughter.

## 6. Terri Anne tells Marco about her first date

- Terri Anne tells Marco that she invited Mike to Mandarin Restaurant for their first date.
- She explains that Mike respected her wish to keep the evening simple.
- Terri Anne tells him that she chose to accompany Mike to his Carlisle mansion after dinner.
- She describes the mansion tour, spending the night, and making homemade blueberry waffles the following morning.
- She does not provide private sexual details, but she makes clear that every part of the date was consensual and freely chosen.
- Marco is surprised but pleased that Mike treated her respectfully.

## 7. Terri Anne explains the secrecy

- Terri Anne explains that Adam discovered Patti Anne and used that knowledge to blackmail her.
- She feared that telling anyone at work could lead to the police, child-welfare authorities, or other people being used against her.
- Her past trafficking experience made it even harder for her to trust coworkers or managers.
- Marco realizes that many of Scout's guarded reactions at the club were survival responses.

## 8. Marco reacts to Adam's abuse

- Marco is angry that Adam used Terri Anne's daughter as a weapon.
- He regrets noticing her fear without understanding enough to help earlier.
- Terri Anne tells him that Adam and Don were skilled at making employees believe that resistance would make everything worse.
- Marco assures her that under Charlene's management, Patti Anne will never be used against her employment.

## 9. Marco separates friendship from management

- Marco explains that as assistant manager, he must treat Scout fairly at work.
- As her friend, he is honoured that she trusted him enough to arrange this meeting.
- He promises not to share her real name or Patti Anne's existence as gossip.
- Any workplace information that must be handled for safety will be discussed with Terri Anne first whenever possible.

## 10. Patti Anne helps ease the conversation

- Patti Anne's presence prevents the meeting from feeling entirely serious.
- Her expressions, movements, and infant sounds make Terri Anne and Marco smile.
- Marco speaks kindly to Patti Anne without assuming he may hold or touch her.
- Terri Anne becomes increasingly comfortable as she sees that Marco respects both of them.

## 11. Closing beat

- Terri Anne finishes the meeting feeling lighter than when she arrived.
- Marco now knows the woman behind Scout and understands why she protected Patti Anne so fiercely.
- At work, he will continue calling her Scout and respecting the identity she chooses to present.
- The chapter ends with Terri Anne, Patti Anne, and Marco sharing a peaceful moment at Tim Hortons outside the control of the Blue Velvet Room.

## Chapter Purpose

This chapter:

- Brings Scout and Marco together outside the Blue Velvet Room.
- Has Scout bring Patti Anne to Tim Hortons.
- Introduces Patti Anne to Marco.
- Reveals Scout's real name as Terri Anne Williams.
- Allows Terri Anne to tell Marco about her first date with Mike.
- Explains why Terri Anne concealed her daughter and identity.
- Shows Marco responding without judgment.
- Establishes that Marco will protect her privacy while treating her fairly at work.
- Deepens the trust between Terri Anne and Marco.
