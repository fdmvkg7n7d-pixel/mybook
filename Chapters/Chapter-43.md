# Chapter Forty-Three — Daniela Hears Ashlee Crying

## 1. Daniela arrives at six o'clock

- At approximately six o'clock in the morning Los Angeles time, Daniela arrives at Ashlee and Geoffrey's mansion.
- Daniela is one of Ashlee's personal support workers and expects to begin an ordinary scheduled visit.
- Nothing outside immediately prepares her for what Victoria has done inside the house.

## 2. She hears Ashlee crying

- Shortly after entering the mansion, Daniela hears Ashlee crying in the bedroom.
- The sound tells her that Ashlee is frightened and in distress rather than merely calling for routine assistance.
- Daniela immediately abandons her normal arrival routine.

## 3. Daniela runs to the bedroom

- Daniela runs through the mansion toward Ashlee and Geoffrey's bedroom.
- She calls out so the couple knows that someone trustworthy has arrived.
- Geoffrey answers and urges her to come quickly.

## 4. Daniela finds Ashlee and Geoffrey

- Daniela enters the bedroom and finds Geoffrey beside Ashlee.
- Ashlee is stranded without a working wheelchair and is still crying after Victoria's attack.
- Daniela sees the damaged wheelchair and realizes that the destruction was deliberate.
- She focuses first on Ashlee's immediate safety and comfort.

## 5. Geoffrey explains the broken phones

- Geoffrey tells Daniela that Victoria broke into the mansion and terrorized them.
- He explains that Victoria destroyed both of their iPhones before running away.
- The broken phones prevented them from immediately calling for help or warning Mike.
- Daniela understands why no emergency call was made before she arrived.

## 6. Daniela gives them her cell phone

- Daniela takes out her own cell phone and gives it to Ashlee and Geoffrey.
- She does not hesitate or insist on making decisions for them.
- The phone restores their ability to contact help, reach security, and attempt to warn Mike.
- Ashlee holds the phone while trying to decide which urgent call must come first.

## 7. Geoffrey remembers Ashlee's special power chair

- Geoffrey realizes that Victoria destroyed Ashlee's regular wheelchair but did not find her special power chair.
- He tells Ashlee and Daniela that he is going to retrieve it.
- The special chair is stored elsewhere in the mansion and remains undamaged.
- Daniela stays in the bedroom with Ashlee while Geoffrey goes to get it.

## 8. The UCLA engineering project

- Ashlee attends UCLA's music program.
- Key Corporation gave a grant to UCLA, and Mike told Ashlee about the funding.
- During Ashlee's first year, UCLA began an engineering-student project overseen by its Engineering Department.
- The students spent eighteen months designing and building the special power chair.
- Ashlee helped test prototypes and cried when she tried the completed chair for the first time.
- The chair was designed around Ashlee's body, mobility, and personal goals.
- Its powered standing function can safely raise and support Ashlee in an upright position.
- The project shows how Key Corporation's funding produced a practical result that increased Ashlee's independence.

## 9. Standing to play pool

- One reason Ashlee values the power chair is that it allows her to stand at a pool table.
- With the chair supporting her, she can play pool with Geoffrey and their friends rather than watching from the side.
- The standing feature gives her a comfortable height and position from which to line up her shots.
- The chair turns an activity that might otherwise exclude her into something she can share with people she loves.

## 10. The game room

- Geoffrey once asked Ashlee whether he could add a pool table to their game room.
- Ashlee agreed because she enjoys spending time in the game room, especially playing board games and putting puzzles together.
- Although board games and puzzles remain personal favourites, the pool table gives the couple and their friends another activity to enjoy together.
- Her special power chair makes the pool table fully meaningful to her rather than merely an addition for Geoffrey.

## 11. Daniela remains with Ashlee

- While Geoffrey retrieves the power chair, Daniela stays beside Ashlee.
- Daniela helps Ashlee remain safely supported without taking control away from her.
- Ashlee tells Daniela that Victoria admitted hiring the assassin whose bullet destroyed her T11 vertebra.
- She explains that Mike was the real target and that Victoria claims to have hired another professional assassin.

## 12. Ashlee calls Mike

- Ashlee decides that warning Mike cannot wait until his airplane lands.
- She uses Daniela's cell phone to call him aboard the Key Corporation airplane.
- Mike answers without recognizing Daniela's number and immediately hears that Ashlee is crying.
- Ashlee tells him that Victoria broke into the mansion, entered their bedroom, and terrorized her and Geoffrey.

## 13. Ashlee and Geoffrey tell Mike about Victoria

- Geoffrey returns with Ashlee's special power chair and joins the urgent call.
- Ashlee and Geoffrey tell Mike that Victoria admitted hiring the assassin who shot fourteen-year-old Ashlee during her horseback ride with Mike.
- They explain that the assassin had been trying to kill Mike.
- Mike finally realizes that the bullet which destroyed Ashlee's T11 vertebra was intended for him.
- They warn him that Victoria claims to have hired another professional assassin.
- Ashlee also tells Mike that Victoria destroyed both iPhones, broke her regular wheelchair, and damaged the adapted pedals on her grand piano.
- Daniela remains with Ashlee and Geoffrey throughout the call.
- Mike understands that Victoria's conspiracy has escalated into an immediate threat to his life and Ashlee's safety.

## 14. The power chair remains available

- Geoffrey has brought Ashlee the university-designed power chair before joining the call.
- The sight of the undamaged chair gives Ashlee back an immediate means of independent mobility.
- Daniela and Geoffrey prepare to assist only as Ashlee directs while she finishes the urgent call.
- Victoria destroyed one chair, but she did not eliminate Ashlee's independence.

## 15. Daniela helps Ashlee shower

- After Ashlee finishes the urgent call with Mike, she wants to take a shower and regain some sense of normalcy.
- Daniela helps Ashlee move safely into the bathroom and provides the personal support Ashlee requests.
- She respects Ashlee's privacy and choices while helping with the shower.
- Afterward, Ashlee tells Daniela that she wants to wear only her orange leggings.
- Daniela follows Ashlee's choice and helps only as requested.
- Ashlee trusts Daniela and does not suspect that her support worker has concealed something important.

## 16. Daniela thinks about helping Victoria

- While she and Ashlee are in the bathroom, Daniela privately thinks about the fact that she **helped Victoria**.
- Ashlee does not know that Daniela assisted the woman who invaded her home and terrorized her.
- Daniela keeps her expression and professional manner under control so Ashlee will not recognize the betrayal.
- The exact assistance Daniela gave Victoria and her reason for doing so remain unrevealed.
- Her private thoughts transform her timely arrival and offer of a cell phone into something more suspicious.

## 17. Geoffrey makes breakfast

- While Daniela helps Ashlee shower, Geoffrey goes to the kitchen to make breakfast.
- He prepares blueberry pancakes for Ashlee and himself.
- The familiar task gives Geoffrey something practical to do after the terror and destruction of the morning.
- He expects Daniela to bring Ashlee to breakfast once she is ready.
- Geoffrey does not know that Daniela helped Victoria.

## 18. Ashlee and Geoffrey eat breakfast

- After Ashlee finishes showering and dressing in her orange leggings, Daniela helps her join Geoffrey.
- Geoffrey serves the blueberry pancakes he prepared.
- Ashlee and Geoffrey eat their breakfast together before allowing themselves to rest.
- The familiar meal gives them a brief moment of comfort after Victoria's attack.
- Daniela remains nearby, maintaining the appearance of a trusted personal support worker.

## 19. Closing hook

- Mike remains aboard the Key Corporation airplane after learning about Victoria's attack and the new assassin.
- Ashlee and Geoffrey finish their blueberry-pancake breakfast before settling in the sitting room to rest.
- They believe Daniela is someone they can trust after the terrifying morning.
- Daniela alone knows how she helped Victoria.
- The chapter ends with Ashlee and Geoffrey preparing to sleep while someone connected to the danger remains inside their home.

## Chapter Purpose

This chapter:

- Introduces Daniela as one of Ashlee's personal support workers.
- Has Daniela arrive around six o'clock Los Angeles time.
- Shows her hearing Ashlee crying and running to the bedroom.
- Reveals the immediate aftermath of Victoria's attack through Daniela's perspective.
- Has Daniela give Ashlee and Geoffrey her cell phone.
- Establishes that Ashlee attends UCLA's music program and introduces the standing power chair UCLA engineering students spent eighteen months building with her prototype feedback.
- Explains how the chair allows Ashlee to stand and play pool with Geoffrey and their friends.
- Establishes that Ashlee enjoys board games and putting puzzles together in their game room.
- Shows that Ashlee agreed when Geoffrey asked to add a pool table.
- Keeps Daniela beside Ashlee while Geoffrey retrieves the power chair.
- Has Ashlee call Mike aboard the Key Corporation airplane and tell him about Victoria.
- Shows Daniela helping Ashlee take a shower after the call.
- Has Ashlee choose to wear only her orange leggings afterward.
- Reveals to the reader that Daniela gave Victoria the mansion's security code, although Ashlee and Geoffrey do not know.
- Keeps the nature and motive of Daniela's assistance hidden.
- Has Geoffrey prepare blueberry pancakes for Ashlee and himself.
- Shows Ashlee and Geoffrey eating their breakfast before they rest.
- Restores Ashlee's mobility when Geoffrey returns with the undamaged special chair.
- Turns Daniela's presence into a hidden threat inside the mansion.
