# Chapter Five — The Weekly Call

## 1. The same day continues

- Chapter Five takes place later on the same day as the breakfast meeting and Mike's conversation with Olivia.
- Mike is at his mansion in **Carlisle, Ontario**.
- Ashlee lives in **Los Angeles** with her boyfriend, **Geoffrey**.
- Los Angeles is three hours behind Carlisle, so Mike and Ashlee coordinate their weekly call across the time difference.
- Their regular calls show that they remain close despite living far apart.

## 2. Ashlee calls Mike

- At their usual time, Ashlee calls Mike from the Los Angeles home she shares with Geoffrey.
- Mike answers in his home office at the mansion in Carlisle.
- The photographs of Ashlee, including the UCLA Music Building photograph and her graduation photograph with the other Four Musketeers, are still nearby.
- Their conversation begins comfortably, reflecting a familiar weekly routine rather than a formal family obligation.

## 3. They catch up

- Mike asks how Ashlee and Geoffrey are doing.
- Ashlee gives him news about their life in Los Angeles.
- Mike does not immediately discuss the investigation at the Blue Velvet Room because it is confidential and unrelated to Ashlee.
- Their conversation gives Mike a welcome moment of normal family connection after the serious breakfast meeting.

## 4. Ashlee raises Christmas

- Ashlee becomes hesitant before bringing up her Christmas plans.
- She asks Mike whether he would mind if she spent Christmas with **Geoffrey's family** instead of returning home to Ontario.
- Her careful wording suggests that Mike's opinion matters deeply to her.
- She does not want him to feel that she is choosing Geoffrey's family over him.

## 5. Mike gives his blessing

- Mike tells Ashlee that he does not mind.
- He reassures her that she is an adult and should spend Christmas where she wants to be.
- Mike makes clear that she does not need his permission, although he appreciates that she wanted to speak with him about it.
- He tells her that he will miss her but does not use guilt to change her decision.

## 6. Ashlee describes Geoffrey's family

- Ashlee tells Mike that Geoffrey's family is very conservative.
- Her comment introduces uncertainty about how comfortable or accepted she will feel in their home.
- Mike listens carefully without making assumptions about people he has not met.
- He asks whether Geoffrey supports her and whether she feels safe and welcome spending Christmas with his relatives.
- Ashlee's answer can later reveal how much Geoffrey has told his family about her life and identity.

## 7. Mike supports Ashlee

- Mike tells Ashlee that she can contact him at any time if the Christmas visit becomes uncomfortable.
- He offers support without trying to control her plans.
- Mike's response reinforces the trust between them—the same trust reflected in his story about the Four Musketeers.
- Ashlee is relieved that Mike respects her choice.

## 8. Mike proposes an early Christmas visit

- Mike tells Ashlee that he can drop by at the beginning of December.
- He suggests celebrating Christmas early with Ashlee and Geoffrey before their other holiday plans begin.
- Although Carlisle and Los Angeles are far apart, Mike speaks casually about the trip because his wealth and private travel arrangements make such a visit possible.
- The suggestion allows Mike and Ashlee to preserve their own Christmas tradition without interfering with her plans to visit Geoffrey's family.

## 9. Ashlee remembers Mike's protection

- Ashlee tells Mike that he is always welcome to visit her home.
- She reminds him that he protected her from their mother and siblings when she was younger.
- Her words reveal that Mike was not simply an affectionate older brother; he was the family member who made her feel safe.
- Mike is moved but does not take credit for doing what he believes an older brother should have done.
- The exchange deepens the contrast between Mike's acceptance of Ashlee and the rest of the Key family's treatment of her.

## 10. Geoffrey has a question

- Before the call ends, Ashlee tells Mike that Geoffrey has a **big question** to ask him.
- She does not reveal the question herself.
- Mike becomes curious, but Ashlee insists that Geoffrey must ask it personally when Mike visits in December.
- The unanswered question creates anticipation around Mike's first or next meeting with Geoffrey.

## 11. Closing beat

- The siblings end their weekly call warmly.
- Mike remains in his office after hanging up and looks again at Ashlee's photographs.
- He wonders what Geoffrey intends to ask him and suspects that the December visit may be more important than Ashlee has admitted.
- The conversation leaves Mike thinking about protection, acceptance, and allowing the people he loves to make their own choices.

## Chapter Purpose

This chapter:

- Shows Mike and Ashlee's close sibling relationship.
- Establishes that Ashlee calls Mike each week across the three-hour time difference between Los Angeles and Carlisle.
- Introduces Ashlee's boyfriend, Geoffrey.
- Establishes that Ashlee lives with Geoffrey in Los Angeles.
- Introduces the Christmas visit with Geoffrey's conservative family.
- Sets up Mike's early-December trip to celebrate Christmas with Ashlee and Geoffrey.
- Reveals that Mike protected Ashlee from their mother and siblings.
- Establishes that Geoffrey has an important question for Mike.
- Shows Mike supporting Ashlee's independence without making her feel guilty.
- Develops the novel's themes of trust, acceptance, protection, privacy, and family.
