# Chapter Twenty-Four — Don't Leave

## 1. Mike looks for Terri Anne

- After Terri Anne runs from the industrial kitchen, Mike leaves Wolfee behind and goes looking for her.
- He follows the direction she took through the mansion.
- Mike becomes concerned when he realizes how deeply the interruption embarrassed her.
- He finds her inside the bathroom connected to the mansion's gym.

## 2. Terri Anne is crying

- Mike hears Terri Anne crying inside the gym bathroom.
- He knocks and speaks to her rather than entering without warning.
- Terri Anne eventually allows him to come in or speaks to him through the partially opened door.
- Her embarrassment has become fear that she has ruined their relationship.

## 3. Terri Anne asks whether she should leave

- Terri Anne asks Mike whether he wants her to leave the mansion.
- She also asks whether he wants her to forget him and pretend their date never happened.
- Her question reveals that past experiences have taught her to expect rejection after moments of vulnerability.
- She assumes that embarrassing Mike in front of his house manager may have changed how he sees her.

## 4. Mike tells her not to leave

- Mike tells Terri Anne clearly that he does not want her to leave.
- He also tells her that he does not want her to forget him.
- Mike assures her that Wolfee's unexpected entrance did not embarrass him or make him regret their night together.
- He is more concerned that she is crying and believes she is unwanted.

## 5. Mike explains Wolfee's role in the family

- Mike explains that Wolfee has managed the mansion for years and has witnessed many awkward family moments.
- Wolfee is trusted by Mike and understands the importance of privacy.
- His apology was sincere, and he will not gossip about what he saw.
- Mike wants Terri Anne to understand that the interruption is not a scandal inside the household.

## 6. Mike mentions Ashlee

- To help Terri Anne see that Wolfee will not judge her, Mike says that Wolfee has accidentally seen Ashlee in her **birthday suit** over the years.
- Mike presents those incidents as ordinary, nonsexual privacy mishaps inside a large household.
- Wolfee always responded discreetly and respectfully.
- Mike's point is not to expose Ashlee's private life but to show that Wolfee knows how to handle accidental embarrassment without cruelty or gossip.

## 7. Terri Anne begins to calm down

- Terri Anne listens as Mike reassures her.
- She realizes that he is not angry and is not asking her to disappear from his life.
- Mike gives her space to recover rather than touching or crowding her without permission.
- Her crying gradually eases.

## 8. Mike separates embarrassment from rejection

- Mike tells Terri Anne that one awkward moment cannot erase the trust they have begun building.
- He reminds her that she chose to stay, trusted him with her real name, and shared breakfast with him.
- None of those choices became meaningless because Wolfee walked into the kitchen.
- Mike again tells her that he wants her to remain at the mansion for as long as she feels comfortable.

## 9. Terri Anne decides to apologize

- Terri Anne tells Mike that she believes she should apologize to Wolfee.
- Mike does not pressure her either to face Wolfee or to avoid him.
- He tells her that Wolfee will understand, but Terri Anne wants to speak to him herself.
- Her decision shows that she is beginning to recover from her embarrassment.

## 10. Terri Anne asks about the housecoat

- Terri Anne notices a housecoat hanging from a hook in the gym bathroom.
- She asks Mike whether she must put it on for Wolfee's sake before leaving the bathroom.
- Mike tells her that the decision is entirely up to her.
- He does not tell her to cover herself or encourage her to remain uncovered.
- His answer leaves control of her body and comfort with Terri Anne.

## 11. Terri Anne leaves the housecoat behind

- Terri Anne stops crying and regains her composure.
- She walks out of the gym bathroom without taking the housecoat off its hook.
- Mike follows while respecting the choice she has made.
- Terri Anne returns to find Wolfee.

## 12. Terri Anne apologizes to Wolfee

- Terri Anne apologizes to Wolfee for running away and for the awkward interruption.
- Wolfee listens respectfully and does not stare or make her feel ashamed.
- He accepts her apology and makes clear that he did not intend to intrude.
- The exchange allows both of them to move past the embarrassment.

## 13. They finish breakfast

- Terri Anne no longer believes Mike wants her to leave and forget him.
- She has also faced Wolfee instead of allowing the embarrassment to drive her from the mansion.
- Terri Anne and Mike return to the industrial kitchen and finish their homemade blueberry-waffle breakfast.
- The earlier tension fades as they settle back into one another's company.

## 14. Mike and Terri Anne shower together

- After breakfast, Mike and Terri Anne take a hot shower together.
- The shared shower is another consensual and affectionate part of their morning.
- They enjoy the closeness without allowing the earlier embarrassment to control the rest of their time together.
- Afterward, they get dressed and prepare to leave the mansion.

## 15. Mike takes Terri Anne home

- Mike drives Terri Anne back to her apartment.
- She is eager to return to Patti Anne after spending the night away.
- Mike respects her responsibility to her daughter and does not pressure her to remain at the mansion longer.
- During the drive, both of them understand that their relationship has changed since the previous evening.

## 16. Closing beat

- Mike brings Terri Anne safely to her apartment.
- She returns home knowing that he did not reject her after an embarrassing and vulnerable morning.
- Mike leaves knowing that Terri Anne trusted him with her real name and a deeply personal part of herself.
- The chapter ends with their first date complete and both of them wondering what comes next.

## Chapter Purpose

This chapter:

- Shows Mike finding Terri Anne crying in the gym bathroom.
- Reveals Terri Anne's fear that Mike wants her to leave and forget him.
- Has Mike clearly reject that idea and reassure her.
- Establishes Wolfee as a discreet and trusted longtime house manager.
- Uses Mike's reference to Wolfee accidentally seeing Ashlee in her birthday suit to normalize the interruption.
- Shows Mike giving Terri Anne space and respecting her boundaries.
- Leaves the decision about wearing the housecoat entirely to Terri Anne.
- Shows Terri Anne regaining her composure and apologizing to Wolfee.
- Has Mike and Terri Anne finish their breakfast and take a hot shower together.
- Ends with Mike driving Terri Anne safely back to her apartment.
- Deepens their relationship through reassurance, trust, and consensual intimacy.
