# Chapter Fifty-Two — Kassandra Meets Geoffrey

## Timeline

- This is part of the future 2029 storyline.
- Ashlee, Kassandra, Yelena, and Isobel are twenty-one when the Four Musketeers reconnect.
- They speak together for the first time in eighteen months.

## 1. Kassandra accepts Ashlee's invitation

- After finishing at Key Porn Studios Los Angeles, Kassandra follows through on her promise to visit Ashlee's mansion.
- Ashlee wants Kassandra to meet Geoffrey and begin returning to her life outside the studio.
- Kassandra is nervous because she knows Geoffrey has become extremely important to Ashlee.
- Ashlee reassures her that the visit is not a test.

## 2. They arrive at the mansion

- Ashlee returns in her specially adapted van with Kassandra accompanying her.
- Mike returns to the mansion with them under the protection of his security detail.
- The mansion remains under increased security after Victoria's break-in.
- Kassandra notices the guarded entrances and understands that Ashlee's recent fear was not exaggerated.
- Ashlee uses her special power chair because Victoria destroyed her regular wheelchair.

## 3. Geoffrey comes home from the university

- Geoffrey returns from his professor-assistant teaching position at the university.
- He is relieved to see Ashlee safely home after her visit to Key Porn Studios.
- Ashlee introduces Kassandra as one of her oldest and closest friends.
- She explains that Mike has known Kassandra for sixteen years, since the girls met at age five.

## 4. Kassandra meets Geoffrey

- Kassandra and Geoffrey greet each other for the first time.
- Geoffrey knows that Kassandra is one of the Four Musketeers and was an important part of Ashlee's earlier life.
- Kassandra knows that Geoffrey remained beside Ashlee while Jack, Ric, and Victoria attacked their relationship.
- Both want Ashlee to feel that the people she loves can trust one another.

## 5. Kassandra sees their relationship

- Kassandra notices the ease with which Geoffrey includes Ashlee in decisions and respects her independence.
- Geoffrey does not treat Ashlee as fragile because she uses a wheelchair.
- Ashlee tells Kassandra that Geoffrey stood beside her throughout Victoria's invasion and the destruction in their home.
- Kassandra understands why Ashlee wanted her to meet him.

## 6. Geoffrey welcomes Kassandra

- Geoffrey tells Kassandra she is welcome in their home.
- He does not judge her work at Key Porn Studios.
- He thanks Kassandra for defending Ashlee when the two actresses mocked her power chair.
- Kassandra says standing up for Ashlee was never a difficult choice.

## 7. Ashlee reminds Kassandra about the call

- Ashlee reminds Kassandra that she offered to help tell Yelena and Isobel about Kassandra's new career.
- Kassandra admits that she is still nervous about how the twins will respond.
- Ashlee promises to stay beside her during the entire conversation.
- Geoffrey gives them privacy while remaining available if Ashlee needs him.

## 8. Ashlee and Kassandra call the twins

- Ashlee and Kassandra place a call to Yelena and Isobel together.
- The twins are surprised to see Kassandra after eighteen months without her in the Four Musketeers' group chat.
- Ashlee explains that Kassandra has something important she wants to tell them herself.
- Kassandra struggles to begin, but Ashlee does not take the story away from her.

## 9. Kassandra reveals her new career

- Kassandra tells Yelena and Isobel that she works as an adult-film performer for Key Porn Studios.
- She explains that a casting agent connected to Key Porn Studios Berlin saw her perform onstage and contacted Charlene.
- Charlene hired her, and Kassandra has since worked on studio productions.
- She admits that embarrassment about her new career contributed to her silence.

## 10. Kassandra explains what happened with her father

- Kassandra tells the twins about her father's presence at the Florida amateur night and his decision to reject her.
- She explains that he appeared again during the mountain scene a couple of months earlier.
- Seeing him caused her to fall into a deep depression.
- She apologizes for leaving the group chat rather than trusting her friends with the truth.

## 11. Yelena and Isobel respond

- Yelena and Isobel are surprised by Kassandra's career but do not reject her.
- They are more hurt that she suffered alone than angry about the work she chose.
- The twins tell Kassandra that leaving the chat did not erase the years they spent as the Four Musketeers.
- They also thank Ashlee for helping Kassandra make the call.

## 12. The Four Musketeers reconnect

- Ashlee, Kassandra, Yelena, and Isobel speak together for the first time in eighteen months.
- Kassandra agrees to return to their group chat when she feels ready.
- Ashlee reminds everyone that rebuilding trust may take time and does not need to happen in one conversation.
- The call gives Kassandra another reason to believe she can heal without hiding her career.

## 13. Geoffrey rejoins them

- After the call, Geoffrey rejoins Ashlee and Kassandra.
- He can see that both women have been emotional.
- Kassandra thanks Geoffrey for giving them privacy and for welcoming her into the home.
- Geoffrey says anyone who loves and protects Ashlee will be treated with respect by him.

## 14. Kassandra remembers their conversations about families

- After the call, Kassandra remembers earlier Four Musketeers conversations about the families they hoped to have someday.
- Ashlee, Kassandra, Yelena, and Isobel once shared their different hopes, fears, and questions about becoming parents.
- Kassandra realizes that Ashlee's disability and transgender identity do not erase her desire to have a family with Geoffrey.
- The restored friendship makes Kassandra think seriously about how she might help.

## 15. Kassandra offers to be their surrogate

- Kassandra tells Ashlee and Geoffrey that she is willing to become their surrogate.
- The offer surprises both of them because it involves her body, health, future, and relationship with their family.
- Kassandra says she is offering because she loves Ashlee and remembers the Four Musketeers' conversations about having families.
- Geoffrey does not accept immediately or treat Kassandra's offer as a simple solution.

## 16. Ashlee asks whether Kassandra is certain

- Ashlee asks Kassandra whether she is certain about offering to carry a baby for them.
- She does not want Kassandra making such a major decision from guilt, depression, or gratitude after the studio confrontation.
- Kassandra says she understands that an offer is only the beginning of a much longer medical, legal, and emotional discussion.
- Ashlee remains deeply moved but refuses to rush the decision.

## 17. Mike explains the previous-pregnancy requirement

- Mike joins the conversation and tells them that Kassandra cannot immediately become an approved surrogate without having carried a baby before.
- He explains that reputable surrogacy programs generally require a potential carrier to have completed at least one prior healthy pregnancy.
- Kassandra has not carried a baby before, so her offer cannot simply proceed as proposed.
- Mike is not rejecting Kassandra's love for Ashlee; he is warning them that medical and program requirements matter.
- Ashlee and Geoffrey thank Kassandra for the offer even though it may not be possible.

## 18. Ashlee and Kassandra start laughing

- Ashlee and Kassandra suddenly begin laughing out loud after Mike explains the previous-pregnancy requirement.
- Their reaction confuses Mike and Geoffrey, who do not yet know about an old Four Musketeers conversation.
- The laughter comes from remembering how simply the friends once imagined solving Ashlee's future family questions.

## 19. They reveal the friends' old offer

- Ashlee and Kassandra tell Mike and Geoffrey that Kassandra, Yelena, and Isobel had all offered to sleep with Ashlee's current boyfriend—Geoffrey—and become pregnant for her.
- The three friends viewed the offer as a direct way to help Ashlee and Geoffrey have a child.
- Kassandra explains that none of them has acted on the idea and that it is an informal offer among extremely close friends, not a medical or legal surrogacy plan.
- Geoffrey is surprised to learn that all three women made that offer involving him.
- Mike understands why Ashlee and Kassandra found his formal explanation so different from the Four Musketeers' old plan.

## 20. Ashlee tells Geoffrey about the movie

- Ashlee tells Geoffrey about the proposed Key Porn Studios movie centred on a disabled woman.
- She explains that the Los Angeles studio has struggled to find a disabled adult woman for the lead role.
- Ashlee tells him that she is quite interested in appearing in the movie.
- She explains that the movie was originally written for straight actors.
- Because Ashlee is a transgender woman, the writer must revise the script and its planned preferences to fit Ashlee if she receives the role.

## 21. Geoffrey asks whether Ashlee is certain

- Geoffrey asks Ashlee whether she is certain that she wants to appear in the movie.
- His question gives Ashlee an opportunity to think aloud rather than deciding for her.
- Ashlee tells him that she is certain she wants to be in the movie.
- She promises that she will let Geoffrey know if and when the filming will happen.

## 22. Mike and Kassandra explain the audition

- Mike and Kassandra remind Ashlee that expressing interest does not place her in the movie immediately.
- Ashlee must still complete an audition test for the role.
- The audition is a separate casting requirement used to assess her performance; it is not required before the writer can change the script.
- Because the original movie was written for straight actors, the writer must revise the script for Ashlee's preferences as a transgender woman.
- If Ashlee completes the audition successfully, she can move forward with the revised movie.
- Before filming, Ashlee is tested for sexually transmitted diseases and HIV as part of the production's health process.
- Geoffrey supports Ashlee receiving all the production information before filming begins.

## 23. Ashlee's audition will become her first movie

- Mike and Kassandra explain that Ashlee's audition test will be recorded as an audition movie.
- Key Porn Studios has a company-wide policy requiring every movie to wait six months after filming before website release.
- Ashlee's recorded audition will be posted on the studio website as her first movie after that six-month policy period.
- Ashlee receives sixty percent of the purchase price each time the first movie is purchased through the website.
- Ashlee later completes a second movie based on the revised production script.
- Both movies are released during UCLA's summer break, when Ashlee is not attending classes.
- When Ashlee and Geoffrey return to UCLA, fans approach her on campus.
- UCLA's president asks whether she wants a porn career or a music career.
- Ashlee says she wants a porn career instead of becoming a composer.
- Ashlee withdraws from UCLA for now so she can concentrate on her new career.
- She keeps open the possibility of returning to her music education later.
- Ashlee films four additional movies over the summer and six more from January through May.
- Including her first two movies, Ashlee has made twelve movies in total.
- Ashlee also works at the Key Porn Studios booth during the AVN Adult Entertainment Expo, representing the studio and meeting fans.
- On the Expo floor, another performer confronts Ashlee out of jealousy over her rapid success and insults her disability.
- Ashlee responds firmly and professionally, refuses to escalate the confrontation, and returns to working at the booth.
- Ashlee and Geoffrey understand that the audition will therefore become public studio content rather than remaining a private casting test.

## 24. Kassandra reveals her movie count

- Kassandra tells Mike, Ashlee, and Geoffrey that she has made **twenty movies in eighteen months** at Key Porn Studios.
- The movie whose filming they witnessed that day is not included in that total.
- That production will be Kassandra's next movie once it is completed and counted.
- Ashlee realizes that Kassandra's new career is far more established than she initially understood.

## 25. Mike explains his departure plans

- Mike tells Kassandra, Ashlee, and Geoffrey about his plans to leave Los Angeles.
- He explains that he will fly overnight and expects to arrive in Toronto at approximately six o'clock in the morning.
- Mike reassures Ashlee that his departure will not remove the protection around her home.
- He will leave security guards stationed around Ashlee and Geoffrey's mansion.
- The guards will protect the couple and monitor the property while the threat from Victoria and the new assassin remains unresolved.
- Kassandra understands that the guards are there because the danger is real, not because Mike doubts Ashlee or Geoffrey.

## 26. Closing beat

- Kassandra has met Geoffrey, disclosed her career to Yelena and Isobel, and renewed her bond with the Four Musketeers.
- Her offer to carry a baby remains appreciated even though Mike identified major surrogacy barriers.
- Ashlee has told Geoffrey that she wants the Key Porn Studios role and understands that her audition will become her first website movie.
- Mike will return to Toronto while leaving guards around Ashlee and Geoffrey's mansion.
- The chapter ends with Ashlee preparing for a public audition while considering Kassandra's experience across twenty completed movies.

## Future in-person reunion

- At the end of the university semester in June, Yelena and Isobel come to Ashlee and Geoffrey's mansion.
- Ashlee, Kassandra, Yelena, and Isobel are all twenty-one.
- Yelena attends Cornell University and studies linguistics.
- Isobel attends the University of Alabama and studies nursing.
- This is the first time the Four Musketeers have been physically together since July of the year they graduated from Hillfield Strathallan College.
- Their mansion reunion continues the reconciliation that began during the call.
- The adult friends revisit the family agreement they discussed during high school but did not act on as minors.
- Ashlee, Geoffrey, Kassandra, Yelena, and Isobel each consent freely as adults.
- Geoffrey later sleeps separately with Kassandra, Yelena, and Isobel as the adult Four Musketeers agree.
- He uses protection during every encounter because the group does not want anyone to become pregnant while university studies are still underway.
- Their long-standing family plan is postponed.
