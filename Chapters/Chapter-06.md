# Chapter Six — The Blessing

## 1. A private moment in Los Angeles

- The chapter begins shortly after Ashlee and Geoffrey have slept together.
- The intimacy remains off-page; the focus is on their quiet conversation afterward.
- They are relaxed together in the Los Angeles home they share.
- The private setting allows them to speak openly about family, Christmas, and their future.

## 2. Ashlee tells Geoffrey about Mike's visit

- Ashlee tells Geoffrey about her weekly telephone call with Mike.
- She explains that Mike does not mind their plan to spend Christmas with Geoffrey's family.
- She also tells Geoffrey that Mike intends to visit them at the beginning of December so the three of them can celebrate Christmas early.
- Ashlee is pleased that she will not have to choose between Geoffrey and the brother who has always protected her.

## 3. Geoffrey is relieved

- Geoffrey tells Ashlee that he is very glad Mike is coming in December.
- His reaction is stronger than Ashlee expects from someone merely anticipating a holiday visit.
- Ashlee realizes that Geoffrey has been waiting for an opportunity to speak with Mike face-to-face.
- Geoffrey admits that Mike's visit will give him the chance he needs.

## 4. Geoffrey reveals his intention

- Geoffrey tells Ashlee that he has a serious question to ask Mike.
- He intends to ask Mike for his blessing concerning their future together.
- Geoffrey understands that Ashlee makes her own decisions and does not need anyone's permission.
- He wants Mike's blessing because Mike is the family member Ashlee trusts most and the person who protected her when their mother and siblings did not.

## 5. Ashlee's response

- Ashlee is touched that Geoffrey respects the importance of her relationship with Mike.
- She reminds him that Mike may ask direct and difficult questions before giving his blessing.
- Geoffrey says he is prepared to answer honestly.
- Their conversation shows that the blessing is not about ownership or permission; it is about welcoming Mike into the future they hope to build.

## 6. The conservative family concern

- Ashlee and Geoffrey briefly discuss the upcoming Christmas visit with his conservative family.
- Ashlee admits that she is nervous about how the visit may unfold.
- Geoffrey reassures her that he will stand beside her.
- His plan to speak with Mike gains additional emotional importance because he wants Mike to know that he intends to protect and support Ashlee.

## 7. Closing moment

- Geoffrey does not rehearse the exact words he plans to use with Mike.
- He says that the question must be asked properly and in person.
- Ashlee realizes that the early Christmas celebration may become a turning point in their lives.
- Ashlee and Geoffrey kiss each other goodnight.
- They tell each other how deeply they love one another.
- The chapter ends with them holding each other and anticipating Mike's December arrival.

## Chapter Purpose

This chapter:

- Shows Ashlee and Geoffrey's loving adult relationship without explicit sexual detail.
- Continues directly from Ashlee's weekly call with Mike.
- Confirms that Mike will visit Los Angeles in early December.
- Reveals that Geoffrey intends to ask Mike for his blessing.
- Explains why Mike's opinion matters so much to the couple.
- Reinforces Ashlee's independence while honoring her bond with Mike.
- Builds anticipation for the December meeting.
