# Chapter Twenty-One — Their First Date

## 1. Saturday evening arrives

- Saturday evening arrives after Mike's disturbing morning conversation with Bryan.
- Mike and Scout's first date is scheduled at **Mandarin Restaurant on Upper James Street**.
- Mike refuses to let Victoria, Cooper, or the investigation take away the evening Scout freely chose to share with him.
- He remains alert to possible danger without telling Scout enough to frighten her before their date begins.

## 2. Scout prepares for the date

- Scout prepares to meet Mike somewhere other than the Blue Velvet Room.
- For the first time, she chooses how she wants him to see her rather than dressing for a shift.
- She feels nervous but reminds herself that she was the one who invited him.
- The date is not repayment for Mike's help; it is something she genuinely wants.

## 3. Mike drives himself

- Mike does not allow Cooper to drive him to the restaurant.
- Cooper does not yet know that Mike saw the photograph from The Well.
- Mike drives himself to Mandarin Restaurant.
- Choosing to drive allows him to keep Cooper away without revealing that he is under investigation.
- Any Key Corporation security presence remains discreet so the evening can still feel like a simple date.

## 4. Mike and Scout meet at Mandarin

- Mike and Scout meet at Mandarin Restaurant on Upper James.
- Mike respects Scout's wish for a simple evening and does not attempt to reserve the entire restaurant or create an extravagant display.
- They greet each other with visible nervousness and genuine happiness.
- Away from the Blue Velvet Room, they must learn how to interact without the roles of customer, dancer, owner, and employee dominating every moment.

## 5. They settle into the date

- Mike and Scout are shown to their table and begin choosing their food.
- The busy restaurant gives them privacy without isolation.
- Their first few moments are slightly awkward, but the familiar ease of their earlier conversations gradually returns.
- Mike lets Scout set the pace of the evening.

## 6. Scout asks about Mike's ordinary life

- Scout asks what Mike enjoys when he is not working or managing Key Corporation.
- She wants to know whether the ordinary man she met at the bar was real.
- Mike answers without turning the conversation into a display of his wealth.
- He admits that being treated normally was one reason he valued their first conversations.

## 7. Mike learns more about Scout

- Mike asks Scout about her interests, hopes, and favorite things without pressing for her real name or private history.
- Scout shares only what she feels ready to share.
- Mike continues calling her Scout and does not reveal what Bryan's report taught him.
- His restraint helps her relax and enjoy the date.

## 8. The investigation remains in Mike's thoughts

- Mike occasionally remembers the photograph of Victoria, Cooper, and Adam at The Well.
- He worries that Victoria may have sabotaged his past relationships and may now target Scout.
- He does not want to make Scout feel watched or use the threat as a reason to control her.
- Mike quietly resolves to strengthen her protection without ruining the evening.

## 9. The will remains unspoken

- Mike also thinks about his mother's will and the eighteen-month deadline.
- He does not tell Scout about the marriage-and-child requirement during their first date.
- He refuses to treat the evening as the beginning of a rushed plan to keep Key Corporation.
- His only goal is to spend time with Scout and allow their relationship to develop naturally.

## 10. Their connection deepens

- As the meal continues, Mike and Scout become more comfortable.
- They laugh together and enjoy being somewhere neither Adam nor the Blue Velvet Room controls.
- Scout sees that Mike can accept her choices without using money to dominate the evening.
- Mike recognizes that his feelings for her are becoming stronger.

## 11. Scout asks to go home with Mike

- When the meal ends, Mike thanks Scout for inviting him.
- He tells her that the simple date meant more to him than an extravagant evening would have.
- Scout is relieved that the relationship feels different outside the club.
- She asks Mike whether he wants to take her back to his home.

## 12. Mike confirms her choice

- Mike is surprised by Scout's request.
- He asks whether she is certain that she wants to go to his home.
- Scout tells him clearly that she is certain.
- Mike accepts her answer only after giving her an opportunity to reconsider without embarrassment or pressure.
- Her invitation is presented as her freely made decision rather than repayment for his protection.

## 13. Scout calls the babysitter

- While Mike drives Scout toward his mansion in Carlisle, she calls the babysitter to check on Patti Anne.
- The babysitter tells her that Patti Anne is sleeping peacefully.
- Scout explains that she is accompanying her date back to his house.
- She reminds the babysitter that her milk for Patti Anne's morning feeding is in the refrigerator.
- The babysitter reassures Scout that it is no problem and that Patti Anne is safe and comfortable.
- Hearing that her daughter is sleeping peacefully and has milk ready for the morning allows Scout to relax during the drive.

## 14. Closing hook

- Mike continues driving Scout toward his Carlisle mansion.
- Scout believes the greatest danger came from Adam and Don, while Mike now suspects Victoria and Cooper are part of something larger.
- Mike remains aware that Scout may change her mind at any time.
- The chapter ends with Scout reassured about Patti Anne and willingly continuing to Mike’s home for the first time.

## Chapter Purpose

This chapter:

- Gives Mike and Scout their first date at Mandarin Restaurant on Upper James.
- Shows Scout choosing the date and controlling its pace.
- Has Mike drive himself so Cooper remains unaware that he is suspected.
- Allows Mike and Scout to relate outside the Blue Velvet Room.
- Keeps Scout's real identity private.
- Shows Mike refusing to let his mother's will rush the relationship.
- Deepens their mutual affection.
- Has Scout call the babysitter and learn that Patti Anne is sleeping peacefully.
- Ends with Scout choosing to accompany Mike to his home after he confirms that she is certain.
- Balances the warmth of the date against the danger posed by Victoria, Cooper, Adam, and Don.
