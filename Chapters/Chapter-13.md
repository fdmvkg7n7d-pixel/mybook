# Chapter Thirteen — The Mysterious Owner

## 1. Scout enters the changing room

- Scout arrives at approximately 7:30 p.m. through the employee entrance.
- The employee door opens directly into the dancers' changing room.
- She does not pass through the public area and therefore does not see Mike's limousine or understand the scale of the security operation.
- She begins preparing for her 8:00 p.m. shift while still believing Adam and Don control the club.

## 2. Scout gets dressed

- Terri Anne changes into Scout's stage outfit:
  - A **yellow babydoll lingerie top**;
  - An **ivory V-string**;
  - **Ivory thigh-high nylons**.
- Getting dressed as Scout requires her to put her private identity as Terri Anne aside.
- She tries to gather the courage to face the shift and the cousins' coercive demand afterward.

## 3. Another dancer brings unexpected news

- While Scout is getting dressed, another dancer enters the changing room.
- The dancer excitedly tells her that the Blue Velvet Room's mysterious owner has arrived.
- She says that the owner has come to fire Adam and Don.
- Scout is stunned because none of the dancers knew who owned the club or expected the owner to appear.
- The news gives her hope, but she is afraid to believe that Adam and Don's control may truly be ending.

## 4. Scout goes directly to the bar

- Scout leaves the changing room and walks straight to the bar.
- She wants confirmation from someone she trusts more than the club's rumors.
- Marco is behind the bar and immediately recognizes how frightened and confused she is.
- Scout asks him what is happening and whether the mysterious owner is really inside the building.

## 5. Marco confirms the news and gives Scout the drinks

- Marco confirms that the mysterious owner has arrived and taken control of the club.
- Scout asks whether Adam and Don are truly being fired.
- Marco tells her that Key Corporation security has secured the building and that the cousins no longer control the employees.
- He prepares and gives Scout two familiar drinks:
  - Mike's **Johnnie Walker on ice**;
  - Scout's **virgin Bloody Mary with extra celery sticks**.
- Scout takes both drinks in her hands.

## 6. The owner wants to meet Scout

- Marco tells Scout that the mysterious owner specifically wants to meet her.
- He explains that the owner is waiting in the manager's office.
- Scout becomes nervous because Adam used that office to threaten her, and Don was present during the ultimatum.
- Marco reassures her that the situation has changed and that Key Corporation security now controls the office.
- Worried about Scout's safety, Marco decides that he will check on her shortly.

## 7. Scout approaches the manager's office

- Carrying both drinks, Scout walks from the bar toward the manager's office.
- Key Corporation security guards remain stationed outside the room.
- Charlene and Olivia are elsewhere in the club carrying out their responsibilities and are not waiting inside the office.
- Every step brings Terri Anne closer to the room where Adam and Don tried to control her.

## 8. The guards open the door

- When Scout reaches the manager's office, the security guards open the door for her.
- She enters cautiously while carrying Mike's drink and her own.
- The office no longer feels like Adam's private territory; Mike's security team controls access to it.

## 9. Scout sees Mike from behind

- Inside the office, Scout sees the mysterious owner only from the back.
- She does not immediately understand that the man before her is the customer she knows as Mike.
- Before turning around, Mike recognizes her arrival and says, **“Hello, Scout. Take a seat on the couch.”**
- Scout recognizes Mike's voice and freezes in surprise.
- The chapter ends as she realizes that Mike and the mysterious owner are the same man.

## Chapter Purpose

This chapter:

- Establishes that the employee entrance opens directly into the changing room.
- Shows Scout preparing for work in her yellow and ivory stage outfit.
- Allows another dancer to announce that the mysterious owner has arrived to fire Adam and Don.
- Sends Scout directly to Marco at the bar.
- Restores the two familiar drinks to Scout's hands before she enters the office.
- Shows Marco becoming concerned about Scout's safety.
- Establishes that the mysterious owner is waiting for Scout while Charlene and Olivia work elsewhere.
- Has security open the manager's office door for Scout.
- Ends with Scout recognizing Mike's voice and realizing that he is the mysterious owner.
