# Chapter Fifty-Five — Mike's Text Message

## 1. An early morning at the Carlisle mansion

- Terri Anne is awake with Patti Anne at the Carlisle mansion early in the morning.
- Mei and Tsumugi are also awake before Tsumugi prepares for school.
- Terri Anne carries Patti Anne while beginning to prepare something for her to eat.
- Patti Anne watches everything her mother does from the security of her arms.

## 2. Terri Anne receives a text

- Terri Anne's phone signals that a new text message has arrived.
- She checks the screen while continuing to hold Patti Anne securely.
- The message is from Mike.
- Terri Anne immediately smiles when she sees his name.

## 3. Mike tells her he landed safely

- Mike writes that his Key Corporation airplane landed safely in Toronto around six o'clock in the morning.
- He explains that he must stop at the investigator's office before returning to the Carlisle mansion.
- He tells Terri Anne that he will come home after that meeting.
- Mike ends the message by telling Terri Anne that he loves her and is looking forward to seeing her and Patti Anne.

## 4. Terri Anne tells Mei and Tsumugi

- Mei notices Terri Anne smiling and asks whether the message is from Mike.
- Terri Anne tells Mei and Tsumugi what Mike wrote.
- She explains that he has landed safely but is going to the investigator's office before coming home.
- Tsumugi understands that Mike is close but not yet ready to walk through the mansion's door.

## 5. Tsumugi uses a travel metaphor

- Tsumugi says Mike is like a book returned to the right library but not yet placed back on its shelf.
- Terri Anne understands that Tsumugi means Mike has returned to Ontario but has not reached home.
- Mei appreciates that Terri Anne listens to the meaning in Tsumugi's metaphor.
- Patti Anne responds to the voices without understanding why her mother is smiling.

## 6. Terri Anne prepares Patti Anne's cookie with her breast milk

- Still carrying Patti Anne, Terri Anne begins preparing a baby-appropriate cookie with her own breast milk.
- She uses a cookie suitable for Patti Anne's age rather than giving her a hard adult cookie.
- Terri Anne softens it with her breast milk and checks the texture carefully.
- Patti Anne recognizes the familiar preparation because Terri Anne has fed her this cookie softened with breast milk before.

## 7. Tsumugi asks about the cookie

- Tsumugi asks why Terri Anne is preparing the cookie with **Terri Anne's breast milk**.
- She wants to know why the cookie must be softened and how Terri Anne knows when it is ready.
- Terri Anne explains that her milk changes the texture and makes the baby-appropriate cookie easier for Patti Anne to manage.
- She checks that it is soft and offers only an appropriate amount while supervising Patti Anne.

## 8. Questions about milk and texture

- Tsumugi asks whether the cookie tastes different after it absorbs the milk.
- She asks whether Patti Anne recognizes the familiar taste of Terri Anne's breast milk in the cookie she has eaten before.
- Terri Anne says Patti Anne recognizes both the milk she receives while nursing and the familiar softened cookie, even though she cannot explain the experience in words.
- Tsumugi compares the repeated preparation to hearing a favourite song played the same comforting way.

## 9. Tsumugi asks how Terri Anne learned

- Tsumugi asks who taught Terri Anne how to prepare food for Patti Anne.
- Terri Anne explains that caring for Patti Anne involves advice, observation, practice, and learning what works safely for her daughter.
- She does not pretend that every baby eats the same foods or follows the same routine.
- Mei thanks Terri Anne for once again treating Tsumugi's questions seriously.

## 10. Patti Anne receives her food

- Terri Anne finishes preparing the cookie softened with her breast milk.
- She keeps Patti Anne close and supervises her throughout the feeding.
- Patti Anne responds comfortably to the familiar softened texture and the taste of Terri Anne's milk.
- Tsumugi watches without crowding mother or baby.

## 11. Mei thanks Terri Anne

- Tsumugi leaves the room to get ready for school.
- While her younger half sister is preparing, Mei privately thanks Terri Anne for treating Tsumugi like a regular teenager.
- Mei explains that many adults will not have a proper conversation with Tsumugi.
- Some speak around her, oversimplify everything, or direct their questions to Mei instead of speaking to Tsumugi herself.
- Mei appreciates that Terri Anne listens to Tsumugi's metaphors, answers her questions seriously, and speaks directly to her.
- Terri Anne tells Mei, **“You're welcome.”**
- She says Tsumugi deserves the same respect as any other teenager.

## 12. Terri Anne thinks about Mike coming home

- Terri Anne looks again at Mike's text while Patti Anne eats.
- She feels relieved that he landed safely and will soon return to them.
- She begins thinking about what she can do to Mike when he returns home and how she wants to welcome him after the trip.
- Terri Anne keeps the exact idea to herself while imagining their reunion.
- The investigator's office reminds her that the danger involving Victoria, Adam, Cooper, and Don has not disappeared.
- Even so, Mike's message gives the household a hopeful beginning to the morning.

## 13. Closing beat

- Terri Anne carries Patti Anne again after the feeding is complete.
- She tells Mei and Tsumugi that Mike will be home after his stop at the investigator's office.
- Tsumugi continues thinking about the cookie, the milk, and her metaphor for Mike's unfinished journey home.
- The chapter ends with Terri Anne smiling at Mike's message while waiting for him to return to the Carlisle mansion.
