# Chapter Forty-Eight — Questions for Don

## 1. Don has been taken off the streets

- Mike's investigator confirms that Don was taken while Mike was aboard the Key Corporation airplane.
- The timing prevents Adam and Cooper from confidently connecting Mike to Don's disappearance.
- Don is moved to a secure private location where he cannot contact Victoria, Adam, or Cooper.
- He does not know where he has been taken or who ultimately ordered the operation.

## 2. The text Mike received

- The text Mike received outside the Key Porn Studios Los Angeles conference room concerned Don.
- The investigator reported that Don had been secured and that questioning was beginning.
- Mike still intends to speak with Don personally, but the investigator will gather as much information as possible first.
- Scott does not see the confidential message.

## 3. Bryan begins questioning Don

- Bryan sits across from Don and begins asking a long series of questions.
- Don initially demands to know who Bryan is and whether Mike sent him.
- Bryan does not reveal the extent of his wider surveillance network or confirm Mike's involvement.
- Every question is designed to determine how much Don knows about Victoria's conspiracy.

## 4. Questions about Terri Anne and Patti Anne

- The investigator asks why Don followed Terri Anne and Patti Anne on Sunday.
- He asks who gave Don the order and what Don intended to do if he caught them.
- Don is questioned about the route, the Y, and why he discarded his blue jacket and hat.
- The investigator does not tell Don that both garments were secretly recovered and reconstructed.

## 5. Questions about Adam and Cooper

- The investigator asks how long Don has worked with Adam and Cooper.
- He asks whether Adam knew about the pursuit and whether Cooper used his access to Mike's household or travel information.
- Don is asked why Adam and Cooper distrust him and why they excluded him from meetings.
- The questions make clear that someone knows far more about the three men than Don expected.

## 6. Questions about Victoria

- The investigator asks when Don first began speaking with Victoria Key.
- He wants to know what she promised, what instructions she gave, and what she said about Mike, Terri Anne, and Key Corporation.
- Don is asked whether Victoria directed the effort to separate Mike and Terri Anne.
- The investigator repeatedly asks what part of Victoria's complete plan remains hidden.

## 7. The fifteen-million-dollar promise

- The investigator asks Don whether Victoria promised him fifteen million dollars.
- He asks whether Adam and Cooper were offered the same amount.
- Don is questioned about where the money would come from and what had to happen before anyone was paid.
- The questions force Don to consider whether Victoria ever intended to keep her promise.

## 8. The physical task

- The investigator asks why Victoria said she still needed Don's strength.
- He demands to know what physical task Don was expected to perform.
- Don is asked whether the task involved Mike, Terri Anne, Patti Anne, or someone else in the Key family.
- The investigator watches Don closely because this may be the most dangerous part of the hidden plan.

## 9. Questions about The Well

- The investigator asks what Don knows about Victoria's meetings with Adam and Cooper at The Well.
- Don insists that he was excluded from the strategy meetings.
- The investigator asks what Adam and Cooper told him afterward and whether they ever discussed the men who approached Adam at the bar.
- Nothing in the questions reveals that one meeting was recorded through a listening bug beneath the table.

## 10. Questions about the assassin

- The investigator asks whether Don knows that Victoria hired an assassin to kill Mike.
- He asks whether Don knows anything about the professional assassin Victoria claims to have hired more recently.
- Don's reaction may help establish whether the assassination plans were shared with Victoria's three promised accomplices.
- The investigator refuses to explain how Mike learned about either threat.

## 11. Don becomes angry

- Don becomes increasingly angry as the questions continue.
- He accuses Adam and Cooper of betraying him or leaving him exposed.
- His resentment grows when he realizes they may still be free while he is being questioned.
- The investigator uses Don's anger to keep the focus on what Victoria, Adam, and Cooper concealed from him.

## 12. The investigator asks the same questions differently

- The investigator returns to important subjects from different directions.
- He compares Don's answers about Sunday, Victoria's promise, and the hidden physical task.
- Don begins to understand that obvious lies will be compared with evidence he has not seen.
- The investigator still does not reveal the photographs, audio, recovered clothing, or complete surveillance report.

## 13. Another investigator whispers nothing

- Bryan is personally present for the crucial stage of Don's questioning.
- Another investigator enters the room and walks over to Bryan.
- The investigator leans close to Bryan's ear and appears to whisper, but deliberately says **nothing**.
- Don cannot hear the silence and assumes Bryan has just received important information.
- The empty whisper is a performance intended to make Don believe that events are moving without him.

## 14. Bryan claims Adam and Cooper are talking

- Bryan looks surprised as though the other investigator has delivered unexpected news.
- He tells Don that Adam and Cooper are spilling everything to other investigators.
- In reality, Adam and Cooper are still free and searching for Don; they are not speaking to investigators.
- Don does not know that Bryan's statement is a bluff.
- He believes Adam and Cooper may be blaming him while trying to protect themselves.

## 15. Don decides to tell everything

- Don realizes that Victoria, Adam, and Cooper left him exposed while protecting themselves.
- He is angry that they excluded him, insulted him, and may now blame him for the conspiracy's failures.
- He is afraid of what Victoria or the new assassin may do if he continues protecting the group.
- He also wants revenge against all three conspirators for using his strength while withholding their complete strategy.
- Bryan's claim that Adam and Cooper are already talking convinces Don that remaining silent will only let them blame him.
- Anger, fear, revenge, and the apparent betrayal finally overcome Don's loyalty.
- He tells Bryan that he will reveal everything he knows.

## 16. Don starts spilling the conspiracy

- Don begins with Victoria's first approach and explains how she recruited him.
- He confirms the fifteen-million-dollar promise and what had to happen before he would be paid.
- He describes Adam's instructions, Cooper's access to Mike, and the information the conspirators kept from one another.
- Don admits why he followed Terri Anne and Patti Anne and what he intended to do if he caught them.
- He starts explaining the hidden physical task for which Victoria needed his strength.
- The investigator records the complete account without revealing how much Bryan's team already knows.

## 17. Closing hook

- Mike remains at Key Porn Studios Los Angeles while Don gives the investigator a full account.
- Adam and Cooper continue searching without realizing that Don is exposing them.
- Don intends his confession to protect himself and punish the three people who abandoned him.
- The chapter ends as the investigator prepares to send Mike and Bryan the first complete version of Victoria's hidden plan.

## Chapter Purpose

This chapter:

- Reveals the meaning of the investigator's text to Mike.
- Confirms that Don was taken while Mike was in the air.
- Has the investigator ask Don many questions about Terri Anne, Patti Anne, Adam, Cooper, and Victoria.
- Questions Don about the recovered clothing without revealing that it was found.
- Focuses on Victoria's fifteen-million-dollar promise and the hidden physical task.
- Asks whether Don knows about either assassin.
- Preserves Bryan's surveillance evidence and network as secrets from Don.
- Has another investigator pretend to whisper information into Bryan's ear while actually saying nothing.
- Shows Bryan bluffing that Adam and Cooper are already confessing to other investigators.
- Has Don decide to reveal everything because he is angry, afraid, seeking revenge, and believes the others betrayed him.
- Begins Don's complete account of Victoria's hidden plan.
