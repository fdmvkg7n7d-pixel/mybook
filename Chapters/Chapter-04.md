# Chapter Four — The Breakfast Report

## 1. A private breakfast at Mike's mansion

- Mike arranges an early-morning breakfast meeting at his mansion.
- **Bryan**, **Charlene**, and **Olivia** join him in a private dining room where they cannot be overheard.
- Breakfast is served, but the atmosphere is serious rather than social.
- Bryan arrives carrying the completed investigation report on Adam and Don.
- He provides copies of the report to Mike, Charlene, and Olivia.

## 2. Bryan presents his findings

- Bryan explains that Mike's instincts were correct: Adam and Don are corrupt.
- He reveals that **Adam and Don are cousins**, which helps explain their loyalty to one another and the way they coordinate their actions.
- The investigation has uncovered evidence that they are systematically blackmailing Scout and several of the other dancers at the Blue Velvet Room.
- Adam gathers private information about the dancers and uses their secrets and vulnerabilities to control them.
- Don supports his cousin by intimidating the women and making Adam's threats appear physically dangerous.
- Their conduct is not an isolated incident; it is an established pattern affecting multiple employees.

## 3. The evidence against Adam and Don

- Bryan's report contains records, witness information, financial evidence, and a timeline of Adam and Don's misconduct.
- The findings show how Adam profits from the dancers' fear while Don helps enforce his control.
- Olivia recognizes that the conduct creates serious legal and corporate consequences.
- Charlene is furious that workers connected to the adult-entertainment business have been exploited by the people responsible for protecting them.
- Mike realizes that Adam's reaction to the five hundred dollars was another expression of the same greed revealed in the report.

## 4. Bryan reveals Scout's real identity

- Bryan turns to the section of the report concerning the dancer known as Scout.
- He explains that her real name is **Terri Anne Williams**.
- She is eighteen years old.
- Mike hears her real name for the first time and quietly repeats it to himself.
- Bryan makes clear that he discovered her identity because her private life is part of the evidence against Adam—not because Mike asked him to investigate her personally.

## 5. Mike learns about Patti Anne

- The report reveals that Terri Anne has an **eight-month-old daughter named Patti Anne**.
- Adam discovered the existence of Patti Anne and has been using that knowledge to blackmail Terri Anne.
- Terri Anne has therefore been terrified to tell anyone at the club—or Mike—that she is a mother.
- Mike immediately understands why she repeatedly avoided questions about her home life.
- He is saddened that Terri Anne believed revealing her daughter would place both of them in danger.

## 6. Mike reacts to the discovery

- Mike's concern for Scout becomes more personal now that he knows her real name and understands what Adam has been doing to her.
- He is angry with Adam and Don but controls his emotions so they can plan carefully.
- Mike also feels uncomfortable possessing such private information before Terri Anne has chosen to trust him with it.
- He decides that he will not confront her with the knowledge or pretend that she told him herself.
- His immediate priority is to end the blackmail and protect Terri Anne, Patti Anne, and the other dancers.

## 7. Olivia and Charlene assess the danger

- Olivia reviews the report from the perspective of Mike and Key Corporation, identifying the evidence that must be preserved.
- Charlene focuses on protecting the employees and preventing Adam or Don from retaliating.
- They agree that a careless confrontation could cause Adam and Don to destroy evidence, threaten the dancers, or flee.
- Mike insists that none of the women should lose income or be punished for anything Adam forced them to do.

## 8. Mike orders Bryan to keep digging

- Although Bryan has already uncovered extensive misconduct, Mike believes there may be more hidden in Adam and Don's backgrounds.
- Mike orders Bryan to continue investigating both cousins.
- He wants Bryan to look for additional victims, concealed assets, former accomplices, and earlier incidents involving threats or blackmail.
- Adam and Don still do not know that Mike owns the Blue Velvet Room or that they have been investigated.
- Their ignorance gives Mike, Olivia, Charlene, and Bryan a temporary advantage.

## 9. Mike gives his other orders

- Mike tells Olivia to prepare the necessary corporate and legal documents.
- He instructs Charlene to develop a plan for protecting the dancers and keeping the club operational under honest management.
- Bryan is to preserve the existing evidence while continuing to investigate Adam and Don.
- Mike makes clear that Terri Anne and the other dancers are to be treated as victims of blackmail, not as suspects.

## 10. Charlene and Bryan leave

- The breakfast meeting comes to an end.
- Charlene leaves the mansion to begin preparing the operational response.
- Bryan departs to continue digging into the cousins' backgrounds.
- Olivia remains behind with Mike because the investigation report and related corporate documents must be secured.

## 11. Mike and Olivia enter his home office

- Mike takes Olivia to his private home office.
- He carries Bryan's report with him so that no household or corporate employee can see it.
- The office contains a wall safe concealed or secured behind part of the room's furnishings.
- Before locking away the report, Mike and Olivia make certain that all copies are accounted for.

## 12. Photographs of Ashlee

- Several photographs displayed on Mike's office walls show his youngest sister, **Ashlee**.
- One photograph shows Mike standing beside Ashlee in front of the **University of California, Los Angeles Music Building**.
- Ashlee is seated in her wheelchair in the photograph.
- A bullet shattered her T11 vertebra, leaving her without muscle control or sensation below T11.
- Ashlee was born on February 29, 2008. She is eighteen during the post-February 2026 storyline and twenty-one during the 2029 adult-career storyline.
- Mike is seventeen years older than Ashlee and is thirty-eight and a half in 2029.
- She was assigned male at birth and is Mike's younger sister.
- Their mother regarded Ashlee's birth as a terrible accident.
- Another photograph shows Ashlee with her three best girlfriends—**Kassandra**, **Yelena**, and **Isobel**—on their graduation day from **Hillfield Strathallan College**.
- Yelena and Isobel are twins.
- Mike affectionately calls Ashlee and her three best friends **the Four Musketeers**; the nickname is his for them, not one they chose for themselves.
- Mike's decision to keep several photographs of Ashlee in his private office shows that she remains important to him despite their mother's attitude.
- The photographs introduce Ashlee and hint that Mike's family history is more complicated than his public image suggests.

## 13. Mike tells Olivia about the Four Musketeers

- The graduation photograph prompts Mike to tell Olivia a fond story about Ashlee, Kassandra, Yelena, and Isobel.
- When the four girls were sixteen, they had a sleepover at Mike's home.
- Mike went looking for them because he had prepared a bedtime snack.
- He found them cuddled closely together on the swimming-pool deck, sharing a private and affectionate moment.
- Rather than intruding or embarrassing them, Mike quietly turned around and left them in peace.
- He tells Olivia that they were sixteen and that he trusted them wholeheartedly.
- The memory reveals Mike's protective but respectful relationship with Ashlee and her closest friends.

## 14. Mike locks away the report

- Mike places Bryan's report inside the wall safe.
- Olivia watches him lock it away and understands how personally invested he has become in Terri Anne's safety.
- Mike now knows the truth Scout was too frightened to tell him, but she still knows almost nothing about him.
- The chapter ends with Mike facing a decision: return to the Blue Velvet Room anonymously once more or reveal his identity and end Adam and Don's control.

## Chapter Purpose

This chapter:

- Confirms that Adam and Don are corrupt cousins working together.
- Reveals that they are blackmailing Scout and other dancers.
- Introduces the completed investigation report and Mike's order for Bryan to keep digging.
- Reveals Scout's real name, Terri Anne Williams, to Mike.
- Reveals that Terri Anne has an eight-month-old daughter named Patti Anne.
- Reframes Terri Anne's secrecy as a response to coercion and fear.
- Shows Mike, Olivia, Charlene, and Bryan planning to protect the victims.
- Introduces Ashlee through the photographs in Mike's home office.
- Introduces Kassandra, Yelena, and Isobel and establishes that Mike calls the four friends the Four Musketeers.
- Uses the nonsexual pool-deck memory to demonstrate Mike's trust in Ashlee and her closest friends.
- Hints at the Key family's difficult history and Mike's bond with his youngest sister.
- Sets up the confrontation that will end Adam and Don's control of the club.
