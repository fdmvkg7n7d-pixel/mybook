# Chapter Forty-Seven — Where Is Don?

## 1. Don stops answering

- Adam attempts to contact Don but receives no answer.
- Don does not return his calls or respond to his messages.
- At first, Adam assumes Don is being careless or deliberately avoiding him.
- As the silence continues, Adam's irritation turns into anger.

## 2. Adam contacts Cooper

- Adam calls Cooper and demands to know whether he has seen Don.
- Cooper has not heard from Don and does not know where he is.
- Adam dislikes Cooper's lack of an answer and accuses him of withholding information.
- Cooper becomes angry at being blamed for something he did not arrange.

## 3. Cooper tries Don again

- Cooper contacts every number he has for Don.
- None of the attempts produces an answer.
- Don has not appeared at any of the places where Cooper expects to find him.
- Cooper begins to understand that Don may not have disappeared voluntarily.

## 4. Adam assumes Don caused another problem

- Adam remembers Don's failed attempt to follow Terri Anne and Patti Anne.
- He argues that Don may have panicked after losing his blue jacket and hat.
- Adam calls Don a liability who acts with strength but not enough thought.
- He is furious that Don may have placed the entire conspiracy at risk again.

## 5. Cooper fears Don has been taken

- Cooper points out that Don usually answers when money is involved.
- With fifteen million dollars promised to him, Don would not casually abandon Victoria's plan.
- Cooper suggests that someone may have taken Don off the streets.
- The possibility turns their anger into fear.

## 6. They argue about who could have him

- Adam wonders whether police or private security identified Don after he followed Terri Anne.
- Cooper questions whether Don created enemies unrelated to Victoria's conspiracy.
- Neither man knows about Bryan or the investigators working for Mike.
- They still believe Mike may rely on security or police, but they have no evidence that he arranged Don's disappearance.

## 7. Adam suspects Don may be talking

- Adam becomes angry at the possibility that Don is being questioned.
- He worries that Don may reveal the meetings, the pursuit, and the promised fifteen-million-dollar payment.
- Cooper reminds Adam that Don resents being excluded and treated as hired strength.
- Both men know that resentment could make Don dangerous if someone offers him a way out.

## 8. Cooper tells Adam everything he knows

- Cooper tells Adam everything he knows about Mike's Los Angeles plans.
- He explains when Mike intended to fly, that Mike used a Key Corporation airplane, and that a large security detail surrounded him after he reached Los Angeles.
- Cooper also reveals that Mike deliberately showed him Terri Anne and Patti Anne were not alone at the Carlisle mansion.
- He names Charlene, Olivia, Mei, and Tsumugi as the guests staying with Terri Anne and Patti Anne.
- Cooper tells Adam that the guests appear to be part of Mike's plan to keep Terri Anne and Patti Anne protected while he is away.
- Although Cooper shares everything he knows, neither man realizes Mike ordered Don taken while the airplane was in the air.

## 9. Their anger turns against each other

- Adam accuses Cooper of failing to protect the plan despite his access to Mike.
- Cooper accuses Adam of provoking Don and treating him like an idiot.
- Each man believes the other may have missed a warning sign.
- Their argument exposes how little trust exists among Victoria's allies.

## 10. They consider contacting Victoria

- Adam wants to call Victoria immediately and demand an explanation.
- Cooper hesitates because Victoria may blame both of them for losing Don.
- They know she still needs Don's physical strength for part of her hidden plan.
- Don's disappearance could force Victoria to change whatever she intended him to do.

## 11. They cannot connect Don to Mike

- Adam and Cooper review the timing and realize Don vanished while Mike was flying to Los Angeles.
- Mike's absence makes it difficult for them to prove that he was involved.
- The timing achieves exactly what Mike intended: neither man can confidently connect him to the operation.
- They remain unaware that Mike ordered his investigator to take Don while he was in the air.

## 12. Closing hook

- Adam and Cooper are left angry, frightened, and uncertain about Don's location.
- They do not know whether Don ran, was arrested, or was taken by someone working outside the police.
- Their greatest fear is that Don is already talking.
- The chapter ends with Adam demanding that Cooper find Don before Victoria discovers how completely they have lost control.

## Chapter Purpose

This chapter:

- Shows Adam discovering that Don is missing and not answering.
- Shows Cooper failing to locate Don.
- Has Cooper tell Adam everything he knows about Mike's travel plans and Terri Anne's mansion guests.
- Turns their anger toward each other.
- Raises their fear that Don may reveal Victoria's conspiracy.
- Preserves the fact that Adam and Cooper do not know about Bryan or Mike's investigators.
- Uses Mike's flight timing to prevent them from confidently linking him to Don's disappearance.
- Threatens Victoria's hidden plan by removing the man whose physical strength she still needs.
