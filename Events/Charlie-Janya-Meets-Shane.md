## Confirmed Facts

- Charlie Janya and Shane begin dating immediately after their first meeting at Caesars Palace.
- Shane’s debts to loan sharks arise from his gambling problem. The loan sharks go to the family’s Las Vegas home because of Shane’s debts, and their arrival frightens Charlie Kasey.
- Shane is at Caesars Palace playing cards when Charlie Janya first sees him. After Charlie Janya finishes meeting her client, she comes downstairs, sees Shane again, and Shane offers to buy her a drink.
- Charlie Janya and Shane do not marry. They live in Las Vegas and later have their daughter, Charlie Kasey, together.
- Shane knows extensive details about Charlie Janya’s bank accounts. He has debts to loan sharks, does not seek treatment for his gambling problem, and spends two million dollars belonging to Charlie Janya on gambling.
- Charlie Janya and Shane are both twenty-four when they meet.
- Charlie Janya works at the Bunny Ranch as an adult.
- Charlie Janya goes to Caesars Palace to meet a client connected to her work.
- Shane is not the client Charlie Janya is scheduled to meet.
- Charlie Janya and Shane meet separately at Caesars Palace.
- All references to Charlie Janya’s adult work remain non-graphic.
