# Shane — Gambling Problem and Charlie Janya’s Money

## Confirmed Facts

- Shane owes the loan sharks one million dollars ($1,000,000) from his gambling problem.
- When the loan sharks confront Charlie Janya at her Las Vegas home, the head loan shark offers to deduct one hundred thousand dollars ($100,000) from the loan if Charlie Janya sleeps with him, knowing she worked at The Bunny Ranch; Charlie Janya tells him to get lost and adamantly refuses, stating she would never ever sleep with him for anything, before taking two-year-old Charlie Kasey out of the room and calling the police.
- Following Shane’s unexplained disappearance, Charlie Janya and Laith eventually have Shane’s two hundred thousand dollar monthly allowance legally terminated through the courts due to his absence.
- Victor Mayfyeld travels to Pineapple Barrens himself to confront Shane and Luther Tucer; Victor’s mob permanently eliminates both Shane and Luther Tucer at Pineapple Barrens, and their bodies are never found.
- Shane does not face any criminal consequences until seventeen-year-old Charlie Kasey tells Victor Mayfyeld everything about the poker games.
- Shane tells Charlie Kasey that he would take Zoey Chantal out into the sunlight and let her die, which compels Charlie Kasey to keep the incident secret from Charlie Janya and Laith.
