# Rebecca Brazilia Gives Her Veterinary Clinic to Patrick

## Confirmed Facts

- Rebecca Brazilia’s will gives the Key Davis veterinary clinic to Patrick and names Patrick as guardian responsible for Merissa Carolynn Fernández, Vera Sophie, Desiree Arissa, and Riley Leah after Rebecca Brazilia’s death. Patrick is already the biological father of the three younger girls. The will formally allows Merissa Carolynn Fernández to remain with Patrick and her sisters, with Luis’s agreement.
- Rebecca Brazilia’s veterinary clinic at the Davis farm is named Key Davis.
- Rebecca Brazilia’s veterinary clinic is located on the farm she shares with Patrick in Davis, California.
- Rebecca Brazilia gives the clinic to Patrick.
- There are four other veterinarians working at the Key Davis veterinary clinic, ensuring veterinary medical care continues after Rebecca Brazilia’s death.
- Patrick wants to remain a veterinary technician rather than training to become a veterinarian because of his responsibilities to his children, especially his infant daughter Riley Leah.
- Rebecca Brazilia and Patrick employ a business manager who handles the clinic’s business operations.
- Patrick continues working at the clinic as a veterinary technician.
- Patrick and the children continue living at the farm.
