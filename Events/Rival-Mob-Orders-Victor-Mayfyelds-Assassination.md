# Rival Mob Orders Victor Mayfyeld’s Assassination

## Confirmed Facts

- A rival mob from Manchester orders Victor Mayfyeld’s assassination because they want Victor's territory.
- The leader of the rival Manchester mob is involved in everything from laundering money to selling drugs and also operates a prostitution ring; Shane's gambling mate, Luther Tucer, serves as an enforcer for this Manchester mob leader.
- Luther's boss lives outside of Manchester and has ten young women who service him for anything he would like to; when Luther takes Charlie Kasey to Manchester, she meets Luther's boss, who likes Charlie Kasey and pays Luther twenty thousand Pounds to take her off his hands, an offer Luther accepts despite knowing how awful his boss could be. Luther tells Charlie Kasey all about his boss and his syndicate, information that Charlie Kasey later discloses to Victor Mayfyeld, directly creating Luther's and Shane's downfall.
- The assassination attempt happens while Charlie Kasey and Victor Mayfyeld are in the bed together; this is how Charlie Kasey discovers that Victor Mayfyeld is a lieutenant in the mob.
- Victor Mayfyeld responds to the threat by issuing the same assassination order against his rival Manchester mob.
- Charlie Kasey remains with Victor Mayfyeld after the assassination attempt and threat become known because she has fallen in love with him so much.
- Charlie Kasey learns that she should not ask Victor Mayfyeld any questions about the mob.
- Charlie Janya and Laith do not know that Charlie Kasey is involved with Victor Mayfyeld; Charlie Kasey keeps their relationship secret from them.
- Charlie Janya and Laith have three children together: Aurora Chase, Aquila Octavio, and Zoey Chantal. Aurora Chase and Aquila Octavio are twins. The three children are Charlie Kasey’s maternal half-siblings. Zoey Chantal has polymorphic light eruption. Because of Zoey Chantal’s condition, Charlie Janya and Laith move their family from Las Vegas to Scotland.
- Charlie Kasey is seventeen and Victor Mayfyeld is twenty-five when they meet and begin dating.
- Charlie Kasey meets Victor Mayfyeld after entering his strip club.
