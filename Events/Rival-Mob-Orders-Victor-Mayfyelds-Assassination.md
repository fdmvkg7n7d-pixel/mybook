## Confirmed Facts

- Charlie Janya and Laith have three children together: Aurora Chase, Aquila Octavio, and Zoey Chantal. Aurora Chase and Aquila Octavio are twins. The three children are Charlie Kasey’s maternal half-siblings. Zoey Chantal has polymorphic light eruption. Because of Zoey Chantal’s condition, Charlie Janya and Laith move their family from Las Vegas to Scotland.
- Charlie Kasey is seventeen and Victor Mayfyeld is twenty-five when they meet and begin dating.
- Charlie Kasey meets Victor Mayfyeld after entering his strip club.
- At a later point, Charlie Kasey is in Victor Mayfyeld’s bed when a rival mob orders his assassination.
- The assassination order is how Charlie Kasey discovers that Victor Mayfyeld is a lieutenant in the mob.
- Charlie Janya and Laith do not know that Charlie Kasey is involved with Victor Mayfyeld.
