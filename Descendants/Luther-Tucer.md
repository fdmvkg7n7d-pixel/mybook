# Luther Tucer

## Confirmed Facts

- Victor Mayfyeld travels to Pineapple Barrens himself to confront Shane and Luther Tucer; Victor’s mob permanently eliminates both Shane and Luther Tucer at Pineapple Barrens, and their bodies are never found.
- When Victor Mayfyeld’s men confront Luther Tucer, Luther tries to run away, but five of Victor’s men confront and apprehend him.
- Pineapple Barrens is the mob's problem-solving location where Victor Mayfyeld's men take Shane and Luther Tucer.
- When Charlie Kasey comes back from Shane's apartment and when he has poker games, she likes playing her Double Bass to forget Luther's hands.
- Victor Mayfyeld was born on April 30 in London, England, and is English. He is twenty-five when seventeen-year-old Charlie Kasey enters his strip club and meets him, and they begin dating. Victor Mayfyeld is a lieutenant in the mob. Charlie Kasey discovers his mob involvement while they are in bed together and a rival mob from Manchester orders his assassination because they want Victor's territory. Victor Mayfyeld is trustworthy, caring, and loving toward Charlie Kasey’s feelings. Charlie Kasey does not ask Victor Mayfyeld to take action against Shane or Luther Tucer. Charlie Janya and Laith do not know that Charlie Kasey is involved with Victor Mayfyeld.
- Luther Tucer is English. Luther Tucer and Shane are both forty when thirteen-year-old Charlie Kasey is taken to her bedroom. Charlie Kasey’s future boyfriend is Victor Mayfyeld. Victor Mayfyeld’s men take Luther Tucer and Shane to Pineapple Barrens, a place used by the mob to deal with its problems.
- Luther Tucer was born on June 24 in London, England, and is the same age as Shane. Luther Tucer and Shane attended the same school in London. Shane owes Luther Tucer five hundred dollars. Nothing further happens after Luther Tucer takes thirteen-year-old Charlie Kasey to her bedroom, but Charlie Kasey keeps the incident secret from Charlie Janya and Laith and therefore receives no immediate medical, emotional, or legal support. Charlie Kasey later tells her future boyfriend, Victor Mayfyeld, what happened. Victor Mayfyeld is involved in the mob, and Luther Tucer later faces Victor Mayfyeld’s men.
- Luther Tucer is one of Shane’s gambling mates.
- Luther Tucer demands immediate payment from Shane.
- Shane allows Luther Tucer to take thirteen-year-old Charlie Kasey in connection with Shane’s gambling debt.
- Charlie Kasey understands that Shane is using her as payment.
- Luther Tucer takes Charlie Kasey to her bedroom.
- Luther Tucer is involved in the mob based in Manchester, a connection that Shane does not know about when Shane arranges for Luther to take Charlie Kasey to Manchester for a couple of days.
- Charlie Kasey is fifteen years old when she visits Manchester with Luther Tucer.
- Luther Tucer serves as an enforcer for his mob boss in Manchester; his mob leader is involved in everything from money laundering to selling drugs and also operates a prostitution ring.
- Before driving to Manchester, Luther Tucer takes Charlie Kasey to her bedroom at Shane's apartment in connection with Shane's gambling debt, where he sees the Spanish mosaic figurine from Barcelona and starts laughing at it mockingly.
- While driving to Manchester, Luther Tucer tells Charlie Kasey everything about his mob boss; when Luther brings Charlie Kasey to Manchester, Charlie Kasey meets Luther's boss, who lives outside of Manchester and has ten young women who service him for anything he would like to.
- Luther's boss likes Charlie Kasey so he pays Luther twenty thousand Pounds to take her off his hands; Luther knows how awful his boss could be, but he is offering twenty thousand Pounds to have Charlie Kasey, and Luther accepts the twenty thousand Pounds.
- Luther Tucer's disclosures to Charlie Kasey and Charlie Kasey meeting Luther's boss lead directly to Luther's downfall, because when seventeen-year-old Charlie Kasey later tells Victor Mayfyeld everything about Luther and his mob boss, Victor's mob uses this intelligence to hunt down, apprehend, and permanently eliminate Luther Tucer and Shane at Pineapple Barrens.
- This storyline treats Charlie Kasey as an endangered child and Luther Tucer’s and Shane’s conduct as abuse and exploitation.
- Any depiction remains non-graphic and focuses on Charlie Kasey’s safety, disclosure, support, and recovery.
