## Confirmed Facts

- Victor Mayfyeld was born on April 30 in London, England, and is English. He is twenty-five when seventeen-year-old Charlie Kasey enters his strip club and meets him, and they begin dating. Victor Mayfyeld is a lieutenant in the mob. Charlie Kasey discovers his mob involvement while she is in his bed and a rival mob orders his assassination. Victor Mayfyeld is trustworthy, caring, and loving toward Charlie Kasey’s feelings. Charlie Kasey does not ask Victor Mayfyeld to take action against Shane or Luther Tucer. Charlie Janya and Laith do not know that Charlie Kasey is involved with Victor Mayfyeld.
- Luther Tucer is English. Luther Tucer and Shane are both forty when thirteen-year-old Charlie Kasey is taken to her bedroom. Charlie Kasey’s future boyfriend is Victor Mayfyeld. Victor Mayfyeld’s men take Luther Tucer and Shane to a place used by the mob to deal with its problems; what happens there remains unresolved.
- Luther Tucer was born on June 24 in London, England, and is the same age as Shane. Luther Tucer and Shane attended the same school in London. Shane owes Luther Tucer five hundred dollars. Nothing further happens after Luther Tucer takes thirteen-year-old Charlie Kasey to her bedroom, but Charlie Kasey keeps the incident secret from Charlie Janya and Laith and therefore receives no immediate medical, emotional, or legal support. Charlie Kasey later tells her future boyfriend, Victor Mayfyeld, what happened. Victor Mayfyeld is involved in the mob, and Luther Tucer later faces Victor Mayfyeld’s men.
- Luther Tucer is one of Shane’s gambling mates.
- Luther Tucer demands immediate payment from Shane.
- Shane allows Luther Tucer to take thirteen-year-old Charlie Kasey in connection with Shane’s gambling debt.
- Charlie Kasey understands that Shane is using her as payment.
- Luther Tucer takes Charlie Kasey to her bedroom.
- This storyline treats Charlie Kasey as an endangered child and Luther Tucer’s and Shane’s conduct as abuse and exploitation.
- Any depiction remains non-graphic and focuses on Charlie Kasey’s safety, disclosure, support, and recovery.
