## Confirmed Facts

- When Madeleine Jaycee Key is three, Carlota Delicia and Keith ask Taylor Raven and Adelisa to become her godmothers, partly because Adelisa’s juvenile idiopathic arthritis means additional support may be important. Carlota Delicia and Keith legally designate both women as Madeleine Jaycee Key’s guardians if they die. Carlota Delicia and Keith are killed in a vehicle crash when Madeleine Jaycee Key is fifteen and she and Caesarius Lavigne are preparing for their first Winter Olympic Games. Madeleine Jaycee Key continues living happily in Orlando with Caesarius Lavigne and Adelisa, who provides day-to-day care. Taylor Raven shares legal guardianship, provides continuing emotional support, and remains available for Madeleine Jaycee Key to call at any time. Chairat Narissara is happy that Taylor Raven is helping her niece, Madeleine Jaycee Key.
- Madeleine Jaycee Key and Caesarius Lavigne attend Lake Highland Preparatory School in Orlando.
- Morgan Marino tells Madeleine Jaycee Key that Caesarius Lavigne can accompany her to physical therapy and support her there. Adelisa, Carlota Delicia, and Keith support Madeleine Jaycee Key without any doubt throughout the emergency and recovery.
- Madeleine Jaycee Key remains hospitalized for seven days. After the swelling decreases and the surgical wound is checked and closed, she receives physical therapy and follows a gradual return-to-skating plan. She returns fully to pair skating after six months, in time to prepare for her first Winter Olympic Games at fifteen.
- After Morgan Marino notices the blood showing through Madeleine Jaycee Key’s tights, Morgan Marino calls Adelisa, Carlota Delicia, and Keith to tell them about the accident and emergency.
- During Annie Coates’s interview, Caesarius Lavigne tells the story of the forgotten ballet bag, the borrowed bodysuit and tights, and the family photograph displayed in the living room. He explains how hard he has worked to become such a strong skater and says he recognizes that he also needs Madeleine Jaycee Key’s partnership and support to perform at that level. Madeleine Jaycee Key kisses Caesarius Lavigne in front of Annie Coates. It is the first time the pair kiss in public. Madeleine Jaycee Key and Caesarius Lavigne then tell Annie Coates that they are a couple.
- When Madeleine Jaycee Key and Caesarius Lavigne are eight, Adelisa forgets to bring Caesarius Lavigne’s ballet bag to their dance class. Carlota Delicia agrees to let Caesarius Lavigne borrow one of Madeleine Jaycee Key’s bodysuits and a pair of her tights. Caesarius Lavigne feels a little embarrassed, and Madeleine Jaycee Key helps him get dressed in the borrowed dance clothes. Carlota Delicia and Adelisa take a photograph of their children together in the bodysuits and tights. They make a print and hang it on a living-room wall that displays many photographs of the family. The other students laugh at Caesarius Lavigne, but Madeleine Jaycee Key tells him, **“Just ignore their laughter, because we know what we are trying to achieve on the ice.”** Her encouragement helps them remain focused, and Caesarius Lavigne participates in the class. Carlota Delicia and Adelisa are impressed by how maturely and purposefully both children behave when the forgotten bag leaves Caesarius Lavigne needing to wear Madeleine Jaycee Key’s borrowed dance clothes.
- After fourteen-year-old Madeleine Jaycee Key and Caesarius Lavigne complete an on-ice practice at RDV Sportsplex Ice Den Pro, Tampa Bay Sun sports reporter Annie Coates interviews them. Annie Coates asks how they feel while practising, whether Caesarius Lavigne’s deafness hurts them while skating, and about their body image and experiences at Cypress Cove. Madeleine Jaycee Key translates Annie Coates’s spoken questions into American Sign Language for Caesarius Lavigne and translates his signed answers back into spoken language. The pair explain that they have a healthy body image and describe Cypress Cove’s swimming pool, tennis courts, beach beside a lake, and dance party held on the first Saturday of every month. Annie Coates writes an excellent, positive article that presents the pair and their answers well and contrasts constructively with Anthony Zimmerman’s misleading article.

He previously sees Madeleine Jaycee Key and Caesarius Lavigne at one of their competitions while attending with his wife. When he later sees the pair leave Cypress Cove, he is driving while they are walking and does not speak to them. His subsequent internet article misleads readers about Cypress Cove.

He publishes the internet article through the Tampa Bay Sun. Andrea Jennifer knows nothing about the article before it appears because she does not run the daily stories across Key Corporation’s newspapers.
