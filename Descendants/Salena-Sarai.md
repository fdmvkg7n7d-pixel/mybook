# Salena Sarai — Individual Backstory Working File

## Confirmed Facts

- **Birthday:** January 31, 2061
- **Birthplace:** Sunnybrook Health Sciences Centre, Toronto
- Salena Sarai is Patti Anne and Aleksander's biological daughter.
- She is Palmer Peter's twin sister.
- Gamaliel Galvin and Klara are both thirteen when the twins are born.
- Salena Sarai later attends Victoria Ballet Academy.
- Gamaliel Galvin and Klara are the first family members to hold newborn Palmer Peter and Salena Sarai.
- Palmer Peter and Salena Sarai are thirty months old during the Willow-and-Kleopatra scene.
- Willow trusts Salena Sarai and Palmer Peter around Willow's puppies.

## Klara's Eighteenth Birthday

- Patti Anne helps Salena Sarai get dressed before Klara's eighteenth-birthday dinner.
- Salena Sarai sees a protective bandage over Klara's memorial tattoo and screams.
- Salena Sarai asks to see the tattoo.
- Klara tells her that she may see it the following morning after the bandage comes off.

## Adult Future

- Salena Sarai later studies ballet at York University in Toronto.
- Because of Palmer Peter's controlling treatment of his wives, Salena Sarai cuts him out of her family life to protect her daughters.
- Patti Anne and Aleksander understand and respect her decision.
