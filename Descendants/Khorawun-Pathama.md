## Confirmed Facts

- Khorawun Pathama attends American Pacific International School.
- Because Taylor Raven and Chairat Narissara are concerned about danger in Chiang Mai, Khorawun Pathama stays at the school’s Inthanon House during the school week.
- Khorawun Pathama does not stay at Inthanon House on weekends.
- **Birthday:** May 31
- Khorawun Pathama is born in Chiang Mai, Thailand.
- Twin sibling Dhipyamongkol Ramkamhaeng, later known as Adranuch Chaisee, is born on June 1.
- Chairat Seni Junior is thirteen years old when the twins are born.
- Khorawun Pathama is one of Taylor Raven and Chairat Narissara’s four children.
- Khorawun Pathama is two years old when Taylor Raven learns about Sabrina Bryna and William’s December visit to Bangkok.
- Khorawun Pathama and Adranuch Chaisee are twins.
- Adranuch Chaisee is assigned male at birth and is originally named Dhipyamongkol Ramkamhaeng.
- Sabrina Bryna and William are Khorawun Pathama’s grandparents.
- Khorawun Pathama’s siblings are Chairat Seni Junior, Thiang Manya-Phathon, and Adranuch Chaisee.
- When the twins are five, Dhipyamongkol Ramkamhaeng has a deeply emotional conversation with Taylor Raven and Chairat Narissara and says that she wants to live as a girl like Khorawun Pathama.
- Taylor Raven and Chairat Narissara accept their daughter without a single doubt. She chooses the name Adranuch Chaisee and uses she/her pronouns afterward.
- Taylor Raven’s family lives in Thailand.

## Sabrina Bryna and William’s Bangkok Visit

- Taylor Raven tells the family that Sabrina Bryna and William will visit Bangkok in December after visiting Australia.
- At two years old, Khorawun Pathama is too young to understand what the visit means and shows no particular reaction to the news.
- Khorawun Pathama’s twin, then known as Dhipyamongkol Ramkamhaeng, is also too young to understand and shows no particular reaction.
