## Confirmed Facts

- Tucker Sullivan is Chris Smyth’s right-hand man in the cult.
- Tucker Sullivan is fifty years old when the cult forces nine-year-old Jordan Lily into a child marriage with him.
- Acting on Chris Smyth’s orders, Tucker Sullivan leads the cult members who kidnap Roberta Katherine and Jordan Lily in Bali.
- Tucker Sullivan prepares kidnapped girls for cult induction and control and for forced child-marriage ceremonies. This material remains non-graphic and is never romanticized.
- Tucker Sullivan murders thirteen-year-old Cam Little, who is held at the cult’s Bali compound. Ten-year-old Jordan Lily witnesses the murder. Cam Little, who is from Los Angeles and has been held for four days, strongly resists Tucker Sullivan and refuses his sexual demands. Tucker Sullivan murders her after that refusal. Cam Little’s resistance never makes her responsible for his violence. The murder remains completely non-graphic and is never Jordan Lily’s fault. Cam Little’s murder makes aggravated trafficking resulting in death and a separate murder charge part of the case against Tucker Sullivan.
- When Jordan Lily is nine years old, the cult forces her into a child marriage with Tucker Sullivan.
- Jordan Lily is a child and cannot freely consent.
- The forced union is abuse and is not treated as a valid marriage or personal choice.
- All material concerning Jordan Lily’s forced child marriage remains non-graphic.
- Tucker Sullivan fathers Jordan Lily’s firstborn daughter, Cam Jamie, through sexual abuse while Jordan Lily is a child captive. Jordan Lily cannot consent, and the abuse and pregnancy remain completely non-graphic.
- Cam Jamie is born on January 31 at the Bali compound when Jordan Lily is fourteen.
- Jordan Lily and Cam Jamie receive poor and inadequate health care during the birth.
- Tucker Sullivan is arrested during the joint rescue operation.
- Indonesian prosecutors charge Tucker Sullivan with directing organized kidnapping, prolonged unlawful confinement, trafficking and exploitation of an adult and a child, forced child marriage and sexual violence against a child, preparing kidnapped girls for cult control and forced marriages, and the murder of Cam Little and aggravated trafficking resulting in her death.
- Tucker Sullivan is convicted of these offences and sentenced to life imprisonment in Indonesia.
