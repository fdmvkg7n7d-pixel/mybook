## Lysa Mandy Discovers the Violin

- When Lysa Mandy is four years old, she and her grandfather Mike are playing together around Mike and Terri Anne's mansion.
- They come across Jessica Isabella's childhood violin, which Mike and Terri Anne have kept at their mansion.
- Lysa Mandy is hesitant to touch the violin.
- Mike notices her interest and asks whether she wants to touch it.
- Lysa Mandy tells him, “Grandpa, I want to touch the violin, but I am very nervous.”
- Mike reassures her and gently shows her how to hold the violin and play “Twinkle, Twinkle, Little Star.”
- Lysa Mandy later plays “Twinkle, Twinkle, Little Star” on the violin for Fallon Glenda.
- Fallon Glenda is impressed by her daughter's playing.
- This supportive moment begins Lysa Mandy's interest in playing music.

## Recognition in Senior Kindergarten

- Fallon Glenda and Charlie are eighteen years old, and Lysa Mandy is five years old.
- After issuing Lysa Mandy's report card, her Senior Kindergarten teacher meets with Fallon Glenda and Charlie.
- Lysa Mandy remains in the classroom during the meeting because Fallon Glenda and Charlie do not have a babysitter.
- The teacher explains that she has noticed a broader pattern of traits that may be consistent with Asperger syndrome and recommends a professional evaluation.
- As one example of Lysa Mandy's attention to detail and precision, the teacher shows them several colouring pictures.
- One picture belongs to Lysa Mandy, while the others belong to her classmates.
- Lysa Mandy has coloured her entire picture without going outside the lines.
- The teacher does not treat the colouring picture alone as proof of Asperger syndrome; it is one observation within a broader pattern.
- The teacher tells Fallon Glenda and Charlie that Lysa Mandy displays unusually strong abilities in particular areas, including colouring, music, and yoga.
- She explains that an uneven cognitive profile—with pronounced strengths in certain activities—can occur in a person with Asperger syndrome.
- The teacher makes clear that these are Lysa Mandy's individual strengths and not abilities shared by every person with Asperger syndrome.
- Fallon Glenda and Charlie are willing to take Lysa Mandy to a qualified professional for an assessment.
- A multidisciplinary assessment team consisting of a doctor and a psychologist evaluates Lysa Mandy.
- The team formally confirms that Lysa Mandy has Asperger syndrome.
- After the meeting, Fallon Glenda, Charlie, and Lysa Mandy go to Pier 39 for clam chowder.

- Because this is a family-camp program, Fallon Glenda stays at French Woods with Lysa Mandy for the entire week.
- This is Lysa Mandy's first experience at French Woods, and she has a great deal of fun.
- The following summer, Lysa Mandy returns to French Woods for nine weeks.
- Her nine-week stay consists of three consecutive three-week sessions.
- Lysa Mandy is happy to return for the longer stay.
- Lysa Mandy has a wonderful time during both visits to French Woods.
- She especially enjoys supervised water activities in the swimming pool and lake.
- During her time at French Woods, Lysa Mandy takes gymnastics, ballet, circus, and swimming lessons.
- She also participates in waterfront activities and band practice.

## Racial Profiling in San Francisco

- Lysa Mandy is eight years old, while Fallon Glenda and Charlie are twenty-one years old.
- Lysa Mandy is very dark-skinned, while Fallon Glenda is white.
- One day, Fallon Glenda, Charlie, and Lysa Mandy are walking together in San Francisco.
- An unnamed person questions Lysa Mandy's background because Lysa Mandy and Fallon Glenda have different skin colours.
- The person wrongly suspects that Fallon Glenda kidnapped Lysa Mandy.
- The suspicion is based on racial profiling and the false belief that a white mother cannot have a dark-skinned daughter.
- The story treats the accusation as racist and harmful.
- The police are called.
- Fallon Glenda shows the officers photographs documenting Lysa Mandy's life from babyhood to the present.
- The most recent photograph shows Lysa Mandy running along Lombard Street in San Francisco.
- After reviewing the photographs, the officers apologize to Fallon Glenda for the distress caused by being called to check on Lysa Mandy's wellbeing.
- Fallon Glenda hides her emotions because she does not want to frighten Lysa Mandy.
- After the family returns to Fallon Glenda and Charlie's house, Fallon Glenda falls apart emotionally in Charlie's arms.
- Fallon Glenda and Charlie have a deeply emotional conversation about the police encounter and Lysa Mandy's hospital stay as a newborn.
- Fallon Glenda explains that the false accusation revived the pain of being prevented by a doctor from seeing Lysa Mandy during her neonatal hospital stay.
- Both experiences forced Fallon Glenda to defend her place as Lysa Mandy's mother.
