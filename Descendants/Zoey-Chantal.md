# Zoey Chantal — Individual Backstory Working File

## Confirmed Facts

- Zoey Chantal uses Laith's surname, Miller (Zoey Chantal Miller).
- Zoey Chantal was born in Las Vegas on June 2; Charlie Janya had Zoey Chantal when she was thirty-five years old.
- Laith Miller is her father.
- Zoey Chantal has a happy personality and does not consider her Polymorphic Light Eruption to be a disability; she loves looking up into the night sky and seeing over a billion stars.
- Zoey Chantal is homeschooled by her parents, Charlie Janya and Laith.
- One of her ambitions is to find a boyfriend who completely understands her Polymorphic Light Eruption; another ambition of hers is to play an electric guitar and a piano.
- Charlie Kasey blames Zoey Chantal for the family's move from Las Vegas to Scotland and for what Shane asks Charlie Kasey to do at his poker games; she is also very jealous of the special attention Zoey Chantal receives from Charlie Janya and Laith because of her condition.
- Thirteen years later, in the middle of Charlie Janya and Laith's conversation in the kitchen about Shane's poker games and the clues they missed with Charlie Kasey, Zoey Chantal comes into the kitchen and asks Charlie Kasey and Victor whether, if she wasn't born, Shane wouldn't do these awful things to Charlie Kasey. Charlie Kasey walks into the kitchen and tells Zoey Chantal that she shouldn't be questioning herself because Shane was a very awful father who was in debt for poker games. Charlie Kasey asks them whether they can forgive her for what she did to their family, and Charlie Janya, Laith, and Zoey Chantal immediately hug Charlie Kasey.
- When Laith takes Charlie Kasey, Aurora Chase, and Aquila Octavio to Barcelona while Charlie Janya and Zoey Chantal are in London, Charlie Kasey dislikes the trip because Charlie Janya is not with them, and she refuses to help Laith with the twins; while on the beach, Laith and Charlie Kasey have a conversation about the trip and her siblings, and Charlie Kasey tells him the half truth.
- Zoey Chantal was diagnosed with polymorphic light eruption immediately in the hospital.
- Albyn Hospital in Aberdeen oversees Zoey Chantal's medical care and precautions for her condition.
- Zoey Chantal stays awake at night and sleeps during the day because of her condition; she plays nighttime footie with Aurora Chase.
- When Zoey Chantal is five years old, Charlie Janya takes her on a nighttime train ride to London, hoping to give Zoey Chantal a great time in London.
- During their London trip when Zoey Chantal is five, Charlie Janya takes her to an indoor performance of the Peter and the Wolf ballet in the East End; Charlie Janya gets backstage passes so Zoey Chantal can meet the cast and dancers.
- On the nighttime return train ride from London to Aberdeen, Zoey Chantal asks Charlie Janya whether she can take ballet lessons. When Charlie Janya asks Zoey Chantal why, Zoey Chantal tells Charlie Janya that she wants to dance and perform grand jetés like the dancers in Peter and the Wolf. Charlie Janya tells Zoey Chantal that she promises—cross my heart and hope to die, stick a needle in my eye—that she will look for a ballet teacher who is willing to teach in the nighttime.
- During their London trip when Zoey Chantal is five, Charlie Janya takes her to Primrose Hill for a midnight picnic on the grass overlooking the city; two London police officers walking through the park notice them having a picnic at midnight and approach with many questions. Charlie Janya explains that Zoey Chantal has polymorphic light eruption and is allergic to sunlight. The officer who recognizes Charlie Janya from The Bunny Ranch is a woman, and Charlie Janya recognizes her as well; her partner knows she is bisexual, and she tells him that she knows Charlie Janya from her past. Their conversation goes well, and when the officers express sympathy and say they are really sorry for her daughter, five-year-old Zoey Chantal quickly speaks up and tells them that they don't need to be sorry for her because her parents are giving her a very wonderful life. When the officers leave, Charlie Janya lightly kisses Zoey Chantal on her little head; when Zoey Chantal asks what the kiss is for, Charlie Janya tells her that the kiss is for showing how proud she is.
- Charlie Janya asks Sabrina Bryna about riding the London Eye; Sabrina Bryna grants the request and calls a contact in the government to arrange for Charlie Janya and Zoey Chantal to ride the London Eye around midnight. On the day of the outing, while Charlie Janya gives Zoey Chantal a bath treatment for her polymorphic light eruption, she tells Zoey Chantal about the midnight London Eye ride; Zoey Chantal jumps out of the bathtub with excitement and enjoyment to see the whole city of London. When her bath is done, they go downstairs to the restaurant as usual and order a typical breakfast; another family dining at the restaurant complains about them eating breakfast during dinner hours, prompting the restaurant manager to step in and explain that Charlie Janya made special arrangements for her daughter.
- During the midnight ride on the London Eye, Zoey Chantal is amazed to see all of the city's lights across the skyline; Sabrina Bryna asks the government to have the wheel stopped in the sky for ten minutes so Zoey Chantal can take it all in. Charlie Janya takes many photographs of Zoey Chantal and sends them to Laith and Sabrina Bryna, along with big thanks to Sabrina Bryna.
- When Laith and the twins return to Aberdeen from Barcelona and London, the twins hug Charlie Janya and Zoey Chantal, and the children go away together discussing their individual trips, and Charlie Janya and Laith have a conversation, too.
- Rebecca Brazilia dies at home with thirteen-year-old Merissa Carolynn Fernández beside her. At the time, identical twins Vera Sophie and Desiree Arissa are seven years and six months old, and Riley Leah is six months old. The family holds a memorial. Charlie Janya attends the memorial alone because Zoey Chantal’s polymorphic light eruption prevents Zoey Chantal from attending and Charlie Janya’s other children are attending school. Merissa Carolynn Fernández begins living with Patrick and her younger sisters immediately after Rebecca Brazilia’s death.
- Charlie Janya and Laith buy a house for their family in Aberdeen. A private school is available for Charlie Kasey, Aurora Chase, and Aquila Octavio, although the school has not yet been selected by name. No school is chosen for six-month-old Zoey Chantal because she is still a baby. Aberdeen has excellent doctors who can evaluate Zoey Chantal and help Charlie Janya and Laith determine the precautions and treatment needed for her polymorphic light eruption. Shane moved to London immediately after his breakup with Charlie Janya, so Charlie Kasey’s move to Aberdeen makes it easier for her to see him. Charlie Janya has British citizenship through her father, Charlie, and the children have British citizenship through their family, allowing them to move to Scotland. Sabrina Bryna approves Charlie Janya and Laith’s request to use a Key Corporation airplane for the move.
- The family moves from Las Vegas to Aberdeen, Scotland, during the summer because of school schedules. Charlie Kasey is ten, Aurora Chase and Aquila Octavio are five, and Zoey Chantal is six months old. Charlie Kasey moves with Charlie Janya and Laith but does not like the move. Aurora Chase and Aquila Octavio are indifferent to it, while Zoey Chantal is still a baby. The reduced sunlight in the Scottish climate greatly improves Zoey Chantal’s polymorphic light eruption. Shane and Laith are British citizens from England and do not need visas to move between England and Scotland. Charlie Janya and Laith use Charlie Janya’s trust fund to support the family in Scotland. They ask Sabrina Bryna for permission to use one of Key Corporation’s airplanes for the move.
- Zoey Chantal is a child of Charlie Janya and Laith.
- Zoey Chantal has twin siblings named Aurora Chase and Aquila Octavio.
- Zoey Chantal is Charlie Kasey’s maternal half-sibling.
- Zoey Chantal has polymorphic light eruption.
- Charlie Janya and Laith move their family from Las Vegas to Scotland because of Zoey Chantal’s condition.
