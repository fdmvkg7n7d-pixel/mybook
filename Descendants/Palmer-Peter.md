# Palmer Peter — Individual Backstory Working File

## Confirmed Facts

- **Birthday:** January 31, 2061
- **Birthplace:** Sunnybrook Health Sciences Centre, Toronto
- Palmer Peter is Patti Anne and Aleksander's biological son.
- He is Salena Sarai's twin brother.
- Gamaliel Galvin and Klara are both thirteen when the twins are born.
- Palmer Peter later attends Victoria Ballet Academy.
- Gamaliel Galvin and Klara are the first family members to hold newborn Palmer Peter and Salena Sarai.
- Palmer Peter and Salena Sarai are thirty months old during the Willow-and-Kleopatra scene.
- Little Palmer Peter later enters the combined family-room-and-kitchen area because he cannot sleep.
- Patti Anne picks him up and carries him out of the room.

## Adult Future

- Palmer Peter enjoys travelling around the world.
- Palmer Peter loves travelling around the world and wants to experience life in another part of it.
- His love of worldwide travel motivates his decision to move to Saudi Arabia before he meets the father of one of his friends.
- During his travels, he later meets his friend's father.
- The father is married to two women and behaves controlling toward both wives.
- He teaches Palmer Peter an interpretation of Islam that he uses to justify bossing and controlling his wives.
- Palmer Peter admires the father's control, revealing an unhealthy desire for power within marriage.
- The encounter contributes to Palmer Peter's decision to convert to Islam, but it does not cause his earlier decision to move to Saudi Arabia.
- The story does not portray the father's controlling interpretation as healthy or representative of Saudi families or Islam.
- Palmer Peter later moves to Saudi Arabia.
- He attends King Abdulaziz University and studies medicine.
- Palmer Peter specializes in gynecology and becomes a gynecologist.
- He converts to Islam.
- Palmer Peter marries Aurora at twenty-one.
- Over approximately three decades, he gradually accumulates nine simultaneous wives and marries Kourtney at fifty-two.
- The arrangement is illegal, is not recognized under Saudi law, and is not permitted by Islamic marriage rules.
- Patti Anne and Aleksander strongly disapprove of Palmer Peter's controlling treatment of his wives because they taught him to treat others better.
- They are deeply disappointed in his choices.
- Patti Anne and Aleksander choose not to cut Palmer Peter out of the family because they want to remain connected to their grandchildren.
- Maintaining contact does not mean they approve of Palmer Peter's conduct.
- Gamaliel Galvin and Salena Sarai make a different decision from Patti Anne and Aleksander.
- They cut Palmer Peter out of their separate family lives because they want to protect their daughters from his conduct.
- Patti Anne and Aleksander understand why Gamaliel Galvin and Salena Sarai make that decision and respect their need to protect their daughters.
