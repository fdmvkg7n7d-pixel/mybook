## Confirmed Facts

- Sun-Young is originally from South Korea.
- Sun-Young's nickname is **Liz**.
- Sabrina Bryna and William become Sun-Young's foster parents when Sun-Young is seven years old.
- Sun-Young is seven during the SickKids storyline.
- Sun-Young has standard-risk acute lymphoblastic leukemia.
- Sun-Young receives chemotherapy.
- Sun-Young is a ward of the state under the care of the provincial child-welfare authority.

## Sun-Young Meets Taylor Raven at SickKids

- Seven-year-old Sun-Young is receiving chemotherapy for standard-risk acute lymphoblastic leukemia at Toronto's Hospital for Sick Children.
- During her own annual physical examination, seven-year-old Taylor Raven learns that a growth has been found inside her left eye and hears William explain retinoblastoma.
- Taylor Raven becomes overwhelmed and slips away from the examination area.
- William contacts hospital security, which reviews the hospital cameras while searching for a barefoot seven-year-old girl wearing a pink swimsuit.
- Security sees Taylor Raven walking into a hospital room and asks William whether he wants them to bring her out.
- William tells security not to remove Taylor Raven because he is already on his way to get her himself.
- William does not know who is inside the hospital room.
- He explains that he does not want the security officers to frighten Taylor Raven or whoever is in the room with her.
- William calls the head nurse for Sun-Young's ward and explains that his daughter has slipped into one of the hospital rooms.
- He tells the head nurse that he is coming up to the ward to get Taylor Raven.
- William brings Taylor Raven's clothes with him.
- The room is Sun-Young's hospital room.
- Taylor Raven and Sun-Young have not met before.
- The girls introduce themselves to each other.
- Taylor Raven and Sun-Young begin talking and share age-appropriate details about themselves and their lives.
- They also talk about what Taylor Raven is wearing in the hospital.
- Taylor Raven explains that she chose her pink two-piece swimsuit instead of a hospital gown.
- Taylor Raven is barefoot and has no shoes on when she enters Sun-Young's room.
- Taylor Raven tells Sun-Young that she came to the hospital for a doctor's appointment and that her daddy, William, is a doctor who works there.
- Taylor Raven explains that she ran out of the doctor's office after receiving bad news about the growth in her left eye.
- William walks into Sun-Young's hospital room and finds Taylor Raven and Sun-Young lying on the bed and talking to each other.
- William introduces himself to Sun-Young.
- He then tells Taylor Raven, “Hello, my very beautiful daughter. You are not in any kind of trouble.”
- William's first response is calm and reassuring rather than angry because he understands how frightened and nervous Taylor Raven is after hearing the news about her left eye.
- Sun-Young asks William whether she is in trouble because Taylor Raven entered her room.
- William tells Sun-Young that she is not in trouble and gently asks why she thought she might be.
- Sun-Young explains that she is a foster child living with a foster family that hates having attention drawn to the family.
- Sun-Young is afraid that Taylor Raven's unexpected visit and the involvement of hospital security could upset her foster parents.
- William sits down on the hospital bed with the girls.
- He explains to Sun-Young that he is a very important doctor at the hospital and can make clear that she did nothing wrong.
- William promises, “Cross my heart and hope to die, stick a needle in my eye,” that Sun-Young will not be in trouble.
- Hearing such fearful words from Sun-Young breaks William's enormous heart.
- William makes a private promise to himself that he will speak with Sabrina Bryna about Sun-Young.
- William then asks Taylor Raven whether she wants to get dressed.
- Taylor Raven tells William that she does.
- After the family returns to Sabrina Bryna and William's mansion, William speaks privately with Sabrina Bryna in their bedroom.
- William tells Sabrina Bryna everything that happened at the hospital, including Taylor Raven entering Sun-Young's room and Sun-Young's fear of getting in trouble with her foster parents.
- Sabrina Bryna becomes deeply emotional for both young girls: Taylor Raven after the frightening news about her eye and Sun-Young because of her leukemia and fear of her foster parents.
- After the conversation, Sabrina Bryna walks into Taylor Raven's bedroom.
- Sabrina Bryna simply wraps her arms around her baby girl and holds Taylor Raven in a long embrace.
- Taylor Raven breaks down crying in Sabrina Bryna's arms.
- Roberta Katherine crawls into Taylor Raven's bedroom.
- Taylor Raven gently picks up her baby sister and tells Roberta Katherine that she loves her so much.
- Sabrina Bryna is impressed that, despite her distress, Taylor Raven focuses on expressing affection to Roberta Katherine.
- For that moment, Taylor Raven is not thinking about her retinoblastoma or Sun-Young; she is thinking about loving and comforting her baby sister.
- Taylor Raven begins playing with Roberta Katherine on her bed.
- While the sisters play, Taylor Raven tells Sabrina Bryna about her new friend, Sun-Young.
- Taylor Raven asks Sabrina Bryna whether she may visit Sun-Young more often.
- Sabrina Bryna explains that Taylor Raven will have many opportunities to visit Sun-Young because Taylor Raven must return to SickKids for retinoblastoma appointments and care.
- Sabrina Bryna tells Taylor Raven that one of William's colleagues must formally examine her left eye.
- The colleague provides an independent medical assessment rather than leaving William to serve as his daughter's primary treating ophthalmologist.
- Sabrina Bryna asks Taylor Raven whether she wants to go downstairs to the kitchen for an afternoon snack.
- Taylor Raven replies, “Sure.”
- Taylor Raven gently carries Roberta Katherine out of the bedroom as they head downstairs.
- The conversation fulfils William's private promise to speak with Sabrina Bryna about Sun-Young.
- The girls' hospital conversation begins the relationship that later leads to them travelling together to Haeundae Marine City.

## Sabrina Bryna and William Become Sun-Young's Foster Parents

- Sun-Young is a ward of the state under the care of the provincial child-welfare authority.
- Sabrina Bryna and William apply to become Sun-Young's foster parents.
- Sabrina Bryna and William are highly respected and important members of their community, giving their application strong references and community support.
- Their application proceeds smoothly.
- The child-welfare authority still completes the required screening, home assessment, background checks, and formal approval before placing Sun-Young with them.
- Sabrina Bryna and William become Sun-Young's foster parents when Sun-Young is seven years old.
