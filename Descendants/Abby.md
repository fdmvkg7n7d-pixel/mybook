## Confirmed Facts

- Abby is the biological daughter of Candice and William Timothy Junior.
- Candice deliberately hides the pregnancy from William Timothy Junior.
- William Timothy Junior does not know about Abby for twenty-four years.
- Abby has a photograph showing Candice and William Timothy Junior together.
- Abby joins the Royal Australian Navy.
- Abby serves at the same Navy location as MacKenzi Bree when William Timothy Junior and Evangeline Eyre visit MacKenzi Bree.
- Abby sees William Timothy Junior and recognizes him as her father from the photograph.
- Abby quietly asks William Timothy Junior and Evangeline Eyre to speak with her privately.
- During the private conversation, Abby shows them the photograph of Candice and William Timothy Junior and reveals that William Timothy Junior is her father.
- This is how William Timothy Junior and Evangeline Eyre learn that Candice had been pregnant and that William Timothy Junior has another daughter.

## Royal Australian Navy Revelation

- The revelation occurs twenty-four years after Candice hides her pregnancy from William Timothy Junior.
- William Timothy Junior and Evangeline Eyre are visiting MacKenzi Bree at her Royal Australian Navy location.
- Abby is serving at the same location.
- Abby recognizes William Timothy Junior from the photograph she possesses.
- Abby does not confront him publicly; she requests a private conversation with William Timothy Junior and Evangeline Eyre.
- Abby shows the photograph and explains her connection to Candice and William Timothy Junior.
- Their emotional reactions and subsequent family relationships remain to be developed.
