# Aurora Chase

## Confirmed Facts

- Aurora Chase uses Laith's surname, Miller (Aurora Chase Miller).
- Aurora Chase is the firstborn twin, and Aquila Octavio is the secondborn twin.
- Aurora Chase was born on February 28 in Las Vegas; Charlie Janya had the twins when she was thirty-one years old.
- Laith Miller is her father.
- The twins Aurora Chase and Aquila Octavio love their older maternal half-sister Charlie Kasey so much.
- Aurora Chase attends the same private school in Aberdeen as Charlie Kasey; Aurora Chase and Aquila Octavio are in the same classroom because the school has a couple of classrooms for each grade.
- Aurora Chase and Aquila Octavio have different personalities; Aurora Chase likes taking gymnastics and ballet.
- When the twins are in high school, they must complete forty hours of volunteer work; Aurora Chase does her forty hours of volunteer work at the gymnastics studio where she learned gymnastics.
- Both twins take swimming lessons and violin lessons (Aurora Chase also takes swimming lessons along with Aquila Octavio and Charlie Kasey).
- Aurora Chase likes playing footie with Zoey Chantal in the nighttime because Zoey Chantal stays up at night and sleeps during the day.
- Laith Miller tries to give Aurora Chase, Aquila Octavio, and Charlie Kasey very special times because of Zoey Chantal's polymorphic light eruption; while Charlie Janya and Zoey Chantal are in London, Laith takes Charlie Kasey, Aurora Chase, and Aquila Octavio on a trip to Barcelona. However, Charlie Kasey does not like the Barcelona trip because Charlie Janya does not come along, feels very jealous of Zoey Chantal's special attention from Charlie Janya and Laith, and does not want to help Laith with the twins on the trip. While ten-year-old twins Aurora Chase and Aquila Octavio are building sand castles on the beach in Barcelona, Laith and Charlie Kasey have a conversation about the trip and her siblings; Charlie Kasey tells him the half truth that she does not like visiting Shane in London, and Laith does not push her to ask for the reason.
- In Barcelona, inquisitive twins Aurora Chase and Aquila Octavio ask Laith many questions about Charlie Kasey's frequent running. When Aurora Chase needs to use the bathroom, she hears Charlie Kasey's cries through the frosted glass of the shower and asks if she is okay; Charlie Kasey quickly stops crying, tells Aurora Chase that she is okay, and asks her to keep it a secret.
- Aurora Chase tells Aquila Octavio about hearing Charlie Kasey crying in the shower through the frosted glass.
- During their Barcelona trip, Laith takes Aurora Chase and Aquila Octavio on a tour of Camp Nou stadium and museum and buys them FC Barcelona jerseys, which Aurora Chase loves because of her passion for footie.
- Following their time in Barcelona, Laith takes Aurora Chase, Aquila Octavio, and Charlie Kasey to London to drop Charlie Kasey off at Shane's West End apartment for her scheduled custody visit before returning home to Aberdeen.
- When Laith and the twins return to Aberdeen and reunite with Charlie Janya and Zoey Chantal, the twins hug Charlie Janya and Zoey Chantal, the children go away together discussing their individual trips, and Charlie Janya and Laith have a conversation, too.
- Aurora Chase cannot keep the secret, so when the family reunites with Charlie Janya in Aberdeen, she tells Charlie Janya everything that happened in the bathroom; Charlie Janya tells ten-year-old Aurora Chase that a teenager like Charlie Kasey is sometimes very sad for no reason.
- Thirteen years later, Aurora Chase asks Charlie Janya whether she is guilty for not pushing Charlie Kasey to tell the truth back then; Charlie Janya reassures Aurora Chase that she was not guilty at all because she was just ten years old.

- Charlie Janya and Laith buy a house for their family in Aberdeen. A private school is available for Charlie Kasey, Aurora Chase, and Aquila Octavio, although the school has not yet been selected by name. No school is chosen for six-month-old Zoey Chantal because she is still a baby. Aberdeen has excellent doctors who can evaluate Zoey Chantal and help Charlie Janya and Laith determine the precautions and treatment needed for her polymorphic light eruption. Shane moved to London immediately after his breakup with Charlie Janya, so Charlie Kasey’s move to Aberdeen makes it easier for her to see him. Charlie Janya has British citizenship through her father, Charlie, and the children have British citizenship through their family, allowing them to move to Scotland. Sabrina Bryna approves Charlie Janya and Laith’s request to use a Key Corporation airplane for the move.
- The family moves from Las Vegas to Aberdeen, Scotland, during the summer because of school schedules. Charlie Kasey is ten, Aurora Chase and Aquila Octavio are five, and Zoey Chantal is six months old. Charlie Kasey moves with Charlie Janya and Laith but does not like the move. Aurora Chase and Aquila Octavio are indifferent to it, while Zoey Chantal is still a baby. The reduced sunlight in the Scottish climate greatly improves Zoey Chantal’s polymorphic light eruption. Shane and Laith are British citizens from England and do not need visas to move between England and Scotland. Charlie Janya and Laith use Charlie Janya’s trust fund to support the family in Scotland. They ask Sabrina Bryna for permission to use one of Key Corporation’s airplanes for the move.
- Aurora Chase is a child of Charlie Janya and Laith.
- Aurora Chase and Aquila Octavio are twins.
- Aurora Chase is Charlie Kasey’s maternal half-sibling.
- Aurora Chase has a younger or older sibling named Zoey Chantal.
- The family moves from Las Vegas to Scotland because Zoey Chantal has polymorphic light eruption.
