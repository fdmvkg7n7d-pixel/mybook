# Brandi Jessica — Individual Backstory Working File

## Confirmed Facts

- **Birthday:** December 25, 2047
- Brandi Jessica is a biological daughter of Kiley Rose and Lazaro.
- Brandi Jessica is Pandora Paige's twin sister.
- A nurse criminally switches Pandora Paige away after the birth, and the hospital falsely tells Kiley Rose and Terri Anne that Pandora Paige died.
- Kiley Rose raises Brandi Jessica as her only known surviving child.

## Retinoblastoma and Blindness

- Brandi Jessica is diagnosed with retinoblastoma on December 21, 2054, when she is six years old.
- The diagnosis occurs four days before her seventh birthday.
- The disease causes Brandi Jessica to lose vision in both eyes.
- Brandi Jessica uses a white cane for independent travel and navigation.

## Kidnapping

- Before the kidnapping, Andrew and Mike enter a business deal together.
- Andrew loses five billion dollars through the deal and blames Mike for the loss.
- Andrew wants revenge against Mike.
- Andrew and Greenlee are cousins.
- Greenlee is a porn star who works for Key Porn Studios.
- Brandi Jessica's mother, Kiley Rose, is also a porn star working for Key Porn Studios.
- Their shared employer does not mean that Kiley Rose knows about or participates in Greenlee and Andrew's plan.
- In the early morning of December 27, 2054, Andrew and Greenlee kidnap Brandi Jessica as revenge against her grandfather.
- Brandi Jessica is seven years old, having celebrated her birthday two days earlier.
- Andrew and Greenlee take her to the United Arab Emirates.
- Andrew and Greenlee illegally sell Brandi Jessica to Prince Ali Javan.
- The transaction is child trafficking and gives Prince Ali Javan no valid ownership over her.
- Prince Ali Javan has worked with Mike for many years.
- Once Brandi Jessica is in his custody, Prince Ali Javan calls Mike about her.
- Kiley Rose and Mike immediately fly to the United Arab Emirates, but they cannot recover Brandi Jessica right away.
- They rescue her on February 2, 2055, and bring her safely home.
- Because Brandi Jessica does not receive timely care during the kidnapping, her retinoblastoma progresses to stage three by the time of the rescue.

## River Way Ranch Camp and the Stanley Park Sighting

- When Brandi Jessica is eleven, she attends River Way Ranch Camp.
- While Brandi Jessica is at camp, Kiley Rose and Julia see another girl at Stanley Park in Vancouver, British Columbia, who closely resembles her.
- Unlike Brandi Jessica, the girl is not using a white cane.
- Because Brandi Jessica is away at camp, Kiley Rose and Julia know that the girl cannot be Brandi Jessica.
- The resemblance becomes the first major clue that Pandora Paige may not have died.

## Later Life

- Brandi Jessica attends Convent of the Sacred Heart in New York City.
- She later shares a Los Angeles mansion with Jamal.
- When her cousin Emerald Ginger runs away while pregnant, Emerald Ginger goes to Brandi Jessica and Jamal's mansion.
- Brandi Jessica contacts Andrea Jennifer and tells her where Emerald Ginger is.
