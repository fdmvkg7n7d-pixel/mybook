# Aquila Octavio

## Confirmed Facts

- Aquila Octavio uses Laith's surname, Miller (Aquila Octavio Miller).
- Aquila Octavio is the secondborn twin, and Aurora Chase is the firstborn twin.
- Aquila Octavio was born on February 28 in Las Vegas; Charlie Janya had the twins when she was thirty-one years old.
- Laith Miller is their father.
- The twins Aquila Octavio and Aurora Chase love their older maternal half-sister Charlie Kasey so much.
- When Aurora Chase tells Aquila Octavio about hearing Charlie Kasey crying in the shower through the frosted glass, Aquila Octavio is concerned about Charlie Kasey and asks Laith about Charlie Kasey.
- Aquila Octavio moves to London to attend Queen Mary University of London, where he studies English Literature and Linguistics.
- Aquila Octavio's career ambition is to become a speech-language pathologist.
- When Aquila Octavio moves to London to attend university, he goes to a new Capoeira studio; his instructor is Cirk McMarden.
- Cirk McMarden has a daughter named Abigale; this is how Aquila Octavio and Abigale meet each other, and they become a couple.
- Abigale does not take Capoeira because it is not her cup of tea; since she was a child, she has been attending The Royal Ballet School, though she sometimes visits her father at his Capoeira studio.
- Abigale is Aquila Octavio's girlfriend; her birthplace is London, England, her birthday is August 13, and she is the same age as Aquila Octavio and Aurora Chase.
- Thirteen years after their childhood trips, when Aquila Octavio learns about what happened to Charlie Kasey, he starts crying and his girlfriend Abigale helps him.
- Aquila Octavio attends the same private school in Aberdeen as Charlie Kasey; Aquila Octavio and Aurora Chase are in the same classroom because the school has a couple of classrooms for each grade.
- Aquila Octavio and Aurora Chase have different personalities; Aquila Octavio likes taking Capoeira.
- When the twins are in high school, they must complete forty hours of volunteer work; Aquila Octavio does his forty hours of volunteer work at a clinic that has Cerebral Palsy kids.
- Both twins take swimming lessons and violin lessons (Aquila Octavio also takes swimming lessons along with Aurora Chase and Charlie Kasey).
- Aquila Octavio likes swimming.
- Laith Miller tries to give Aquila Octavio, Aurora Chase, and Charlie Kasey very special times because of Zoey Chantal's polymorphic light eruption; while Charlie Janya and Zoey Chantal are in London, Laith takes Charlie Kasey, Aurora Chase, and Aquila Octavio on a trip to Barcelona. However, Charlie Kasey does not like the Barcelona trip because Charlie Janya does not come along, feels very jealous of Zoey Chantal's special attention from Charlie Janya and Laith, and does not want to help Laith with the twins on the trip. While ten-year-old twins Aurora Chase and Aquila Octavio are building sand castles on the beach in Barcelona, Laith and Charlie Kasey have a conversation about the trip and her siblings; Charlie Kasey tells him the half truth that she does not like visiting Shane in London, and Laith does not push her to ask for the reason.
- In Barcelona, inquisitive twins Aquila Octavio and Aurora Chase ask Laith many questions about Charlie Kasey's frequent running.
- During their Barcelona trip, Laith takes ten-year-old twins Aquila Octavio and Aurora Chase on a tour of Camp Nou stadium and museum and buys them FC Barcelona jerseys.
- Following their time in Barcelona, Laith takes Aquila Octavio, Aurora Chase, and Charlie Kasey to London to drop Charlie Kasey off at Shane's West End apartment for her scheduled custody visit before returning home to Aberdeen.
- When Laith and the twins return to Aberdeen and reunite with Charlie Janya and Zoey Chantal, the twins hug Charlie Janya and Zoey Chantal, the children go away together discussing their individual trips, and Charlie Janya and Laith have a conversation, too.

- Charlie Janya and Laith buy a house for their family in Aberdeen. A private school is available for Charlie Kasey, Aurora Chase, and Aquila Octavio, although the school has not yet been selected by name. No school is chosen for six-month-old Zoey Chantal because she is still a baby. Aberdeen has excellent doctors who can evaluate Zoey Chantal and help Charlie Janya and Laith determine the precautions and treatment needed for her polymorphic light eruption. Shane moved to London immediately after his breakup with Charlie Janya, so Charlie Kasey’s move to Aberdeen makes it easier for her to see him. Charlie Janya has British citizenship through her father, Charlie, and the children have British citizenship through their family, allowing them to move to Scotland. Sabrina Bryna approves Charlie Janya and Laith’s request to use a Key Corporation airplane for the move.
- The family moves from Las Vegas to Aberdeen, Scotland, during the summer because of school schedules. Charlie Kasey is ten, Aurora Chase and Aquila Octavio are five, and Zoey Chantal is six months old. Charlie Kasey moves with Charlie Janya and Laith but does not like the move. Aurora Chase and Aquila Octavio are indifferent to it, while Zoey Chantal is still a baby. The reduced sunlight in the Scottish climate greatly improves Zoey Chantal’s polymorphic light eruption. Shane and Laith are British citizens from England and do not need visas to move between England and Scotland. Charlie Janya and Laith use Charlie Janya’s trust fund to support the family in Scotland. They ask Sabrina Bryna for permission to use one of Key Corporation’s airplanes for the move.
- Aquila Octavio is a child of Charlie Janya and Laith.
- Aquila Octavio and Aurora Chase are twins.
- Aquila Octavio is Charlie Kasey’s maternal half-sibling.
- Aquila Octavio has a younger or older sibling named Zoey Chantal.
- The family moves from Las Vegas to Scotland because Zoey Chantal has polymorphic light eruption.
