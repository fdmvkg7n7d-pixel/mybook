# Binita Souparnika — Individual Backstory Working File

## Confirmed Facts

- Binita Souparnika is the sixth child of Jordan Lisa and Pehlaj Singh.
- Jordan Lisa is thirty-five and Pehlaj Singh is thirty-six when she is born.
- During the pregnancy, Jordan Lisa and Pehlaj Singh learn that Binita Souparnika has severe phocomelia affecting all four limbs.
- Pehlaj Singh's family pressures Jordan Lisa to have an abortion because they regard Binita Souparnika's condition as a source of family shame.
- The story identifies that reaction as disability prejudice and rejects the family's attempt to pressure Jordan Lisa.
- Jordan Lisa refuses to end the pregnancy, and Pehlaj Singh supports her decision.
- Jordan Lisa and Pehlaj Singh believe that Binita Souparnika's life is important.
- Jordan Lisa already loves Binita Souparnika during the pregnancy.
- Jordan Lisa believes that her daughter can live a full and meaningful life with severe limb differences and appropriate support.
- Binita Souparnika is born with severe phocomelia affecting all four limbs.
- Phocomelia is a rare congenital condition in which the long bones of the limbs are absent or severely shortened, with the hands or feet positioned close to the torso.
- Binita Souparnika's limbs are profoundly shortened or malformed rather than completely absent.
- Her limb difference does not diminish her personhood, intelligence, relationships, or place within the family.
