## Confirmed Facts

- Chairat Seni Junior attends American Pacific International School.
- Because Taylor Raven and Chairat Narissara are concerned about danger in Chiang Mai, Chairat Seni Junior stays at the school’s Inthanon House during the school week.
- Chairat Seni Junior does not stay at Inthanon House on weekends.
- Chairat Seni Junior is one of Taylor Raven’s four children.
- Chairat Narissara is Chairat Seni Junior’s father.
- Chairat Narissara is born in Bangkok on May 31.
- Chairat Narissara is the president of Key Corporation’s division in Chiang Mai, Thailand.
- Taylor Raven is twenty-two and Chairat Narissara is thirty-eight when Taylor Raven becomes pregnant.
- Taylor Raven and Chairat Narissara first meet by pure accident when Chairat Narissara bumps into Taylor Raven in York University’s cafeteria, causing her to spill her lunch tray. Taylor Raven is angry, but she accepts Chairat Narissara’s kind offer to buy her a new lunch.
- Chairat Seni Junior is conceived after Taylor Raven and Chairat Narissara attend York University’s Spring Fling Dance at the end of March and sleep together at Chairat Narissara’s apartment. The scene remains non-graphic.
- Chairat Seni Junior is born in Chiang Mai, Thailand, on January 1.
- Chairat Seni Junior is thirteen years old when twins Khorawun Pathama and Dhipyamongkol Ramkamhaeng, later known as Adranuch Chaisee, are born in Chiang Mai.
- Khorawun Pathama is born on May 31, and Dhipyamongkol Ramkamhaeng is born on June 1.
- Thiang Manya-Phathon is born in Chiang Mai, Thailand, on February 28.
- Taylor Raven and Chairat Narissara have a very excellent relationship.
- Taylor Raven and Chairat Narissara move to Thailand after Taylor Raven’s graduation ceremony at York University.
- Sabrina Bryna and William are Chairat Seni Junior’s grandparents.
- Chairat Seni Junior’s siblings are Thiang Manya-Phathon, Adranuch Chaisee, and Khorawun Pathama. Adranuch Chaisee and Khorawun Pathama are twins. Adranuch Chaisee is originally named Dhipyamongkol Ramkamhaeng and begins living as a girl under her chosen name at age five with Taylor Raven and Chairat Narissara’s complete support.
- Taylor Raven’s family lives in Thailand.

## First Meeting with Karolina Dadeshkeliani and Akilina Dadeshkeliani

- Karolina Dadeshkeliani and Akilina Dadeshkeliani are sisters who attend American Pacific International School.
- Karolina Dadeshkeliani and Chairat Seni Junior are in grade one, while Akilina Dadeshkeliani is in grade two.
- They meet on the Sunday before school starts inside Chairat Seni Junior’s bedroom at Inthanon House.
- Karolina Dadeshkeliani and Akilina Dadeshkeliani enter because they want to say hello to their new neighbor, Chairat Seni Junior.
- Taylor Raven is with Chairat Seni Junior and helps him put his clothes away.
- Karolina Dadeshkeliani has amniotic band syndrome. During fetal development, an amniotic band constricts her right arm and restricts its blood flow.
- Karolina Dadeshkeliani is born with a congenital transverse right upper-limb deficiency below the elbow and has no right hand or right forearm below the elbow.

- Chairat Seni Junior attends grade one at American Pacific International School.
- While Chairat Seni Junior is in grade one, Taylor Raven is twelve weeks pregnant with Thiang Manya-Phathon.
- Taylor Raven and Chairat Narissara tell Chairat Seni Junior about the pregnancy together.

## Sabrina Bryna and William’s Bangkok Visit

- Taylor Raven tells Chairat Seni Junior that Sabrina Bryna and William will visit Bangkok in December after visiting Australia.
