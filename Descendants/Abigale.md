# Abigale — Individual Backstory Working File

## Confirmed Facts

- Abigale is Aquila Octavio's girlfriend.
- Abigale was born in London, England.
- Her birthday is August 13.
- Abigale is the same age as Aquila Octavio and Aurora Chase.
- Her father is Cirk McMarden, who is a Capoeira instructor and runs or teaches at a Capoeira studio in London.
- Abigale does not take Capoeira because it is not her cup of tea.
- Since childhood, Abigale has been attending The Royal Ballet School in London.
- Abigale has sometimes visited her father, Cirk McMarden, at his Capoeira studio.
- When Aquila Octavio moves to London to attend Queen Mary University of London (where he studies English Literature and Linguistics and aspires to be a speech-language pathologist), he joins Cirk McMarden's Capoeira studio; this is how Aquila Octavio and Abigale meet each other, and they become a couple.
- Aquila Octavio is a son of Charlie Janya and Laith Miller and the twin brother of Aurora Chase.
- Charlie Kasey is Aquila Octavio's older maternal half-sister.
- Thirteen years after their childhood trips, when Aquila Octavio learns about what happened to Charlie Kasey at Shane's London apartment and with Luther Tucer, he starts crying and Abigale helps him.
