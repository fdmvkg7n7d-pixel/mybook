# Victor Mayfyeld

## Questions to Answer Later

## Confirmed Facts

- Victor Mayfyeld travels to Pineapple Barrens himself to confront Shane and Luther Tucer; Victor’s mob permanently eliminates both Shane and Luther Tucer at Pineapple Barrens, and their bodies are never found.
- Victor Mayfyeld gently reassures Charlie Kasey that she is safe and will never have to worry about Shane or Luther again, without giving her violent specifics.
- When Luther Tucer faces Victor Mayfyeld's men, Luther tries to run away, but five of Victor's men confront and apprehend him.
- Victor Mayfyeld is his complete legal name.
- Pineapple Barrens is the mob's problem-solving location where Victor Mayfyeld's men take Shane and Luther Tucer.
- The rival mob that orders Victor Mayfyeld's assassination is from Manchester; they want Victor's territory.
- The leader of the rival Manchester mob is involved in extensive criminal enterprises ranging from money laundering to selling drugs, as well as operating a prostitution ring; Luther Tucer is an enforcer for this Manchester mob leader.
- Luther's boss lives outside of Manchester and has ten young women who service him for anything he would like to; when Luther takes Charlie Kasey to Manchester, Charlie Kasey meets Luther's boss, who likes Charlie Kasey and pays Luther twenty thousand Pounds to take her off his hands, an offer Luther accepts despite knowing how awful his boss could be. Luther tells Charlie Kasey all about him and his syndicate, which later provides Victor Mayfyeld with the intelligence that leads to Luther Tucer's and Shane's downfall.
- The assassination attempt happens while Charlie Kasey and Victor Mayfyeld are in the bed together; Victor responds by issuing the same assassination order against his rival mob.
- Charlie Kasey remains with Victor Mayfyeld because she has fallen in love with him so much.
- Seventeen-year-old Charlie Kasey enters Victor Mayfyeld's strip club because there is an amateur night and she wants to dance at the amateur night.
- When seventeen-year-old Charlie Kasey tells Victor Mayfyeld about the poker games and what Shane and Luther Tucer did, Victor responds in a caring and loving manner toward her, hugging her stomach from behind her.
- Charlie Kasey learns that she should not ask Victor Mayfyeld any questions about the mob.
- Charlie Kasey does not know anything about Luther Tucer and Shane being taken by Victor Mayfyeld's men.
- Victor Mayfyeld was born on April 30 in London, England, and is English. He is twenty-five when seventeen-year-old Charlie Kasey enters his strip club and meets him, and they begin dating. Victor Mayfyeld is a lieutenant in the mob. Charlie Kasey discovers his mob involvement while they are in bed together and a rival mob from Manchester orders his assassination because they want Victor's territory. Victor Mayfyeld is trustworthy, caring, and loving toward Charlie Kasey’s feelings. Charlie Kasey does not ask Victor Mayfyeld to take action against Shane or Luther Tucer. Charlie Janya and Laith do not know that Charlie Kasey is involved with Victor Mayfyeld.
- Charlie Kasey is seventeen years old when she meets Victor Mayfyeld and they begin dating.
- Luther Tucer is English. Luther Tucer and Shane are both forty when thirteen-year-old Charlie Kasey is taken to her bedroom. Charlie Kasey’s future boyfriend is Victor Mayfyeld. Victor Mayfyeld’s men take Luther Tucer and Shane to a place used by the mob to deal with its problems; what happens there remains unresolved.
- Victor Mayfyeld eventually becomes Charlie Kasey’s boyfriend.
- Victor Mayfyeld is involved in the mob.
- Charlie Kasey keeps Shane’s and Luther Tucer’s conduct secret until she tells Victor Mayfyeld.
- Victor Mayfyeld’s men later confront Luther Tucer.
- Charlie Kasey’s involvement with a person in the mob becomes part of the story’s central conflict.
- Thirteen years after the Manchester visit, Victor accompanies Charlie Kasey when she visits Charlie Janya and Laith's house; when Zoey Chantal comes into the kitchen and asks Charlie Kasey and Victor whether, if she wasn't born, Shane wouldn't do these awful things to Charlie Kasey, Charlie Kasey walks into the kitchen and tells Zoey Chantal that she shouldn't question herself because Shane was an awful father who was in debt for poker games. Charlie Kasey asks them whether they can forgive her for what she did to their family, and Charlie Janya, Laith, and Zoey Chantal immediately hug Charlie Kasey.
