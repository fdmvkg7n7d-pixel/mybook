# Jordan Lisa — Individual Backstory Working File

## Family and Education

- **Birthday:** December 25
- Jordan Lisa is one of Ashley Erin and Georgi's five adopted children.
- Ashley Erin and Georgi adopt Jordan Lisa on January 30, 2077.
- Ashley Erin and Georgi are both thirty-nine at the time of the adoption.
- Jordan Lisa shares a biological mother with Hadleigh Patterson and Britany Madelena but has a different biological father.
- Jordan Lisa is interracial.
- Jordan Lisa attends North London Collegiate School whenever she is not filming.
- When she is making a movie, she studies on the movie set so her education continues during production.
- Jordan Lisa begins playing the violin at four.
- She becomes an actor like her adoptive mother, Ashley Erin.

## The Gates of Morning

- At fourteen, Jordan Lisa accepts a role in the controversial movie *The Gates of Morning* with Ashley Erin and Georgi's permission.
- The movie adapts H. De Vere Stacpoole's novel of the same title.
- Jordan Lisa plays Katafa, the principal girl associated with the island of Karolin in the source story.
- The controversy concerns race, culture, colonialism, and the portrayal of South Pacific peoples.
- Jordan Lisa's performance remains age-appropriate and does not sexualize her.

## Jordan Lisa and Pehlaj Singh

- Pehlaj Singh comes from Goa, India.
- Jordan Lisa's relationship with him increases her interest in travelling to India.
- Jordan Lisa and Pehlaj Singh are both thirty when they get married.
- Their wedding ceremony takes place in Panaji on December 31.
- Their wedding celebration lasts five days.
- Pehlaj Udvansh, Bahudha Ekshika, Chaidya Maahir, Rajalakshmi Eakam, and Kaashin Fraavash attend their parents' wedding.
- During the wedding celebration, Pehlaj Udvansh and Bahudha Ekshika are nine years old.
- Chaidya Maahir and Rajalakshmi Eakam are six years old.
- Kaashin Fraavash is two years old.
- Jordan Lisa and Pehlaj Singh have six children in total.

## Diwali

- Diwali becomes an important family celebration for Jordan Lisa after she marries Pehlaj Singh.
- The celebration connects Jordan Lisa and their children with Pehlaj Singh's family background in Goa, India.
- The family lights diyas and creates rangoli designs.
- Their celebration includes fireworks and firecrackers, handled safely and used only where legally permitted.
- Jordan Lisa and Pehlaj Singh give presents to the children.
- The family shares sweets and a five-course dinner.
- The story treats Diwali respectfully rather than using it only as decoration.

## The Children's Education and Move to London

- Pehlaj Udvansh, Bahudha Ekshika, Chaidya Maahir, Rajalakshmi Eakam, and Kaashin Fraavash attend Mayoor Chopasni School.
- When Binita Souparnika is five, Chaidya Maahir and Rajalakshmi Eakam are sixteen, Kaashin Fraavash is twelve, and Pehlaj Udvansh and Bahudha Ekshika are nineteen.
- When Binita Souparnika is approaching school-entry age, Jordan Lisa and Pehlaj Singh decide to move the family to London.
- By that time, Pehlaj Udvansh and Bahudha Ekshika leave the family home to attend university.
- They make the move so Binita Souparnika can receive suitable education and accessibility support within the school system.
- Chaidya Maahir, Rajalakshmi Eakam, and Kaashin Fraavash hate having to move to London but understand why the family is doing it for Binita Souparnika.
- All five older siblings are fiercely protective of Binita Souparnika.
- Binita Souparnika uses her voice to complete classroom tests and later formal examinations, with voice-recognition or transcription accommodations recording her answers.
