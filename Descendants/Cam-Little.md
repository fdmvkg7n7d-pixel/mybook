## Confirmed Facts

- Cam Little is a thirteen-year-old girl held by Chris Smyth’s cult at its Bali compound.
- Cam Little is born on April 23.
- Cam Little’s parents are Trey Little and Jenny Little.
- Cam Little is the only child of Trey Little and Jenny Little.
- Cam Little is from Los Angeles, California.
- Chris Smyth’s cult kidnaps Cam Little from Los Angeles when she is thirteen and traffics her to the Bali compound.
- Cam Little is held at the compound for four days.
- Cam Little strongly resists Tucker Sullivan and refuses his sexual demands. Her resistance is never portrayed as wrongdoing and does not make her responsible for his violence.
- Tucker Sullivan murders Cam Little.
- Tucker Sullivan murders Cam Little after she refuses his sexual demands; the event remains completely non-graphic.
- Jordan Lily is ten years old when she witnesses Tucker Sullivan murder Cam Little.
- Cam Little’s murder is kept completely non-graphic.
- Jordan Lily is not responsible for Cam Little’s murder and is unable to prevent it while being held captive by the cult.
- Tucker Sullivan is charged with and convicted of Cam Little’s murder, in addition to kidnapping, prolonged unlawful confinement, human trafficking and exploitation, forced child marriage, and other cult-related offences.
- Cam Little’s murder supports Tucker Sullivan’s life sentence in Indonesia.
- After the rescue, Jordan Lily provides police with extensive information she absorbed about the cult, its members, and its operations.
- Authorities locate Cam Little’s family and inform them of her death.
- Ten years after the rescue, when Jordan Lily is twenty-five, she personally tracks down Cam Little’s family and apologizes because she could not save Cam Little.
- Cam Little’s parents tell Jordan Lily that she has nothing to apologize for because she was only ten years old and could not have stopped Tucker Sullivan.
- Jordan Lily names her firstborn daughter, [Cam Jamie](Cam-Jamie.md), in honour of Cam Little.
- Cam Jamie is eleven when Jordan Lily meets Trey Little and Jenny Little.
- Cam Jamie accompanies Jordan Lily to the meeting.
- Jordan Lily answers Cam Jamie’s questions about her name, biological background, birth, and family history honestly and in an age-appropriate way.
