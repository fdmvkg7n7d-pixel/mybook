## Confirmed Facts

- When Madeleine Jaycee Key is three, Carlota Delicia and Keith ask Taylor Raven and Adelisa to become her godmothers, partly because Adelisa’s juvenile idiopathic arthritis means additional support may be important. Carlota Delicia and Keith legally designate both women as Madeleine Jaycee Key’s guardians if they die. Carlota Delicia and Keith are killed in a vehicle crash when Madeleine Jaycee Key is fifteen and she and Caesarius Lavigne are preparing for their first Winter Olympic Games. Madeleine Jaycee Key continues living happily in Orlando with Caesarius Lavigne and Adelisa, who provides day-to-day care. Taylor Raven shares legal guardianship, provides continuing emotional support, and remains available for Madeleine Jaycee Key to call at any time. Chairat Narissara is happy that Taylor Raven is helping her niece, Madeleine Jaycee Key.
- **Birthday:** May 31
- Chairat Narissara is born in Bangkok, Thailand.
- Chairat Narissara is the president of Key Corporation’s division in Chiang Mai, Thailand.
- Chairat Narissara is thirty-eight during the York University Spring Fling storyline.
- Chairat Narissara is the father of Chairat Seni Junior.
- All four of Taylor Raven and Chairat Narissara’s children attend American Pacific International School.
- Because Taylor Raven and Chairat Narissara are concerned about danger in Chiang Mai, they decide that their children will stay at the school’s Inthanon House during the school week.
- The children do not stay at Inthanon House on weekends.

## Business Visit and First Meeting with Taylor Raven

- Before Chairat Narissara meets Taylor Raven, he does not know Sabrina Bryna personally.
- Chairat Narissara visits Key Corporation for business meetings.
- During the visit, Chairat Narissara recruits potential employees at York University.
- Chairat Narissara and Taylor Raven meet by pure accident in York University’s cafeteria.
- Chairat Narissara accidentally bumps into Taylor Raven, causing her to spill her lunch tray.
- Taylor Raven is angry with Chairat Narissara.
- Chairat Narissara kindly offers to buy Taylor Raven a new lunch.
- Taylor Raven accepts the offer despite still being angry.

## Spring Fling and Pregnancy

- Taylor Raven is twenty-two and Chairat Narissara is thirty-eight.
- They attend York University’s Spring Fling Dance at the end of March.
- After the dance, Taylor Raven and Chairat Narissara sleep together at Chairat Narissara’s apartment. The scene remains non-graphic.
- Taylor Raven becomes pregnant with Chairat Seni Junior.
- Chairat Seni Junior is born in Chiang Mai on January 1.
## Chairat Seni Junior Meets Karolina Dadeshkeliani and Akilina Dadeshkeliani

- Karolina Dadeshkeliani and Akilina Dadeshkeliani are sisters who attend American Pacific International School.
- Karolina Dadeshkeliani and Chairat Seni Junior are in grade one, while Akilina Dadeshkeliani is in grade two.
- They meet on the Sunday before school starts inside Chairat Seni Junior’s bedroom at Inthanon House.
- Karolina Dadeshkeliani and Akilina Dadeshkeliani enter because they want to say hello to their new neighbor, Chairat Seni Junior.
- Taylor Raven is with Chairat Seni Junior and helps him put his clothes away.
- Karolina Dadeshkeliani has amniotic band syndrome, which causes a congenital transverse right upper-limb deficiency below the elbow. She has no right hand or right forearm below the elbow.
- Chairat Seni Junior and Karolina Dadeshkeliani begin dating in grade nine. Akilina Dadeshkeliani loves Chairat Seni Junior deeply and tries to sabotage their relationship, though the method remains to be established.

## Birthdays of Thiang Manya-Phathon and the Twins

- Thiang Manya-Phathon is born in Chiang Mai, Thailand, on February 28.
- Khorawun Pathama and Dhipyamongkol Ramkamhaeng, later known as Adranuch Chaisee, are twins born in Chiang Mai.
- Khorawun Pathama is born on May 31.
- Dhipyamongkol Ramkamhaeng is born on June 1.
- Chairat Seni Junior is thirteen years old when the twins are born.

## Adranuch Chaisee’s Gender Identity

- Adranuch Chaisee and Khorawun Pathama are twins.
- Adranuch Chaisee is assigned male at birth and is originally named Dhipyamongkol Ramkamhaeng.
- At age five, Dhipyamongkol Ramkamhaeng tells Taylor Raven and Chairat Narissara during a deeply emotional conversation that she feels she is living in the wrong body and wants to live as a girl like Khorawun Pathama.
- Taylor Raven and Chairat Narissara see how unhappy their child is while being treated as a boy.
- They accept their daughter without a single doubt. She chooses the name Adranuch Chaisee and uses she/her pronouns afterward.

## Relationship and Move to Thailand

- Taylor Raven is twelve weeks pregnant with Thiang Manya-Phathon when Taylor Raven and Chairat Narissara tell Chairat Seni Junior about the pregnancy.
- Chairat Seni Junior is then in grade one at American Pacific International School.
- Taylor Raven and Chairat Narissara have a very excellent relationship.
- After Taylor Raven’s graduation ceremony at York University, Taylor Raven and Chairat Narissara move to Thailand.
