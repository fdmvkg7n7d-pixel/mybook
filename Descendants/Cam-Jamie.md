# Cam Jamie — Individual Backstory Working File

## Confirmed Facts

- Cam Jamie is Jordan Lily’s firstborn daughter.
- Cam Jamie is her complete legal name.
- Cam Jamie is Roberta Katherine’s granddaughter.
- Jordan Lily names Cam Jamie in honour of thirteen-year-old Cam Little, whose murder Jordan Lily witnessed during captivity.
- Cam Jamie is born on January 31 at the cult’s Bali compound.
- Jordan Lily is fourteen when Cam Jamie is born.
- Jordan Lily and Cam Jamie receive poor and inadequate health care during the birth at the compound.
- Tucker Sullivan is Cam Jamie’s biological father.
- Tucker Sullivan fathers Cam Jamie through sexual abuse while Jordan Lily is a child captive in the cult’s forced child marriage.
- Jordan Lily cannot consent; the abuse and pregnancy remain completely non-graphic and are never Jordan Lily’s fault.
- Cam Jamie is approximately one year old when the joint Indonesian–Australian operation rescues Roberta Katherine, Jordan Lily, and Cam Jamie from the compound.
- After the rescue, Cam Jamie receives excellent, age-appropriate care and support.
- Cam Jamie and Roberta Katherine develop an exceptionally close, loving relationship as granddaughter and grandmother.
- Cam Jamie is eleven when twenty-five-year-old Jordan Lily personally meets Cam Little’s parents, Trey Little and Jenny Little.
- Cam Jamie accompanies Jordan Lily to that meeting.
- Whenever Cam Jamie asks Jordan Lily a question about her name, biological background, birth, or family history, Jordan Lily answers her honestly in an age-appropriate way.
