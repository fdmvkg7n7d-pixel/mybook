## Confirmed Facts

- Adranuch Chaisee attends American Pacific International School.
- Because Taylor Raven and Chairat Narissara are concerned about danger in Chiang Mai, Adranuch Chaisee stays at the school’s Inthanon House during the school week.
- Adranuch Chaisee does not stay at Inthanon House on weekends.
- **Birthday:** June 1
- Adranuch Chaisee is born in Chiang Mai, Thailand, under the birth name Dhipyamongkol Ramkamhaeng.
- Twin sister Khorawun Pathama is born on May 31, shortly before Adranuch Chaisee’s June 1 birth.
- Chairat Seni Junior is thirteen years old when the twins are born.
- Adranuch Chaisee is one of Taylor Raven and Chairat Narissara’s four children.
- Adranuch Chaisee and Khorawun Pathama are twins.
- Chairat Seni Junior and Thiang Manya-Phathon are their older siblings.
- Sabrina Bryna and William are their grandparents.
- Adranuch Chaisee and Khorawun Pathama are two years old when Taylor Raven learns about Sabrina Bryna and William’s December visit to Bangkok.

## Early Identity and Chosen Name

- Adranuch Chaisee is assigned male at birth and is originally named Dhipyamongkol Ramkamhaeng.
- Before age five, the family uses the name Dhipyamongkol Ramkamhaeng and he/him pronouns.
- At age five, Dhipyamongkol Ramkamhaeng feels that she is living in the wrong body and tells Taylor Raven and Chairat Narissara that she wants to live as a girl like her twin sister, Khorawun Pathama.
- She has a deeply emotional conversation with Taylor Raven and Chairat Narissara about her gender identity.
- Taylor Raven and Chairat Narissara can see how unhappy their child is while being treated as a boy.
- Taylor Raven and Chairat Narissara accept their daughter without a single doubt and support her request to live as a girl.
- She chooses the name Adranuch Chaisee and uses she/her pronouns from that conversation onward.

## Sabrina Bryna and William’s Bangkok Visit

- At two years old, before she chooses the name Adranuch Chaisee, Dhipyamongkol Ramkamhaeng is too young to understand what Sabrina Bryna and William’s visit means and shows no particular reaction to the news.
