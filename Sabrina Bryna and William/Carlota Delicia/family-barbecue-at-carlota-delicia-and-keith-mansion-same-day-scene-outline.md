# Scene Outline: Family Barbecue at Carlota Delicia and Keith’s Mansion

## Placement

**When:** The same day as Carlota Delicia’s Blizzard Beach outing and Sabrina Bryna and William’s kitchen conversation about Danica’s Budapest crisis.

**Where:** **Carlota Delicia and Keith’s mansion** in Orlando, mainly the kitchen, patio, backyard / barbecue area, and nearby family rooms.

**Characters:** Carlota Delicia, Keith, Sabrina Bryna, William, Taylor Raven, Sun-Young, Roberta Katherine, William Timothy Junior, Madeleine Jaycee Holiday Key, **Adelisa**, and **Caesarius**.

**Offstage / referenced:** Orin Lorin Junior, Danica, Pandora Paige, Danica’s father, the canceled Budapest movie shoot.

## Scene Purpose

1. Bring the local family group back together at **Carlota Delicia and Keith’s mansion** on the same day.
2. Show the whole family having a **barbecue** after the Blizzard Beach outing and the kitchen crisis conversation.
3. Let Carlota Delicia return from Blizzard Beach with Taylor Raven, Sun-Young, and Madeleine Jaycee.
4. Keep Roberta Katherine and William Timothy Junior present as the twins who stayed at the mansion and had earlier come into the kitchen arguing like they do.
5. Include **Adelisa** and **Caesarius** at the barbecue.
6. Show Sabrina Bryna and William carrying the emotional weight of Danica’s crisis while still participating in family life.
7. Let Keith host through practical steadiness: grill, food, drinks, seating, shade, and ordinary hospitality.
8. Preserve the boundary that the children do not need to carry the full adult Danica crisis.
9. Let Taylor Raven and Sun-Young thank Sabrina Bryna and William for taking them on this vacation.
10. Give the day a domestic family shape after multiple emotionally heavy scenes.

---

## Core Emotional Throughline

The day has split into two worlds.

At Blizzard Beach, Carlota gave Taylor Raven and Sun-Young a safe, fun water-park day and told them she would do anything for them because she loves them so much.

At the mansion, Sabrina Bryna and William absorbed the news that Pandora Paige canceled Danica’s Budapest movie shoot after Danica’s father ambushed her in the foyer, revealed she was adopted, and sent her running into a bathroom.

By barbecue time, the family is physically back together at Carlota Delicia and Keith’s mansion. The children feel the day as water, food, cousins, and the smell of a grill. Adelisa and Caesarius are there too, part of Carlota and Keith’s household rhythm. The adults feel the day as both family dinner and quiet emergency response.

The barbecue does not erase the crisis. It gives the family somewhere to stand while the crisis is being handled.

## Scene Structure

### Beat 1: Returning From Blizzard Beach

- Carlota Delicia returns to the mansion with Taylor Raven, Sun-Young, and Madeleine Jaycee.
- They are wet-haired, tired, sun-warmed, and carrying the smell of water park sunscreen.
- Madeleine Jaycee is sleepy or overstimulated in the way a small child can be after a water-park day.
- Taylor Raven and Sun-Young are still grateful and emotionally softened by Carlota’s care.
- The Blizzard Beach day has given them a bright contrast to the heavier adult events happening elsewhere.

### Beat 2: The Mansion Has Shifted

- When they return, the mansion feels different.
- Sabrina Bryna and William have already had the kitchen conversation about Danica’s crisis.
- Roberta Katherine and William Timothy Junior are still around, possibly calmer after Sabrina’s one-word **“Enough.”**
- The twins’ earlier argument has not disappeared from the family texture, but the house has settled into barbecue preparation.
- Carlota reads the adults’ faces and knows something serious has happened.
- Sabrina and William do not unload the full details onto Taylor Raven, Sun-Young, or the twins.

### Beat 3: Keith Starts the Barbecue

- Keith takes practical control of the barbecue.
- He prepares the grill and sets out food.
- Possible food:
  - burgers;
  - hot dogs;
  - grilled chicken;
  - corn;
  - vegetables;
  - fruit;
  - chips;
  - lemonade / iced tea / water;
  - something simple for Madeleine Jaycee.
- Keith’s steadiness matters: he is not trying to fix Danica’s crisis; he is giving the household an ordinary meal.
- Carlota helps while still keeping an eye on Madeleine.

### Beat 4: The Whole Local Family Gathers

- The whole local family group is back at the mansion:
  - Sabrina Bryna;
  - William;
  - Carlota Delicia;
  - Keith;
  - Taylor Raven;
  - Sun-Young;
  - Roberta Katherine;
  - William Timothy Junior;
  - Madeleine Jaycee;
  - **Adelisa**;
  - **Caesarius**.
- Orin Lorin Junior and Danica are not physically present because they are in Budapest.
- Their absence is emotionally present for Sabrina and William.
- The younger children experience the gathering as a family barbecue, not as a crisis meeting.

### Beat 5: Adelisa and Caesarius Are There Too

- **Adelisa** is present at the barbecue.
- **Caesarius** is there too.
- Adelisa and Caesarius are part of Carlota and Keith’s household life, not surprise guests.
- No one needs to make room for Adelisa; she already belongs in the mansion’s daily rhythm.
- Caesarius’s presence adds another child’s rhythm to the evening.
- Adelisa helps as part of the household routine in ways that fit her body and energy.

### Beat 6: Taylor Raven and Sun-Young Stay Near Carlota

- Taylor Raven and Sun-Young drift toward Carlota naturally after the Blizzard Beach outing.
- They already thanked her for taking them.
- Carlota’s line from the water park — that she would do anything for them because she loves them so much — still sits warmly between them.
- Taylor Raven may help set plates or carry drinks.
- Sun-Young may help with Madeleine Jaycee or sit close and watch.
- Carlota lets them help without turning them into little adults.

### Beat 7: The Twins at the Barbecue

- Roberta Katherine and William Timothy Junior recover quickly from being shut down in the kitchen.
- They are still the twins.
- They may begin a new argument over:
  - who gets the first burger;
  - who sits where;
  - whether a hot dog counts as barbecue;
  - who gets to hold a plate first;
  - whether Madeleine Jaycee smiled at one of them more.
- Sabrina’s earlier **“Enough”** remains enough of a warning that they do not escalate too far.
- The twins’ bickering gives the barbecue ordinary family noise.

### Beat 8: Sabrina Bryna and William Hold the Adult Line

- Sabrina and William are present at the barbecue, but their emotional attention keeps returning to Danica.
- They may step aside for a short call or message update from Pandora Paige / Orin.
- They keep the adult information contained.
- Sabrina is still carrying guilt about sending Danica into a lion’s den.
- William continues grounding her without minimizing what happened.
- They both keep watching the children eat and laugh, aware of how much a family must hold at once.

### Beat 9: Carlota Notices Sabrina

- Carlota notices Sabrina’s emotional state.
- She does not demand details in front of the children.
- A small adult-to-adult look passes between Carlota and Sabrina.
- Carlota may quietly ask if Danica is safe.
- Sabrina gives the simplest possible answer: the shoot is canceled, Pandora is handling the studio side, and Orin is with Danica.
- Carlota understands enough not to ask more at the table.
- This shows Carlota as an adult daughter who knows how to read a room.

### Beat 10: Barbecue as a Holding Pattern

- The barbecue becomes a holding pattern.
- Food is served.
- Kids eat.
- Madeleine Jaycee needs attention, which helps keep everyone grounded in ordinary tasks.
- Caesarius is part of the child noise / family rhythm too.
- Taylor Raven and Sun-Young are tired from Blizzard Beach and quieter than usual.
- During the barbecue, Taylor Raven and Sun-Young thank Sabrina Bryna and William for taking them on this vacation.
- Their thanks is simple and sincere: the vacation, Blizzard Beach, and being with Carlota Delicia’s family all feel special to them.
- Roberta Katherine and William Timothy Junior are hungry and noisy.
- Keith keeps the grill moving.
- Carlota keeps the household rhythm intact.
- Sabrina and William stay in the family space while still waiting for updates.

### Beat 11: Taylor Raven and Sun-Young Thank Sabrina Bryna and William

- At a quieter point in the barbecue, Taylor Raven and Sun-Young turn their attention to Sabrina Bryna and William.
- They thank Sabrina Bryna and William for taking them on this vacation.
- The thanks should feel sincere and natural, not formal.
- Taylor Raven may be more direct and enthusiastic.
- Sun-Young may be quieter but just as sincere.
- Sabrina Bryna and William receive the thanks warmly.
- The moment gives Sabrina and William a small emotional counterweight to the Danica crisis: even on a hard day, the vacation is giving their children good memories.

### Beat 12: A Quiet Toast / Family Moment

- No alcohol is necessary.
- The family may raise lemonade / water / iced tea.
- Keith can say something simple about everyone being back at the house.
- Carlota may keep it very plain: everyone eats, everyone rests, and nobody has to solve everything before dinner.
- The moment should not become a formal speech.
- It is a barbecue, not a press conference.

### Beat 13: The Children Feel Safe

- Taylor Raven and Sun-Young feel safe after a long water-park day.
- Roberta Katherine and William Timothy Junior feel safe enough to argue, eat, and be themselves.
- Madeleine Jaycee feels safe because her routine is being protected.
- Caesarius is included in the child-safe family atmosphere.
- The children do not need to know the full shape of Danica’s crisis to benefit from the adults holding it.
- That is the emotional purpose of the barbecue.

### Beat 14: Closing Image

- Possible closing image:
  - Keith at the grill;
  - Carlota with Madeleine Jaycee nearby;
  - Adelisa and Caesarius included in the family barbecue rhythm;
  - Taylor Raven and Sun-Young eating quietly after Blizzard Beach and after thanking Sabrina Bryna and William for the vacation;
  - Roberta Katherine and William Timothy Junior mid-argument but quieter after one look from Sabrina;
  - Sabrina Bryna and William standing together in the warm evening light, carrying Danica’s crisis but not letting it swallow the family whole.
- The scene ends with the whole local family back at Carlota Delicia and Keith’s mansion, held together by barbecue smoke, ordinary noise, and adults who know what must stay adult.
