# Scene Outline: Carlota Delicia Takes Taylor Raven, Sun-Young, and Madeleine Jaycee to Blizzard Beach

## Placement

**When:** The same day and same general time that Sabrina Bryna and William are in the kitchen at **Carlota Delicia and Keith’s mansion** talking about Danica’s Budapest crisis.

**Where:** **Disney’s Blizzard Beach** water park at Walt Disney World.

**Characters:** Carlota Delicia, Taylor Raven, Sun-Young, Madeleine Jaycee Holiday Key.

**Parallel Scene:** This scene runs at the same time as `../Orin Lorin Junior and Danica/18 years/sabrina-bryna-william-conversation-about-danica-budapest-crisis-scene-outline.md`.

---

## Scene Purpose

1. Show Carlota Delicia taking **Taylor Raven**, **Sun-Young**, and **Madeleine Jaycee** to **Disney’s Blizzard Beach**.
2. Establish that Blizzard Beach is the second Walt Disney World water park option after Typhoon Lagoon.
3. Establish that because Carlota Delicia is a Disney employee, they can get into the park free.
4. Give Taylor Raven and Sun-Young a water-park day away from the adult crisis happening at the mansion.
5. Show Carlota Delicia as a confident adult, mother, and Disney employee who knows how to navigate the park.
6. Show Madeleine Jaycee having a safe, age-appropriate water-park experience.
7. Keep this scene parallel to Sabrina Bryna and William’s conversation about Danica’s Budapest crisis, without making the children responsible for that crisis.

---

## Core Emotional Throughline

While Sabrina Bryna and William are in the kitchen at Carlota Delicia and Keith’s mansion handling the heavy news about Danica, Carlota Delicia gives Taylor Raven, Sun-Young, and Madeleine Jaycee a completely different kind of day.

Blizzard Beach is bright, loud, wet, silly, and crowded. The ski-resort-in-Florida theme makes the world feel playful instead of dangerous. Carlota knows the park because she works for Disney. She can move through it with confidence.

Taylor Raven and Sun-Young get to be teenagers / older girls at a water park instead of witnesses to another adult emergency. Madeleine Jaycee gets held, splashed, shaded, fed, changed, and watched with the fierce practical care of a mother who knows exactly where every exit, bathroom, baby-care space, and quiet corner is.

The emotional contrast matters: family crisis at the mansion; water, sunscreen, laughter, and vigilance at Blizzard Beach.

---

## Scene Structure

### Beat 1: Leaving Carlota Delicia and Keith’s Mansion

- Carlota Delicia decides to take Taylor Raven, Sun-Young, and Madeleine Jaycee to **Blizzard Beach**.
- Sabrina Bryna and William remain at Carlota Delicia and Keith’s mansion.
- This is the same time frame as Sabrina Bryna and William’s kitchen conversation about Danica’s Budapest crisis.
- Roberta Katherine and William Timothy Junior are not part of this water-park outing; they remain back at the mansion and later come into the kitchen arguing like they do.
- Carlota packs carefully for a water-park day:
  - towels;
  - sunscreen;
  - water bottles;
  - snacks;
  - spare clothes;
  - Madeleine Jaycee’s baby / toddler supplies;
  - swim diapers if needed;
  - a sun hat or cover-up for Madeleine;
  - anything Taylor Raven or Sun-Young needs for comfort.
- Carlota is not frantic. She is practical.

### Beat 2: Cast Member Admission

- At Blizzard Beach, Carlota uses her Disney employee access / cast member benefit.
- Because Carlota Delicia is an employee, **they can get into the park free**.
- The free entry should feel like a practical benefit of her Disney work, not a magical loophole.
- Taylor Raven and Sun-Young are impressed because the park feels like a special privilege.
- Carlota is matter-of-fact about it: this is one of the advantages of working for Disney.

### Beat 3: Entering Blizzard Beach

- The girls enter the park and take in the Blizzard Beach theme.
- The joke of a melted ski resort in Florida lands immediately:
  - fake snow;
  - ski-lift theming;
  - bright water;
  - tropical heat;
  - families in swimwear moving past winter-themed signs.
- Taylor Raven reacts with energy and curiosity.
- Sun-Young is quieter but observant.
- Madeleine Jaycee is focused on water, sound, movement, and being close to Carlota.
- Carlota checks sight lines, shade, and where the nearest bathrooms / changing spaces are.

### Beat 4: Choosing a Home Base

- Carlota finds a shaded area as their home base.
- She chooses a place that works for Madeleine Jaycee first.
- Taylor Raven and Sun-Young help carry towels or bags.
- Carlota sets clear rules:
  - no disappearing without telling her;
  - stay together or check in before separating;
  - drink water;
  - reapply sunscreen;
  - Madeleine Jaycee’s needs come first;
  - if anyone feels overwhelmed, they stop.
- The rules are calm, not controlling.
- Taylor Raven and Sun-Young accept them because Carlota sounds like someone who knows the park.

### Beat 5: First Water Moment for Madeleine Jaycee

- Carlota brings Madeleine Jaycee to a gentle water area appropriate for her age.
- Taylor Raven and Sun-Young stay close.
- Madeleine Jaycee reacts to the splash, sound, and movement.
- Carlota holds her securely and reads her face carefully.
- The moment should feel ordinary and tender:
  - small hands in water;
  - baby/toddler surprise;
  - Carlota smiling despite watching everything;
  - Taylor Raven and Sun-Young trying to make Madeleine laugh.
- Carlota does not let the older girls turn it into too much stimulation.
- Safety and joy stay together.

### Beat 6: Taylor Raven and Sun-Young Explore

- Once Madeleine Jaycee is settled and safe, Taylor Raven and Sun-Young are allowed to enjoy more of the park.
- They may do a lazy river, wave pool edge, or age-appropriate slides depending on comfort.
- Carlota keeps the rules clear.
- Taylor Raven may be more excited and physically bold.
- Sun-Young may prefer to watch first, then try.
- Carlota allows them fun while keeping check-ins structured.
- The girls are not responsible for Madeleine Jaycee, but they can help in small ways.

### Beat 7: The Lazy River / Cross Country Creek Beat

- A good family-friendly option is the lazy river / Cross Country Creek style experience.
- Carlota can choose it because it lets everyone move together without overwhelming Madeleine Jaycee.
- Taylor Raven and Sun-Young can float near Carlota.
- Madeleine Jaycee can be held safely or kept in an appropriate child-safe arrangement depending on park rules and age.
- The slow current gives the scene a calmer middle.
- Taylor Raven jokes about the snow theme in Florida.
- Sun-Young notices how relaxed Carlota looks when she is navigating a Disney environment she understands.

### Beat 8: Parallel Cut — The Mansion Conversation Exists Elsewhere

- This scene does not need to show Sabrina Bryna and William’s full conversation.
- However, the outline should acknowledge the parallel:
  - while Carlota is at Blizzard Beach with Taylor Raven, Sun-Young, and Madeleine Jaycee;
  - Sabrina Bryna and William are back at Carlota Delicia and Keith’s mansion in the kitchen;
  - they are processing Danica’s Budapest crisis;
  - Roberta Katherine and William Timothy Junior later come into the kitchen arguing like they do.
- The water-park scene should not turn Taylor Raven or Sun-Young into messengers of that adult crisis.
- The contrast is enough.

### Beat 9: Lunch / Snack Break

- Carlota calls a break before anyone is too tired.
- They eat something simple at Blizzard Beach.
- Carlota makes sure everyone drinks water.
- Madeleine Jaycee gets fed / changed / settled according to her needs.
- Taylor Raven and Sun-Young talk about what they want to do next.
- Carlota listens, but she keeps the day paced around Madeleine Jaycee’s limits.

### Beat 10: A Moment of Carlota’s Motherhood

- Carlota has a quiet moment with Madeleine Jaycee.
- She may hold her in a towel, kiss her wet hair, or rock her in the shade.
- Taylor Raven and Sun-Young see Carlota as a mother, not only as their older sister / family member.
- The scene can quietly show how far Carlota has come:
  - she is safe;
  - she is working for Disney;
  - she is a mother;
  - she can bring children to a water park and manage the whole day.
- Do not over-explain her trauma, but let the contrast exist.

### Beat 11: One More Water Activity

- If Madeleine Jaycee is still comfortable, Carlota lets the outing continue.
- If Madeleine is tired, Carlota chooses shade / quiet over more attractions.
- Taylor Raven and Sun-Young can take one more supervised turn at an attraction.
- Carlota makes the choice without guilt.
- The point is not to “do everything.”
- The point is that the outing is safe, free, and good.

### Beat 12: Heading Back

- Carlota decides when it is time to leave.
- The girls may want more time, but they accept her decision.
- Towels are gathered.
- Wet hair, sunscreen smell, tired legs, and water-park noise all mark the end of the outing.
- Carlota uses her employee knowledge again to make the exit smooth.
- The free admission has made the day possible without making it feel cheap or careless.
- Before they leave, **Taylor Raven and Sun-Young thank Carlota Delicia** for taking them to Blizzard Beach.
- Their thanks matters because Carlota did not only give them free admission; she gave them a safe, fun day away from the adult crisis at the mansion.
- Carlota tells the girls that she would do anything for them because she loves them so much.
- The line lands warmly and sincerely, not dramatically; this is Carlota being an older sister / adult family anchor.

### Beat 13: Closing Image

- Possible closing image:
  - Carlota carrying or pushing sleepy Madeleine Jaycee out of Blizzard Beach;
  - Taylor Raven and Sun-Young walking beside her, wet-haired and tired, after thanking Carlota Delicia and hearing her say she would do anything for them because she loves them so much;
  - the fake snow / ski-resort theming behind them;
  - Carlota already thinking about baths, dry clothes, and getting everyone back to the mansion safely.
- The scene closes with the awareness that while heavy adult conversations happened elsewhere, Carlota gave these children a water-park day.

> Nobody disappears. You tell me before you go anywhere.

> Madeleine decides our pace today.

> I would do anything for you girls because I love you so much.

> It looks like snow, but it is hot.

> This is so weird. I love it.

> Can we do one more thing?

> Thank you for bringing us, Carlota.

### Sun-Young

> I want to watch first.

> Madeleine likes the water.

> This is better than I thought it would be.
