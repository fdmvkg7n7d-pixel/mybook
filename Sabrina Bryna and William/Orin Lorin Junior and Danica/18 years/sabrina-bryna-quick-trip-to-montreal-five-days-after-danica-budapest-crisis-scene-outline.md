**When:** Five days after Danica’s Budapest crisis and the canceled movie shoot.

**Where:** **Ferme Clé**, the farm in Quebec where Orin Lorin Junior and Danica are staying.

**Characters:** Sabrina Bryna, Orin Lorin Junior, Danica.

**Offstage / referenced:** William, Pandora Paige, Danica’s father, Key Porn Studios, the canceled Budapest movie shoot, Carlota Delicia and Keith’s mansion.

## Scene Purpose

1. Show Sabrina Bryna making a quick trip to **Ferme Clé**, the farm where Orin Lorin Junior and Danica are staying, five days after Danica’s Budapest crisis.
2. Show Sabrina going in person to see Orin Lorin Junior and Danica instead of only calling.
3. Continue Sabrina’s guilt that she feels she sent Danica into a lion’s den, while preserving William’s grounding that Sabrina did not say what Danica’s father said.
4. Let Sabrina support Danica after the adoption revelation.
5. Let Sabrina affirm that the canceled movie shoot is not Danica’s failure.
6. Keep the Budapest movie voluntary: Danica wanted to be the star, and that truth still matters, but any future work must come only from Danica’s fresh, willing choice.
7. Establish that Danica still wants to be in the movies and wants to be a star without any single doubt because she wants to prove something to her parents, especially to her adopted father.
8. Show Orin as Danica’s steady emotional anchor.
9. Keep the scene focused on family support, career choice, adoption shock, and emotional repair.

## Core Emotional Throughline

Sabrina Bryna cannot leave the crisis as a phone call.

Five days after Pandora Paige canceled the Budapest movie shoot, Sabrina gets herself to **Ferme Clé** quickly because Danica needs to see, not just hear, that the family has not withdrawn from her.

Danica was hurt in two ways at once: her father shamed her in the foyer before the film, and he revealed that she was adopted in the cruelest possible way. Sabrina understands that no executive summary, no incident report, and no studio protocol can hold that kind of damage by itself.

So Sabrina goes to the farm.

She does not go to fix Danica in one conversation. She goes to sit with her, tell her the shoot is not her failure, tell her the movie was voluntary and still belongs to Danica’s choice, and make clear that being adopted does not make Danica less loved, less real, or less herself.

Danica still wants to be in the movies. She wants to be a star without any single doubt. Part of that desire is now tangled with wanting to prove something to her parents, especially to her adopted father. Sabrina does not dismiss the desire, but she refuses to let Danica confuse proving herself with owing anyone her pain.

## Scene Structure

### Beat 1: Sabrina Decides She Has to Go

- Five days have passed since the Budapest crisis.
- Sabrina has already backed Pandora Paige’s executive decision to cancel the movie shoot.
- Sabrina has already spoken with William about the crisis at Carlota Delicia and Keith’s mansion.
- William told Sabrina she should not feel that guilty because she did not say what Danica’s father said.
- Sabrina accepts that intellectually, but emotionally she still feels the weight of having sent Danica into a lion’s den.
- She decides that a phone call is not enough.
- She quickly arranges a trip to **Ferme Clé** to see Orin Lorin Junior and Danica in person.

### Beat 2: The Quick Trip to Ferme Clé

- Sabrina travels quickly and deliberately.
- The trip is not framed as a vacation or planned family visit.
- It is a protective trip.
- Sabrina brings only what she needs:
  - her phone;
  - any necessary executive notes;
  - perhaps the incident summary;
  - and the emotional clarity that Danica will not be treated as a failed performer.
- William may remain offstage but knows why she is going.
- Sabrina is not going to interrogate Danica.
- Sabrina is going to see her.

### Beat 3: Arrival at the Farm

- Sabrina arrives at **Ferme Clé**, where Orin and Danica are staying.
- Orin meets her first or opens the door.
- He is tired, protective, and still carrying the emotional aftermath of the bathroom crisis.
- Sabrina reads his face immediately.
- Orin does not need to over-explain for Sabrina to know that Danica is still fragile.
- The first exchange between Sabrina and Orin should be quiet and exact.

### Beat 4: Orin’s Update

- Orin tells Sabrina how Danica has been since Budapest.
- He focuses on practical and emotional facts:
  - Danica is safe;
  - she has been eating / sleeping only unevenly if that is true;
  - she is still processing the adoption revelation;
  - she is not ready for career decisions;
  - she knows Pandora canceled the shoot;
  - she knows Orin stayed with her.
- Orin does not speak over Danica’s feelings.
- He gives Sabrina enough to understand the ground before Sabrina sees Danica.
- Sabrina respects his steadiness.

### Beat 5: Sabrina Sees Danica

- Danica is emotionally raw.
- She may be quiet, exhausted, embarrassed, angry, numb, or moving between all of those states.
- Sabrina does not begin with studio business.
- Sabrina begins with care.
- Because Sabrina Bryna is deaf and uses ASL, communication should be accessible:
  - Sabrina signs;
  - Orin can bridge ASL / spoken language only if needed and only in a way that does not make Danica feel managed;
  - written text / phone notes can be used if Danica needs direct wording she can hold.
- Sabrina makes sure Danica receives the message clearly: Sabrina came because Danica matters.

### Beat 6: The First Thing Sabrina Says

- Sabrina does not ask Danica whether she still wants the film.
- Sabrina does not ask for an explanation of the bathroom crisis.
- Sabrina does not ask Danica to comfort her guilt.
- Sabrina’s first message is simple:
  - Danica did nothing wrong;
  - the canceled shoot is not Danica’s failure;
  - Danica is not responsible for her father’s cruelty;
  - being adopted does not make Danica less herself;
  - Sabrina and William love her.

### Beat 7: Sabrina Names Her Own Guilt Carefully

- Sabrina may tell Danica that she feels guilty she sent her into a lion’s den.
- She must not make Danica responsible for soothing that guilt.
- If Sabrina says it, she frames it as accountability, not as a request for forgiveness.
- Orin may be present, steady and watchful.
- Danica may not know what to say.
- Sabrina makes clear that Danica does not have to answer that.

### Beat 8: Danica and Adoption Shock

- Danica’s central wound is that she did not know she was adopted.
- The fact itself is shocking.
- The way she learned it is devastating.
- Sabrina does not pretend the adoption reveal is small.
- She also refuses to let Danica’s father define adoption as shame.
- Sabrina says that adoption is not a stain, not a lesser form of belonging, and not a word that should have been thrown at her like a weapon.
- Sabrina does not force Danica to talk about biological family, records, or next steps immediately.
- She offers help later, only if Danica wants it.

### Beat 9: The Voluntary Movie and Danica’s Career

- Sabrina brings up the career question only after emotional safety is established.
- She says the Budapest movie was voluntary.
- She acknowledges that Danica wanted to be the star.
- She also says that wanting the movie does not mean Danica had to work through a traumatic revelation.
- Pandora Paige canceled the shoot correctly.
- Danica tells Sabrina that she still wants to be in the movies.
- Danica is clear that she wants to be a star without any single doubt.
- Danica admits that part of her wants to prove something to her parents, especially to her adopted father.
- Sabrina does not shame that feeling.
- Sabrina also does not let Danica build her worth around proving something to people who hurt her.
- Sabrina tells Danica that if she stays in the movies, it must be because Danica chooses the work for herself, not because her adopted father called her a word or tried to reduce her.
- If Danica ever wants to reopen the Budapest movie later, it will require Danica’s fresh, willing yes.
- Sabrina will protect Danica’s boundaries and treat the work as voluntary adult work.
- Danica’s future career belongs to Danica, and Danica’s stated choice is that she wants to be a star.

### Beat 10: Orin’s Support

- Orin remains close but does not dominate the conversation.
- Sabrina sees that Orin is doing what William trusted him to do: anchoring Danica without controlling her.
- Orin may tell Sabrina that he will stay with Danica whatever she decides.
- The scene should echo the Eiffel Tower dinner in Paris: Orin already knew Danica wanted to be the star, and he backed her up because he loved her.
- Now he is backing her up through the cancellation and adoption shock too.

### Beat 11: What Sabrina Offers

- Sabrina offers practical help without forcing decisions:
  - legal / records help if Danica wants to learn more about her adoption;
  - therapist / counselor support;
  - studio performer-care support;
  - time away from work;
  - a protected way to speak with Pandora Paige again.
- Sabrina does not overwhelm Danica with options.
- She offers them as doors, not demands.

### Beat 12: A Quiet Family Moment

- The visit becomes less formal after the hardest points are said.
- Maybe they drink tea, water, or coffee.
- Maybe Danica sits close to Orin.
- Maybe Sabrina sits across from them and lets silence be useful.
- The point is that Sabrina came quickly and physically entered the room where Danica was hurting.
- Danica does not have to perform strength.

### Beat 13: Closing Image

- Possible closing image:
  - Sabrina sitting with Orin and Danica at Ferme Clé;
  - Danica tired but no longer alone with the adoption revelation;
  - Orin beside her, steady;
  - Sabrina’s hands still for once, not signing, not issuing instructions, simply present.
- The scene ends with no demand for a schedule decision, but Danica’s career desire is clear: she still wants to be in the movies and wants to be a star.
- The win is that Danica has been seen, believed, and protected in person.
