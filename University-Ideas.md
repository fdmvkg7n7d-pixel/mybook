### 13. York University

### 14. Universitat de Barcelona

- **Location:** Barcelona, Catalonia, Spain
- **Character:** Haley Alexis
- **Field:** Law
- **Career goal:** Prosecutor in the specialized vulnerable-victims section of the Barcelona Provincial Prosecutor’s Office
- **Established detail:** Haley Alexis attends the Universitat de Barcelona to study law. Joaquin’s family opposes the relationship, and Kiko and Querida want Joaquin to become involved with his second cousin Agota. Kiko supports the plan, and Adelina, Abdénago, Fruxtuoso, and Honesto knowingly help plan every part of the crime. Querida pays Jan, Kiko’s brother, to sexually assault Haley Alexis. After Haley Alexis has lived in Barcelona for eighteen months, Jan carries out the assault, which includes vaginal and anal penetration for legal classification but is never portrayed graphically. Haley Alexis reports the crime, and police arrest Kiko, Querida, Adelina, Abdénago, Fruxtuoso, Honesto, and Jan in connection with the plan and assault. The family deliberately exploits Haley Alexis’s isolation as a foreign student, believing that being far from her home and family will make her easier to intimidate. The prosecution seeks the maximum lawful penalty for each accused person based on that person’s proven role and emphasizes this calculated exploitation. The trial court convicts Kiko, Querida, Adelina, Abdénago, Fruxtuoso, Honesto, and Jan. The presiding judge delivers the judgment imposing the maximum lawful sentence available for each person’s proven role. After the criminal proceedings and appeals are complete, Haley Alexis passes Spain’s professional legal-access examination and qualifies as a lawyer. She is later assigned to work as a court legal assistant in the same presiding judge’s court, helping with legal research and case preparation before pursuing her prosecutorial career. The university fully supports Haley Alexis as a foreign student and assigns a counselor to her. Joaquin finds Haley Alexis afterward and supports her without a single doubt. When he learns that his family was involved, he feels disgusted by them and their actions. Joaquin is deeply upset and asks Haley Alexis whether she wants to break up with him because of what his family did. Haley Alexis hugs Joaquin very tightly because she loves him and tells him, **“Don’t be silly. Your family is not you. I want to stay in this relationship with you.”** The non-graphic event strengthens her determination to prosecute sexual violence, child abuse, and other crimes involving vulnerable victims.
- **Still to decide:** the remaining details of the path Haley Alexis follows to qualify as a prosecutor.

## Australia

### 15. Western Australian Academy of Performing Arts at Edith Cowan University

- **Location:** Perth, Western Australia
- **Character:** Roberta Katherine
- **Program:** Bachelor of Arts (Dance)
- **Established detail:** Roberta Katherine graduates from Victoria Ballet Academy in June and begins a dance-and-sightseeing journey through Australia in Hobart. She and William Timothy Junior expect a simple Christmas at their Alice Springs hotel until Sabrina Bryna and William secretly travel there and surprise them. She then starts university the following February and completes the three-year contemporary-dance-focused degree, with ballet, performance, choreography, and academic study supporting her training.
- **Career after graduation:** Roberta Katherine becomes a professional dancer with a resident performing-arts company that regularly rehearses and performs at the Sydney Opera House. After both twins graduate, Roberta Katherine and William Timothy Junior share a house in Sydney. Roberta Katherine and Joseph Corowa are both twenty-two when they meet at ivy Sydney. They begin dating one month later. Eighteen months after they begin dating, William Timothy Junior offers to move out of the Sydney house he shares with Roberta Katherine because he is moving into Candice’s apartment. Joseph Corowa, a Constable with the Australian Federal Police, moves into the house with Roberta Katherine. William Timothy Junior is happy and reassured about leaving because he trusts Joseph Corowa to treat Roberta Katherine well. Roberta Katherine and Joseph Corowa have a daughter, Cassandra Penelope, who is born on July 1 and is the same age as MacKenzi Bree. After Roberta Katherine and Joseph Corowa separate, they share custody of Cassandra Penelope across Sydney and Perth. Cassandra Penelope alternates Christmas, New Year’s Eve, and Easter between her parents each year: she spends all three holidays with one parent in one year and with the other parent the following year. During Joseph Corowa’s years, Cassandra Penelope travels from Sydney to Perth to celebrate with him. When Joseph Corowa is thirty-one, he accepts a position in the Special Crimes Unit in Perth. Roberta Katherine does not want to relocate, so she remains in Sydney. Their disagreement ends their relationship, and Joseph Corowa moves to Perth. Roberta Katherine knows that she must remain emotionally strong for Cassandra Penelope during the breakup and separation. When Cassandra Penelope is eight years old, Roberta Katherine meets Chris Smyth at a ballet function. Roberta Katherine and Chris Smyth begin dating immediately. Joseph Corowa recognizes Chris Smyth because Joseph Corowa’s Australian Federal Police Special Crimes Unit is investigating him. Joseph Corowa immediately informs his supervisors that his former partner is dating the subject of an investigation, allowing them to manage the personal connection and any potential conflict of interest. The investigation does not itself establish Chris Smyth’s guilt. Chris Smyth is the head of a cult, and the Australian Federal Police Special Crimes Unit investigates suspected criminal activity connected to the cult. Joseph Corowa’s supervisors instruct him not to reveal the investigation to Roberta Katherine or Cassandra Penelope, so Joseph Corowa cannot warn them about Chris Smyth. Roberta Katherine and Chris Smyth have a daughter, Jordan Lily, who is born in Sydney when Cassandra Penelope is ten years old. Cassandra Penelope is extremely excited when she learns about Roberta Katherine’s pregnancy because she is going to become a big sister. Jordan Lily is two years old when Chris Smyth is arrested. Roberta Katherine promptly ends the relationship and retains custody of Jordan Lily. While Chris Smyth is held in jail awaiting trial, Roberta Katherine does not permit him to see Jordan Lily and does not arrange contact. Chris Smyth is later convicted of serious offences connected to operating his cult, including the cult’s promotion of child marriages. He is sentenced in Western Australia to life imprisonment without the possibility of parole. While serving the life sentence, Chris Smyth bribes prison guards, escapes custody, and flees to Bali, Indonesia. The corrupt guards conceal the escape, so Roberta Katherine and Joseph Corowa are not told that Chris Smyth is free. Cassandra Penelope and Jordan Lily also remain unaware of the escape. Chris Smyth remains hidden in Bali until the joint rescue operation at the cult compound. During the winter school break, seven-year-old Jordan Lily travels with Roberta Katherine while seventeen-year-old Cassandra Penelope and MacKenzi Bree and sixteen-year-old Haley Alexis visit Barcelona. Roberta Katherine takes Jordan Lily to visit Chris Smyth’s parents. Chris Smyth’s father repeatedly violates Jordan Lily’s personal boundaries and subjects her to inappropriate sexual touching. The abuse remains completely non-graphic and is never Jordan Lily’s fault. Roberta Katherine notices Jordan Lily’s discomfort, recognizes the abuse, immediately intervenes, and removes Jordan Lily from the situation. The experience deepens Roberta Katherine’s suspicion that Chris Smyth’s parents belong to the same cult as their son. Roberta Katherine and Jordan Lily then travel to Bali for the remainder of the winter break, unaware that Chris Smyth is hiding there after his prison escape. Chris Smyth learns that they are in Bali and orders members of his cult to kidnap them. Tucker Sullivan, Chris Smyth’s right-hand man, leads the cult members who kidnap Roberta Katherine and Jordan Lily in Bali and hold them at the cult’s compound. Taylor Raven is visiting Madeleine Jaycee Key and Madeleine Jaycee Key’s boyfriend, Caesarius Lavigne, in Orlando when she learns that Roberta Katherine and Jordan Lily have disappeared. Madeleine Jaycee Key is Carlota Delicia’s daughter. Taylor Raven immediately flies to Barcelona, personally tells Cassandra Penelope about the kidnapping, and stays to protect seventeen-year-old Cassandra Penelope, seventeen-year-old MacKenzi Bree, and sixteen-year-old Haley Alexis. Their captivity lasts about eight years, from the time Jordan Lily is seven until she is fifteen. When Jordan Lily is nine years old, the cult forces her into a child marriage with fifty-year-old Tucker Sullivan, Chris Smyth’s right-hand man. Jordan Lily is a child and cannot freely consent; the forced union is abuse and is not treated as a valid marriage or personal choice. All related material remains non-graphic and is never romanticized. When Jordan Lily is ten years old, she witnesses Tucker Sullivan murder thirteen-year-old Cam Little at the Bali compound. Cam Little’s murder remains completely non-graphic and is never Jordan Lily’s fault. Cam Little is the only child of Trey Little and Jenny Little and is from Los Angeles. She is kidnapped and trafficked to the Bali compound at thirteen. After four days in captivity, Cam Little strongly resists Tucker Sullivan and refuses his sexual demands. Tucker Sullivan murders her; Cam Little’s resistance is never portrayed as wrongdoing and never makes her responsible for his violence. After the rescue, Jordan Lily gives police extensive information she absorbed about the cult and its operations. Authorities locate Cam Little’s family and inform them of her death. Ten years after the rescue, when Jordan Lily is twenty-five, she personally finds Cam Little’s family and apologizes because she could not save Cam Little. Cam Little’s parents reassure Jordan Lily that she has nothing to apologize for because she was only ten and could not have prevented the murder. Jordan Lily names her firstborn daughter, Cam Jamie, in honour of Cam Little. Cam Jamie is born on January 31 at the Bali compound when Jordan Lily is fourteen. Jordan Lily and Cam Jamie receive poor and inadequate health care during the birth. Tucker Sullivan is Cam Jamie’s biological father and fathers her through sexual abuse while Jordan Lily is a child captive in the forced child marriage. Jordan Lily cannot consent; the abuse and pregnancy remain completely non-graphic and are never Jordan Lily’s fault. Cam Jamie is approximately one when the joint operation rescues Roberta Katherine, Jordan Lily, and Cam Jamie. After the rescue, Cam Jamie receives excellent, age-appropriate care and support. Cam Jamie and Roberta Katherine develop an exceptionally close, loving relationship as granddaughter and grandmother. Cam Jamie is eleven when twenty-five-year-old Jordan Lily meets Trey Little and Jenny Little. Cam Jamie accompanies Jordan Lily to the meeting. Whenever Cam Jamie asks Jordan Lily about her name, biological background, birth, or family history, Jordan Lily answers honestly in an age-appropriate way. Jordan Lily witnesses Chris Smyth repeatedly sexually assault Roberta Katherine during captivity. The repeated assaults are described only non-graphically, never imply Roberta Katherine’s consent, and are never Roberta Katherine’s or Jordan Lily’s fault. Jordan Lily repeatedly supports and comforts Roberta Katherine afterward and remains emotionally close to her. Roberta Katherine is surprised and deeply moved by how kind Jordan Lily is to her despite Jordan Lily’s own trauma. Jordan Lily’s loving support strengthens their bond, while trained professionals remain responsible for providing both survivors with trauma-informed care. Indonesian police lead a lawful joint rescue operation with support from the Australian Federal Police, rescuing Roberta Katherine, fifteen-year-old Jordan Lily, and approximately one-year-old Cam Jamie from the compound. Authorities capture Chris Smyth during the joint operation and return him to Australia, where he resumes serving his life sentence without the possibility of parole. Cassandra Penelope feels profound guilt because she was not with Roberta Katherine and Jordan Lily when they were kidnapped or during their captivity. Cassandra Penelope is not responsible: she was seventeen, was travelling in Barcelona, did not know what was happening, and could not have prevented the cult’s crimes. After the rescue, the Australian government provides Roberta Katherine and Jordan Lily with fully funded, intensive, full-time trauma therapy and recovery support. Jordan Lily also receives medical care, security protection, and comprehensive victim-support services. Tucker Sullivan is arrested during the rescue operation. Indonesian prosecutors charge him with directing organized kidnapping, prolonged unlawful confinement, trafficking and exploitation of an adult and a child, forced child marriage and sexual violence against a child, preparing kidnapped girls for cult control and forced marriages, and the murder of thirteen-year-old Cam Little and aggravated trafficking resulting in her death. Tucker Sullivan is convicted and sentenced to life imprisonment in Indonesia. The cult’s forced child-marriage ceremony has no legal validity, so Jordan Lily does not need an annulment or other legal process to end it. Even so, Jordan Lily finds freedom extremely difficult because she continues to feel psychologically trapped in the forced marriage. She resumes her education very slowly and at a pace guided by her recovery needs. Jordan Lily is born on December 31. When Jordan Lily is six, Roberta Katherine begins telling her the truth about Chris Smyth in small, age-appropriate pieces rather than all at once. Jordan Lily responds by hugging Roberta Katherine. Jordan Lily and Cassandra Penelope have an excellent relationship as sisters.
- **Still to decide:** Roberta Katherine’s strongest dance specialties, important university events, the resident company, and specific productions.
