# Desiree Arissa and Riley Leah — Sisters

## Confirmed Facts

- Desiree Arissa Hegsmith was born on July 31 in Davis, California.
- Riley Leah Hegsmith was born on August 4 in Davis, California.
- Desiree Arissa is older than Riley Leah.
- Desiree Arissa loves Capoeira and playing violin, and wants to attend the United States Air Force Academy.
- Riley Leah is a follower who follows the twins (Vera Sophie and Desiree Arissa) everywhere, and attends the United States Coast Guard Academy.
- Desiree Arissa and Riley Leah share a bedroom while growing up.
- Desiree Arissa and Riley Leah are close to their older maternal half-sister, Merissa Carolynn Fernández; Riley Leah was only six months old when Rebecca Brazilia died and has no memory of her mother, so she has a lot of questions about Rebecca Brazilia for Merissa Carolynn.
- During the family's Las Vegas trip to support Charlie Janya and Charlie Kasey, Riley Leah was not yet born, and Desiree Arissa was an eighteen-month-old toddler.
- As their big sister, Merissa Carolynn Fernández supports Vera Sophie, Desiree Arissa, and Riley Leah because they are too young to fully understand Rebecca Brazilia’s death.
- Rebecca Brazilia’s stage three breast cancer eventually becomes terminal, and she is dying from the disease. Rebecca Brazilia asks Luis whether Merissa Carolynn Fernández can remain with Patrick and her younger sisters, Vera Sophie, Desiree Arissa, and Riley Leah. Luis agrees because he does not want to disrupt Merissa Carolynn Fernández’s schooling or friendships. Luis trusts Patrick without hesitation and knows he can continue visiting Merissa Carolynn Fernández and taking her on many vacations.
- Vera Sophie and Desiree Arissa are identical twin daughters of Rebecca Brazilia and Patrick. Both twins are eighteen months old when Rebecca Brazilia takes them and Merissa Carolynn Fernández to Las Vegas. Riley Leah is not yet born at the time of the visit and is later born as a singleton. Merissa Carolynn Fernández is seven years old during the trip.
- Desiree Arissa and Riley Leah are daughters of Rebecca Brazilia and Patrick.
- Riley Leah is a singleton and is not Desiree Arissa’s twin.
- Merissa Carolynn Fernández is their maternal half-sister.
- Merissa Carolynn Fernández is thirteen years old when Rebecca Brazilia is pregnant with Riley Leah and is diagnosed with stage three breast cancer.
