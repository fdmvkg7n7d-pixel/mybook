## Confirmed Facts

- After Roberta Katherine and William Timothy Junior have both graduated from their universities, the twins share a house in Sydney.
- The shared house allows Roberta Katherine and William Timothy Junior to live together while they begin their professional dance careers at the Sydney Opera House.
- Joseph Corowa is born on May 15 in the same year as Roberta Katherine and William Timothy Junior.
- Joseph Corowa serves as a Constable, the entry-level sworn police rank, with the Australian Federal Police.
- Roberta Katherine and Joseph Corowa are both twenty-two when they meet at **ivy Sydney**. They begin dating one month later. Eighteen months after they begin dating, William Timothy Junior offers to move out of the Sydney house he shares with Roberta Katherine because he is moving into Candice’s apartment. William Timothy Junior is happy and reassured about leaving because Joseph Corowa is a police officer with the Australian Federal Police and William Timothy Junior trusts him to treat Roberta Katherine well.
- The twins’ shared-house arrangement ends when William Timothy Junior moves into Candice’s apartment.
- Joseph Corowa then moves into the Sydney house with Roberta Katherine.
- Roberta Katherine and Joseph Corowa have a daughter, [Cassandra Penelope](../Descendants/Cassandra-Penelope.md).
- Cassandra Penelope is born on July 1 and is the same age as MacKenzi Bree.
- After the separation, Roberta Katherine and Joseph Corowa share custody of Cassandra Penelope across Sydney and Perth.
- Cassandra Penelope alternates Christmas, New Year’s Eve, and Easter between her parents each year: she spends all three holidays with one parent in one year and with the other parent the following year. During Joseph Corowa’s years, Cassandra Penelope travels from Sydney to Perth to celebrate with him.
- When Joseph Corowa is thirty-one, he accepts a position in the Special Crimes Unit in Perth. Roberta Katherine does not want to relocate, so she remains in Sydney. Their disagreement ends their relationship, and Joseph Corowa moves to Perth. Roberta Katherine knows that she must remain emotionally strong for Cassandra Penelope during the breakup and separation.
- When Cassandra Penelope is eight years old, Roberta Katherine meets Chris Smyth at a ballet function. Roberta Katherine and Chris Smyth begin dating immediately. Joseph Corowa recognizes Chris Smyth because Joseph Corowa’s Australian Federal Police Special Crimes Unit is investigating him. Joseph Corowa immediately informs his supervisors that his former partner is dating the subject of an investigation, allowing them to manage the personal connection and any potential conflict of interest. The investigation does not itself establish Chris Smyth’s guilt.
- Chris Smyth is the head of a cult, and the Australian Federal Police Special Crimes Unit investigates suspected criminal activity connected to the cult. Joseph Corowa’s supervisors instruct him not to reveal the investigation to Roberta Katherine or Cassandra Penelope, so Joseph Corowa cannot warn them about Chris Smyth. Roberta Katherine and Chris Smyth have a daughter, Jordan Lily, who is born in Sydney when Cassandra Penelope is ten years old. Cassandra Penelope is extremely excited when she learns about Roberta Katherine’s pregnancy because she is going to become a big sister. Jordan Lily is two years old when Chris Smyth is arrested. Roberta Katherine promptly ends the relationship and retains custody of Jordan Lily. While Chris Smyth is held in jail awaiting trial, Roberta Katherine does not permit him to see Jordan Lily and does not arrange contact. Chris Smyth is later convicted of serious offences connected to operating his cult, including the cult’s promotion of child marriages. He is sentenced in Western Australia to life imprisonment without the possibility of parole. While serving the life sentence, Chris Smyth bribes prison guards, escapes custody, and flees to Bali, Indonesia. The corrupt guards conceal the escape, so Roberta Katherine and Joseph Corowa are not told that Chris Smyth is free. Cassandra Penelope and Jordan Lily also remain unaware of the escape. Chris Smyth remains hidden in Bali until the joint rescue operation at the cult compound. Jordan Lily is born on December 31. When Jordan Lily is six, Roberta Katherine begins telling her the truth about Chris Smyth in small, age-appropriate pieces rather than all at once. Jordan Lily responds by hugging Roberta Katherine. Jordan Lily and Cassandra Penelope have an excellent relationship as sisters.
