## Confirmed Facts

- Tucker Sullivan is Chris Smyth’s right-hand man in the cult.
- Tucker Sullivan is fifty years old when the cult forces the child marriage.
- Tucker Sullivan prepares kidnapped girls for cult induction and control and for forced child-marriage ceremonies; all such material remains non-graphic.
- Tucker Sullivan murders thirteen-year-old Cam Little at the cult’s Bali compound. Jordan Lily is ten when she witnesses the murder. Cam Little, who is from Los Angeles and has been held for four days, strongly resists Tucker Sullivan and refuses his sexual demands. Tucker Sullivan murders her after that refusal. Cam Little’s resistance never makes her responsible for his violence. The event remains completely non-graphic and is never Jordan Lily’s fault.
- The cult forces nine-year-old Jordan Lily into a child marriage with Tucker Sullivan.
- Jordan Lily cannot consent to the forced union.
- The forced union is abuse, not a valid relationship or personal choice.
- The story never romanticizes the forced child marriage and keeps all related material non-graphic.
- Tucker Sullivan fathers Jordan Lily’s firstborn daughter, Cam Jamie, through sexual abuse while Jordan Lily is a child captive. Jordan Lily cannot consent.
- Cam Jamie is born on January 31 at the Bali compound when Jordan Lily is fourteen.
- Jordan Lily and Cam Jamie receive poor and inadequate health care during the birth.
- The forced ceremony is illegal and has no legal validity, so Jordan Lily does not need an annulment.
- Tucker Sullivan is arrested during the rescue operation. He is convicted of directing organized kidnapping, prolonged unlawful confinement, human trafficking and exploitation, forced child marriage, sexual violence against a child, and the murder of Cam Little and aggravated trafficking resulting in her death. He receives life imprisonment in Indonesia.
- Although legally free, Jordan Lily feels psychologically trapped in the forced marriage and struggles to adjust to freedom.
- Jordan Lily resumes her education slowly, at a pace guided by her recovery needs.
- After Jordan Lily is rescued at fifteen, the Australian government provides fully funded, intensive, full-time trauma therapy and recovery support to Jordan Lily and Roberta Katherine.
