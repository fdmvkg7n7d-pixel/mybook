## Confirmed Facts

- When Madeleine Jaycee Key is three, Carlota Delicia and Keith ask Taylor Raven and Adelisa to become her godmothers, partly because Adelisa’s juvenile idiopathic arthritis means additional support may be important. Carlota Delicia and Keith legally designate both women as Madeleine Jaycee Key’s guardians if they die. Carlota Delicia and Keith are killed in a vehicle crash when Madeleine Jaycee Key is fifteen and she and Caesarius Lavigne are preparing for their first Winter Olympic Games. Madeleine Jaycee Key continues living happily in Orlando with Caesarius Lavigne and Adelisa, who provides day-to-day care. Taylor Raven shares legal guardianship, provides continuing emotional support, and remains available for Madeleine Jaycee Key to call at any time. Chairat Narissara is happy that Taylor Raven is helping her niece, Madeleine Jaycee Key.
- The reporter is Anthony Zimmerman, who has worked for the Tampa Bay Sun for twenty-five years. Anthony Zimmerman does not take or publish a photograph of Madeleine Jaycee Key and Caesarius Lavigne. He publishes the internet article through the Tampa Bay Sun. Andrea Jennifer knows nothing about the article before it appears because she does not run the daily stories across Key Corporation’s newspapers. Andrea Jennifer cannot fire Anthony Zimmerman because of his twenty-five-year tenure and because Madeleine Jaycee Key and Caesarius Lavigne are public figures.
- Carlota Delicia calls her aunt, Andrea Jennifer, after the internet article appears. Andrea Jennifer is in charge of the internet newspaper that publishes the story about seeing fourteen-year-old Madeleine Jaycee Key and Caesarius Lavigne leave Cypress Cove.
- Carlota Delicia, Keith, and Adelisa have two trailers at Cypress Cove and teach Madeleine Jaycee Key and Caesarius Lavigne to accept their bodies. When the pair are fourteen, a reporter driving down the road sees them leave Cypress Cove and cross to the nearby Chipotle Mexican Grill, then publishes an internet article about seeing them. Madeleine Jaycee Key and Caesarius Lavigne laugh at the reporter because Cypress Cove has nothing to do with their skating abilities. Cypress Cove publicly states that children are permitted and that the resort is a safe and fun place for families.
- When Madeleine Jaycee Key and Caesarius Lavigne are fourteen, a news reporter sees them coming out of Cypress Cove in Kissimmee, Florida, as they cross the street to eat at the nearby Chipotle Mexican Grill. Carlota Delicia, Keith, and Adelisa believe the two fourteen-year-olds have every right to eat at a restaurant without being harassed or judged. This reporter is different from the announcer who later makes discriminatory remarks during a Winter Olympic Games in a United States host city.
- Carlota Delicia and Keith are Canadian citizens.
- Carlota Delicia and Keith marry.
- They are both twenty-three when Madeleine Jaycee Key and Caesarius Lavigne are born.
- Madeleine Jaycee Key is their daughter.
- Carlota Delicia and Keith invite Adelisa and two-year-old Caesarius Lavigne into their Orlando home so Adelisa has substantial practical support.
- Carlota Delicia and Keith help raise Madeleine Jaycee Key and support Adelisa in caring for Caesarius Lavigne within the shared household.
- Carlota Delicia and Keith help the children pursue pair skating despite repeated discrimination.
- Carlota Delicia and Keith give Morgan Marino the opportunity to coach Madeleine Jaycee Key and Caesarius Lavigne.
- Carlota Delicia and Keith are killed in a vehicle crash during the period surrounding one of the pair’s Winter Olympic Games in a United States host city.
- Their deaths remain non-graphic.
- An announcer broadcasts their deaths to the world and makes discriminatory remarks about Caesarius Lavigne’s deafness and the pair’s Olympic prospects.
