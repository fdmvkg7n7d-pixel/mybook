# Aquila Octavio and Abigale — Relationship Working File

## Confirmed Relationship

- Aquila Octavio Miller is a son of Charlie Janya and Laith Miller, and the secondborn twin brother of Aurora Chase.
- Abigale is Aquila Octavio's girlfriend, born in London on August 13 and the same age as Aquila Octavio and Aurora Chase.
- Abigale's father is Cirk McMarden, who runs or teaches at a Capoeira studio in London.
- Aquila Octavio moves to London to attend Queen Mary University of London, where he studies English Literature and Linguistics and aspires to be a speech-language pathologist.
- Upon moving to London for university, Aquila Octavio joins a new Capoeira studio instructed by Cirk McMarden.
- Abigale does not take Capoeira because it is not her cup of tea; since she was a child, she has been attending The Royal Ballet School in London, though she has sometimes visited her father at his Capoeira studio.
- Through Cirk McMarden's Capoeira studio, Aquila Octavio and Abigale meet each other and become a couple.
- Thirteen years after the family's childhood trips, when Aquila Octavio learns about what happened to his older maternal half-sister Charlie Kasey, he starts crying and Abigale helps and comforts him.
