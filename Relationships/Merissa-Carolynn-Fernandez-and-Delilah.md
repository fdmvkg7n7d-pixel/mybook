# Merissa Carolynn Fernández and Delilah Hamilton — Friendship Working File

## Confirmed Relationship

- Delilah Hamilton is the daughter of David Hamilton and Hadas Hamilton.
- Merissa Carolynn Fernández attends Grace Valley Christian Academy, and she and Delilah Hamilton have been best girlfriends there since Senior Kindergarten.
- Starting when they are ten years old, Merissa and Delilah have many sleepovers at each other's homes.
- By this point, Merissa and Delilah are beginning to fall in love with each other. Shortly before the summer holidays, they share an innocent kiss on the lips.
- When Merissa is fifteen, Luis takes her to La Jenny and Cap d'Agde in France, the same resorts Rebecca Brazilia previously visited. Delilah and her mother Hadas are visiting both resorts, and the girls are surprised to see each other at each one.
- At the end of the contest at La Jenny, Merissa and Delilah hold hands and tell their parents they are officially a couple. David, Hadas, and Luis tell the girls they have known for eighteen months because they have heard them talking about each other over that time.
- The girls apologize for not telling their parents sooner about their feelings and become emotional, fearing they would be in trouble for being lesbians. Luis, David, and Hadas hug them and promise to support them without a single doubt, whether they choose each other or someone else.
- Merissa asks Luis whether Patrick would support her too; Luis reassures her that Patrick would support her without a single doubt.
- David and Luis invite them to play the pool game called chicken. The girls agree and change into their swimsuit bottoms before entering the pool. Merissa sits on David's shoulders, Delilah sits on Luis's, and they try to push each other into the water.
- Before the trips, Merissa and Delilah do not know that their families are naturists.
- Luis accompanies Merissa to the mother-daughter dinners held before the Beauty Junior Contests. Several other mothers and daughters complain about Luis's presence, but Delilah and her mother Hadas do not because the girls are best girlfriends.
