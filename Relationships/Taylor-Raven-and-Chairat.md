## First Meeting at York University

- Before Chairat Narissara meets Taylor Raven, he does not know Sabrina Bryna personally.
- Taylor Raven and Chairat Narissara meet by pure accident in York University’s cafeteria.
- Chairat Narissara accidentally bumps into Taylor Raven.
- Taylor Raven spills her tray of lunch.
- Taylor Raven is angry with Chairat Narissara.
- Chairat Narissara kindly offers to buy Taylor Raven a new lunch.
- Although Taylor Raven remains angry, she accepts Chairat Narissara’s offer.
- This cafeteria accident is their first meeting.
