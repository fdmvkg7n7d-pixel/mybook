# Charlie Kasey and Victor Mayfyeld

## Confirmed Facts

- Victor Mayfyeld is his complete legal name.
- Seventeen-year-old Charlie Kasey enters Victor Mayfyeld's strip club because there is an amateur night and she wants to dance at the amateur night.
- When seventeen-year-old Charlie Kasey tells Victor Mayfyeld about the poker games and what Shane and Luther Tucer did, Victor responds in a caring and loving manner toward her, hugging her stomach from behind her.
- Charlie Kasey learns that she should not ask Victor Mayfyeld any questions about the mob.
- The assassination attempt happens while Charlie Kasey and Victor Mayfyeld are in the bed together; Victor responds by issuing the same assassination order against his rival Manchester mob.
- Charlie Kasey remains with Victor Mayfyeld after the assassination attempt and threat become known because she has fallen in love with him so much.
- Charlie Kasey does not know anything about Luther Tucer and Shane being taken by Victor Mayfyeld's men.
- When Luther Tucer faces Victor Mayfyeld's men, Luther tries to run away, but five of Victor's men confront and apprehend him.
- Shane tells Charlie Kasey that he would take Zoey Chantal out into the sunlight and let her die; this threat is why Charlie Kasey obeys Shane and keeps the poker games secret from Charlie Janya and Laith until telling Victor Mayfyeld at seventeen.
- Shane does not face any criminal consequences until Charlie Kasey tells Victor Mayfyeld everything about the poker games.
- Charlie Kasey and Victor Mayfyeld keep their relationship a secret from everyone because Victor is in the mob; consequently, Charlie Janya and Laith do not discover that they are dating.
- Charlie Kasey uses Shane's surname until she marries Victor Mayfyeld (the timing of their marriage is not yet established).
- Charlie Kasey is seventeen years old when she tells Victor Mayfyeld about the poker games and what Shane and Luther Tucer did.
- Victor Mayfyeld does not disclose anything about Shane's poker games or conduct to Charlie Janya or Laith.
- Because of what Shane did to her, Charlie Kasey does not want to see her family (including Charlie Janya) for ten years, distancing herself from them during her recovery while remaining in her relationship with Victor.
- Charlie Janya and Laith have three children together: Aurora Chase, Aquila Octavio, and Zoey Chantal. Aurora Chase and Aquila Octavio are twins. The three children are Charlie Kasey’s maternal half-siblings. Zoey Chantal has polymorphic light eruption. Because of Zoey Chantal’s condition, Charlie Janya and Laith move their family from Las Vegas to Scotland.
- Charlie Kasey loves playing the Double Bass; when she comes back from Shane's apartment and when he has poker games, she likes playing her Double Bass to forget Luther's hands.
- Victor Mayfyeld was born on April 30 in London, England, and is English. He is twenty-five when seventeen-year-old Charlie Kasey enters his strip club and meets him, and they begin dating. Victor Mayfyeld is a lieutenant in the mob. Charlie Kasey discovers his mob involvement while they are in bed together and a rival mob from Manchester orders his assassination because they want Victor's territory. Victor Mayfyeld is trustworthy, caring, and loving toward Charlie Kasey’s feelings. Charlie Kasey does not ask Victor Mayfyeld to take action against Shane or Luther Tucer. Charlie Janya and Laith do not know that Charlie Kasey is involved with Victor Mayfyeld.
- Charlie Kasey is seventeen years old when she meets Victor Mayfyeld and they begin dating.
- Luther Tucer is English. Luther Tucer and Shane are both forty when thirteen-year-old Charlie Kasey is taken to her bedroom. Charlie Kasey’s future boyfriend is Victor Mayfyeld. Victor Mayfyeld’s men take Luther Tucer and Shane to Pineapple Barrens, a place used by the mob to deal with its problems. Victor Mayfyeld travels to Pineapple Barrens himself to confront Shane and Luther Tucer; Victor’s mob permanently eliminates both Shane and Luther Tucer at Pineapple Barrens, and their bodies are never found. Victor Mayfyeld gently reassures Charlie Kasey that she is safe and will never have to worry about Shane or Luther again, without giving her violent specifics.
- Luther Tucer serves as an enforcer for the leader of the rival mob based in Manchester, whose syndicate operates everything from laundering money and selling drugs to running a prostitution ring, although Shane does not know that Luther is in the Manchester mob when Shane arranges for Luther to take Charlie Kasey to Manchester; on their drive to Manchester and during the trip, Charlie Kasey meets Luther's boss, who lives outside of Manchester and has ten young women who service him for anything he would like to. Luther's boss likes Charlie Kasey so he pays Luther twenty thousand Pounds to take her off his hands; Luther knows how awful his boss could be, but he is offering twenty thousand Pounds to have Charlie Kasey, and Luther accepts the twenty thousand Pounds. Luther tells Charlie Kasey everything about his boss, which Charlie Kasey later discloses to Victor Mayfyeld, leading directly to Luther’s downfall.
- Victor Mayfyeld eventually becomes Charlie Kasey’s boyfriend.
- Victor Mayfyeld is involved in the mob.
- Charlie Kasey tells Victor Mayfyeld what Shane and Luther Tucer did after keeping it secret from Charlie Janya and Laith.
- Victor Mayfyeld’s men later confront Luther Tucer.
- Charlie Kasey’s relationship with a person involved in the mob is a key part of the story.
- Charlie Kasey is fifteen years old when she visits Manchester with Luther Tucer.
- Thirteen years after the Manchester visit, Victor accompanies Charlie Kasey when she returns to visit Charlie Janya and Laith's house; when Zoey Chantal comes into the kitchen and asks Charlie Kasey and Victor whether, if she wasn't born, Shane wouldn't do these awful things to Charlie Kasey, Charlie Kasey walks into the kitchen and tells Zoey Chantal that she shouldn't question herself because Shane was a very awful father who was in debt for poker games. Charlie Kasey asks them whether they can forgive her for what she did to their family, and Charlie Janya, Laith, and Zoey Chantal immediately hug Charlie Kasey.
